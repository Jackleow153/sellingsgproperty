FAQ

What you must know as a first time HDB buyer

What is cash over valuation (COV)?

Cash over valuation is the amount by which the resale price you agree exceeds HDB's value of the flat. You pay that gap in cash. If the price is the same as or lower than HDB's value, there is no COV. It is part of the price you pay the seller. It is not a fee charged by HDB.

Can I use CPF to pay cash over valuation?

No. CPF savings can be used only up to the lower of the agreed price and HDB's value. The amount above HDB's value has to be cash.

Can a housing loan pay cash over valuation?

No. An HDB loan or a bank loan is based on the lower of the agreed price and HDB's value. The amount above that value is not financed.

How is cash over valuation calculated?

Subtract HDB's value from the price you agreed. If you agree $600,000 and HDB's value is $570,000, the COV is $30,000 in cash. If the price is not higher, COV is zero. The estimate on this page is a sense-check before you agree a price. It is not HDB's value and it is not your COV.

When will I know if I have to pay COV?

After the seller grants the Option to Purchase. Buyers who use CPF or a housing loan must submit a Request for Value by the next working day. The fee is $120 including GST and is not refunded. HDB usually makes the value available within about 10 working days. The Option period is 21 days, so check the value before you exercise. The value stays valid for 3 months.

How much cash should I prepare for COV?

There is no single amount for every flat. The cash is whatever your agreed price exceeds HDB's value, on top of the option fee of not more than $1,000 and a total deposit of not more than $5,000. Know what cash you can pay before you pay the option fee. This estimate helps you judge the price. It does not tell you the exact cash HDB will require.

What if the HDB valuation is lower than the price I agreed?

The difference is cash over valuation, and you pay it in cash. You can pay it, ask the seller to revise the price, or let the Option lapse. The option fee may not be returned if you do not exercise. Call 8062-1538 before you pay that fee if the asking price already looks high.

Does cash over valuation apply to BTO flats?

No. COV arises on the resale market, where the price you agree and HDB's value can differ. A flat bought directly from HDB is sold at HDB's price.

Can I avoid paying COV?

Yes. You can refuse a price that would leave you paying cash above HDB's value. Negotiate, or walk away before you exercise the Option. This estimate gives you a published-market price to start from. It does not guarantee that the seller will accept it, or that HDB's value will match it.

Who pays cash over valuation?

The buyer pays it, in cash, as part of the price. The seller receives it. HDB does not charge it as a separate fee.

Is this calculation reliable?

It is a careful estimate drawn from published HDB resale transactions, so it is grounded in prices buyers and sellers have registered. You can use it with confidence as a sense-check before you negotiate. It is not HDB's valuation of this flat, and it is not a guarantee of the price, the valuation, or a zero-COV purchase. Condition, renovation, remaining lease, and the day of valuation can move the official figure.

Is this HDB's official valuation?

No. HDB issues the valuation after the Option to Purchase, through a Request for Value. Use this estimate before that step. Confirm the official figure with HDB before you exercise the Option.

The asking price is higher than the estimate. Should I still buy?

Only if you can pay the gap in cash, on top of your deposit and other costs. If HDB's valuation comes in lower than the price, that gap is cash over valuation. Negotiate, or look at another flat, rather than agreeing to cash you did not plan for. Call 8062-1538 if you want an adviser to review the flat before you pay the option fee.
