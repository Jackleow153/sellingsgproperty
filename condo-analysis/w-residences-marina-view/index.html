W Residences Marina View

Marina View / Downtown, D01 · IOI Properties (run under the W brand by Marriott)

Overall score 6.6 out of 10. Avoid for now unless you get a deep discount. Lowest exit score in the whole list. A year after launch about 2% is sold, and the recorded deals were done far below asking, which tells you the asking prices are not the market price. The lease has already lost 5 years, sizes include balcony and air-con ledge (so the psf looks lower than it is), and completed Marina Bay condos resell at about $1,830-2,100 psf.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 15 sold and 2 reserved of 683 (about 2%) in Sep 2026
  • Price: Asking about $3,500-3,740 psf for most types; actual recorded sales $2,609-2,811 psf
  • Land cost: $1,379 psf of buildable floor area ($1.508B, sole bidder, 2021)
  • Completion: Keys by 31 Mar 2029; one place says H2 2028
  • Lease: 99 years from 27 Dec 2021
  • MRT: Shenton Way MRT exit about 110 m, Tanjong Pagar about 310 m, Downtown about 430 m (straight line, OneMap)
  • Food and shops: Marina Bay Link Mall (supermarket) named; office-district eateries. No hawker centre named in the source.
  • Primary schools: None within 1 km (Cantonment Primary just over 1 km)

Scores

  • Location: 8/10. Prime city (7) + in the business district (+1)
  • Entry price: 4/10. Asking prices are about 87% above District 1's 99-year resale middle price ($1,930 psf); the few recorded sales were about 40% above
  • Exit plan: 4/10. Start 5; +1 big project; +1 MRT within 500 m; -1 supply (One Marina Gardens and more Marina South plots); -1 price gap over 75%; -1 about 2% sold a year after launch
  • Nearby food: 8/10. Amoy Street Food Centre about 460 m and Maxwell Food Centre about 700 m; office-district food around
  • MRT closeness: 9/10. Shenton Way MRT exit about 110 m

Pros

  • Business district address with five MRT stations around
  • Hotel services and facilities
  • Rare 4 and 5-bedroom units in District 1
  • Recorded deals show large discounts are possible

Cons

  • About 2% sold after a year; 666 units overhang
  • Lease from 2021: about 92 years left at handover
  • Unit sizes include air-con ledge and balcony
  • Rental return estimated at only about 2.6-2.9% at entry price
  • 70% of units are 1 and 2-bedders aimed at investors
