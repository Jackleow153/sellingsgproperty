W Residences Marina View: analysis and verdict

Marina View / Downtown, D01 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,620 psf. A 3-bed here at about $3,250 psf is 30% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,500 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,500 psf. 2-bed is the most wanted size in this rental and investment area. The 4-bed has a very thin resale pool: buy only to live in.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed at or below $2,500 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,220 sq ft is about $3.05M.
  • Best size here: 2-bed: this is an rental and investment area, where smaller units suit investors and tenants.
  • Walk away: Above $2,620 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.20M). The current average $3,250 psf is above that.
  • Own stay only: The 4-bed: few buyer groups could fund it today, so buy it only to live in long term.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with V On Shenton ($1,853 psf, 3-bed size, last 12 months (3)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,620 psf.
  • Watch: The Marina Gardens Lane (2026 GLS site) launch nearby: likely at about $2,730 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (V On Shenton, Marina One Residences, One Shenton, The Clift, Altez) work out to about $2,500 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 20% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 7 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: V On Shenton (87 m away, 510 homes, completed 2017): its 3-bed-size resales (about 1,040 to 1,400 sq ft) averaged $1,853 psf over the last 12 months (3 resales), against a median $1,854 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $3,253 psf is $1,400 psf (+76%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.95M.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $3,253 psf, the base case makes about −36% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (−10.6% a year); bought at $2,500 psf it makes about −17%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $3.97M; about $2.71M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders no; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: narrow.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (-4.4% a year): One and a half times the base-case fall: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (-2.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): V On Shenton 0.3%, Marina One Residences -6.1%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (-2.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
