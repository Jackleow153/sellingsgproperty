One Marina Gardens: analysis and verdict

Marina South, D01 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,580 psf. The current average $2,959 psf is 20% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,460 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,460 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Marina Bay Residences, Marina One Residences, V On Shenton) work out to about $2,460 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $2,959 psf, the base case makes about −29% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (−8.1% a year); bought at $2,460 psf it makes about −14%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 970 sq ft costs about $2.18M; a buyer then needs about $14,200 a month and $624k upfront, against $750k to $925k that typical Bukit Merah and Kallang/Whampoa upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,460 psf. Walk away above: $2,580 psf. Potential gain: −29% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,959 psf (−8.1% a year). Bear −32%, bull −29%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 645 sq ft): Walk away above $2,580 psf. Buy at or below $2,460 psf ($1.59M); walk away above $2,580 psf ($1.66M). At $2,959 psf: about −28% by 2030. Resale about $1.45M needs about $9,400 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 970 sq ft): Walk away above $2,580 psf. Buy at or below $2,460 psf ($2.39M); walk away above $2,580 psf ($2.50M). At $2,959 psf: about −29% by 2030. Resale about $2.18M needs about $14,200 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,645 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,460 psf ($4.05M); walk away above $2,580 psf ($4.24M). At $2,959 psf: about −29% by 2030. Resale about $3.70M needs about $24,100 a month.

Closest competitors

1 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Marina Bay Residences (980 m away, 428 homes, Resale): $2,353 psf, about $2,841 psf as a new launch here; -0.9% a year over 4 years.
  • Marina One Residences (1.0 km away, 1042 homes, Resale): $1,910 psf, about $2,306 psf as a new launch here; -6.1% a year over 4 years.
  • V On Shenton (1.5 km away, 510 homes, Resale): $1,854 psf, about $2,239 psf as a new launch here; 0.3% a year over 4 years.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,460 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,580 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,320 to $2,460 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (-3.3% a year): One and a half times the base-case fall: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (-2.2% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Marina Bay Residences -0.9%, Marina One Residences -6.1%, V On Shenton 0.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (-2.2% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
