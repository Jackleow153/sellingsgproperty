Dover Drive (GLS): analysis and verdict

Dover Drive (Dover-Medway, one-north) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,520 psf. The expected $2,800 to $2,900 psf is 19% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,400 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,400 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Rochester Residences, Hudson Place Residences, The Clement Canopy, Clavon) work out to about $2,400 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $2,850 psf, the base case makes about +4% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (0.8% a year); bought at $2,400 psf it makes about +24%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $3.00M; a buyer then needs about $19,500 a month and $870k upfront, against $652k to $1.09M that typical Queenstown and Clementi upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,400 psf. Walk away above: $2,520 psf. Potential gain: +4% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,850 psf (0.8% a year). Bear −9%, bull +4%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 680 sq ft): Walk away above $2,520 psf. Buy at or below $2,400 psf ($1.63M); walk away above $2,520 psf ($1.71M). At $2,850 psf: about +5% by 2032. Resale about $2.15M needs about $14,000 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 950 sq ft): Walk away above $2,520 psf. Buy at or below $2,400 psf ($2.28M); walk away above $2,520 psf ($2.39M). At $2,850 psf: about +4% by 2032. Resale about $3.00M needs about $19,500 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,250 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,400 psf ($3.00M); walk away above $2,520 psf ($3.15M). At $2,850 psf: about +4% by 2032. Resale about $3.95M needs about $25,700 a month.

Closest competitors

1 of the 4 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • The Rochester Residences (850 m away, 366 homes, Resale): $1,746 psf, about $2,108 psf as a new launch here; 6.1% a year over 4 years.
  • Hudson Place Residences (1.6 km away, 327 homes, Launched May 2026): $2,458 psf, about $2,483 psf as a new launch here.
  • The Clement Canopy (1.7 km away, 505 homes, Resale): $1,999 psf, about $2,414 psf as a new launch here; 5.2% a year over 4 years.
  • Clavon (1.9 km away, 640 homes, Resale): $2,159 psf, about $2,607 psf as a new launch here.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,400 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,520 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,510 to $2,650 psf, so expect the launch above our buy-at price.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.8% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Rochester Residences 6.1%, The Clement Canopy 5.2%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.7% a year): The Dover-Medway precinct is Planned, not Confirmed, and one-north MRT is already open, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is entering a supply phase: the land price set a city-fringe record, and up to about 6,000 more homes are planned in Dover-Medway.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

The new precinct is Planned, not Confirmed, and it adds supply as well as amenities, so it adds nothing to the bull case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 7/10. Between one-north and NUS, about 350 m from Fairfield Methodist School (primary) (OneMap).
  • Entry price: 3/10. Expected $2,800 to $2,900 psf against about $2,400 psf from the closest competitors (+17% to +21%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 5/10. Shops planned on the first storey (tender). Hawker centres within a 5-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 7/10. one-north MRT about 527 m in a straight line (OneMap): about a 7-minute walk. Kent Ridge MRT about 717 m.

Overall 5.2 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
