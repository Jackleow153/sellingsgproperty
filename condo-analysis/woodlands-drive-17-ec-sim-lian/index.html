Woodlands Drive 17 EC (Sim Lian): analysis and verdict

Woodlands Drive 17 (Woodlands South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $1,530 psf. The tested $1,750 to $1,850 psf is 23% above what the closest competitors support (about $1,460 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $1,460 psf.

  • The closest competitors (Twin Fountains, Forestville, La Casa) work out to about $1,460 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the tested $1,800 psf, the base case makes about +22% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2036 (2.3% a year); bought at $1,460 psf it makes about +51%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $2.21M; a buyer then needs about $14,400 a month and $634k upfront, against $398k to $505k that typical Woodlands and Sembawang upgraders bring.
  • At launch, a household at the $16,000 income ceiling can borrow at most about $1.01M under the 30% mortgage servicing ratio, so a 3-bed at $1,800 psf ($1.71M) needs about $760k in cash and CPF, including stamp duty.

Buy at or below: $1,460 psf. Walk away above: $1,530 psf. Potential gain: +22% (Base case after costs by 2036, bought at $1,800 psf (2.3% a year). Bear −3%, bull +22%).

Potential gain

No Seller's Stamp Duty: the sale is after the 5-year minimum occupation period. Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Twin Fountains 6.4%, Forestville 4.8%, La Casa 4.8%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst within reach of the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is in a supply phase: about 1,000 new EC homes on one road reach their minimum occupation period together, around 2036.

The 3-bed, 4-bed and 5-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Questions

Is Clovelle of Woodlands a good investment?

At the tested $1,750 to $1,850 psf, the base case makes about +22% by 2036 after stamp duty and selling costs (2.3% a year). Bought at about $1,460 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +51%.

Can I sell before 2036?

Not on the open market. An executive condo must be lived in for 5 years from TOP before it can be sold to any Singaporean or PR (HDB). Its tender closed before 8 May 2026, so the 5-year rule applies; EC sites closing later have 10 years.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $1,460 psf, about $1.39M for 950 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $1,530 psf ($1.45M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
