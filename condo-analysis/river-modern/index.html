River Modern

River Valley Green, D09 · GuocoLand

Overall score 8.2 out of 10. Top-tier convenience in River Valley, at a top-tier price. River Modern links underground to Great World MRT and mall, with River Valley Primary about 100 m away, which few prime-district condos can match. It was about 90% sold on its launch weekend and is now 95% sold. The units left average about $3,573 psf, about 62% above District 9 99-year resale, and several other launches nearby will also be completing, so buy it to live in rather than for a quick resale.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 434 of 455 (95%) on 5 Nov 2026, 21 left (PropertyNet); 95.4% sold on 3 Nov 2026 (Yahoo News); 410 (90%) on the launch weekend
  • Price: $3,266 psf average on the launch weekend (410 of 455 sold, 7 to 8 Mar 2026, GuocoLand); units left average about $3,573 psf (PropertyNet, 5 Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: Not stated in the sources checked
  • Completion: Expected around 2H 2030 (vacant possession date not published)
  • Lease: 99 years
  • MRT: Direct sheltered underground link to Great World MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) and Great World City mall
  • Food and shops: Great World City mall through the same link; Zion Riverside Food Centre close by (distance not given); Robertson Quay riverside restaurants
  • Primary schools: River Valley Primary about 175 m (straight line, OneMap)

Scores

  • Location: 9/10. Prime city (7) + mall linked next door (+1) + primary school within 1 km (+1)
  • Entry price: 5/10. Units left average about $3,573 psf, about 62% above the District 9 median for 99-year resale condos ($2,208 psf, URA, 12 months to Sep 2026)
  • Exit plan: 8/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; +1 big project; +1 MRT within 500 m; +1 primary school within 1 km; -1 nearby supply (River Green, Zyon Grand, Promenade Peak, River Valley Green Parcel C and the Valley Point redevelopment)
  • Nearby food: 9/10. Mall food through a sheltered link and Zion Riverside Food Centre about 410 m
  • MRT closeness: 10/10. Sheltered underground link to an open MRT station

Pros

  • About 95% sold
  • Sheltered underground link to Great World MRT and mall
  • River Valley Primary about 100 m
  • Riverside dining at Robertson Quay
  • Big project from a large developer

Cons

  • Units left average about $3,573 psf, about 62% above District 9 99-year resale
  • Several new launches nearby complete around the same time
  • Only 2 three-bedroom units left; 4-bedroom from $6.174M
  • 99-year lease in a district where buyers also have freehold choices
  • Land price and vacant possession date not published in the sources checked
