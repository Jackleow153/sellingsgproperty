Bagnall Haus: analysis and verdict

Upper East Coast Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,330 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,510 psf is 13% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,220 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,220 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,220 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,035 sq ft is about $2.30M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,330 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.41M). The launch average $2,510 psf is above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Eastwood Regency ($1,495 psf, 3-bed size estimated from its other sizes) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,330 psf.
  • Watch: The Bedok Rise (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Eastwood Regency, East Village, Uber 388) work out to about $2,220 psf as a new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (40% to 50% here): launches selling now in the OCR charge about 9% over a just-completed resale and about 2.6 points more for each year of its age (30 launches against 107 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Eastwood Regency (368 m away) resold at a median $1,682 psf over 12 months (6 resales), all sizes; too few 3-bed-size resales to compare like for like.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the launch average $2,505 psf, the base case makes about −2% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (−0.6% a year); bought at $2,220 psf it makes about +10%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.59M; about $2.69M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.
  • Supply and land prices: Supply risk here: low. The price is above what same-size competitors support, whatever the land cost. A cheaper new launch may come nearby. Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. The price is above what same-size competitors support, whatever the land cost. A cheaper new launch may come nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 113 homes, about 895 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Bedok Rise (GLS), Vela Bay); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 368 resales a year. Our test of 33 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the outer suburbs (11 km or more from the CBD): those with more than 1,817 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 4.0% a year over the past 4 years, against 4.3% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (High concern)

Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Low concern)

95% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with most units sold, there is little pressure to cut prices.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Moderate concern)

The next launch nearby, Bedok Rise (GLS), is likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf, close to this project's 3-bed. A cheaper neighbour later could cap this project's resale price; compare both before committing.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.
