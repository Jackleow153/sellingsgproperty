Holland Plain (Sim Lian, 2nd site): analysis and verdict

Holland Plain (off Old Holland Road) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $3,140 psf. The expected $3,000 to $3,100 psf is a little above what the closest competitors support (about $2,990 psf), so aim for the lower end. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Amberwood at Holland, Mayfair Modern, Dunearn House, Parksuites) work out to about $2,990 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $3,050 psf, the base case makes about +16% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (3.0% a year); bought at $2,990 psf it makes about +18%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $3.76M; a buyer then needs about $24,500 a month and $1.11M upfront, against $652k to $1.09M that typical Queenstown and Clementi upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,990 psf. Walk away above: $3,140 psf. Potential gain: +16% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,050 psf (3.0% a year). Bear +3%, bull +16%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Buy only below $3,140 psf. Buy at or below $2,990 psf ($2.09M); walk away above $3,140 psf ($2.20M). At $3,050 psf: about +16% by 2032. Resale about $2.63M needs about $17,100 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,990 psf ($2.99M); walk away above $3,140 psf ($3.14M). At $3,050 psf: about +16% by 2032. Resale about $3.76M needs about $24,500 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,350 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,990 psf ($4.04M); walk away above $3,140 psf ($4.24M). At $3,050 psf: about +15% by 2032. Resale about $5.08M needs about $33,100 a month.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): No condo nearby has a suitable record (a 3-bed price change over 4 years with at least 10 resales in the past year), so we use URA's islandwide non-landed index: 4.7% a year from Q3 2016 to Q3 2026.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Overall 5.2 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
