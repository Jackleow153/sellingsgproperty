Telok Blangah Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Telok Blangah Road (Berlayar, former Keppel Club) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $2,810 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,700 to $2,800 psf is a little above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,680 psf as a new launch), so aim for the lower end. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,680 psf; a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft is about $2.55M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,810 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.67M). The expected $2,700 to $2,800 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Reef At King's Dock ($2,470 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (5)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,810 psf.
  • Watch: The Berlayar Drive (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,190 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (The Reef At King's Dock, Corals At Keppel Bay, Reflections At Keppel Bay) work out to about $2,680 psf as a new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (8% to 41% here): launches selling now in the RCR charge about 3% over a just-completed resale and about 2.6 points more for each year of its age (30 launches against 107 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Reef At King's Dock (585 m away, 429 homes, completed 2024): its 3-bed-size resales (about 710 to 1,190 sq ft) averaged $2,470 psf over the last 6 months (5 resales), against a median $2,462 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,749 psf is $279 psf (+11%) above that.

  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $2,749 psf, the base case makes about +2% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (0.5% a year); bought at $2,680 psf it makes about +5%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.61M; about $2.85M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders yes; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 745 homes, about 499 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (The Island Residence (Keppel Bay), Berlayar Drive (GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 236 resales a year. Our test of 27 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the city and city fringe (under 6 km from the CBD): those with more than 2,367 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.5% a year over the past 4 years, against 0.5% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Moderate concern)

Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS). That pushes them to price to sell at launch; slow sellers sometimes offer deals near the deadline, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Berlayar Drive (GLS), is likely at about $3,190 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.
