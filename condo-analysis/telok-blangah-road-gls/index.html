Telok Blangah Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Telok Blangah Road (Berlayar, former Keppel Club) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,660 psf. The expected $2,700 to $2,800 psf is 9% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,530 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,530 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Corals At Keppel Bay, Reflections At Keppel Bay, The Reef At King's Dock) work out to about $2,530 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $2,750 psf, the base case makes about −3% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−0.6% a year); bought at $2,530 psf it makes about +6%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $2.70M; a buyer then needs about $17,600 a month and $779k upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,530 psf. Walk away above: $2,660 psf. Potential gain: −3% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,750 psf (−0.6% a year). Bear −8%, bull −3%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 680 sq ft): Walk away above $2,660 psf. Buy at or below $2,530 psf ($1.72M); walk away above $2,660 psf ($1.81M). At $2,750 psf: about −3% by 2032. Resale about $1.93M needs about $12,600 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 950 sq ft): Walk away above $2,660 psf. Buy at or below $2,530 psf ($2.40M); walk away above $2,660 psf ($2.53M). At $2,750 psf: about −3% by 2032. Resale about $2.70M needs about $17,600 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,250 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,530 psf ($3.16M); walk away above $2,660 psf ($3.33M). At $2,750 psf: about −3% by 2032. Resale about $3.55M needs about $23,100 a month.

Closest competitors

3 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Corals At Keppel Bay (430 m away, 366 homes, Resale): $2,105 psf, about $2,542 psf as a new launch here; -4.9% a year over 4 years.
  • Reflections At Keppel Bay (430 m away, 1129 homes, Resale): $1,722 psf, about $2,079 psf as a new launch here; 0.6% a year over 4 years.
  • The Reef At King's Dock (590 m away, 429 homes, Resale): $2,462 psf, about $2,973 psf as a new launch here.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,530 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,660 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,230 to $2,360 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.2% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: The Interlace (1.9 km): Reflections At Keppel Bay 0.6%, The Interlace 4.1%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (2.4% a year): No Confirmed catalyst near the site within the holding period: the Greater Southern Waterfront is a long-term plan, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is entering a supply phase: the first private homes of the Greater Southern Waterfront, with Berlayar Drive and Keppel Bay launches close by, and older waterfront condos that have not grown.

Future buyers

The 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

The Greater Southern Waterfront is a long-term plan, not a Confirmed catalyst within the holding period, so it adds nothing to the bull case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 7/10. City fringe beside Mount Faber and Keppel Bay, about 0.8 km from HarbourFront and VivoCity (OneMap). Few comparable new condos nearby.
  • Entry price: 4/10. Expected $2,700 to $2,800 psf against about $2,530 psf from the closest competitors (+7% to +11%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 6/10. HarbourFront and VivoCity about 0.8 km away (HarbourFront MRT 762 m, OneMap). Hawker centres within a 5-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 7/10. Telok Blangah MRT (Circle Line) about 528 m in a straight line (OneMap): about a 7-minute walk.

Overall 5.6 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
