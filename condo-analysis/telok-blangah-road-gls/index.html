Telok Blangah Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Telok Blangah Road (Berlayar, former Keppel Club) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,710 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,700 to $2,800 psf is 7% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,580 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,580 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,580 psf; a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft is about $2.45M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,710 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.57M). The expected $2,700 to $2,800 psf is partly above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Reef At King's Dock ($2,470 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (5)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,710 psf.
  • Watch: The Berlayar Drive (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,190 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (The Reef At King's Dock, Corals At Keppel Bay, Reflections At Keppel Bay) work out to about $2,580 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 19% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the RCR charge over their closest resale, 9 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Reef At King's Dock (585 m away, 429 homes, completed 2024): its 3-bed-size resales (about 710 to 1,190 sq ft) averaged $2,470 psf over the last 6 months (5 resales), against a median $2,462 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,749 psf is $279 psf (+11%) above that.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Berlayar Drive (GLS) (1,269 m, $1,515 psf ppr), is 14% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,510 psf, and is likely to launch at about $3,190 psf (break-even plus the 27% that launches since 2023 priced above theirs).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $2,749 psf, the base case makes about −1% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−0.3% a year); bought at $2,580 psf it makes about +5%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.61M; about $2.74M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders yes; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.2% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: The Interlace (1.9 km): Reflections At Keppel Bay 0.6%, The Interlace 3.9%.   • Bull (2.2% a year): No Confirmed catalyst near the site within the holding period: the Greater Southern Waterfront is a long-term plan, so the bull case equals the base case.
