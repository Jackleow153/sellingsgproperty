Dorset Gardens: analysis and verdict

Dorset Road (Farrer Park) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. The tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $2,710 psf). Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Piccadilly Grand, Uptown @ Farrer, The Collective at One Sophia) work out to about $2,710 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the tested $2,700 psf, the base case makes about +19% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (3.5% a year); bought at $2,710 psf it makes about +18%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $3.24M; a buyer then needs about $21,100 a month and $944k upfront, against $750k to $900k that typical Kallang/Whampoa and Toa Payoh upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,710 psf. Walk away above: $2,850 psf. Potential gain: +19% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,700 psf (3.5% a year). Bear +6%, bull +19%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 680 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $2,710 psf ($1.84M); walk away above $2,850 psf ($1.94M). At $2,700 psf: about +19% by 2032. Resale about $2.32M needs about $15,100 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 950 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $2,710 psf ($2.57M); walk away above $2,850 psf ($2.71M). At $2,700 psf: about +19% by 2032. Resale about $3.24M needs about $21,100 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,250 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,710 psf ($3.39M); walk away above $2,850 psf ($3.56M). At $2,700 psf: about +18% by 2032. Resale about $4.26M needs about $27,700 a month.

Closest competitors

2 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Piccadilly Grand (340 m away, 407 homes, Resale): $2,386 psf, about $2,881 psf as a new launch here.
  • Uptown @ Farrer (610 m away, 116 homes, Resale): $1,958 psf, about $2,364 psf as a new launch here.
  • The Collective at One Sophia (1.3 km away, 367 homes, Launched Nov 2024): $2,750 psf, about $2,876 psf as a new launch here.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,710 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,850 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,240 to $2,370 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): No condo nearby has a suitable record (a 3-bed price change over 4 years with at least 10 resales in the past year), so we use URA's islandwide non-landed index: 4.7% a year from Q3 2016 to Q3 2026.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: Farrer Park MRT has been open since 2003, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is mature: little large-condo supply right here, though several city-fringe launches within 3 km compete for the same buyers.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

No major transformation is planned near this site. Any growth has to come from the location itself and the wider market.

Scorecard

  • Location: 8/10. City fringe next to Farrer Park MRT, Little India and Farrer Park Primary School (about 94 m, OneMap).
  • Entry price: 7/10. Tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf against about $2,710 psf from the closest competitors (−4% to +3%).
  • Exit plan: 5/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. Little India and the Tekka area about 0.6 km away (Little India MRT 615 m, OneMap). Hawker centres within a 5-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 9/10. Farrer Park MRT about 302 m in a straight line (OneMap): about a 4-minute walk.

Overall 7.4 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
