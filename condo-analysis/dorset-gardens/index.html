Dorset Gardens: analysis and verdict

Dorset Road (Farrer Park) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,600 to $2,800 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,820 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,820 psf; a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft is about $2.68M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,960 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.81M). The tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Piccadilly Grand ($2,450 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (3)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,960 psf.
  • Watch: The Peck Hay Road (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,400 to $3,900 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Piccadilly Grand, Sturdee Residences, Soleil @ Sinaran) work out to about $2,820 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 19% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the RCR charge over their closest resale, 9 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Piccadilly Grand (338 m away, 407 homes, completed 2025): its 3-bed-size resales (about 810 to 1,090 sq ft) averaged $2,450 psf over the last 6 months (3 resales), against a median $2,386 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,701 psf is $251 psf (+10%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $3.18M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Peck Hay Road (GLS) (1,360 m, $1,865 psf ppr), is 39% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,990 psf, and analysts expect it to launch at $3,400-3,900 (PropNex, ERA, CBRE, Knight Frank).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the tested $2,701 psf, the base case makes about +30% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (5.3% a year); bought at $2,820 psf it makes about +24%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.57M; about $3.54M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders yes; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.
