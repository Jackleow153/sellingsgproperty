Hudson Place Residences: analysis and verdict

Media Circle (one-north / Queenstown planning area) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,470 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,720 psf as a new launch). 2-bed is the most wanted size in this rental and investment area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,720 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,010 sq ft is about $2.75M.
  • Best size here: 2-bed: this is an rental and investment area, where smaller units suit investors and tenants.
  • Walk away: Above $2,860 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.89M). The current average $2,470 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Margaret Ville ($2,475 psf, 3-bed size, last 12 months (5)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,860 psf.
  • Watch: The Dover Drive (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $2,800 to $2,900 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Margaret Ville, One-North Eden, Normanton Park) work out to about $2,720 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (9% to 16% here): launches selling now in the RCR charge about 3% over a just-completed resale and about 2.5 points more for each year of its age (29 launches against 104 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Margaret Ville (1,112 m away, 309 homes, completed 2021): its 3-bed-size resales (about 860 to 1,160 sq ft) averaged $2,475 psf over the last 12 months (5 resales), against a median $2,315 psf for all sizes. Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 327 homes, about 625 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Dover Drive (GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 528 resales a year. Our test of 19 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the middle ring (6 to 11 km from the CBD): those with more than 862 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.9% a year over the past 4 years, against 3.6% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Low concern)

Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Low concern)

80% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with most units sold, there is little pressure to cut prices.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Dover Drive (GLS), is likely at about $2,800 to $2,900 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: One-North Residences (1.1 km), Commonwealth Towers (1.3 km): One-North Residences 4.4%, Commonwealth Towers 6.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise. That averages 5.3% a year, above the market's 10-year pace, so the base case is capped at 4.7% (URA non-landed index, 2016 to 2026): 2022 to 2026 included an unusual rise, and a new launch bought at a premium rarely outgrows the market for years.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
