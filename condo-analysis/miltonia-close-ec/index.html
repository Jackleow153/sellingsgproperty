Miltonia Close EC: analysis and verdict

Miltonia Close (Yishun) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. The expected $1,700 to $1,800 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $1,840 psf). Skip the 4-bed and 5-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Criterion, Signature At Yishun, The Canopy) work out to about $1,840 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 36% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches).
  • The Criterion (257 m away, 505 homes, completed 2018) resold at a median $1,380 psf in the last 6 months (21 resales). At $1,750 psf this project is $370 psf (+27%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.49M ($1,385 psf).
  • Bought at the expected $1,750 psf, the base case makes about +66% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2036 (5.8% a year); bought at $1,840 psf it makes about +58%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $2.93M; a buyer then needs about $19,100 a month and $848k upfront, against $400k to $543k that typical Yishun and Sembawang upgraders bring.
  • At launch, a household at the $16,000 income ceiling can borrow at most about $1.01M under the 30% mortgage servicing ratio, so a 3-bed at $1,750 psf ($1.66M) needs about $710k in cash and CPF, including stamp duty.

Buy at or below: $1,840 psf. Walk away above: $1,930 psf. Potential gain: +66% (Base case after costs by 2036, bought at $1,750 psf (5.8% a year). Bear +29%, bull +66%).

Potential gain

No Seller's Stamp Duty: the sale is after the 5-year minimum occupation period. Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.9% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Criterion 8.3%, Signature At Yishun 3.6%, The Canopy 5.8%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Questions

Is the Miltonia Close EC a good investment?

At the expected $1,700 to $1,800 psf, the base case makes about +66% by 2036 after stamp duty and selling costs (5.8% a year). Bought at about $1,840 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +58%.

Can I sell before 2036?

Not on the open market. An executive condo must be lived in for 5 years from TOP before it can be sold to any Singaporean or PR (HDB). Its tender closed before 8 May 2026, so the 5-year rule applies; EC sites closing later have 10 years.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $1,840 psf, about $1.75M for 950 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $1,930 psf ($1.83M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
