Narra Residences: analysis and verdict

Dairy Farm Walk · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,070 psf. The current average $2,180 psf is 11% above what the closest competitors support (about $1,970 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $1,970 psf.

  • The closest competitors (Dairy Farm Residences, Foresque Residences, Eco Sanctuary, Tree House) work out to about $1,970 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $2,180 psf, the base case makes about +7% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (1.8% a year); bought at $1,970 psf it makes about +19%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 990 sq ft costs about $2.46M; a buyer then needs about $16,000 a month and $707k upfront, against $420k to $660k that typical Bukit Panjang and Bukit Batok upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $1,970 psf. Walk away above: $2,070 psf. Potential gain: +7% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,180 psf (1.8% a year). Bear −4%, bull +7%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 670 sq ft): Walk away above $2,070 psf. Buy at or below $1,970 psf ($1.32M); walk away above $2,070 psf ($1.39M). At $2,180 psf: about +8% by 2030. Resale about $1.66M needs about $10,800 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 990 sq ft): Walk away above $2,070 psf. Buy at or below $1,970 psf ($1.95M); walk away above $2,070 psf ($2.05M). At $2,180 psf: about +7% by 2030. Resale about $2.46M needs about $16,000 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,265 sq ft): Walk away above $2,070 psf. Buy at or below $1,970 psf ($2.49M); walk away above $2,070 psf ($2.62M). At $2,180 psf: about +7% by 2030. Resale about $3.14M needs about $20,400 a month.

Closest competitors

4 of the 4 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Foresque Residences (220 m away, 496 homes, Resale): $1,593 psf, about $1,924 psf as a new launch here; 5.9% a year over 4 years.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $1,970 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,070 psf. The developer's floor is about $1,860 to $1,970 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.0% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Foresque Residences 5.9%, Eco Sanctuary 6.0%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

  • Entry price: 4/10. Current average $2,180 psf against about $1,970 psf from the closest competitors (+11%).
  • Exit plan: 5/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 5/10. A few shops on site; malls a short trip away
  • MRT proximity: 4/10. Hillview MRT exit about 1.03 km

Overall 5.0 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
