Narra Residences: analysis and verdict

Dairy Farm Walk · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,170 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,320 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,320 psf; a 3-bed of about 990 sq ft is about $2.30M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,440 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.42M). The current average $2,170 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Dairy Farm Residences ($1,731 psf, last 12 months) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,440 psf.
  • Watch: The Dairy Farm Walk (2026 GLS site) launch nearby: likely at about $2,050 to $2,250 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Dairy Farm Residences, Eco Sanctuary, Foresque Residences, Tree House, The Skywoods) work out to about $2,320 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Dairy Farm Residences (211 m away) resold at a median $1,731 psf over 12 months (4 resales), all sizes; too few 3-bed-size resales to compare like for like.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,168 psf, the base case makes about +25% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (5.7% a year); bought at $2,320 psf it makes about +16%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.15M; about $2.84M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders with some savings; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.5% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Eco Sanctuary 6.0%, Foresque Residences 5.9%, The Skywoods 4.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.5% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
