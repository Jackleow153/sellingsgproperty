Parktown Residence

Tampines North, D18 · UOL Group, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development (Topaz Residential / Topaz Commercial)

Overall score 7.8 out of 10. Best-connected family buy in Tampines, but only big units are left. Parktown has what most suburban condos lack: a mall, hawker centre and bus interchange downstairs and a link to a future MRT station. At about 98% sold, resale demand looks well supported. What is left are 4- and 5-bedroom units from $3.32M, priced about half again above nearby resale condos per sq ft, so it suits families who will stay long rather than short-term investors.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: About 1,168 to 1,176 of 1,193 (98%) in Nov 2026; sources differ: 17 left on 5 Nov 2026 (PropertyNet), 25 left (Listings.sg)
  • Price: $2,360 psf at launch (Feb 2025); units left average about $2,346 psf (PropertyNet, 5 Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: $885 psf of buildable floor area (about $1.21B, 2023 tender; 13.9% above the second bid)
  • Completion: Vacant possession by 30 Jun 2030 (developer); legal completion 30 Jun 2033
  • Lease: 99 years from 9 Oct 2023
  • MRT: Sheltered link to Tampines North MRT on the Cross Island Line, expected to open around 2030; not open yet
  • Food and shops: Hawker centre, mall, community club and bus interchange in the same development (open at completion); Tampines Regional Centre malls a bus ride away
  • Primary schools: Angsana Primary about 300 m; Meridian Primary and Poi Ching School near the 1 km line (PropertyNet, checked 25 Sep 2026)

Scores

  • Location: 7/10. Suburbs (5) + mall and hawker centre on site (+1) + primary school within 1 km (+1)
  • Entry price: 6/10. Units left average about $2,346 psf, about 52% above the middle price of the five nearest 99-year resale condos ($1,545 psf, 2026 average) and about 34% above the newest of them, The Tapestry ($1,750)
  • Exit plan: 8/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; +1 big project with a wide unit mix; +1 MRT within 500 m (Tampines North, opens about 2030); +1 primary school within 1 km; -1 nearby supply (Pinery Residences and Rivelle Tampines EC launched in 2026)
  • Nearby food: 9/10. Hawker centre and mall in the development, open at completion
  • MRT closeness: 9/10. Sheltered link to Tampines North MRT, but the station is not open yet (Cross Island Line, about 2030)

Pros

  • About 98% sold
  • Mall, hawker centre, community club and bus interchange in the same development
  • Linked to the future Tampines North MRT (Cross Island Line)
  • Angsana Primary about 300 m
  • Units left are priced close to the launch average (about $2,346 psf)

Cons

  • Only 4- and 5-bedroom units left, from $3.322M
  • The MRT station is not open yet; Cross Island Line timing can slip
  • Keys around mid-2030
  • About 52% above the middle price of nearby 99-year resale condos
  • 1,193 homes plus Pinery Residences and Rivelle Tampines EC nearby will compete at resale
