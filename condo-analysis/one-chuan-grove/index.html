One Chuan Grove: analysis and verdict

Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy only below $2,880 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,660 to $2,860 psf is a little above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,740 psf as a new launch), so aim for the lower end. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed or 5-bed at or below $2,740 psf; a 3-bed of about 893 sq ft is about $2.45M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,880 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.57M). The expected $2,660 to $2,860 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2031 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Scala ($1,989 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (5)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,880 psf.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Chuan Park, The Scala, Cardiff Residence) work out to about $2,740 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Scala (149 m away, 468 homes, completed 2013): its 3-bed-size resales (about 760 to 1,030 sq ft) averaged $1,989 psf over the last 6 months (5 resales), against a median $1,991 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,757 psf is $768 psf (+39%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.10M.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $2,757 psf, the base case makes about +20% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (4.8% a year); bought at $2,740 psf it makes about +21%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.46M; about $3.15M by 2031 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.3% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (6.6% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Chiltern Park (0.2 km): The Scala 5.3%, Chiltern Park 7.8%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.6% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: the Circle Line loop opened in July 2026 and is already in today's prices, so the bull case equals the base case.

Questions

Is One Chuan Grove a good investment?

At the expected $2,660 to $2,860 psf, the base case makes about +20% by 2031 after stamp duty and selling costs (4.8% a year). Bought at about $2,740 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +21%.

How does it compare with Chuan Park?

Chuan Park launched at $2,579 psf in November 2024 at Lorong Chuan MRT and is 99% sold (EdgeProp). One Chuan Grove is expected at $2,600 to $2,800 psf, two years later, about 400 m from the station.
