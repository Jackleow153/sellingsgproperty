One Chuan Grove: analysis and verdict

Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy only below $2,740 psf. The expected $2,600 to $2,800 psf is a little above what the closest competitors support (about $2,610 psf), so aim for the lower end. Skip the 4-bed and 5-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Chuan Park, The Scala, Cardiff Residence) work out to about $2,610 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 36% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches).
  • The Scala (149 m away, 468 homes, completed 2013) resold at a median $1,995 psf in the last 6 months (6 resales). At $2,700 psf this project is $705 psf (+35%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.10M ($1,960 psf).
  • Bought at the expected $2,700 psf, the base case makes about +17% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (4.0% a year); bought at $2,610 psf it makes about +21%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 893 sq ft costs about $3.00M; a buyer then needs about $19,600 a month and $871k upfront, against $470k to $832k that typical Serangoon and Ang Mo Kio and Bishan upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,610 psf. Walk away above: $2,740 psf. Potential gain: +17% (Base case after costs by 2031, bought at $2,700 psf (4.0% a year). Bear +3%, bull +17%).

  • 3-bed (about 893 sq ft): Buy only below $2,740 psf. Buy at or below $2,610 psf ($2.33M); walk away above $2,740 psf ($2.45M). At $2,700 psf: about +17% by 2031. Resale about $3.00M needs about $19,600 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,200 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,610 psf ($3.13M); walk away above $2,740 psf ($3.29M). At $2,700 psf: about +17% by 2031. Resale about $4.04M needs about $26,300 a month.
  • 5-bed (about 1,561 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,610 psf ($4.07M); walk away above $2,740 psf ($4.28M). At $2,700 psf: about +16% by 2031. Resale about $5.25M needs about $34,200 a month.

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +43%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +31% over theirs (30 launches; +36% in the OCR, 13 launches). Above what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.5% gross. Competitors' median rent about $5.60 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $6,000 a month.
  • New supply within 2 km: 1 project. About 916 homes launched since 2024 or coming: Chuan Park.
  • Price to land cost: 1.99x. Price psf divided by land psf ppr.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.3% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (6.6% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Chiltern Park (0.2 km): The Scala 5.3%, Chiltern Park 7.8%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.6% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: the Circle Line loop opened in July 2026 and is already in today's prices, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is mature and liquid, but entering an oversupplied phase around the exit: One Chuan Grove, Chuan Park and the next Chuan Grove site add about 2,900 homes within a few hundred metres.

Future buyers

The 3-bed, 4-bed and 5-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

No major transformation is planned near this site. Any growth has to come from the project itself and the wider market; the only nearby change is more housing supply.

Scorecard

  • Location: 7/10. Mature Serangoon area on the Circle Line, one stop from two interchanges, with popular primary schools within about 1 km (reported).
  • Entry price: 6/10. Expected $2,600 to $2,800 psf against about $2,610 psf from the closest competitors (−0% to +7%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed, 5-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 5/10. Seven shops and a childcare centre in the project (developer). Hawker centres within a 10-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 8/10. Lorong Chuan MRT within about 400 m (Confirmed by the developer's partner filing): about a 5-minute walk.

Overall 6.0 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is One Chuan Grove a good investment?

At the expected $2,600 to $2,800 psf, the base case makes about +17% by 2031 after stamp duty and selling costs (4.0% a year). Bought at about $2,610 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +21%.

How does it compare with Chuan Park?

Chuan Park launched at $2,579 psf in November 2024 at Lorong Chuan MRT and is 99% sold (EdgeProp). One Chuan Grove is expected at $2,600 to $2,800 psf, two years later, about 400 m from the station.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,610 psf, about $2.33M for 893 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,740 psf ($2.45M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
