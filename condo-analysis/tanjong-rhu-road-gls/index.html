Tanjong Rhu Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Tanjong Rhu Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,320 psf. The expected $2,900 to $3,000 psf is 33% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,210 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,210 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Water Place, Sanctuary Green, Riveredge) work out to about $2,210 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $2,950 psf, the base case makes about −10% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−2.0% a year); bought at $2,210 psf it makes about +21%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $2.69M; a buyer then needs about $17,500 a month and $777k upfront, against $488k to $900k that typical Kallang/Whampoa and Marine Parade upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,210 psf. Walk away above: $2,320 psf. Potential gain: −10% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,950 psf (−2.0% a year). Bear −20%, bull −10%).

  • 3-bed (about 950 sq ft): Walk away above $2,320 psf. Buy at or below $2,210 psf ($2.10M); walk away above $2,320 psf ($2.20M). At $2,950 psf: about −10% by 2032. Resale about $2.69M needs about $17,500 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,250 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,210 psf ($2.76M); walk away above $2,320 psf ($2.90M). At $2,950 psf: about −10% by 2032. Resale about $3.54M needs about $23,100 a month.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,210 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,320 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,380 to $2,520 psf, so expect the launch above our buy-at price.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.5% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.1% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Water Place 5.6%, Sanctuary Green 4.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.1% a year): Tanjong Rhu MRT opened in 2024 and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Overall 5.4 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
