Tanjong Rhu Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Tanjong Rhu Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $2,970 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,900 to $3,000 psf is a little above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,830 psf as a new launch), so aim for the lower end. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,830 psf; a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft is about $2.69M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,970 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.82M). The expected $2,900 to $3,000 psf is partly above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Riveredge ($1,827 psf, 3-bed size estimated from its other sizes) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,970 psf.
  • Watch: The Kallang Close (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,030 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Riveredge, Concourse Skyline, City Gate, Waterbank At Dakota) work out to about $2,830 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (24% to 49% here): launches selling now in the RCR charge about 3% over a just-completed resale and about 2.6 points more for each year of its age (30 launches against 107 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Riveredge (706 m away) resold at a median $1,879 psf over 12 months (7 resales), all sizes; too few 3-bed-size resales to compare like for like.
  • Also note: The Kallang Close (GLS) site nearby (1,865 m, land $1,415 psf ppr) is likely to launch at about $3,030 psf: more than this project's price.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $2,949 psf, the base case makes about +4% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (0.8% a year); bought at $2,830 psf it makes about +9%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.80M; about $3.11M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.
  • Supply and land prices: Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby. Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 520 homes, about 765 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Arina East Residences, Aurea, Kallang Close (GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 390 resales a year. Our test of 27 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the city and city fringe (under 6 km from the CBD): those with more than 2,367 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.5% a year over the past 4 years, against 0.5% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Moderate concern)

This site's land ($1,455 psf ppr) cost −13% more than Meyer Blue's ($1,668), which lifts the developer's break-even by about −11%. A 3-bed here at about $2,949 psf is −10% above Meyer Blue's $3,260 launch average; private prices have risen about +5% since then (URA index), so in today's money you pay about 13% less than its buyers did, while the developer's costs rose more: the developer absorbs most of the higher land bid. Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS). That pushes them to price to sell at launch; slow sellers sometimes offer deals near the deadline, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Kallang Close (GLS), is likely at about $3,030 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.5% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Concourse Skyline 0.3%, Waterbank At Dakota 5.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (2.9% a year): Tanjong Rhu MRT opened in 2024 and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
