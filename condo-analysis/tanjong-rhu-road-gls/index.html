Tanjong Rhu Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Tanjong Rhu Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,270 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,910 to $3,010 psf is 37% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,160 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,160 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,160 psf; a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft is about $2.05M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,270 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.16M). The expected $2,910 to $3,010 psf is above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Water Place ($1,892 psf, last 6 months) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,270 psf.
  • Watch: The Kallang Close (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,030 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Water Place, Sanctuary Green, Riveredge) work out to about $2,160 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 19% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the RCR charge over their closest resale, 9 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Water Place (433 m away) resold at a median $1,915 psf over 12 months (11 resales), all sizes; too few 3-bed-size resales to compare like for like.
  • Also note: The Kallang Close (GLS) site nearby (1,865 m, land $1,415 psf ppr) is likely to launch at about $3,030 psf: more than this project's price.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $2,956 psf, the base case makes about −12% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−2.6% a year); bought at $2,160 psf it makes about +20%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.81M; about $2.62M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.5% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.0% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Water Place 5.4%, Sanctuary Green 4.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.0% a year): Tanjong Rhu MRT opened in 2024 and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
