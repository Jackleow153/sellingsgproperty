Emerald of Katong

Jalan Tembusu · District 15 (RCR) · Sim Lian Group Pte Ltd)

Emerald of Katong launched in Nov 2024 at a reported average of about $2,621 psf. Sales status: 2 of 846 left (Sep 2026). 844 of 846 units sold. 835 (98.7%) went on the launch weekend of 15 to 16 Nov 2024 at an average of $2,621 psf (Sim Lian, reported by EdgeProp).

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Overall score 6.4 out of 10. Nearly sold out: strong for families, an average walk to the MRT. Emerald of Katong sold 99% on its launch weekend. Tanjong Katong Primary is about 220 m away, and Tanjong Katong MRT about 500 m. Food and malls are a short trip rather than a short walk. With about 2 units left, most buyers will be looking at resale after completion.
