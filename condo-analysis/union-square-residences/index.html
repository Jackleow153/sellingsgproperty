Union Square Residences: analysis and verdict

Havelock Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. The current average $3,003 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $3,020 psf). Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (One Pearl Bank, Martin Modern, Dorsett Residences) work out to about $3,020 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $3,003 psf, the base case makes about +10% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (2.3% a year); bought at $3,020 psf it makes about +9%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 2-bed of about 700 sq ft costs about $2.44M; a buyer then needs about $15,900 a month and $703k upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,020 psf. Walk away above: $3,170 psf. Potential gain: +10% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $3,003 psf (2.3% a year). Bear +2%, bull +10%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $3,020 psf ($2.11M); walk away above $3,170 psf ($2.22M). At $3,003 psf: about +10% by 2030. Resale about $2.44M needs about $15,900 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,520 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,020 psf ($4.59M); walk away above $3,170 psf ($4.82M). At $3,003 psf: about +9% by 2030. Resale about $5.31M needs about $34,600 a month.

Closest competitors

3 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • One Pearl Bank (690 m away, 774 homes, Resale): $2,463 psf, about $2,974 psf as a new launch here.
  • Martin Modern (860 m away, 450 homes, Resale): $2,822 psf, about $3,408 psf as a new launch here.
  • Dorsett Residences (940 m away, 68 homes, Resale): $2,230 psf, about $2,693 psf as a new launch here.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $3,020 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $3,170 psf. No developer floor can be worked out: the developers already owned the land.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.8% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (3.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: River Place (0.3 km): River Place 3.7%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (3.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 8/10. City fringe (6) + shops on site (+1) + walk to the business district (+1). School not counted: it sits on the 1 km line.
  • Entry price: 7/10. Current average $3,003 psf against about $3,020 psf from the closest competitors (−1%).
  • Exit plan: 5/10. The 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 9/10. Dining on site; People's Park Food Centre about 355 m and Hong Lim Food Centre about 390 m
  • MRT proximity: 9/10. Clarke Quay and Chinatown MRT exits within about 280 m

Overall 7.6 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
