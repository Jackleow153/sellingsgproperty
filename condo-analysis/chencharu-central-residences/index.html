Chencharu Central Residences: analysis and verdict

Chencharu Close (Khatib, Yishun) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $1,930 psf for the 3-bed: a good project where the price decides. At the expected $2,300 to $2,450 psf the base case earns about 0.0% to 1.3% a year after costs; walk away above about $2,120 psf ($1.87M). Skip the 5-bed unless you will live in it for 10 years or more.

  • The 5% price is reachable: about $1,930 psf, above the developer's floor of about $1,880 psf, but below the expected $2,300 to $2,450 psf.
  • Comparable condos grew 2.4% to 3.3% a year from launch to a year after completion (median 2.7%, our base case). A 5% net return needs about 6.1% to 6.3% a year in price growth.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 880 sq ft costs about $2.29M; a buyer then needs about $14,900 a month and $657k upfront, against $400k to $543k that typical Yishun and Sembawang upgraders bring.

Suits: Buyers who want to live here for 10 years or more and can buy within the walk-away price. Most buyers at resale will be HDB upgraders from Yishun and Sembawang, plus Chencharu BTO owners later (their 5-year minimum occupation periods end mostly after 2032). Skip it if: Investors who need 5% a year, buyers who must sell within 5 to 6 years, and anyone stretching for the largest units.

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 600 sq ft): Buy only below $1,930 psf. Buy at or below $1,930 psf ($1.16M); walk away above $2,130 psf ($1.28M). Exit about $1.56M needs about $10,200 a month (within reach of a median household) and $438k upfront.
  • 3-bed (about 880 sq ft): Buy only below $1,930 psf. Buy at or below $1,930 psf ($1.70M); walk away above $2,120 psf ($1.87M). Exit about $2.29M needs about $14,900 a month (within reach of a median household) and $657k upfront.
  • 4-bed (about 1,090 sq ft): Buy only below $1,920 psf. Buy at or below $1,920 psf ($2.09M); walk away above $2,110 psf ($2.30M). Exit about $2.84M needs about $18,500 a month (about 1.5 times the median household income) and $821k upfront.
  • 5-bed (about 1,330 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $1,920 psf ($2.55M); walk away above $2,110 psf ($2.81M). Exit about $3.46M needs about $22,600 a month (about 1.8 times the median household income) and $1.01M upfront.

Buy-at and walk-away prices

Buy-at price = the lower of fair value ($2,280) and the 5% ceiling ($1,930) = about $1,930 psf. That is above the developer floor, so a launch at this level is possible. Walk-away price = the entry at which the base case falls to about 3% a year, roughly what a home loan costs: about $2,120 psf for the 3-bed.

The 5% test

Because of stamp duty and selling costs, a 5% net return needs roughly 6.1% to 6.3% a year in price growth. Fail. At the expected $2,300 to $2,450 psf, the base case earns about 0.0% to 1.3% a year after costs. It would pass at about $1,930 psf.

Competitor performance

Comparable projects made 2.4% to 3.3% a year from launch to a year after completion. To earn 5% net, Chencharu Central Residences must grow about 6.2% a year, about 2.9 points more than the best of them, which is unlikely without a lower launch price.

District

This district is in a steady, value phase: Yishun condos grew about 2.5% to 2.7% a year, and the private resale pool near Khatib is thin, so the exit depends on HDB upgraders.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed and 5-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

The estate plan and the North-South Corridor are Confirmed but modest catalysts; no new MRT line is planned near Khatib, so the base case gets no transport premium.

Scorecard

  • Location: 6/10. A new estate inside mature Yishun, one stop from Northpoint City at Yishun MRT; large upgrader pool (796 four-room resales a year in Yishun). Not a town centre.
  • Entry price: 6/10. Expected $2,300 to $2,450 psf against our fair value of about $2,280 (+1% to +7%).
  • Exit plan: 5/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed, 5-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 9/10. A hawker centre of at least 3,500 sq m and a supermarket inside the development (Confirmed, Gamuda filing). Not open until the project is complete.
  • MRT proximity: 7/10. Within 450 m of Khatib MRT (Confirmed, Gamuda filing): about a 6-minute walk.

The scorecard (6.6) agrees with the price test.

Questions

Is Chencharu Central Residences a good investment?

Not at the expected $2,300 to $2,450 psf. After stamp duty and selling costs, the base case earns about 0.0% to 1.3% a year. It would earn 5% at about $1,930 psf.

How does it compare with Chencharu BTO flats?

They serve different buyers: a Chencharu Acres 4-room BTO flat is $353,000 to $428,000 before grants (HDB, Nov 2026), with income and ownership rules. See our Chencharu guide for the side-by-side.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

Our walk-away price is about $2,120 psf, about $1.87M for 880 sq ft. Above that, the base case falls below about 3% a year. These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
