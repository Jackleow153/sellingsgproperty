Rivelle Tampines (EC): analysis and verdict

Tampines Street 95 (Tampines West) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Sold out. An EC cannot be sold on before completion and can be resold only after its 5-year minimum occupation period, so later buyers will buy resales from about 2035; pay no more than about $1,930 psf in today's money. At launch: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $1,790 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $1,840 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $1,840 psf; a 3-bed of about 915 sq ft is about $1.68M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $1,930 psf (a 3-bed above about $1.77M). The launch average $1,790 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2035 (9 years): the 5-year minimum occupation period applies.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Arc At Tampines ($1,394 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (6)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $1,930 psf.
  • Watch: The Bedok Rise (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Arc At Tampines, The Tampines Trilliant) work out to about $1,840 psf as a new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (37% to 40% here): launches selling now in the OCR charge about 9% over a just-completed resale and about 2.6 points more for each year of its age (30 launches against 107 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Arc At Tampines (1,589 m away, 574 homes, completed 2014): its 3-bed-size resales (about 780 to 1,050 sq ft) averaged $1,394 psf over the last 6 months (6 resales), against a median $1,407 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $1,793 psf is $398 psf (+29%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.62M.
  • Also note: The Bedok Rise (GLS) site nearby (1,935 m, land $1,330 psf ppr) is likely to launch at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf: more than this project's price.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the launch average $1,793 psf, the base case makes about +47% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2035 (4.3% a year); bought at $1,840 psf it makes about +43%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $1.64M; about $2.54M by 2035 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders yes; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.
  • Cash needed under EC loan rules: At launch, a household at the $16,000 income ceiling can borrow at most about $1.01M under the 30% mortgage servicing ratio, so a 3-bed at $1,793 psf ($1.64M) needs about $690k in cash and CPF, including stamp duty.
  • Supply and land prices: Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby. Besides this project's own 572 homes, about 968 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Pinery Residences, Bedok Rise (GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 94 resales a year.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 572 homes, about 968 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Pinery Residences, Bedok Rise (GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 94 resales a year. Our test of 33 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the outer suburbs (11 km or more from the CBD): those with more than 1,817 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 4.0% a year over the past 4 years, against 4.3% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Low concern)

Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Low concern)

100% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with most units sold, there is little pressure to cut prices.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Bedok Rise (GLS), is likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

No Seller's Stamp Duty: the sale is after the 5-year minimum occupation period. Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Arc At Tampines 6.7%, The Tampines Trilliant 8.0%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise. That averages 7.3% a year, above the market's 10-year pace, so the base case is capped at 4.7% (URA non-landed index, 2016 to 2026): 2022 to 2026 included an unusual rise, and a new launch bought at a premium rarely outgrows the market for years.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
