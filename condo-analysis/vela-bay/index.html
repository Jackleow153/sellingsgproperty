Vela Bay: analysis and verdict

Bayshore Road / Bayshore Walk (Bayshore precinct, Bedok) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,780 psf. The current average $2,886 psf is 9% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,650 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,650 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Eco, Grandeur Park Residences, Costa Del Sol) work out to about $2,650 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 35% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Eco (1,362 m away, 748 homes, completed 2017) resold at a median $1,589 psf in the last 6 months (20 resales). At $2,886 psf this project is $1,297 psf (+82%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.80M ($1,751 psf).
  • The newest land bid nearby, New Upper Changi Road (GLS) (1,885 m, $1,537 psf ppr), is 11% above this site's land cost; that launch needs about $2,480 psf just to cover costs.
  • Bought at the current average $2,886 psf, the base case makes about +6% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (1.6% a year); bought at $2,650 psf it makes about +16%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 960 sq ft costs about $3.14M; a buyer then needs about $20,400 a month and $913k upfront, against $462k to $670k that typical Bedok and Tampines upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,650 psf. Walk away above: $2,780 psf. Potential gain: +6% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,886 psf (1.6% a year). Bear −4%, bull +6%).

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +47%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +31% over theirs (29 launches; +35% in the OCR, 12 launches). Above what the market usually pays.

  • Sales pace: About 57 a month. 397 of 515 homes sold in about 7 months since launch; 118 left, about 2 months at that pace.
  • Price to land cost: 2.08x. Price psf divided by land psf ppr.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Eco 4.3%, Costa Del Sol 6.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.4% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
