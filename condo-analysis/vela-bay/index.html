Vela Bay: analysis and verdict

Bayshore Road / Bayshore Walk (Bayshore precinct, Bedok) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,740 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,860 psf is 10% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,610 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,610 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,610 psf; a 3-bed of about 960 sq ft is about $2.51M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,740 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.63M). The current average $2,860 psf is above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Eco ($1,706 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (7)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,740 psf.
  • Watch: The New Upper Changi Road (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,230 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Eco, Grandeur Park Residences, Costa Del Sol) work out to about $2,610 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 31% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Eco (1,362 m away, 748 homes, completed 2017): its 3-bed-size resales (about 820 to 1,100 sq ft) averaged $1,706 psf over the last 6 months (7 resales), against a median $1,571 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,865 psf is $1,159 psf (+68%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.80M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, New Upper Changi Road (GLS) (1,885 m, $1,537 psf ppr), is 11% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,540 psf, and is likely to launch at about $3,230 psf (break-even plus the 27% that launches since 2023 priced above theirs).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,865 psf, the base case makes about +6% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (1.4% a year); bought at $2,610 psf it makes about +16%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.75M; about $3.09M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders no; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Eco 4.3%, Costa Del Sol 6.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.4% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
