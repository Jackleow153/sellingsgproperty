Lentor Central (GLS, 4th site): analysis and verdict

Lentor Central (Lentor Hills) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,520 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,570 to $2,760 psf is 11% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,400 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,400 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,400 psf; a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft is about $2.28M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,520 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.39M). The tested $2,570 to $2,760 psf is above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Panorama ($2,102 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (9)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,520 psf.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Lentor Gardens Residences, The Panorama, Grandeur 8) work out to about $2,400 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Panorama (1,123 m away, 698 homes, completed 2017): its 3-bed-size resales (about 810 to 1,090 sq ft) averaged $2,102 psf over the last 6 months (9 resales), against a median $2,094 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,666 psf is $563 psf (+27%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.25M.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the tested $2,666 psf, the base case makes about +7% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (1.3% a year); bought at $2,400 psf it makes about +19%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.53M; about $2.88M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.8% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Panorama 5.5%, Grandeur 8 4.0%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.8% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst: Lentor MRT has been open since 2021 and the estate is already planned, so the bull case equals the base case.
