Lentor Central (GLS, 4th site): analysis and verdict

Lentor Central (Lentor Hills) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,360 psf. The tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf is 20% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,250 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,250 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Lentor Gardens Residences, The Panorama, Grandeur 8) work out to about $2,250 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the tested $2,700 psf, the base case makes about −1% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−0.1% a year); bought at $2,250 psf it makes about +19%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $2.71M; a buyer then needs about $17,600 a month and $782k upfront, against $400k to $832k that typical Ang Mo Kio and Bishan and Yishun upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,250 psf. Walk away above: $2,360 psf. Potential gain: −1% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,700 psf (−0.1% a year). Bear −12%, bull −1%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 3-bed (about 950 sq ft): Walk away above $2,360 psf. Buy at or below $2,250 psf ($2.14M); walk away above $2,360 psf ($2.24M). At $2,700 psf: about −1% by 2032. Resale about $2.71M needs about $17,600 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,250 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,250 psf ($2.81M); walk away above $2,360 psf ($2.95M). At $2,700 psf: about −1% by 2032. Resale about $3.56M needs about $23,200 a month.

Closest competitors

1 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Lentor Gardens Residences (270 m away, 499 homes, Launched Jul 2026): $2,350 psf, about $2,350 psf as a new launch here.
  • The Panorama (1.1 km away, 698 homes, Resale): $2,090 psf, about $2,524 psf as a new launch here; 5.5% a year over 4 years.
  • Grandeur 8 (1.7 km away, 579 homes, Resale): $1,544 psf, about $1,864 psf as a new launch here; 4.2% a year over 4 years.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,250 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,360 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,170 to $2,300 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Panorama 5.5%, Grandeur 8 4.2%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst: Lentor MRT has been open since 2021 and the estate is already planned, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is in a supply-heavy phase: several Lentor projects complete within a few years of each other, and this one paid the highest land price of them.

Future buyers

The 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

No major transformation beyond the Lentor estate itself is planned near this site. The estate brings amenities and competing supply together.

Scorecard

  • Location: 6/10. A new private estate on the Thomson-East Coast Line, with Lentor Modern's mall nearby; few older amenities yet.
  • Entry price: 3/10. Tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf against about $2,250 psf from the closest competitors (+16% to +24%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 6/10. Lentor Modern's mall and supermarket about 500 m away. Hawker centres within a 10-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 7/10. Lentor MRT about 490 m away (straight line): about a 6-minute walk.

Overall 5.2 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

How does it compare with Lentor Gardens Residences?

Lentor Gardens Residences launched at $2,350 psf in July 2026 on land that cost $920 psf ppr. Lentor Signature paid $1,278, so it is likely to launch well above that.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,250 psf, about $2.14M for 950 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,360 psf ($2.24M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
