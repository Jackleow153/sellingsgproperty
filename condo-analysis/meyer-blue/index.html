Meyer Blue

Meyer Road / East Coast, D15 · UOL Group and Singapore Land Group (United Venture Development (Meyer))

Overall score 5.6 out of 10. Freehold by East Coast Park for big-family buyers, at a big premium. Meyer Blue is a freehold condo across the road from East Coast Park, from UOL and Singapore Land, and 81% sold. What is left is mostly large: 4-bedroom and 5-bedroom units with private lifts from about $4.5M, plus one 3-bedroom. The price is far above District 15 resale and there is no primary school within 1 km, so it suits families who want freehold space by the sea for the long term.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 184 of 226 (81%) on 5 Nov 2026, 42 left (PropertyNet); 114 on launch day
  • Price: $3,260 psf average at launch (Oct 2024); units left average about $3,180 psf (PropertyNet, Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: $1,668 psf of buildable floor area ($392.2M collective sale, Feb 2023)
  • Completion: Vacant possession by 31 Dec 2028 (developer)
  • Lease: Freehold
  • MRT: Katong Park MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) about 550 to 600 m, 6 to 9 minutes (sources differ)
  • Food and shops: Katong and Joo Chiat food streets, i12 Katong and Parkway Parade nearby (distances not given); East Coast Park across the road via an underpass
  • Primary schools: No primary school within 1 km; Tanjong Katong Primary about 1.3 km, Kong Hwa School about 1.6 km

Scores

  • Location: 7/10. City fringe (6) + East Coast Park across the road (+1); no primary school within 1 km
  • Entry price: 5/10. Units left average about $3,180 psf, about 71% above the District 15 condo resale median ($1,858 psf, URA, 12 months to Sep 2026). Many District 15 condos are older 99-year projects, which widens the gap for a freehold launch
  • Exit plan: 5/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; -1 nearby supply (East Coast Road and Tanjong Rhu land sites, other Katong launches)
  • Nearby food: 5/10. Katong food streets and malls a short trip away, distances not measured
  • MRT closeness: 6/10. Katong Park MRT about 550 to 600 m

Pros

  • Freehold
  • East Coast Park across the road via an underpass
  • 81% sold
  • Large units with private lifts
  • Keys by Dec 2028, earlier than most launches still selling

Cons

  • Units left average about $3,180 psf, about 71% above District 15 resale
  • Mostly 4- and 5-bedroom units left; cheapest is one 3-bedroom at $3.551M
  • No primary school within 1 km
  • Katong Park MRT about 550 to 600 m
  • More land sites coming nearby (East Coast Road, Tanjong Rhu)
