Verdict: Walk away above $3,180 psf. The expected $3,400 to $3,600 psf is 16% above what the closest competitors support (about $3,030 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $3,030 psf. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.2% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Amaryllis Ville (0.5 km), Draycott Eight (0.7 km): Amaryllis Ville 5.1%, Draycott Eight 3.4%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.2% a year): Newton MRT is already an interchange and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
