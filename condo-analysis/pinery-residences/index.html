Pinery Residences

Tampines West, D18 · Hoi Hup Realty and Sunway MCL (Hoi Hup Sunway Tampines Residential / Commercial)

Overall score 7.2 out of 10. Most convenient address in Tampines, but priced well above resale. Pinery puts a mall, supermarket and food court downstairs and an underground link to Tampines West MRT at the door, with four primary schools within 1 km. It sold 92.5% on its launch weekend. The catch is price: units left average about $2,625 psf, close to 80% above the District 18 resale median, and only 4- and 5-bedroom units remain. Best for families who will live in it for the long term.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 556 of 588 (95%) on 5 Nov 2026, 32 left (PropertyNet); 544 (92.5%) on the launch weekend
  • Price: $2,546 psf average on the launch weekend (28 to 29 Mar 2026); units left average about $2,625 psf (PropertyNet, 5 Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: About $1,004 psf of buildable floor area ($668.3M, tender closed Sep 2024; 1.9% above the second bid)
  • Completion: About Q4 2029 (expected TOP); vacant possession by 9 Oct 2031
  • Lease: 99 years from 7 Jan 2025
  • MRT: Direct underground link to Tampines West MRT (Downtown Line); Tampines interchange one stop
  • Food and shops: Pinery Mall on the first storey: supermarket, food court and about 30 food and drink brands; Our Tampines Hub about 17 minutes' walk
  • Primary schools: Junyuan Primary about 350 m, St. Hilda's Primary about 550 m, Tampines Primary about 700 m, St. Anthony's Canossian Primary about 850 m (straight line, OneMap block addresses)

Scores

  • Location: 7/10. Suburbs (5) + mall and supermarket on site (+1) + primary school within 1 km (+1); Bedok Reservoir Park about 10 minutes away, not beside
  • Entry price: 5/10. Units left average about $2,625 psf, about 79% above the District 18 median for 99-year resale condos ($1,468 psf, URA, 12 months to Sep 2026) and about 45% above the newest large resale condo nearby, Treasure at Tampines (about $1,813 psf, 2026 average)
  • Exit plan: 6/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; +1 big project with a wide unit mix; +1 MRT within 500 m; +1 primary school within 1 km; -1 nearby supply (Parktown Residence, Rivelle Tampines EC); -1 price gap above 75%
  • Nearby food: 8/10. Supermarket and food court on site
  • MRT closeness: 10/10. Direct underground link to an open MRT station

Pros

  • About 95% sold
  • Pinery Mall, supermarket and food court on the first storey
  • Underground link to Tampines West MRT
  • Four primary schools within 1 km, Junyuan Primary about 350 m
  • Tampines interchange one stop away

Cons

  • Only 4- and 5-bedroom units left, from $2.972M
  • About 79% above the District 18 resale median per sq ft
  • Parktown Residence and Rivelle Tampines EC nearby will compete at resale
  • Vacant possession as late as Oct 2031
  • Units left are priced above the launch average (about $2,625 against $2,546 psf)

Sources: PropertyNet · Stacked Homes: Treasure at Tampines
