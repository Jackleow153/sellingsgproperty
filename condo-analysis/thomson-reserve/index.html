Thomson Reserve: analysis and verdict

Bright Hill Drive (Upper Thomson; former Thomson View) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,790 psf. The current average $2,900 psf is 9% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,660 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,660 psf. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Jadescape, Thomson Three, Thomson Impressions, Thomson Grand) work out to about $2,660 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 22% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the RCR charge over their closest resale, 8 launches).
  • Jadescape (1,200 m away, 1,206 homes, completed 2022) resold at a median $2,291 psf in the last 6 months (35 resales). At $2,900 psf this project is $609 psf (+27%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.85M ($2,605 psf).
  • The newest land bid nearby, Lorong Puntong (Sin Ming Avenue GLS) (357 m, $1,612 psf ppr), is 37% above this site's land cost; that launch needs about $2,620 psf just to cover costs, and is expected at $3,000 to $3,100 (Stacked Homes).
  • Bought at the current average $2,900 psf, the base case makes about +14% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (2.6% a year); bought at $2,660 psf it makes about +24%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,055 sq ft costs about $3.71M; a buyer then needs about $24,100 a month and $1.09M upfront, against $470k to $832k that typical Bishan and Ang Mo Kio upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,660 psf. Walk away above: $2,790 psf. Potential gain: +14% (Base case after costs by 2031, bought at $2,900 psf (2.6% a year). Bear −1%, bull +19%).

  • 4-bed (about 1,365 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,660 psf ($3.63M); walk away above $2,790 psf ($3.81M). At $2,900 psf: about +13% by 2031. Resale about $4.80M needs about $31,200 a month.

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +33%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +31% over theirs (29 launches; +22% in the RCR, 8 launches). Above what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.3% gross. Competitors' median rent about $5.50 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $5,420 a month.

  • Sales pace: About 1,141 a month. 1,141 of 1,268 homes sold in about 1 month since launch; 127 left, about 0 months at that pace.
  • Price to land cost: 2.46x. Price psf divided by land psf ppr.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.9% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Thomson Three 6.7%, Thomson Impressions 5.9%, Thomson Grand 4.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.7% a year): Base plus 1 point a year for the Cross Island Line (Phase 1) at Bright Hill station, Confirmed for 2030.
