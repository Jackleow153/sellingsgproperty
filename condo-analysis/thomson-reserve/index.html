Thomson Reserve

Upper Thomson / Bright Hill, D20 · CapitaLand Development 50%, UOL 40%, Singapore Land 10%

Overall score 8.4 out of 10. Strong project, but most of the good-value units are gone. It scores highest in this batch because the MRT, mall, school and parks are all at the doorstep, and 90% sold on launch shows real demand. The catch is what is left: 125 units, and the developers have not published which types. Expect mostly bigger or higher-floor units that cost well above the starting prices.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 1,143 of 1,268 (90%) at launch; 125 left
  • Price: $2,900 psf (average of units sold, developers' statement)
  • Land cost: $1,178 psf of buildable floor area (agency figure, includes lease top-up)
  • Completion: 2H 2030 (developers); one site says 2029
  • Lease: 99 years from 14 Jul 2026
  • MRT: Next to Upper Thomson MRT by sheltered walkway, about 2 minutes to Exit 2
  • Food and shops: Thomson Plaza across the road by the MRT underpass; cafes and eateries along Upper Thomson Road. No hawker centre named in sources.
  • Primary schools: Ai Tong School about 0.5 km

Scores

  • Location: 9/10. City fringe (6) + mall across the road (+1) + reservoir and nature parks beside (+1) + primary school within 1 km (+1)
  • Entry price: 7/10. Average $2,900 psf is about 39% above the nearest 99-year resale condo, Thomson Three (about $2,080 psf)
  • Exit plan: 8/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; +1 big project with 2 to 5 bedrooms; +1 MRT within 500 m; +1 primary school within 1 km; -1 two more launches nearby (Upper Thomson Parcel A about 595 homes, Lorong Puntong about 140)
  • Nearby food: 8/10. Mall with supermarket and food across the road, plus the Upper Thomson food stretch
  • MRT closeness: 10/10. Sheltered link to an open MRT station, about 2 minutes

Pros

  • Sheltered 2-minute walk to Upper Thomson MRT; Bright Hill becomes a Cross Island Line interchange (expected by 2030)
  • Ai Tong School within 1 km
  • Thomson Plaza across the road
  • 90% sold at launch, the top-selling 2026 launch by units
  • Towers cover only 12% of the 5-hectare site

Cons

  • Only 125 units left; no full price list published
  • 56% of all units are 2-bedroom, so future resale competition among 2-bedders will be heavy
  • Starting prices were 11-21% above resale at JadeScape and Thomson Three
  • Keys only around 2H 2030 to Feb 2031
  • Two more new projects coming within about 500 m to 1 km

Sources: SG Properties review · Stacked Homes · SG Property Index
