Thomson Reserve: analysis and verdict

Bright Hill Drive (Upper Thomson; former Thomson View) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy only below $2,960 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,880 psf is a little above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,820 psf as a new launch), so negotiate or choose a cheaper stack. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,820 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,055 sq ft is about $2.98M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,960 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.12M). The current average $2,880 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2031 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Jadescape ($2,600 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (9)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,960 psf.
  • Watch: The Lorong Puntong (Sin Ming Avenue GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,000 to $3,100 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Jadescape, Thomson Three, Thomson Grand) work out to about $2,820 psf as a new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (12% to 29% here): launches selling now in the RCR charge about 2% over a just-completed resale and about 2.5 points more for each year of its age (29 launches against 103 of their resale competitors, this one and the 7 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Jadescape (1,200 m away, 1,206 homes, completed 2022): its 3-bed-size resales (about 900 to 1,210 sq ft) averaged $2,600 psf over the last 6 months (9 resales), against a median $2,296 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,879 psf is $279 psf (+11%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.85M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Lorong Puntong (Sin Ming Avenue GLS) (357 m, $1,612 psf ppr), is 37% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,650 psf, and analysts expect it to launch at $3,000 to $3,100 (Stacked Homes).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,879 psf, the base case makes about +16% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (3.0% a year); bought at $2,820 psf it makes about +18%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $3.04M; about $3.74M by 2031 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders with some savings; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.
  • Supply and land prices: Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby. Besides this project's own 1,268 homes, about 140 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Lorong Puntong (Sin Ming Avenue GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 243 resales a year.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 1,268 homes, about 140 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Lorong Puntong (Sin Ming Avenue GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 243 resales a year. Our test of 19 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the middle ring (6 to 11 km from the CBD): those with more than 862 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.9% a year over the past 4 years, against 3.6% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Moderate concern)

Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Low concern)

90% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with most units sold, there is little pressure to cut prices.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Lorong Puntong (Sin Ming Avenue GLS), is likely at about $3,000 to $3,100 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Thomson Three 6.7%, Thomson Grand 4.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise. That averages 5.7% a year, above the market's 10-year pace, so the base case is capped at 4.7% (URA non-landed index, 2016 to 2026): 2022 to 2026 included an unusual rise, and a new launch bought at a premium rarely outgrows the market for years.
  • Bull (5.7% a year): Base plus 1 point a year for the Cross Island Line (Phase 1) at Bright Hill station, Confirmed for 2030.
