River Valley Green (Parcel C): analysis and verdict

River Valley Green (Great World) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. The expected $3,300 to $3,500 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $3,440 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (River Modern, Riviere, Martin Modern, Irwell Hill Residences) work out to about $3,440 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $3,400 psf, the base case makes about +19% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (3.6% a year); bought at $3,440 psf it makes about +18%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $4.33M; a buyer then needs about $28,200 a month and $1.28M upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,440 psf. Walk away above: $3,610 psf. Potential gain: +19% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,400 psf (3.6% a year). Bear +6%, bull +19%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $3,440 psf ($2.41M); walk away above $3,610 psf ($2.53M). At $3,400 psf: about +20% by 2032. Resale about $3.03M needs about $19,700 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,440 psf ($3.44M); walk away above $3,610 psf ($3.61M). At $3,400 psf: about +19% by 2032. Resale about $4.33M needs about $28,200 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,350 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,440 psf ($4.64M); walk away above $3,610 psf ($4.87M). At $3,400 psf: about +19% by 2032. Resale about $5.84M needs about $38,000 a month.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $3,440 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $3,610 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,760 to $2,920 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): No condo nearby has a suitable record (a 3-bed price change over 4 years with at least 10 resales in the past year), so we use URA's islandwide non-landed index: 4.7% a year from Q3 2016 to Q3 2026.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): Great World MRT is already open and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 9/10. Prime River Valley next to Great World MRT and River Valley Primary School (about 137 m, OneMap).
  • Entry price: 7/10. Expected $3,300 to $3,500 psf against about $3,440 psf from the closest competitors (−4% to +2%).
  • Exit plan: 3/10. The 2-bed, 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. Great World mall by Great World MRT, about 120 m away (OneMap).
  • MRT proximity: 9/10. Great World MRT about 121 m in a straight line (OneMap): about a 2-minute walk.

Overall 7.2 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
