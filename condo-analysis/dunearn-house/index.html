Dunearn House: analysis and verdict

760–770 Dunearn Road (Bukit Timah Turf City) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $3,070 psf. A 3-bed here at about $3,150 psf is 8% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,920 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,920 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,920 psf; a 3-bed of about 870 sq ft is about $2.54M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,070 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.67M). The current average $3,150 psf is above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2031 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Fourth Avenue Residences ($2,747 psf, 3-bed size, last 24 months (6)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,070 psf.
  • Watch: The Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site) launch nearby: likely at about $3,000 to $3,300 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. The price is above what same-size competitors support, whatever the land cost. A cheaper new launch may come nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 380 homes, about 822 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site), Amberwood at Holland, Holland Plain (Sim Lian, 2nd site)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 226 resales a year. Our test of 19 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the middle ring (6 to 11 km from the CBD): those with more than 862 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.9% a year over the past 4 years, against 3.6% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (High concern)

This site's land ($1,410 psf ppr) cost −2% more than Amberwood at Holland's ($1,432), which lifts the developer's break-even by about −1%. A 3-bed here at about $3,149 psf is +4% above Amberwood at Holland's $3,019 launch average; private prices have risen about 0% since then (URA index), so in today's money you pay about 4% more than its buyers did, about as much as the developer's higher costs: the land bid is being passed on. Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

72% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with many units left, late discounts or deals on slower stacks are possible as the deadline nears, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Moderate concern)

The next launch nearby, Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site), is likely at about $3,000 to $3,300 psf, close to this project's 3-bed. A cheaper neighbour later could cap this project's resale price; compare both before committing.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.
