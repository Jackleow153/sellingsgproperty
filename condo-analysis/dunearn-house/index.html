Dunearn House: analysis and verdict

760–770 Dunearn Road (Bukit Timah Turf City) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $3,000 psf. The current average $3,140 psf is 10% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,860 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,860 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Fourth Avenue Residences, Amberwood at Holland, Mayfair Gardens, Mayfair Modern) work out to about $2,860 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $3,140 psf, the base case makes about +6% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (1.1% a year); bought at $2,860 psf it makes about +16%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 870 sq ft costs about $3.07M; a buyer then needs about $20,000 a month and $892k upfront, against $652k to $1.09M that typical Queenstown and Clementi upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,860 psf. Walk away above: $3,000 psf. Potential gain: +6% (Base case after costs by 2031, bought at $3,140 psf (1.1% a year). Bear −5%, bull +6%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 615 sq ft): Walk away above $3,000 psf. Buy at or below $2,860 psf ($1.76M); walk away above $3,000 psf ($1.84M). At $3,140 psf: about +6% by 2031. Resale about $2.17M needs about $14,100 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 870 sq ft): Walk away above $3,000 psf. Buy at or below $2,860 psf ($2.49M); walk away above $3,000 psf ($2.61M). At $3,140 psf: about +6% by 2031. Resale about $3.07M needs about $20,000 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,300 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,860 psf ($3.72M); walk away above $3,000 psf ($3.90M). At $3,140 psf: about +5% by 2031. Resale about $4.59M needs about $29,900 a month.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Duchess Crest (1.5 km): Duchess Crest 4.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

  • MRT proximity: 8/10. About 450 m, 4 minutes

Overall 5.8 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
