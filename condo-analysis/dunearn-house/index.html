Dunearn House: analysis and verdict

760–770 Dunearn Road (Bukit Timah Turf City) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy only below $3,180 psf. A 3-bed here at about $3,150 psf is a little above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $3,030 psf as a new launch), so negotiate or choose a cheaper stack. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $3,030 psf; a 3-bed of about 870 sq ft is about $2.64M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,180 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.77M). The current average $3,150 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2031 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Fourth Avenue Residences ($2,747 psf, 3-bed size, last 24 months (6)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,180 psf.
  • Watch: The Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site) launch nearby: likely at about $3,000 to $3,300 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Fourth Avenue Residences, Amberwood at Holland, Mayfair Gardens, Mayfair Modern) work out to about $3,030 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 23% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 7 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Fourth Avenue Residences (609 m away, 476 homes, completed 2022): its 3-bed-size resales (about 740 to 1,000 sq ft) averaged $2,747 psf over the last 24 months (6 resales), against a median $2,601 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $3,149 psf is $402 psf (+15%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $3.05M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site) (0 m, $1,625 psf ppr), is 15% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,660 psf, and analysts expect it to launch at $3,000-3,300 (PropNex, SRI, CBRE).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $3,149 psf, the base case makes about +12% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (2.2% a year); bought at $3,030 psf it makes about +16%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.74M; about $3.25M by 2031 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders with some savings; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.
