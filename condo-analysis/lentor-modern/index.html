Floor plans

  • 1-bedroom, Type A1S (527 sq ft): layout score 6/10. Pros: Bigger than most new 1-bedrooms at 527 sq ft; Bedroom fits a queen bed and full wardrobe; Small store for the distribution board. Cons: The galley kitchen doubles as the entrance passage; No kitchen window.
  • 2-bedroom, Type B2 (732 sq ft): layout score 8/10. Pros: Dumbbell: the two bedrooms are on opposite sides of the living room, good for sharing or renting out a room; A flex corner for a study desk; Small balcony and AC ledge (5 and 3 sq m), so little paid area is lost. Cons: Open kitchen without a window; Living room is narrow between the two bedrooms.
  • 3-bedroom, Type C3 (990 sq ft): layout score 5/10. Pros: Three bedrooms plus a flex corner in 990 sq ft; All bedrooms face the same side with full-width windows. Cons: Long entrance passage from the door to the living room; AC ledge as large as the balcony (6 sq m each); Narrow living and dining room; open kitchen without a window.
  • 4-bedroom, Type D1 (1,528 sq ft): layout score 10/10. Pros: Dumbbell: the junior master is on the other side of the living room; Dry and wet kitchens with a yard, WC and household shelter; A flex room that works as a study. Cons: Large AC ledge (7 sq m) and balcony (12 sq m) together are about 13% of the area; Bedroom 4 is small.

Overall score 8.0 out of 10. Integrated mall and MRT link, at a premium to its neighbours. Lentor Modern has its own mall, opened in January 2026, directly connected to Lentor MRT, with Anderson Primary about 700 m away. It resells about 14% above nearby condos, and more Lentor launches will complete around it.
