8@BT: analysis and verdict

6 / 8 Bukit Timah Link · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,680 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $3,000 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $3,000 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft is about $3.00M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,150 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.15M). The current average $2,680 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Mayfair Modern ($2,295 psf, 3-bed size, last 12 months (5)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,150 psf.
  • Watch: The Holland Plain (Sim Lian, 2nd site) launch nearby: likely at about $3,150 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Mayfair Modern, Mayfair Gardens, Daintree Residence, The Hillford, Verdale) work out to about $3,000 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 34% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Mayfair Modern (661 m away, 171 homes, completed 2022): its 3-bed-size resales (about 850 to 1,150 sq ft) averaged $2,295 psf over the last 12 months (5 resales), against a median $2,230 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,676 psf is $381 psf (+17%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.51M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Holland Plain (Sim Lian, 2nd site) (1,799 m, $1,491 psf ppr), is 11% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,480 psf, and is likely to launch at about $3,150 psf (break-even plus the 27% that launches since 2023 priced above theirs).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,676 psf, the base case makes about +30% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (6.8% a year); bought at $3,000 psf it makes about +16%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.68M; about $3.70M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders no; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.
