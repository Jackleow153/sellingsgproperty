8@BT

Beauty World / Bukit Timah Link, D21 · Bukit Sembawang Estates

Overall score 7.6 out of 10. Most convenient spot in Beauty World, with keys in 2027. 8@BT sits about 125 m from Beauty World MRT, across the road from Beauty World's malls and supermarkets, with Pei Hwa Presbyterian Primary within 1 km. It completes around Q4 2027, earlier than most launches still selling. About three quarters had sold by July 2026, and recent prices are about a third above the nearest completed 99-year condo. A small project, so it suits owner-occupiers more than investors.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

  • Sales so far: At least 117 of 158 (74%) by Jul 2026, based on caveats lodged; the developer has not published a units-left count (latest sale reported 22 Oct 2026)
  • Price: Caveats at launch (Sep 2024) ranged $2,527 to $3,031 psf; 12-month median about $2,675 psf (29 caveats, to Jul 2026)
  • Land cost: $1,343 psf of buildable floor area ($200M)
  • Completion: Expected Q4 2027
  • Lease: 99 years from Feb 2023
  • MRT: Beauty World MRT (Downtown Line) about 125 m, 2 minutes
  • Food and shops: Beauty World Centre and Plaza, Bukit Timah Plaza and The Reserve Mall nearby, with Cold Storage and FairPrice Finest
  • Primary schools: Pei Hwa Presbyterian Primary within 1 km (distance not given); Bukit Timah Primary and Methodist Girls' within 2 km
