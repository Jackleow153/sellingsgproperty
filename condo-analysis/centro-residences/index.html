Centro Residences

Ang Mo Kio, D20 · 99 years · completed 2014 · 329 homes

Deep dive: facts at a glance, average price and profit year by year for each unit type, actual resale deals, growth against the district, who buys here, an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator, floor plans with a layout score and nearby competitors.

Key facts

  • Developer: Eunos Link Technology Park Ltd (Far East Organisation, per Stacked Homes)
  • Address: Ang Mo Kio Avenue 8
  • Lease: 99 years from 11 Dec 2007
  • Unit mix: 122 2-bedroom (743 - 1,582 sq ft), 172 3-bedroom (904 - 1,561 sq ft), 33 4-bedroom (1,281 - 1,281 sq ft)
  • Nearest MRT: Ang Mo Kio, about 140 m in a straight line
  • Primary schools within 1 km: Jing Shan Primary School (about 420 m), Teck Ghee Primary School (about 590 m), Mayflower Primary School (about 880 m), Ang Mo Kio Primary School (about 980 m)
  • Profitable resales: 114 of 135 resales from Feb 2017 to Oct 2026 made a profit (EdgeProp)
  • Average price growth: 2.98% a year since 2015, against 3.85% for the District 20 average
  • Biggest profit: a 3-bedroom bought Oct 2020 for about $1,691k and sold Oct 2025 for about $2,508k
  • Buyers in the past 12 months: 62.6% Singaporeans, 11.9% permanent residents, 25.1% foreigners; 39.5% came from HDB flats
  • Nearby competitors: The Panorama (1.7 km), AMO Residence (1.7 km), Sky Vue (2 km), Sky Habitat (2 km)

Floor plans

  • 2-bedroom, 2-bedroom: layout score 7/10. Pros: Large master bedroom and a big living and dining room; Household shelter and a yard by the kitchen. Cons: Bay windows in both bedrooms count in the size you pay for; No balcony; Open kitchen facing the living room.
  • 3-bedroom, Type C1c (b): layout score 7/10. Pros: Yard and utility room behind the kitchen; Wide living and dining room with a balcony; Short corridor. Cons: Three bay windows: area you pay for but cannot walk on; Open kitchen with an island facing the dining room; Not a dumbbell.
  • 4-bedroom, 4-bedroom (1,281 sq ft): layout score 6/10. Pros: Four bedrooms with a yard, WC and utility room for a helper; Large living and dining room with a balcony. Cons: Four bay windows: a big share of the 1,281 sq ft cannot be walked on; A long corridor to the bedrooms; Open kitchen.

Overall score 7.3 out of 10. The most convenient address in the group at the lowest price. Centro Residences sits at Ang Mo Kio town centre: AMK Hub about 100 m, Ang Mo Kio MRT about 140 m, a market and food centre about 290 m and four primary schools within 1 km. It is the oldest condo here (2014), which helps explain the lowest price psf.
