Centro Residences

Ang Mo Kio, D20 · 99 years · completed 2014 · 329 homes

Deep dive: average price and profit year by year for each unit type, rents, comparison with The Panorama, an HDB upgrader affordability calculator and a break-even resale price calculator.

Overall score 7.3 out of 10. The most convenient address in the group at the lowest price. Centro Residences sits at Ang Mo Kio town centre: AMK Hub about 100 m, Ang Mo Kio MRT about 140 m, a market and food centre about 290 m and four primary schools within 1 km. It is the oldest condo here (2014), which helps explain the lowest price psf.
