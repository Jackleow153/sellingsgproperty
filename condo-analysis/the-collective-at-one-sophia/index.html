The Collective at One Sophia

Dhoby Ghaut / Sophia Road, D09 · CEL Development (Chip Eng Seng), SingHaiyi Crystal and Ultra Infinity (Sophia Residential)

Overall score 7.0 out of 10. Priced close to District 9 resale, but sales have been slow. One Sophia sits in the Dhoby Ghaut and Bras Basah arts district, with four MRT stations within about 700 m and shops in its own podium. Its units left average about $2,746 psf, only about a quarter above District 9 99-year resale, which is low for a prime-district launch. The weak point is demand: only 27% sold in two years, and most units are studios to 2-bedrooms, so resale will face many similar units. Best for long-term own stay or renting to students and city workers.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 98 of 367 (27%) on 5 Nov 2026, 269 left (PropertyNet, developer availability)
  • Price: $2,739 psf average in the launch month (Nov 2024, URA, 54 sales); $2,766 psf across all 98 new sales to Jun 2026 (URA); units left average about $2,746 psf (5 Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: About $1,426 psf of buildable floor area including lease top-up (former Peace Centre and Peace Mansion, $650M collective sale, Dec 2021)
  • Completion: Expected Dec 2029
  • Lease: 99 years from 6 Dec 2023
  • MRT: Four stations within about 700 m (developer map): Dhoby Ghaut, Bencoolen, Rochor and Bras Basah; walking distance to each not stated
  • Food and shops: 127 shops in the project's own podium (open at completion); Plaza Singapura at Dhoby Ghaut nearby (distance not stated)
  • Primary schools: St. Margaret's School (Primary) about 350 to 550 m (source gives both); Farrer Park Primary about 1.1 km

Scores

  • Location: 9/10. Prime city (7) + shops on site (+1) + primary school within 1 km (+1)
  • Entry price: 8/10. Units left average about $2,746 psf, about 24% above the District 9 median for 99-year resale condos ($2,208 psf, URA, 12 months to Sep 2026)
  • Exit plan: 5/10. Start 5; +1 big project; +1 primary school within 1 km; -1 under 30% sold a month or more after launch (27% after two years); -1 nearby supply (Sophia Meadow, Peck Hay Road and the Newton land sites)
  • Nearby food: 7/10. Shops and eateries on site at completion; Plaza Singapura nearby
  • MRT closeness: 6/10. Four stations within about 700 m; the walk to each is not measured

Pros

  • Units left priced only about 24% above District 9 99-year resale
  • Wide choice: 269 units left, studios to 3-bedrooms
  • Four MRT stations within about 700 m
  • Shops in the project's own podium; Plaza Singapura nearby
  • St. Margaret's Primary within 1 km; LaSalle, NAFA and SMU close by for tenants

Cons

  • Only 27% sold after two years
  • Mostly small units, so resale will face many similar homes
  • Keys around Dec 2029
  • Several new projects and land sites nearby (Sophia Meadow, Peck Hay Road, Newton)
  • Walking distances to the MRT stations not measured
