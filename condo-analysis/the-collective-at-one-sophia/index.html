The Collective at One Sophia: analysis and verdict

Sophia Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,840 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,890 psf as a new launch). 2-bed is the most wanted size in this rental and investment area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed at or below $2,890 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,125 sq ft is about $3.25M.
  • Best size here: 2-bed: this is an rental and investment area, where smaller units suit investors and tenants.
  • Walk away: Above $3,030 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.41M). The current average $2,840 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Sophia Hills ($2,288 psf, 3-bed size, last 12 months (3)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,030 psf.
  • Watch: The Peck Hay Road (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,400 to $3,900 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Sophia Hills, The M, Sophia Meadow) work out to about $2,890 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 24% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 7 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: Sophia Hills (188 m away, 493 homes, completed 2018): its 3-bed-size resales (about 960 to 1,290 sq ft) averaged $2,288 psf over the last 12 months (3 resales), against a median $2,109 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,839 psf is $551 psf (+24%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.35M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Peck Hay Road (GLS) (1,460 m, $1,865 psf ppr), is 31% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,990 psf, and analysts expect it to launch at $3,400-3,900 (PropNex, ERA, CBRE, Knight Frank).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,839 psf, the base case makes about +6% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (1.5% a year); bought at $2,890 psf it makes about +4%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $3.19M; about $3.61M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders with some savings; HDB 4-room upgraders with some savings; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.

Buy at or below: $2,890 psf. Walk away above: $3,030 psf. Potential gain: +6% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,839 psf (1.5% a year). Bear +1%, bull +6%).

  • 3-bed (about 1,125 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $2,890 psf ($3.25M); walk away above $3,030 psf ($3.41M). At about $2,839 psf: about +6% by 2030. Resale about $3.61M in 2030: good resale pool. Own-stay size in a rental area.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.3% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.6% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: 8 @ Mount Sophia (0.4 km): Sophia Hills 3.1%, 8 @ Mount Sophia 2.2%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (2.6% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
