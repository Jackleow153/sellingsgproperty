Wynwood Grand (EC): analysis and verdict

Woodlands Drive 17 (Woodlands South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $1,820 psf. The expected $1,750 to $1,850 psf is a little above what the closest competitors support (about $1,730 psf), so aim for the lower end. Skip the 5-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Twin Fountains, Forestville, La Casa) work out to about $1,730 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 36% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches).
  • Twin Fountains (1,074 m away, 418 homes, completed 2016) resold at a median $1,338 psf in the last 6 months (10 resales). At $1,800 psf this project is $462 psf (+35%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.49M ($1,331 psf).
  • Bought at the expected $1,800 psf, the base case makes about +44% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2036 (4.2% a year); bought at $1,730 psf it makes about +50%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $2.62M; a buyer then needs about $17,000 a month and $754k upfront, against $398k to $505k that typical Woodlands and Sembawang upgraders bring.
  • At launch, a household at the $16,000 income ceiling can borrow at most about $1.01M under the 30% mortgage servicing ratio, so a 3-bed at $1,800 psf ($1.71M) needs about $760k in cash and CPF, including stamp duty.

Buy at or below: $1,730 psf. Walk away above: $1,820 psf. Potential gain: +44% (Base case after costs by 2036, bought at $1,800 psf (4.2% a year). Bear +15%, bull +44%).

Potential gain

No Seller's Stamp Duty: the sale is after the 5-year minimum occupation period. Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.6% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Twin Fountains 6.3%, Forestville 4.8%, La Casa 4.8%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst within reach of the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Questions

Is Wynwood Grand a good investment?

At the expected $1,750 to $1,850 psf, the base case makes about +44% by 2036 after stamp duty and selling costs (4.2% a year). Bought at about $1,730 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +50%.

Can I sell before 2036?

Not on the open market. An executive condo must be lived in for 5 years from TOP before it can be sold to any Singaporean or PR (HDB). Its tender closed before 8 May 2026, so the 5-year rule applies; EC sites closing later have 10 years.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $1,730 psf, about $1.64M for 950 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $1,820 psf ($1.73M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
