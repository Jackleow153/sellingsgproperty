Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site): analysis and verdict

Dunearn Road (Bukit Timah Turf City) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $3,260 psf. The expected $3,000 to $3,300 psf is a little above what the closest competitors support (about $3,100 psf), so aim for the lower end. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Dunearn House, Fourth Avenue Residences, Amberwood at Holland) work out to about $3,100 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $3,150 psf, the base case makes about +14% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (2.6% a year); bought at $3,100 psf it makes about +16%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $3.83M; a buyer then needs about $24,900 a month and $1.13M upfront, against $652k to $1.09M that typical Queenstown and Clementi upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,100 psf. Walk away above: $3,260 psf. Potential gain: +14% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,150 psf (2.6% a year). Bear +3%, bull +14%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Buy only below $3,260 psf. Buy at or below $3,100 psf ($2.17M); walk away above $3,260 psf ($2.28M). At $3,150 psf: about +14% by 2032. Resale about $2.68M needs about $17,400 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,100 psf ($3.10M); walk away above $3,260 psf ($3.26M). At $3,150 psf: about +14% by 2032. Resale about $3.83M needs about $24,900 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,350 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,100 psf ($4.18M); walk away above $3,260 psf ($4.40M). At $3,150 psf: about +13% by 2032. Resale about $5.17M needs about $33,600 a month.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Duchess Crest (1.5 km): Duchess Crest 4.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.3% a year): Sixth Avenue MRT is already open and the Turf City plans are not Confirmed for the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
