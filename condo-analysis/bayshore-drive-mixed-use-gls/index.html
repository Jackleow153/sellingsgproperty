Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS): analysis and verdict

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy only below $2,470 psf for the 3-bed: a good project where the price decides. At the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf the base case earns about 1.0% to 2.1% a year after costs; walk away above about $2,720 psf ($2.61M). Skip the 4-bed unless you will live in it for 10 years or more.

  • The 5% price is reachable: about $2,470 psf, above the developer's floor of about $2,300 psf, but below the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf.
  • Comparable condos grew -0.9% to 4.7% a year from launch to a year after completion (median 2.9%, our base case). A 5% net return needs about 6.1% to 6.3% a year in price growth.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft costs about $3.22M; a buyer then needs about $20,900 a month and $937k upfront, against $462k to $670k that typical Bedok and Tampines upgraders bring.

Suits: Buyers who want to live here for 10 years or more and can buy within the walk-away price. Most buyers at resale will be HDB upgraders from Bedok and Tampines, private-to-private movers in the East Coast, and families near Temasek Primary. Skip it if: Investors who need 5% a year, buyers who must sell within 5 to 6 years, and anyone stretching for the largest units.

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 650 sq ft): Buy only below $2,490 psf. Buy at or below $2,490 psf ($1.62M); walk away above $2,730 psf ($1.77M). Exit about $2.18M needs about $14,200 a month (within reach of a median household) and $624k upfront.
  • 3-bed (about 958 sq ft): Buy only below $2,470 psf. Buy at or below $2,470 psf ($2.37M); walk away above $2,720 psf ($2.61M). Exit about $3.22M needs about $20,900 a month (about 1.7 times the median household income) and $937k upfront.
  • 4-bed (about 1,275 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,470 psf ($3.15M); walk away above $2,710 psf ($3.46M). Exit about $4.28M needs about $27,900 a month (about 2.2 times the median household income) and $1.27M upfront.

Buy-at and walk-away prices

Buy-at price = the lower of fair value ($2,910) and the 5% ceiling ($2,470) = about $2,470 psf. That is above the developer floor, so a launch at this level is possible. Walk-away price = the entry at which the base case falls to about 3% a year, roughly what a home loan costs: about $2,720 psf for the 3-bed.

The 5% test

Because of stamp duty and selling costs, a 5% net return needs roughly 6.1% to 6.3% a year in price growth. Fail. At the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf, the base case earns about 1.0% to 2.1% a year after costs. It would pass at about $2,470 psf.

Competitor performance

Comparable projects made -0.9% to 4.7% a year from launch to a year after completion. To earn 5% net, Bayshore Drive site must grow about 6.2% a year, about 1.5 points more than the best of them, which is unlikely without a lower launch price.

District

This district is in an early, supply-heavy phase: the first two projects set record suburban prices, and about 10,000 homes are planned around them.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

The MRT station and the integrated mall are Confirmed, but the station opens before you buy, so it is already in the price. The precinct plan brings amenities and supply together.

Scorecard

  • Location: 6/10. East Coast Park and the sea next door, Temasek Primary within 1 km, but a precinct still to be built with no shops yet.
  • Entry price: 7/10. Tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf against our fair value of about $2,910 (-2% to +3%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. A mall of about 22,100 sq m in the development (Confirmed) and East Coast Lagoon Food Village through the park.
  • MRT proximity: 10/10. Bedok South MRT directly beneath the site (URA site details via ERA and The Edge), opening 26 Dec 2026.

The scorecard (7.0) agrees with the price test.

Questions

Is the Bayshore Drive condo a good investment?

Not at the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf. After stamp duty and selling costs, the base case earns about 1.0% to 2.1% a year. It would earn 5% at about $2,470 psf.

How does it compare with Vela Bay?

Vela Bay, next door, launched at $2,886 psf in April 2026 and was 77% sold by November. This site paid about 5% less for its land and adds its own MRT station, mall and bus interchange, but it is more than twice Vela Bay's size.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

Our walk-away price is about $2,720 psf, about $2.61M for 958 sq ft. Above that, the base case falls below about 3% a year. These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
