Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS): analysis and verdict

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,780 psf. The tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf is 10% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,650 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,650 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Vela Bay, Costa Del Sol, Eco, Grandeur Park Residences) work out to about $2,650 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium and 10% for this project being integrated.
  • Bought at the tested $2,925 psf, the base case makes about +11% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2033 (2.1% a year); bought at $2,650 psf it makes about +22%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft costs about $3.30M; a buyer then needs about $21,500 a month and $963k upfront, against $462k to $670k that typical Bedok and Tampines upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,650 psf. Walk away above: $2,780 psf. Potential gain: +11% (Base case after costs by 2033, bought at $2,925 psf (2.1% a year). Bear −3%, bull +13%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 650 sq ft): Walk away above $2,780 psf. Buy at or below $2,650 psf ($1.72M); walk away above $2,780 psf ($1.81M). At $2,925 psf: about +11% by 2033. Resale about $2.24M needs about $14,600 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 958 sq ft): Walk away above $2,780 psf. Buy at or below $2,650 psf ($2.54M); walk away above $2,780 psf ($2.66M). At $2,925 psf: about +11% by 2033. Resale about $3.30M needs about $21,500 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,275 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,650 psf ($3.38M); walk away above $2,780 psf ($3.54M). At $2,925 psf: about +10% by 2033. Resale about $4.39M needs about $28,600 a month.

2 of the 4 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,650 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,780 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,300 to $2,440 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Costa Del Sol 6.5%, Eco 4.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.9% a year): Base plus 0.5 point as the Bayshore precinct fills in, with the mall around end-2030 and SAFRA next door in 2030 (Reported); Bedok South MRT opens on 26 Dec 2026 (Confirmed), before you buy, so it is already in the price.

District

This district is in an early, supply-heavy phase: the first two projects set record suburban prices, and about 10,000 homes are planned around them.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

The MRT station and the integrated mall are Confirmed, but the station opens before you buy, so it is already in the price. The precinct plan brings amenities and supply together.

Scorecard

  • Location: 6/10. East Coast Park and the sea next door, Temasek Primary within 1 km, but a precinct still to be built with no shops yet.
  • Entry price: 4/10. Tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf against about $2,650 psf from the closest competitors (+8% to +13%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. A mall of about 22,100 sq m in the development (Confirmed) and East Coast Lagoon Food Village through the park.
  • MRT proximity: 10/10. Bedok South MRT directly beneath the site (URA site details via ERA and The Edge), opening 26 Dec 2026.

Overall 6.4 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is the Bayshore Drive condo a good investment?

At the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf, the base case makes about +11% by 2033 after stamp duty and selling costs (2.1% a year). Bought at about $2,650 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +22%.

How does it compare with Vela Bay?

Vela Bay, next door, launched at $2,886 psf in April 2026 and was 77% sold by November. This site paid about 5% less for its land and adds its own MRT station, mall and bus interchange, but it is more than twice Vela Bay's size.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,650 psf, about $2.54M for 958 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,780 psf ($2.66M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
