Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS): analysis and verdict

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,850 to $3,000 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,990 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,990 psf; a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft is about $2.86M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,140 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.01M). The tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2033 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Eco ($1,706 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (7)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,140 psf.
  • Watch: The Bedok Rise (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: moderate. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. A cheaper new launch may come nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Moderate concern)

Besides this project's own 1,280 homes, about 895 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Vela Bay, Bedok Rise (GLS)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 473 resales a year. Our test of 19 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the middle ring (6 to 11 km from the CBD): those with more than 862 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.9% a year over the past 4 years, against 3.6% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Expect some competition when units complete together, mainly on rents and resale speed, but no collapse: the local buyer pool is deep.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Low concern)

This site's land ($1,323 psf ppr) cost −5% more than Vela Bay's ($1,388), which lifts the developer's break-even by about −4%. A 3-bed here at about $2,925 psf is +1% above Vela Bay's $2,886 launch average; private prices have risen about +1% since then (URA index), so in today's money you pay about the same as its buyers did, about as much as the developer's higher costs: the land bid is being passed on. Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS). That pushes them to price to sell at launch; slow sellers sometimes offer deals near the deadline, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Moderate concern)

The next launch nearby, Bedok Rise (GLS), is likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf, below this project's 3-bed. A cheaper neighbour later could cap this project's resale price; compare both before committing.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Costa Del Sol (0.6 km): Eco 4.3%, Costa Del Sol 6.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise. That averages 5.4% a year, above the market's 10-year pace, so the base case is capped at 4.7% (URA non-landed index, 2016 to 2026): 2022 to 2026 included an unusual rise, and a new launch bought at a premium rarely outgrows the market for years.
  • Bull (5.2% a year): Base plus 0.5 point as the Bayshore precinct fills in, with the mall around end-2030 and SAFRA next door in 2030 (Reported); Bedok South MRT opens on 26 Dec 2026 (Confirmed), before you buy, so it is already in the price.
