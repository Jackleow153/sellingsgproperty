Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS): analysis and verdict

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. The tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $2,970 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Eco, Grandeur Park Residences, Vela Bay) work out to about $2,970 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 36% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches) and 10% for this project being integrated.
  • Eco (1,071 m away, 748 homes, completed 2017) resold at a median $1,589 psf in the last 6 months (20 resales). At $2,925 psf this project is $1,336 psf (+84%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.80M ($1,751 psf).
  • Bought at the tested $2,925 psf, the base case makes about +24% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2033 (4.4% a year); bought at $2,970 psf it makes about +22%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft costs about $3.70M; a buyer then needs about $24,100 a month and $1.09M upfront, against $462k to $670k that typical Bedok and Tampines upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,970 psf. Walk away above: $3,120 psf. Potential gain: +24% (Base case after costs by 2033, bought at $2,925 psf (4.4% a year). Bear +9%, bull +27%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 650 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $2,970 psf ($1.93M); walk away above $3,120 psf ($2.03M). At $2,925 psf: about +25% by 2033. Resale about $2.51M needs about $16,300 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 958 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,970 psf ($2.85M); walk away above $3,120 psf ($2.99M). At $2,925 psf: about +24% by 2033. Resale about $3.70M needs about $24,100 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,275 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,970 psf ($3.79M); walk away above $3,120 psf ($3.98M). At $2,925 psf: about +24% by 2033. Resale about $4.93M needs about $32,100 a month.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Costa Del Sol (0.6 km): Eco 4.3%, Costa Del Sol 6.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.9% a year): Base plus 0.5 point as the Bayshore precinct fills in, with the mall around end-2030 and SAFRA next door in 2030 (Reported); Bedok South MRT opens on 26 Dec 2026 (Confirmed), before you buy, so it is already in the price.

Scorecard

  • Location: 6/10. East Coast Park and the sea next door, Temasek Primary within 1 km, but a precinct still to be built with no shops yet.
  • Entry price: 7/10. Tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf against about $2,970 psf from the closest competitors (−4% to +1%).
  • Exit plan: 3/10. The 2-bed, 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. A mall of about 22,100 sq m in the development (Confirmed) and East Coast Lagoon Food Village through the park.
  • MRT proximity: 10/10. Bedok South MRT directly beneath the site (URA site details via ERA and The Edge), opening 26 Dec 2026.

Overall 6.8 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is the Bayshore Drive condo a good investment?

At the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf, the base case makes about +24% by 2033 after stamp duty and selling costs (4.4% a year). Bought at about $2,970 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +22%.

How does it compare with Vela Bay?

Vela Bay, next door, launched at $2,886 psf in April 2026 and was 77% sold by November. This site paid about 5% less for its land and adds its own MRT station, mall and bus interchange, but it is more than twice Vela Bay's size.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,970 psf, about $2.85M for 958 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $3,120 psf ($2.99M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
