Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS)

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · Frasers Property-led consortium with Sunway MCL, Sekisui House & Lum Chang

Upcoming project, not launched yet. Expected launch: Not announced. Land cost: $2.128B (about $1,323 psf ppr). 3 bids at the land tender.

  • Land cost is 5% below Vela Bay's, so it can launch at or below Vela Bay's $2,886
  • About 1,700 more private homes planned in Bayshore
  • The listing site used here is part of this project's sales team

Distance, nearby plots and fair entry

  • New Upper Changi Road (Sep 2026): about 2.0 km. About 1,010 homes planned there (ERA). Straight-line distance. Neither plot has a street address yet, so treat it as rough.
  • Earlier next-door site: Vela Bay (Mar 2025): about 400 m.
  • Bedok Rise: Residential, Awarded, about 1.4 km, 380 homes. Won by Allgreen at about $1,330 psf ppr. About 380 homes are planned (ERA, September 2026).
  • Fair entry. Outside Central Region (District 16), but the site will have an MRT station and shops built in. Expect pricing above a typical suburban launch and closer to the city fringe. The developer has not released prices. This range is our estimate from the land price, not a quote. New launches measure units without the air-con ledge (rules from June 2023), so their psf reads higher than an older condo's for the same home.

Sources: Property Insider
