Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS)

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · Frasers Property-led consortium with Sunway MCL, Sekisui House & Lum Chang

Upcoming project, not launched yet. Expected launch: Not announced. Land cost: $2.128B (about $1,323 psf ppr). 3 bids at the land tender.

  • Land cost is 5% below Vela Bay's, so it can launch at or below Vela Bay's $2,886
  • About 1,700 more private homes planned in Bayshore
  • The listing site used here is part of this project's sales team

Distance, nearby plots and fair entry

  • New Upper Changi Road (Sep 2026): about 2.0 km. Straight line in this site's nearby file, both ways. Another compilation put New Upper Changi Road at 2.4 km, so the distance is not settled. About 1,010 homes: this site's project list and an ERA tender note.
  • Earlier next-door site: Vela Bay (Mar 2025): about 0.4 km. Straight line in this site's nearby file, both ways. The land note already calls Vela Bay the next-door site.
  • Bedok Rise: Residential, Awarded, about 1.4 km, 380 homes. Allgreen, about $1,330 psf ppr and about 380 homes (this site's project file and an ERA note, September 2026). This site's file dates the tender to November 2025; the ERA note says December 2025. Straight-line distance about 1.4 km in this site's nearby file. Another compilation said 3.1 km.
  • The Bayshore: 99 years from 31 May 1993, about 720 m. 3-bedroom size Not screened. Yearly psf not compiled from caveats yet.
  • Costa Del Sol: 99 years from 5 May 1997, about 660 m. 3-bedroom size Not screened. Yearly psf not compiled from caveats yet.
  • These are 99-year resale condos already inside 1 km on this site's nearby list. Distances are straight lines from that file. The Bayshore lease start is 31 May 1993 in this site's nearby file, an EdgeProp note and a project listing. Costa Del Sol is 5 May 1997 in this site's nearby file and an EdgeProp project note. A buyer guide put Costa Del Sol's lease in 2000; the 1997 date is kept. Unit sizes were not screened, so this is not yet a like-for-like 3-bedroom set. Yearly 3-bedroom psf, and the change over the past three years, are not compiled from caveats yet.
  • Fair entry. Outside Central Region, District 16, on a mixed-use site with a station and shops planned inside it. The land-multiple range sits above a plain OCR reading and closer to city-fringe pricing because of that station and the retail. This is a land-multiple reading, not a price from the developer. The developer has not released prices. The only price line stated is the land-multiple range already shown above.
