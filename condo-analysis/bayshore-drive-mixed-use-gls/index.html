Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS): analysis and verdict

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,850 to $3,000 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $3,000 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $3,000 psf; a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft is about $2.87M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,150 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.02M). The tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2033 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Eco ($1,706 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (7)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,150 psf.
  • Watch: The Bedok Rise (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $2,300 to $2,700 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Eco, Grandeur Park Residences, Vela Bay) work out to about $3,000 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches) and 10% for this project being integrated.
  • Latest transactions nearby: Eco (1,071 m away, 748 homes, completed 2017): its 3-bed-size resales (about 810 to 1,100 sq ft) averaged $1,706 psf over the last 6 months (7 resales), against a median $1,571 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,925 psf is $1,219 psf (+71%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.80M.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the tested $2,925 psf, the base case makes about +25% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2033 (4.6% a year); bought at $3,000 psf it makes about +22%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.80M; about $3.74M by 2033 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders no; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.

Buy at or below: $3,000 psf. Walk away above: $3,150 psf. Potential gain: +25% (Base case after costs by 2033, bought at $2,925 psf (4.6% a year). Bear +10%, bull +28%).

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Costa Del Sol (0.6 km): Eco 4.3%, Costa Del Sol 6.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.9% a year): Base plus 0.5 point as the Bayshore precinct fills in, with the mall around end-2030 and SAFRA next door in 2030 (Reported); Bedok South MRT opens on 26 Dec 2026 (Confirmed), before you buy, so it is already in the price.

Questions

Is the Bayshore Drive condo a good investment?

At the tested $2,850 to $3,000 psf, the base case makes about +25% by 2033 after stamp duty and selling costs (4.6% a year). Bought at about $3,000 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +22%.

How does it compare with Vela Bay?

Vela Bay, next door, launched at $2,886 psf in April 2026 and was 77% sold by November. This site paid about 5% less for its land and adds its own MRT station, mall and bus interchange, but it is more than twice Vela Bay's size.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $3,000 psf, about $2.87M for 958 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $3,150 psf ($3.02M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
