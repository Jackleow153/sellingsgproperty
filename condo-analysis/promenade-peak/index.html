Promenade Peak: analysis and verdict

Zion Road / River Valley, D03 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. The current average $3,127 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $3,510 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Riviere, River Modern, Martin Modern, Irwell Hill Residences, Oue Twin Peaks) work out to about $3,510 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 25% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the RCR charge over their closest resale, 8 launches).
  • Riviere (238 m away, 455 homes, completed 2023) resold at a median $2,872 psf in the last 6 months (6 resales). At $3,127 psf this project is $255 psf (+9%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $3.53M ($2,857 psf).
  • Bought at the current average $3,127 psf, the base case makes about +20% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (3.1% a year); bought at $3,510 psf it makes about +7%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,165 sq ft costs about $4.69M; a buyer then needs about $30,500 a month and $1.39M upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,510 psf. Walk away above: $3,690 psf. Potential gain: +20% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,127 psf (3.1% a year). Bear +13%, bull +20%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 720 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $3,510 psf ($2.53M); walk away above $3,690 psf ($2.66M). At $3,127 psf: about +21% by 2032. Resale about $2.90M needs about $18,900 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,165 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,510 psf ($4.09M); walk away above $3,690 psf ($4.30M). At $3,127 psf: about +20% by 2032. Resale about $4.69M needs about $30,500 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,420 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,510 psf ($4.98M); walk away above $3,690 psf ($5.24M). At $3,127 psf: about +20% by 2032. Resale about $5.71M needs about $37,200 a month.

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +9%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +32% over theirs (29 launches; +25% in the RCR, 8 launches). Below what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 3.2% gross. Competitors' median rent about $8.38 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $9,280 a month.
  • New supply within 2 km: 7 projects. About 1,849 homes launched since 2024 or coming: Union Square Residences, River Modern, Sophia Meadow, River Opus, River Valley Green (Parcel C)….
  • Sales pace: About 30 a month. 453 of 596 homes sold in about 15 months since launch; 143 left, about 5 months at that pace.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Highline Residences (0.9 km): Oue Twin Peaks 0.1%, Highline Residences 4.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (2.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
