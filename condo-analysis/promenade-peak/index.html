Promenade Peak: analysis and verdict

Zion Road / River Valley, D03 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $3,060 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $3,360 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $3,360 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,165 sq ft is about $3.91M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,530 psf (a 3-bed above about $4.11M). The current average $3,060 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (6 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Riviere ($2,925 psf, 3-bed size, last 12 months (5)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,530 psf.
  • Watch: The Orchard Boulevard (2026 GLS site) launch nearby: likely at about $3,610 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Riviere, River Modern, Martin Modern, Irwell Hill Residences, Oue Twin Peaks) work out to about $3,360 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (10% to 32% here): launches selling now in the RCR charge about 5% over a just-completed resale and about 2.5 points more for each year of its age (29 launches against 103 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out). A launch nearby from the last 2 years counts for half: it is the most direct evidence of what new homes here cost today.
  • Latest transactions nearby: Riviere (238 m away, 455 homes, completed 2023): its 3-bed-size resales (about 990 to 1,340 sq ft) averaged $2,925 psf over the last 12 months (5 resales), against a median $2,851 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $3,056 psf is $131 psf (+4%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $3.53M.
  • Also note: The Orchard Boulevard (2026 GLS site) site nearby (1,420 m, land $1,755 psf ppr) is likely to launch at about $3,610 psf: more than this project's price.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $3,056 psf, the base case makes about +18% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (2.8% a year); bought at $3,360 psf it makes about +7%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $3.56M; about $4.49M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders with some savings; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: narrow.
  • Supply and land prices: Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby. Besides this project's own 596 homes, about 1,849 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (River Modern, River Valley Green (Parcel C), River Opus, Union Square Residences); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 1,166 resales a year.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: low. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Low concern)

Besides this project's own 596 homes, about 1,849 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (River Modern, River Valley Green (Parcel C), River Opus, Union Square Residences); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 1,166 resales a year. Our test of 27 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the city and city fringe (under 6 km from the CBD): those with more than 2,367 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.5% a year over the past 4 years, against 0.5% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Supply nearby is not a big concern here.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Low concern)

Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

76% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with many units left, late discounts or deals on slower stacks are possible as the deadline nears, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Orchard Boulevard (2026 GLS site), is likely at about $3,610 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

  • Bear (1.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Highline Residences (0.9 km): Oue Twin Peaks 0.1%, Highline Residences 4.5%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
