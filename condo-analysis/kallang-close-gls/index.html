Kallang Close (GLS): analysis and verdict

Kallang Close (Kallang River) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,940 psf. The expected $3,000 to $3,100 psf is 9% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,800 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,800 psf. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Sturdee Residences, Eight Riversuites, Aurea) work out to about $2,800 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 23% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the RCR charge over their closest resale, 9 launches).
  • Sturdee Residences (953 m away, 305 homes, completed 2019) resold at a median $2,198 psf in the last 12 months (7 resales). At $3,050 psf this project is $852 psf (+39%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.33M ($2,250 psf).
  • Bought at the expected $3,050 psf, the base case makes about +12% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (2.4% a year); bought at $2,800 psf it makes about +23%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 950 sq ft costs about $3.47M; a buyer then needs about $22,600 a month and $1.01M upfront, against $590k to $802k that typical Kallang/Whampoa and Geylang upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,800 psf. Walk away above: $2,940 psf. Potential gain: +12% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,050 psf (2.4% a year). Bear −1%, bull +12%).

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.5% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Kerrisdale (1.0 km): Eight Riversuites 4.6%, Kerrisdale 6.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.5% a year): Kallang MRT is already open and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
