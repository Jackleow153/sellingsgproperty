Dairy Farm Walk (2026 GLS site): analysis and verdict

Dairy Farm Walk (Dairy Farm) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,160 psf. The expected $2,150 to $2,300 psf is 8% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,060 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,060 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Dairy Farm Residences, Narra Residences, The Skywoods, Foresque Residences) work out to about $2,060 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $2,225 psf, the base case makes about +13% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (2.4% a year); bought at $2,060 psf it makes about +22%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $2.67M; a buyer then needs about $17,400 a month and $770k upfront, against $420k to $660k that typical Bukit Panjang and Bukit Batok upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,060 psf. Walk away above: $2,160 psf. Potential gain: +13% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,225 psf (2.4% a year). Bear −1%, bull +13%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Walk away above $2,160 psf. Buy at or below $2,060 psf ($1.44M); walk away above $2,160 psf ($1.51M). At $2,225 psf: about +13% by 2032. Resale about $1.87M needs about $12,200 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away above $2,160 psf. Buy at or below $2,060 psf ($2.06M); walk away above $2,160 psf ($2.16M). At $2,225 psf: about +13% by 2032. Resale about $2.67M needs about $17,400 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,300 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,060 psf ($2.68M); walk away above $2,160 psf ($2.81M). At $2,225 psf: about +13% by 2032. Resale about $3.47M needs about $22,600 a month.

Closest competitors

4 of the 4 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Dairy Farm Residences (170 m away, 460 homes, Resale): $1,731 psf, about $2,090 psf as a new launch here.
  • Narra Residences (200 m away, 540 homes, Launched Jan 2026): $2,180 psf, about $2,200 psf as a new launch here.
  • The Skywoods (290 m away, 420 homes, Resale): $1,678 psf, about $2,026 psf as a new launch here; 4.7% a year over 4 years.
  • Foresque Residences (400 m away, 496 homes, Resale): $1,593 psf, about $1,924 psf as a new launch here; 5.9% a year over 4 years.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,060 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,160 psf. The developer's floor is about $1,790 to $1,890 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.6% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (5.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Skywoods 4.7%, Foresque Residences 5.9%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: Hillview MRT on the Downtown Line has been open since 2015, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is in a supply phase: Narra Residences and this site (about 1,020 homes together) complete at about the same time, next to several mid-2010s condos.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

No major transformation is planned near this site. Any growth has to come from the project itself and the wider market.

Scorecard

  • Location: 6/10. Quiet, green and low-density next to the Dairy Farm and Bukit Timah nature parks; CHIJ Our Lady Queen of Peace about 685 m (OneMap).
  • Entry price: 4/10. Expected $2,150 to $2,300 psf against about $2,060 psf from the closest competitors (+4% to +12%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 4/10. Little within a short walk. Hawker centres within a 10-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 5/10. Hillview MRT about 837 m in a straight line (OneMap): about a 12-minute walk.

Overall 4.6 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is the Dairy Farm Walk condo a good investment?

At the expected $2,150 to $2,300 psf, the base case makes about +13% by 2032 after stamp duty and selling costs (2.4% a year). Bought at about $2,060 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +22%.

How does it compare with Narra Residences?

Narra Residences, next door, launched at $2,180 psf in January 2026 on land that cost $1,020 psf ppr, about 6% more than this site. This site can launch near or below Narra's price, which keeps it competitive at the exit.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,060 psf, about $2.06M for 1,000 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,160 psf ($2.16M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
