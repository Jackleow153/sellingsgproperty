Newport Residences: analysis and verdict

80 Anson Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $3,590 psf. The current average $3,721 psf is 9% above what the closest competitors support (about $3,420 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $3,420 psf. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Sky Everton, Spottiswoode Suites, Spottiswoode 18, Spottiswoode Residences) work out to about $3,420 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 32% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 7 launches).
  • Sky Everton (667 m away, 262 homes, completed 2023) resold at a median $2,929 psf in the last 6 months (7 resales). At $3,721 psf this project is $792 psf (+27%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $3.94M ($2,932 psf).
  • Bought at the current average $3,721 psf, the base case makes about −2% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (−0.4% a year); bought at $3,420 psf it makes about +7%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,095 sq ft costs about $4.29M; a buyer then needs about $27,900 a month and $1.27M upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,420 psf. Walk away above: $3,590 psf. Potential gain: −2% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $3,721 psf (−0.4% a year). Bear −8%, bull −2%).

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +43%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +31% over theirs (29 launches; +32% in the CCR, 7 launches). Above what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.4% gross. Competitors' median rent about $7.36 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $6,320 a month.
  • New supply within 2 km: 3 projects. About 448 homes launched since 2024 or coming: Union Square Residences, Tan Boon Liat Building Redevelopment, 51 Anson.
  • Sales pace: About 20 a month. 204 of 246 homes sold in about 10 months since launch; 42 left, about 2 months at that pace.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (3.5% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Aspen Heights (2.4 km), Valley Park (2.8 km): Aspen Heights 4.2%, Valley Park 2.7%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (3.5% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
