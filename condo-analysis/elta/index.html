ELTA: analysis and verdict

Clementi Avenue 1, D05 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. The current average $2,537 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $2,640 psf). Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Clavon, The Clement Canopy, Seahill, The Vision, Hundred Trees) work out to about $2,640 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 36% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Clavon (121 m away, 640 homes, completed 2024) resold at a median $2,171 psf in the last 6 months (19 resales). At $2,537 psf this project is $366 psf (+17%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.68M ($2,282 psf).
  • The newest land bid nearby, Dover Drive (GLS) (1,823 m, $1,556 psf ppr), is 24% above this site's land cost; that launch needs about $2,510 psf just to cover costs, and is expected at $2,800-2,900+ (CBRE, PropNex).
  • Bought at the current average $2,537 psf, the base case makes about +17% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (4.0% a year); bought at $2,640 psf it makes about +12%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 925 sq ft costs about $2.92M; a buyer then needs about $19,000 a month and $845k upfront, against $652k to $1.09M that typical Clementi and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,640 psf. Walk away above: $2,770 psf. Potential gain: +17% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,537 psf (4.0% a year). Bear +7%, bull +22%).

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.3% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.5% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Westcove Condominium (1.0 km): The Clement Canopy 5.2%, Westcove Condominium 3.9%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (5.5% a year): Base plus 1 point a year for the Cross Island Line (Phase 2) at Clementi station, Confirmed for 2032.
