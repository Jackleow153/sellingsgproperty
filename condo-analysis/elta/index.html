ELTA

Clementi Avenue 1, D05 · Sunway MCL (formerly MCL Land) and CSC Land

Overall score 6.4 out of 10. A safe, mature-town choice for big families; small units are gone. Two earlier condos on this road sold out and every recorded Clavon resale was profitable. What is left is 4 and 5-bedroom units, priced below the launch average per sq ft. Good for a large household; too big for most investors.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 437 of 501 (87%) on 5 Nov 2026
  • Price: $2,537 psf at launch
  • Land cost: $1,250 psf of buildable floor area ($633.5M, Nov 2023)
  • Completion: Sources conflict: Q1 2028, or 31 Mar 2029
  • Lease: 99 years
  • MRT: Clementi MRT, described as a walk away; no distance published
  • Food and shops: The Clementi Mall, 321 Clementi and Grantral Mall named; distances not published. No hawker centre named.
  • Primary schools: Pei Tong Primary about 850 m

Scores

  • Location: 6/10. Suburbs (5) + primary school within 1 km (+1). Malls are a walk away, not next door.
  • Entry price: 7/10. Launch average about 29% above District 5's 99-year resale middle price ($1,965 psf); 23% above Clavon next door
  • Exit plan: 8/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; +1 big project; +1 primary school within 1 km. No deduction: no further land plots remain on this road. No MRT point: distance not published.
  • Nearby food: 6/10. Town-centre malls within a walk
  • MRT closeness: 5/10. Distance not published, so scored in the middle

Pros

  • 87% sold
  • Clavon next door: all 172 recorded resales profitable (per the source)
  • Last land plot on Clementi Avenue 1
  • Large units now priced from about $2,230-2,450 psf

Cons

  • Only 1 three-bedder and a few 1-bedroom + study left; the rest are 4 and 5-bedders
  • Launch price 23% above Clavon
  • MRT and mall distances not published
  • Mature town, so growth is likely to be steady, not fast
