Aurea: analysis and verdict

Beach Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,650 psf. The current average $3,133 psf is 24% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,520 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,520 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (City Gate, Concourse Skyline, Duo Residences) work out to about $2,520 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $3,133 psf, the base case makes about −22% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (−6.0% a year); bought at $2,520 psf it makes about −3%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $2.61M; a buyer then needs about $17,000 a month and $752k upfront, against $590k to $802k that typical Kallang/Whampoa and Geylang upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,520 psf. Walk away above: $2,650 psf. Potential gain: −22% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $3,133 psf (−6.0% a year). Bear −23%, bull −22%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 635 sq ft): Walk away above $2,650 psf. Buy at or below $2,520 psf ($1.60M); walk away above $2,650 psf ($1.68M). At $3,133 psf: about −22% by 2030. Resale about $1.66M needs about $10,800 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away above $2,650 psf. Buy at or below $2,520 psf ($2.52M); walk away above $2,650 psf ($2.65M). At $3,133 psf: about −22% by 2030. Resale about $2.61M needs about $17,000 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,620 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,520 psf ($4.08M); walk away above $2,650 psf ($4.29M). At $3,133 psf: about −22% by 2030. Resale about $4.22M needs about $27,500 a month.

Closest competitors

3 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • City Gate (400 m away, 311 homes, Resale): $2,116 psf, about $2,555 psf as a new launch here; 1.0% a year over 4 years.
  • Concourse Skyline (540 m away, 360 homes, Resale): $1,909 psf, about $2,305 psf as a new launch here; 0.3% a year over 4 years.
  • Duo Residences (980 m away, 660 homes, Resale): $2,230 psf, about $2,693 psf as a new launch here; 1.4% a year over 4 years.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,520 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,650 psf. No developer floor can be worked out: the developers already owned the land.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (0.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (0.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Concourse Skyline 0.3%, Duo Residences 1.4%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (0.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
