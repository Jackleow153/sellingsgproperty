Aurea: analysis and verdict

Beach Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. The current average $3,133 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $3,460 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (City Gate, Midtown Bay, Concourse Skyline) work out to about $3,460 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 34% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 7 launches).
  • City Gate (400 m away, 311 homes, completed 2018) resold at a median $2,140 psf in the last 6 months (7 resales). At $3,133 psf this project is $993 psf (+46%) above it.
  • Bought at the current average $3,133 psf, the base case makes about +12% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (2.9% a year); bought at $3,460 psf it makes about +1%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $3.74M; a buyer then needs about $24,300 a month and $1.10M upfront, against $590k to $802k that typical Kallang/Whampoa and Geylang upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,460 psf. Walk away above: $3,630 psf. Potential gain: +12% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $3,133 psf (2.9% a year). Bear +8%, bull +12%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 635 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $3,460 psf ($2.20M); walk away above $3,630 psf ($2.31M). At $3,133 psf: about +12% by 2030. Resale about $2.37M needs about $15,500 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,460 psf ($3.46M); walk away above $3,630 psf ($3.63M). At $3,133 psf: about +12% by 2030. Resale about $3.74M needs about $24,300 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,620 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,460 psf ($5.61M); walk away above $3,630 psf ($5.88M). At $3,133 psf: about +11% by 2030. Resale about $6.05M needs about $39,400 a month.

Closest competitors

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +22%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +32% over theirs (29 launches; +34% in the CCR, 7 launches). Below what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.6% gross. Competitors' median rent about $6.76 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $3,450 a month.
  • New supply within 2 km: 5 projects. About 1,581 homes launched since 2024 or coming: The Collective at One Sophia, Duet@Emily, Tanjong Rhu Road (GLS), Kallang Close (GLS), Marina Square Residences (Raffles Boulevard).
  • Sales pace: About 4 a month. 75 of 188 homes sold in about 20 months since launch; 113 left, about 30 months at that pace.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.0% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (1.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Costa Rhu (0.7 km): Concourse Skyline 0.3%, Costa Rhu 3.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (1.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
