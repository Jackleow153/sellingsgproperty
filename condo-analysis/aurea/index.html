Aurea

Beach Road / Golden Mile, D07 · Far East Organization, Sino Land and Perennial Holdings (GMC Property)

Overall score 6.6 out of 10. A landmark address with plenty of choice left, but slow sales make resale harder. Aurea is the new tower in the redevelopment of the conserved Golden Mile Complex, close to Nicoll Highway MRT and the Kallang waterfront. Unlike most launches here, there is real choice: 113 of 188 homes are left, including 3-bedrooms from $2.667M. Sales have been slow (40% after 20 months), many large units are unsold, and there is no primary school within 1 km, so it suits buyers who want the address for the long term.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 75 of 188 (40%) on 5 Nov 2026, 113 left (PropertyNet)
  • Price: About $3,005 psf average at launch (Mar 2025); units left average about $3,133 psf (PropertyNet, 5 Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: Not stated in the sources checked
  • Completion: Expected around Q2 2029; vacant possession by 31 Mar 2030
  • Lease: 99 years from 18 Nov 2024
  • MRT: Nicoll Highway MRT (Circle Line) about 340 to 430 m, 5 to 7 minutes (sources differ); Lavender MRT about 800 m
  • Food and shops: Shops and eateries in the conserved Golden Mile podium, linked by a bridge; more in Bugis a short ride away
  • Primary schools: No primary school within 1 km; Farrer Park Primary about 1.9 km

Scores

  • Location: 8/10. Prime city (7) + retail linked on site (+1); no primary school within 1 km
  • Entry price: 6/10. Units left average about $3,133 psf, about 47% above the District 7 condo resale median ($2,138 psf, all tenures, URA, 12 months to Sep 2026)
  • Exit plan: 5/10. Start 5; +1 MRT within 500 m; -1 under 30% sold a month or more after launch (about 54 sold in the first year)
  • Nearby food: 7/10. Shops and eateries in the linked Golden Mile podium
  • MRT closeness: 7/10. Nicoll Highway MRT about 340 to 430 m

Pros

  • Wide choice left: 2- to 5-bedroom units and penthouses
  • Part of the conserved Golden Mile Complex redevelopment, with retail linked by a bridge
  • Nicoll Highway MRT about 5 to 7 minutes' walk
  • Units left priced close to the launch average
  • Big-name developers (Far East Organization, Sino Land, Perennial)

Cons

  • Only 40% sold after about 20 months
  • No primary school within 1 km
  • Units left average about $3,133 psf, about 47% above District 7 resale
  • Most 4-bedroom and 5-bedroom units are unsold, so large-unit resale will be competitive
  • Small project (188 homes)

Sources: PropertyNet · Stacked Homes review · 99.co
