Peck Hay Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Peck Hay Road (Newton) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $3,300 psf. The expected $3,400 to $3,900 psf is 16% above what the closest competitors support (about $3,140 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $3,140 psf. Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Scotts Tower, Kopar At Newton, Cairnhill Nine, Rochelle At Newton) work out to about $3,140 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 8 launches).
  • The Scotts Tower (348 m away, 231 homes, completed 2016) resold at a median $1,997 psf in the last 6 months (12 resales). At $3,650 psf this project is $1,653 psf (+83%) above it.
  • Bought at the expected $3,650 psf, the base case makes about −1% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−0.2% a year); bought at $3,140 psf it makes about +16%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $3.87M; a buyer then needs about $25,200 a month and $1.14M upfront, against $638k to $900k that typical Toa Payoh and Bishan upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,140 psf. Walk away above: $3,300 psf. Potential gain: −1% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,650 psf (−0.2% a year). Bear −11%, bull −1%).

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.2% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Amaryllis Ville (0.7 km), Draycott Eight (0.7 km): Amaryllis Ville 5.1%, Draycott Eight 3.4%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.2% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
