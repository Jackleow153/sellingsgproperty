Peck Hay Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Peck Hay Road (Newton) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $3,200 psf. A 3-bed here at about $3,390 to $3,890 psf is 19% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $3,050 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $3,050 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $3,050 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft is about $3.05M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,200 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.20M). The expected $3,390 to $3,890 psf is above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Scotts Tower ($2,090 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (3)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,200 psf.
  • Watch: The Bukit Timah Road (Newton GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,400 to $3,600 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (The Scotts Tower, Kopar At Newton, Cairnhill Nine, Rochelle At Newton) work out to about $3,050 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 24% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 8 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Scotts Tower (348 m away, 231 homes, completed 2016): its 3-bed-size resales (about 850 to 1,150 sq ft) averaged $2,090 psf over the last 6 months (3 resales), against a median $1,997 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $3,644 psf is $1,554 psf (+74%) above that.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $3,644 psf, the base case makes about −4% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−0.7% a year); bought at $3,050 psf it makes about +16%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $3.64M; about $3.76M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders no; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.
