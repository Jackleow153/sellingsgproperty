Peck Hay Road (GLS): analysis and verdict

Peck Hay Road (Newton) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,840 psf. The expected $3,400 to $3,900 psf is 35% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,700 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,700 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Scotts Tower, Kopar At Newton, Cairnhill Nine, Rochelle At Newton) work out to about $2,700 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the expected $3,650 psf, the base case makes about −13% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−2.7% a year); bought at $2,700 psf it makes about +18%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $3.40M; a buyer then needs about $22,100 a month and $993k upfront, against $638k to $900k that typical Toa Payoh and Bishan upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,700 psf. Walk away above: $2,840 psf. Potential gain: −13% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,650 psf (−2.7% a year). Bear −22%, bull −13%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Walk away above $2,840 psf. Buy at or below $2,700 psf ($1.89M); walk away above $2,840 psf ($1.99M). At $3,650 psf: about −12% by 2032. Resale about $2.38M needs about $15,500 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away above $2,840 psf. Buy at or below $2,700 psf ($2.70M); walk away above $2,840 psf ($2.84M). At $3,650 psf: about −13% by 2032. Resale about $3.40M needs about $22,100 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,350 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,700 psf ($3.65M); walk away above $2,840 psf ($3.83M). At $3,650 psf: about −13% by 2032. Resale about $4.59M needs about $29,900 a month.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): No condo nearby has a suitable record (a 3-bed price change over 4 years with at least 10 resales in the past year), so we use URA's islandwide non-landed index: 4.7% a year from Q3 2016 to Q3 2026.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
