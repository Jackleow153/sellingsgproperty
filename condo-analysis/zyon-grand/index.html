Zyon Grand

Zion Road / River Valley, D03 · City Developments (CDL) and Mitsui Fudosan

Overall score 7.8 out of 10. Strong, but the units left are the extremes: tiny or very large. Same strengths as Promenade Peak and 91% sold. What remains is mostly the 474 sq ft 1-bedroom + study and the $5M+ private-lift units; all 18 five-bedders are unsold. Unless one of those fits, Promenade Peak has the better choice of 2-bedders.

  • Price: $3,050 psf at launch; units left average about $3,293 psf
