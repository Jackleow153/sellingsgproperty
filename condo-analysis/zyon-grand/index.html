Zyon Grand: analysis and verdict

Zion Road / River Valley, D03 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. The current average $3,293 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $3,440 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Riviere, River Modern, Martin Modern, Irwell Hill Residences) work out to about $3,440 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $3,293 psf, the base case makes about +35% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2033 (4.4% a year); bought at $3,440 psf it makes about +30%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 820 sq ft costs about $3.89M; a buyer then needs about $25,300 a month and $1.15M upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,440 psf. Walk away above: $3,610 psf. Potential gain: +35% (Base case after costs by 2033, bought at $3,293 psf (4.4% a year). Bear +15%, bull +35%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 720 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $3,440 psf ($2.48M); walk away above $3,610 psf ($2.60M). At $3,293 psf: about +36% by 2033. Resale about $3.42M needs about $22,200 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 820 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,440 psf ($2.82M); walk away above $3,610 psf ($2.96M). At $3,293 psf: about +35% by 2033. Resale about $3.89M needs about $25,300 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,615 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,440 psf ($5.56M); walk away above $3,610 psf ($5.83M). At $3,293 psf: about +34% by 2033. Resale about $7.66M needs about $49,900 a month.

Closest competitors

4 of the 4 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • Riviere (220 m away, 455 homes, Resale): $2,845 psf, about $3,435 psf as a new launch here.
  • River Modern (320 m away, 455 homes, Launched Mar 2026): $3,266 psf, about $3,295 psf as a new launch here.
  • Martin Modern (590 m away, 450 homes, Resale): $2,822 psf, about $3,408 psf as a new launch here.
  • Irwell Hill Residences (720 m away, 540 homes, Resale): $2,997 psf, about $3,619 psf as a new launch here.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $3,440 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $3,610 psf. No developer floor can be worked out: the developers already owned the land.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): No condo nearby has a suitable record (a 3-bed price change over 4 years with at least 10 resales in the past year), so we use URA's islandwide non-landed index: 4.7% a year from Q3 2016 to Q3 2026.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 8/10. City fringe (6) + shops on site (+1) + primary school within 1 km (+1)
  • Entry price: 7/10. Current average $3,293 psf against about $3,440 psf from the closest competitors (−4%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 2-bed, 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 9/10. Shops and food on site, Zion Riverside Food Centre about 340 m and Great World mall nearby
  • MRT proximity: 9/10. Havelock MRT exit about 60 m

Overall 7.4 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
