Coastal Cabana (EC): analysis and verdict

Jalan Loyang Besar · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $1,550 psf. The current average $1,787 psf is 21% above what the closest competitors support (about $1,480 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $1,480 psf.

  • The closest competitors (Sea Horizon, Watercolours, The Esparis) work out to about $1,480 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $1,787 psf, the base case makes about +26% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2034 (3.0% a year); bought at $1,480 psf it makes about +53%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 915 sq ft costs about $2.19M; a buyer then needs about $14,200 a month and $625k upfront, against $485k to $665k that typical Pasir Ris and Tampines upgraders bring.
  • At launch, a household at the $16,000 income ceiling can borrow at most about $1.01M under the 30% mortgage servicing ratio, so a 3-bed at $1,787 psf ($1.64M) needs about $680k in cash and CPF, including stamp duty.

Buy at or below: $1,480 psf. Walk away above: $1,550 psf. Potential gain: +26% (Base case after costs by 2034, bought at $1,787 psf (3.0% a year). Bear −0%, bull +26%).

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $1,480 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $1,550 psf. The developer's floor is about $1,510 to $1,590 psf, so expect the launch above our buy-at price.

Potential gain

No Seller's Stamp Duty: the sale is after the 5-year minimum occupation period. Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (6.2% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Sea Horizon 5.0%, Watercolours 6.7%, The Esparis 6.8%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.2% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
