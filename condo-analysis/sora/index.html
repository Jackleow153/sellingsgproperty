Sora: analysis and verdict

72 Yuan Ching Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. The current average $2,327 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $2,580 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Lucerne Grand, Lakeville, The Lakefront Residences, Caspian, Lake Grande) work out to about $2,580 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 37% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Lakeville (1,205 m away, 696 homes, completed 2017) resold at a median $1,886 psf in the last 6 months (15 resales). At $2,327 psf this project is $441 psf (+23%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.37M ($1,927 psf).
  • Bought at the current average $2,327 psf, the base case makes about +33% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (7.4% a year); bought at $2,580 psf it makes about +20%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,100 sq ft costs about $3.62M; a buyer then needs about $23,600 a month and $1.06M upfront, against $384k to $562k that typical Jurong East and Jurong West upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,580 psf. Walk away above: $2,710 psf. Potential gain: +33% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,327 psf (7.4% a year). Bear +18%, bull +33%).

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (6.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Lakeville 5.8%, The Lakefront Residences 6.6%, Caspian 6.1%, Lake Grande 6.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
