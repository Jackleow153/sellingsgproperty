Sora: analysis and verdict

72 Yuan Ching Road · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,310 psf. The current average $2,327 psf is 6% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,200 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,200 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Lakefront Residences, Lucerne Grand, Lakeville, Caspian) work out to about $2,200 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $2,327 psf, the base case makes about +13% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (3.1% a year); bought at $2,200 psf it makes about +20%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,100 sq ft costs about $3.07M; a buyer then needs about $20,000 a month and $893k upfront, against $384k to $562k that typical Jurong East and Jurong West upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,200 psf. Walk away above: $2,310 psf. Potential gain: +13% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,327 psf (3.1% a year). Bear +0%, bull +13%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Walk away above $2,310 psf. Buy at or below $2,200 psf ($1.54M); walk away above $2,310 psf ($1.62M). At $2,327 psf: about +14% by 2030. Resale about $1.96M needs about $12,700 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,100 sq ft): Walk away above $2,310 psf. Buy at or below $2,200 psf ($2.42M); walk away above $2,310 psf ($2.54M). At $2,327 psf: about +13% by 2030. Resale about $3.08M needs about $20,000 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,530 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,200 psf ($3.37M); walk away above $2,310 psf ($3.53M). At $2,327 psf: about +12% by 2030. Resale about $4.28M needs about $27,800 a month.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,200 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,310 psf. No developer floor can be worked out: the developers already owned the land.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (6.2% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Lakefront Residences 6.6%, Lakeville 5.8%, Caspian 6.1%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.2% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
