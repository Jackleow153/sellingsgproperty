Amberwood at Holland: analysis and verdict

30–52 Holland Link (Holland Plain) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $3,000 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $3,110 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $3,110 psf; a 3-bed of about 925 sq ft is about $2.88M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $3,270 psf (a 3-bed above about $3.02M). The current average $3,000 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2031 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Mayfair Modern ($2,173 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (4)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $3,270 psf.
  • Watch: The Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site) launch nearby: likely at about $3,000 to $3,300 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,996 psf, the base case makes about +20% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (3.8% a year); bought at $3,110 psf it makes about +16%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.77M; about $3.55M by 2031 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders with some savings; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.

Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: high. You are not paying for the developer's land bid: you pay what same-size competitors support. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (High concern)

Besides this project's own 212 homes, about 1,700 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Holland Plain (Sim Lian, 2nd site), Dunearn House, Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site), Nava Grove); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 287 resales a year. Our test of 19 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the middle ring (6 to 11 km from the CBD): those with more than 862 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 1.9% a year over the past 4 years, against 3.6% for those with fewer (URA caveats). That points to a real drag on resale prices around completion: plan to hold longer, and pick the unit types and stacks that stand out.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (Low concern)

This site's land ($1,432 psf ppr) cost +2% more than Dunearn House's ($1,410), which lifts the developer's break-even by about +1%. A 3-bed here at about $2,996 psf is −5% above Dunearn House's $3,140 launch average; private prices have risen about 0% since then (URA index), so in today's money you pay about 5% less than its buyers did, while the developer's costs rose more: the developer absorbs most of the higher land bid. Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

16% sold so far. Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS); with many units left, late discounts or deals on slower stacks are possible as the deadline nears, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, Dunearn Road (Turf City 2nd site), is likely at about $3,000 to $3,300 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.
