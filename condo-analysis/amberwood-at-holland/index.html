Amberwood at Holland: analysis and verdict

30–52 Holland Link (Holland Plain) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy only below $3,030 psf. The current average $3,019 psf is a little above what the closest competitors support (about $2,890 psf), so negotiate or choose a cheaper stack. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (Dunearn House, Fourth Avenue Residences, Mayfair Modern, Mayfair Gardens) work out to about $2,890 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $3,019 psf, the base case makes about +13% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2031 (2.5% a year); bought at $2,890 psf it makes about +18%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 925 sq ft costs about $3.36M; a buyer then needs about $21,900 a month and $982k upfront, against $652k to $1.09M that typical Queenstown and Clementi upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,890 psf. Walk away above: $3,030 psf. Potential gain: +13% (Base case after costs by 2031, bought at $3,019 psf (2.5% a year). Bear +1%, bull +13%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 3-bed (about 925 sq ft): Buy only below $3,030 psf. Buy at or below $2,890 psf ($2.67M); walk away above $3,030 psf ($2.80M). At $3,019 psf: about +13% by 2031. Resale about $3.36M needs about $21,900 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,185 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,890 psf ($3.42M); walk away above $3,030 psf ($3.59M). At $3,019 psf: about +13% by 2031. Resale about $4.31M needs about $28,100 a month.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.7% a year): No condo nearby has a suitable record (a 3-bed price change over 4 years with at least 10 resales in the past year), so we use URA's islandwide non-landed index: 4.7% a year from Q3 2016 to Q3 2026.
  • Bull (4.7% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
