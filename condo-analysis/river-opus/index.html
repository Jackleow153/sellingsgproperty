River Opus: analysis and verdict

491 River Valley Road (Singapore River) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. The tested $3,200 to $3,400 psf is at or below what the closest competitors support (about $3,860 psf). Skip the 3-bed and 4-bed: their resale prices would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (8 Saint Thomas, Rv Residences, Gramercy Park) work out to about $3,860 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the CCR charge over their closest resale, 8 launches).
  • 8 Saint Thomas (1,082 m away, 250 homes, completed 2018) resold at a median $3,002 psf in the last 12 months (6 resales). At $3,300 psf this project is $298 psf (+10%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $3.89M ($2,987 psf).
  • Bought at the tested $3,300 psf, the base case makes about +30% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (5.4% a year); bought at $3,860 psf it makes about +11%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $4.57M; a buyer then needs about $29,800 a month and $1.36M upfront, against $788k to $1.09M that typical Bukit Merah and Queenstown upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $3,860 psf. Walk away above: $4,050 psf. Potential gain: +30% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $3,300 psf (5.4% a year). Bear +19%, bull +30%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Buy. Buy at or below $3,860 psf ($2.70M); walk away above $4,050 psf ($2.83M). At $3,300 psf: about +30% by 2032. Resale about $3.20M needs about $20,800 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,860 psf ($3.86M); walk away above $4,050 psf ($4.05M). At $3,300 psf: about +30% by 2032. Resale about $4.57M needs about $29,800 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,350 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $3,860 psf ($5.21M); walk away above $4,050 psf ($5.47M). At $3,300 psf: about +29% by 2032. Resale about $6.17M needs about $40,200 a month.

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +14%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +31% over theirs (30 launches; +33% in the CCR, 8 launches). Below what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.6% gross. Competitors' median rent about $7.03 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $9,300 a month.
  • New supply within 2 km: 5 projects. About 1,401 homes launched since 2024 or coming: Union Square Residences, River Modern, River Valley Green (Parcel C), Orchard Boulevard (2026 GLS site), Tan Boon Liat Building Redevelopment.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (3.5% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Valley Park (0.2 km), Aspen Heights (1.5 km): Valley Park 2.7%, Aspen Heights 4.2%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (3.5% a year): Great World MRT is already open and no new Confirmed catalyst is due in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
