Duet@Emily

Mount Emily Road (Nos. 2, 4 & 6) · District 09 (CCR) · ZACD Property Pte Ltd (ZACD Group)

Duet@Emily launched in 2026 at a reported average of about $2,500 psf. Sales status: about 16 to 19 of 20 left (mostly unsold) (Sep 2026). Only a few of the 20 freehold homes had sold by mid-Sep 2026: sources record between 1 and 4 sales (Roncasa: 1 of 20 on 14 Sep 2026; PropNex: three caveats in Jul and Aug 2026, about $2,433 to $2,685 psf).

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Overall score 8.0 out of 10. Freehold in the city near two MRT lines, but very few buyers so far. Duet@Emily is a 20-home freehold block on Mount Emily Road, about 130 m from Little India MRT and close to Tekka Centre and Tekka Place. Prices are close to District 9 resale. Few units had sold by Sep 2026, and a project this small has a thin resale market.
