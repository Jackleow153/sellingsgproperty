Bedok Rise (GLS): analysis and verdict

Bedok Rise (Tanah Merah) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,200 psf. The tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf is 29% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,100 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,100 psf.

  • The closest competitors (The Glades, Optima @ Tanah Merah, Grandeur Park Residences, Eco) work out to about $2,100 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the tested $2,700 psf, the base case makes about −12% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−2.5% a year); bought at $2,100 psf it makes about +13%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft costs about $2.42M; a buyer then needs about $15,700 a month and $695k upfront, against $462k to $670k that typical Bedok and Tampines upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,100 psf. Walk away above: $2,200 psf. Potential gain: −12% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,700 psf (−2.5% a year). Bear −20%, bull −12%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 650 sq ft): Walk away above $2,200 psf. Buy at or below $2,100 psf ($1.36M); walk away above $2,200 psf ($1.43M). At $2,700 psf: about −12% by 2032. Resale about $1.64M needs about $10,700 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 958 sq ft): Walk away above $2,200 psf. Buy at or below $2,100 psf ($2.01M); walk away above $2,200 psf ($2.11M). At $2,700 psf: about −12% by 2032. Resale about $2.42M needs about $15,700 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,275 sq ft): Walk away above $2,200 psf. Buy at or below $2,100 psf ($2.68M); walk away above $2,200 psf ($2.81M). At $2,700 psf: about −12% by 2032. Resale about $3.22M needs about $21,000 a month.

Closest competitors

4 of the 4 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,100 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,200 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,230 to $2,360 psf, so expect the launch above our buy-at price.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.9% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (3.8% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Glades 3.2%, Eco 4.3%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (3.8% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: Tanah Merah MRT is long open, and Bayshore's new station opens before you buy, so the bull case equals the base case.

District

This district is mature but entering a supply-heavy phase: about 2,800 new private homes are planned within 2 km, completing around the same years.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

No major transformation is planned within walking distance; the nearby plans add supply as much as amenities. Any growth has to come from the project and the wider market.

Scorecard

  • Location: 7/10. Mature Tanah Merah by the MRT, with East Coast Park, Changi Airport and schools nearby; deep resale market (78 condos within 3 km).
  • Entry price: 3/10. Tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf against about $2,100 psf from the closest competitors (+24% to +33%).
  • Exit plan: 4/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; heavy competing supply at the exit.
  • Nearby food: 6/10. Shops and food around Tanah Merah MRT and Bedok; hawker centres within a 10-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 9/10. Tanah Merah MRT about 130 m away (straight line): about a 2-minute walk.

Overall 5.8 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is the Bedok Rise condo a good investment?

At the tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf, the base case makes about −12% by 2032 after stamp duty and selling costs (−2.5% a year). Bought at about $2,100 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +13%.

How does it compare with Vela Bay?

Vela Bay launched at $2,886 psf in April 2026 next to East Coast Park and Bayshore MRT. Bedok Rise paid a similar land price ($1,330 against $1,388 psf ppr) for a smaller site next to Tanah Merah MRT, without the sea views.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,100 psf, about $2.01M for 958 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,200 psf ($2.11M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
