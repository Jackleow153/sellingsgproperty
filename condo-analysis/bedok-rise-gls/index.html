Bedok Rise (GLS): analysis and verdict

Bedok Rise (Tanah Merah) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,620 psf. The tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf is 8% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,500 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,500 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Glades, Grandeur Park Residences, Optima @ Tanah Merah, Eco, Urban Vista) work out to about $2,500 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 36% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches).
  • The Glades (104 m away, 726 homes, completed 2016) resold at a median $1,774 psf in the last 6 months (16 resales). At $2,700 psf this project is $926 psf (+52%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.83M ($1,803 psf).
  • The newest land bid nearby, New Upper Changi Road (GLS) (1,983 m, $1,537 psf ppr), is 16% above this site's land cost; that launch needs about $2,480 psf just to cover costs.
  • Bought at the tested $2,700 psf, the base case makes about −0% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (-0.0% a year); bought at $2,500 psf it makes about +8%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft costs about $2.75M; a buyer then needs about $17,900 a month and $793k upfront, against $462k to $670k that typical Bedok and Tampines upgraders bring.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.8% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Glades 3.8%, Eco 4.3%, Urban Vista 0.2%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (2.8% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: Tanah Merah MRT is long open, and Bayshore's new station opens before you buy, so the bull case equals the base case.
