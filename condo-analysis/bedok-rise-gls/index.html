Bedok Rise (GLS): analysis and verdict

Bedok Rise (Tanah Merah) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,610 psf. A 3-bed here at about $2,600 to $2,800 psf is 8% above what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,490 psf as a new launch). Buy only at or below about $2,490 psf. 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,490 psf; a 3-bed of about 958 sq ft is about $2.38M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,610 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.50M). The tested $2,600 to $2,800 psf is partly above that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Glades ($1,816 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (7)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,610 psf.
  • Watch: The New Upper Changi Road (GLS) launch nearby: likely at about $3,230 psf (our estimate). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (The Glades, Grandeur Park Residences, Optima @ Tanah Merah, Eco, Urban Vista) work out to about $2,490 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a new-launch premium that rises with each competitor's age (24% to 45% here): launches selling now in the OCR charge about 9% over a just-completed resale and about 2.6 points more for each year of its age (30 launches against 107 of their resale competitors, this one and the 6 most extreme pairs left out).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Glades (104 m away, 726 homes, completed 2016): its 3-bed-size resales (about 810 to 1,100 sq ft) averaged $1,816 psf over the last 6 months (7 resales), against a median $1,739 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,700 psf is $884 psf (+49%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.83M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, New Upper Changi Road (GLS) (1,983 m, $1,537 psf ppr), is 16% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,540 psf, and is likely to launch at about $3,230 psf (break-even plus the 27% that launches since 2023 priced above theirs).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the tested $2,700 psf, the base case makes about −1% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (−0.1% a year); bought at $2,490 psf it makes about +8%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.59M; about $2.73M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders with some savings; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.
  • Supply and land prices: Supply risk here: moderate. The price is above what same-size competitors support, whatever the land cost. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby. Supply and land prices: should you worry?

Supply risk here: moderate. The price is above what same-size competitors support, whatever the land cost. No cheaper new launch is coming nearby.

Will too many new condos nearby hurt my price? (Moderate concern)

Besides this project's own 380 homes, about 3,490 new homes within 2 km launch within about 2 years of it (Bagnall Haus, Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS), Vela Bay, Rivelle Tampines (EC)); the condo resale market within 2 km handles about 486 resales a year. Our test of 33 condos completed 2016 to 2022 in the outer suburbs (11 km or more from the CBD): those with more than 1,817 other new homes completing within 2 km and 2 years grew 4.0% a year over the past 4 years, against 4.3% for those with fewer (URA caveats). Expect some competition when units complete together, mainly on rents and resale speed, but no collapse: the local buyer pool is deep.

The developer paid more for the land. Am I paying more? (High concern)

This site's land ($1,330 psf ppr) cost −4% more than Vela Bay's ($1,388), which lifts the developer's break-even by about −3%. A 3-bed here at about $2,700 psf is −6% above Vela Bay's $2,886 launch average; private prices have risen about +1% since then (URA index), so in today's money you pay about 7% less than its buyers did, while the developer's costs rose more: the developer absorbs most of the higher land bid. Across 16 launches since 2023, launch prices rose roughly dollar for dollar with break-even (about $103 per $100), so developers kept a similar dollar margin while their percentage margin shrank (22% above break-even on land of $1,200 psf ppr or more, 31% on cheaper land). Part of that is location: dearer land is usually where homes sell for more anyway. So a high bid does show up in prices, but only as far as buyers will pay against nearby homes. The test that protects you is the price against same-size competitors: our buy-at and walk-away prices.

Will the developer cut prices later? (Moderate concern)

Developers must sell every unit within 5 years of buying the land or repay most of a 40% stamp duty (IRAS). That pushes them to price to sell at launch; slow sellers sometimes offer deals near the deadline, but the best stacks rarely get cheaper.

Should I wait for the next launch nearby? (Low concern)

The next launch nearby, New Upper Changi Road (GLS), is likely at about $3,230 psf, above this project's 3-bed. Waiting for it is unlikely to get you a cheaper new home here, and a dearer neighbour helps support this project's resale price.

What about supply across Singapore? (Moderate concern)

Islandwide, about 18,296 private homes complete in 2027 and 2028 and 15,810 homes in the pipeline were still unsold at mid-2026 (URA). That is enough to keep price growth moderate, but local supply and the local buyer pool matter more for your resale.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (1.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (2.8% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Glades 3.8%, Eco 4.3%, Urban Vista 0.2%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (2.8% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site: Tanah Merah MRT is long open, and Bayshore's new station opens before you buy, so the bull case equals the base case.
