Lucerne Grand: analysis and verdict

Lakeside Drive (Jurong Lake District) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,480 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,720 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this mixed own-stay and rental area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,720 psf; a 3-bed of about 910 sq ft is about $2.48M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an mixed own-stay and rental area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,860 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.60M). The current average $2,480 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with Lake Grande ($1,928 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (14)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,860 psf.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (Lake Grande, Lakeville, The Lakefront Residences, Caspian) work out to about $2,720 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 34% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches) and 10% for this project being integrated.
  • Latest transactions nearby: Lake Grande (497 m away, 710 homes, completed 2019): its 3-bed-size resales (about 770 to 1,050 sq ft) averaged $1,928 psf over the last 6 months (14 resales), against a median $1,925 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,476 psf is $548 psf (+28%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.33M.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,476 psf, the base case makes about +32% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (7.2% a year); bought at $2,720 psf it makes about +20%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.25M; about $3.16M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders no; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing no; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: narrow.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.1% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (6.3% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): Lake Grande 6.6%, Lakeville 5.8%, The Lakefront Residences 6.6%, Caspian 6.1%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (6.3% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
