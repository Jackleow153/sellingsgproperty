Lentor Gardens Residences: analysis and verdict

Lentor Gardens (Lentor Hills) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,320 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,580 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,580 psf; a 3-bed of about 870 sq ft is about $2.25M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,710 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.36M). The current average $2,320 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2030 (4 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Panorama ($1,998 psf, 3-bed size, last 12 months (6)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,710 psf.
  • Watch: The Lentor Central (GLS, 4th site) launch nearby: likely at about $2,600 to $2,700 psf (analysts). It sets the next price benchmark for this area.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (The Panorama, Grandeur 8, Springleaf Residence, Thomson Grand, Centro Residences) work out to about $2,580 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 34% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Panorama (1,065 m away, 698 homes, completed 2017): its 3-bed-size resales (about 740 to 1,000 sq ft) averaged $1,998 psf over the last 12 months (6 resales), against a median $2,094 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,317 psf is $319 psf (+16%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.25M.
  • Land bids nearby: The newest land bid nearby, Lentor Central (GLS, 4th site) (272 m, $1,278 psf ppr), is 39% above this site's land cost. It breaks even at about $2,190 psf, and analysts expect it to launch at From about $2,600, average above $2,700 (Knight Frank).
  • Potential gain: Bought at the current average $2,317 psf, the base case makes about +27% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (6.1% a year); bought at $2,580 psf it makes about +14%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.02M; about $2.72M by 2030 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders yes; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: wide.

Buy at or below: $2,580 psf. Walk away above: $2,710 psf. Potential gain: +27% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,317 psf (6.1% a year). Bear +16%, bull +27%).

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.9% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Panorama 5.5%, Grandeur 8 4.0%, Thomson Grand 4.6%, Centro Residences 5.4%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.9% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
