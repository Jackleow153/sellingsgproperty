Lentor Gardens Residences: analysis and verdict

Lentor Gardens (Lentor Hills) · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,310 psf. The current average $2,350 psf is 7% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,200 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,200 psf.

  • The closest competitors (The Panorama, Grandeur 8, Springleaf Residence, Thomson Grand) work out to about $2,200 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium.
  • Bought at the current average $2,350 psf, the base case makes about +6% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (1.5% a year); bought at $2,200 psf it makes about +14%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 870 sq ft costs about $2.31M; a buyer then needs about $15,000 a month and $661k upfront, against $400k to $832k that typical Ang Mo Kio and Yishun upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,200 psf. Walk away above: $2,310 psf. Potential gain: +6% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,350 psf (1.5% a year). Bear −3%, bull +6%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 645 sq ft): Walk away above $2,310 psf. Buy at or below $2,200 psf ($1.42M); walk away above $2,310 psf ($1.49M). At $2,350 psf: about +7% by 2030. Resale about $1.71M needs about $11,100 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 870 sq ft): Walk away above $2,310 psf. Buy at or below $2,200 psf ($1.91M); walk away above $2,310 psf ($2.01M). At $2,350 psf: about +6% by 2030. Resale about $2.31M needs about $15,000 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,185 sq ft): Walk away above $2,310 psf. Buy at or below $2,200 psf ($2.61M); walk away above $2,310 psf ($2.74M). At $2,350 psf: about +6% by 2030. Resale about $3.14M needs about $20,400 a month.

Closest competitors

Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,200 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,310 psf. The developer's floor is about $1,740 to $1,840 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.4% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.8% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Panorama 5.5%, Grandeur 8 4.2%, Thomson Grand 4.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.8% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 5/10. Suburbs (5); mall is nearby but not next door, park still being built, no primary school within 1 km
  • Entry price: 4/10. Current average $2,350 psf against about $2,200 psf from the closest competitors (+7%).
  • Exit plan: 5/10. The 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 6/10. A mall within about 500 m; little else within walking distance
  • MRT proximity: 7/10. Lentor MRT exit about 410 m

Overall 5.4 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.
