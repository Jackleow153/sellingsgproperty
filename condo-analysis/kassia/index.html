Kassia

Flora Drive (Loyang / Pasir Ris), D17 · Hong Leong Holdings, City Developments and TID (Tripartite Developers)

Overall score 5.0 out of 10. Affordable freehold in the east, if you drive. Kassia is a freehold launch at about $2,047 psf for the units left, with 3-bedrooms with yards from $2.145M and a primary school about 550 m away. It is 86% sold. The trade-off is daily convenience: no MRT within walking distance until the Cross Island Line opens around 2030, and little food nearby. It suits families with a car who value freehold and space.

This file includes an HDB upgrader affordability calculator, a break-even resale price calculator and a comparison with other launches in the same region.

Key facts

  • Sales so far: 236 of 276 (86%), 40 left (PropertyNet, 24 Oct and 5 Nov 2026); 144 (52%) on the launch weekend
  • Price: Launch prices $1,821 to $2,177 psf (Jul 2024); units left average about $2,047 psf (PropertyNet, 5 Nov 2026)
  • Land cost: Not stated in the sources checked
  • Completion: Expected around 2H 2027; vacant possession by 1 Nov 2029
  • Lease: Freehold
  • MRT: No MRT within a comfortable walk today; Loyang station on the Cross Island Line is expected around 2030 (distance not given)
  • Food and shops: Little within walking distance; Pasir Ris and Changi malls are a drive away
  • Primary schools: East Spring Primary about 550 m; White Sands Primary close to the 1 km line (straight line)

Scores

  • Location: 6/10. Suburbs (5) + primary school within 1 km (+1)
  • Entry price: 6/10. Units left average about $2,047 psf, about 59% above the District 17 median for 99-year resale condos ($1,287 psf, URA, 12 months to Sep 2026). Kassia is freehold, which accounts for part of the gap
  • Exit plan: 6/10. Start 5; +1 over 60% sold; +1 primary school within 1 km; -1 nearby supply (Loyang Valley redevelopment)
  • Nearby food: 4/10. Little within walking distance today
  • MRT closeness: 3/10. No comfortable walk to an MRT today; Loyang CRL station expected around 2030

Pros

  • Freehold at about $2,047 psf for the units left
  • 86% sold
  • East Spring Primary about 550 m
  • 3-bedroom with yard from $2.145M
  • Changi Airport and Jewel about 10 minutes' drive

Cons

  • No MRT within walking distance until about 2030
  • Little food and few shops within walking distance
  • About 59% above District 17 99-year resale
  • Loyang Valley redevelopment nearby will add supply
  • Keys as late as Nov 2029
