Kassia: analysis and verdict

Flora Drive · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Medium

Verdict: Walk away above $2,030 psf. The current average $2,047 psf is 6% above what the closest competitors support (about $1,930 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $1,930 psf.

  • The closest competitors (Parc Komo, The Gale, Ferraria Park Condominium, Edelweiss Park Condominium, Dahlia Park Condominium) work out to about $1,930 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 35% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 12 launches).
  • Parc Komo (662 m away, 276 homes, completed 2023) resold at a median $1,716 psf in the last 12 months (7 resales). At $2,047 psf this project is $331 psf (+19%) above it; a 3-bed there sold for a median $1.60M ($1,598 psf).
  • Bought at the current average $2,047 psf, the base case makes about +6% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2030 (1.5% a year); bought at $1,930 psf it makes about +13%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,055 sq ft costs about $2.44M; a buyer then needs about $15,900 a month and $701k upfront, against $485k to $665k that typical Pasir Ris and Tampines upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $1,930 psf. Walk away above: $2,030 psf. Potential gain: +6% (Base case after costs by 2030, bought at $2,047 psf (1.5% a year). Bear −3%, bull +6%).

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +43%. Against its resale competitors' latest prices (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +31% over theirs (29 launches; +35% in the OCR, 12 launches). Above what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.1% gross. Competitors' median rent about $3.59 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $4,130 a month.

  • Sales pace: About 8 a month. 237 of 276 homes sold in about 28 months since launch; 39 left, about 5 months at that pace.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (2.3% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (4.6% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year): The Gale 5.1%, Ferraria Park Condominium 3.0%, Edelweiss Park Condominium 5.7%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (4.6% a year): No new Confirmed catalyst near the site in the holding period, so the bull case equals the base case.
