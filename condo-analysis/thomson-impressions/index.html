Floor plans

  • 1-bedroom, Type A1 (463 sq ft): layout score 4/10. Pros: Simple, squarish 1-bedroom at 463 sq ft; The bedroom opens off the living room, away from the door. Cons: The galley kitchen doubles as the entrance passage; The AC ledge is almost as large as the balcony.
  • 2-bedroom, Type B2 (764 sq ft): layout score 6/10. Pros: Squarish main area with the living room, dining and kitchen together; Store for the distribution board and household items. Cons: A long entrance passage before you reach the kitchen and living room; A wide AC ledge along the bathrooms; Bedrooms side by side, not a dumbbell.
  • 3-bedroom, Type C1 (1,055 sq ft): layout score 5/10. Pros: A study nook as well as three bedrooms; Separate galley kitchen, set away from the living room; Wide living and dining room with a full-width balcony. Cons: Two AC ledges and a large balcony take a big share of the area; Bedrooms are grouped on one side, not a dumbbell; No yard or utility room.
