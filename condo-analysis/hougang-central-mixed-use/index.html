Hougang Central (mixed-use): analysis and verdict

Hougang Central / Hougang Avenue 10 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Buy. A 3-bed here at about $2,500 to $2,600 psf is at or below what the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales support (about $2,780 psf as a new launch). 3-bed and 4-bed are the most wanted sizes in this own-stay family area.

Jack's recommendation

  • Target: 2-bed or 3-bed or 4-bed at or below $2,780 psf; a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft is about $2.78M.
  • Best size here: 3-bed and 4-bed: this is an own-stay family area, and family sizes sell fastest (Thomson Reserve's 3- and 4-bedders sold out at launch; its 2-bedders were the slowest).
  • Walk away: Above $2,920 psf (a 3-bed above about $2.92M). The expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf is within that.
  • Hold: Plan to keep it until about 2032 (5 years): Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 4 years.
  • Before you book: Compare your unit's psf with The Florence Residences ($2,025 psf, 3-bed size, last 6 months (17)) and ask for the price of each stack; a higher floor or better facing should not push you past $2,920 psf.

Key points

  • What the closest competitors support: The closest competitors (The Florence Residences, Riverfront Residences, Parc Vera) work out to about $2,780 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 33% new-launch premium (what launches selling now in the OCR charge over their closest resale, 13 launches) and 10% for this project being integrated.
  • Latest transactions nearby: The Florence Residences (779 m away, 1,410 homes, completed 2023): its 3-bed-size resales (about 850 to 1,150 sq ft) averaged $2,025 psf over the last 6 months (17 resales), against a median $1,904 psf for all sizes. A 3-bed here at about $2,547 psf is $522 psf (+26%) above that; a 3-bed there sold for a median $2.12M.
  • Potential gain: Bought at the expected $2,547 psf, the base case makes about +44% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (7.5% a year); bought at $2,780 psf it makes about +31%.
  • Who can buy it from you later: Who can buy a 3-bed from you (tested at today's $2.55M; about $3.89M by 2032 in the base case): HDB 5-room upgraders with some savings; HDB 4-room upgraders no; Landed owners right-sizing yes; Condo owners nearby moving or upgrading yes. Resale pool: good.

Buy at or below: $2,780 psf. Walk away above: $2,920 psf. Potential gain: +44% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,547 psf (7.5% a year). Bear +22%, bull +50%).

Other checks

  • Premium over closest resale: +22%. A 3-bed here against the competitors' 3-bed-size resales (after the 5% area adjustment). Launches selling now averaged +26% over theirs (30 launches; +33% in the OCR, 13 launches). Below what the market usually pays.
  • Rental yield at this price: 2.3% gross. Competitors' median rent about $4.90 psf a month (URA rental contracts, 26q1, 26q2, 26q3, 26q4); a 3-bed rents for about $4,430 a month.
  • New supply within 2 km: 0 projects. No other project launched since 2024 or coming within 2 km.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.5% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (7.0% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Kovan Regency (1.1 km): Parc Vera 7.5%, Kovan Regency 6.4%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (8.0% a year): Base plus 1 point for the Cross Island Line interchange at Hougang, Confirmed for 2030.

District

This district is maturing: prices have risen steadily and there is little competing supply, but the expected launch price is well above what the nearest condos support.

Future buyers

Master Plan

Scorecard

  • Location: 8/10. Hougang town centre on an MRT interchange (North East Line now, Cross Island Line 2030, Confirmed) with the town's largest mall on site; deep upgrader pool (591 four-room resales a year in Hougang).
  • Entry price: 7/10. A 3-bed here at about $2,500 to $2,600 psf against about $2,780 psf from the closest competitors' 3-bed-size resales (−10% to −6%).
  • Exit plan: 7/10. The 3-bed has a good resale pool in this own-stay family area (2 buyer groups can fund the down payment today); little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. A mall of about 300,000 sq ft on site (Confirmed) and Hougang Mall about 200 m away (OneMap). Hawker centres within a 5-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 10/10. Integrated with Hougang MRT (NE14), Confirmed by HDB's tender and the developers.

Overall 8.0 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is Hougang Central a good investment?

At the expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf, the base case makes about +44% by 2032 after stamp duty and selling costs (7.5% a year). Bought at about $2,780 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +31%.

When will the price list be out?

Not announced. UOL targets a launch in the second half of 2027. This analysis will be updated with the real prices and sizes.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,780 psf, about $2.78M for 1,000 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,920 psf ($2.92M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
