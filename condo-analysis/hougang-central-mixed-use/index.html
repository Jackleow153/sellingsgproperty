Hougang Central (mixed-use): analysis and verdict

Hougang Central / Hougang Avenue 10 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Own-stay only. At any realistic launch price this won't deliver 5% a year, so buy the 2-bed or 3-bed only to live in, and walk away above about $2,320 psf (3-bed about $2.32M). Skip the 4-bed unless you will live in it for 10 years or more.

  • The 5% price is below the developer's cost. To earn 5% a year after costs you would have to buy at about $2,110 psf, but land, building, finance and a normal margin put the developer's realistic floor at about $2,130 to $2,250 psf.
  • Comparable condos grew 2.5% to 4.1% a year from launch to a year after completion (Sengkang Grand, Woodleigh, Watertown, The Florence, Riverfront). A 5% net return here needs about 6.2% a year in price growth, which none of them came close to.
  • At the base-case resale price in 2032, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $2.86M. A buyer then needs about $18,600 a month and $828k upfront, more than a typical Hougang or Serangoon 5-room upgrader brings.

Suits: Families in Hougang, Kovan and Serangoon who want to live above the MRT interchange and the new mall for 10 years or more, and who would buy within the walk-away price. Skip it if: Investors who need 5% a year, buyers who must sell within 5 to 6 years, and anyone stretching to a 4-bed.

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Own-stay only. Buy at or below $2,120 psf ($1.48M); walk away above $2,330 psf ($1.63M). Widest resale pool: base-case exit about $2.00M, needing about $13,000 a month, close to the median household.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Own-stay only. Buy at or below $2,110 psf ($2.11M); walk away above $2,320 psf ($2.32M). Exit about $2.86M needs about $18,600 a month and $828k upfront: exit risk.
  • 4-bed (about 1,345 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,110 psf ($2.84M); walk away above $2,320 psf ($3.12M). Exit about $3.85M needs about $25,100 a month and $1.13M upfront, beyond most local upgraders.

Buy-at and walk-away prices

Buy-at price = the lower of fair value ($2,410) and the 5% ceiling ($2,110) = about $2,110 psf. That is below the developer's floor of about $2,130, so at any realistic launch price this won't deliver 5% a year: buy it only to live in. Walk-away price = the entry at which the base case falls to about 3% a year, roughly what a home loan costs: about $2,320 psf for a 3-bed and $2,330 for a 2-bed.

The 5% test

Because of stamp duty and selling costs, a 5% net return needs roughly 6.1% to 6.3% a year in price growth. Fail. At the expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf, the base case earns about 0.7% to 1.5% a year after costs. It would pass only at about $2,110 psf, below the developer's floor.

Competitor performance

Comparable projects made 2.5% to 4.1% a year from launch to a year after completion; the integrated ones (2.5% to 3.1%) were at the low end, because they launched at a premium. To earn 5% net, Hougang Central must grow about 6.2% a year, roughly 2 points a year more than the best of them. That is unlikely without a much lower launch price.

District

This district is maturing: prices have risen steadily and there is little competing supply, but the premium for a brand-new integrated condo over 3-year-old neighbours is already very wide.

Future buyers

The 2-bed's resale price stays within reach of a median household. The 3-bed's base-case exit price needs about 1.5 times the median income and more upfront money than a typical 5-room upgrader brings, so expect a thinner buyer pool. The 4-bed's needs about 2 times the median income: exit risk.

Master Plan

Only the Cross Island Line interchange and the integrated hub itself are Confirmed, and they are already in the expected launch price. Nothing else Confirmed within 2 km lifts the base case.

Scorecard

  • Location: 8/10. Hougang town centre on an MRT interchange (North East Line now, Cross Island Line 2030, Confirmed) with the town's largest mall on site; deep upgrader pool (591 four-room resales a year in Hougang).
  • Entry price: 4/10. Expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf is 4% to 8% above our fair value of about $2,410.
  • Exit plan: 5/10. Little competing supply within 2 km, but the 3-bed and 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. A mall of about 300,000 sq ft on site (Confirmed) and Hougang Mall about 200 m away (OneMap). Hawker centres within a 5-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 10/10. Integrated with Hougang MRT (NE14), Confirmed by HDB's tender and the developers.

The scorecard (7.0) says this is a good place to live; the price test says it is a poor investment at the expected price. For investment buyers, the price test wins.

Questions

Is Hougang Central a good investment?

Not at the expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf. After stamp duty and selling costs, the base case earns about 0.7% to 1.5% a year by 2032. It would earn 5% only at about $2,110 psf, below what the developer can realistically sell at.

What price should I pay for a 3-bedroom?

Our walk-away price is about $2,320 psf, about $2.32M for 1,000 sq ft. Above that, the base case falls below about 3% a year. These are estimates until the developer releases prices and sizes.

Who should buy Hougang Central?

Families who want to live above the MRT interchange and the new mall for 10 years or more. It does not suit buyers who need 5% a year or must sell within 5 to 6 years.

When will the price list be out?

Not announced. UOL targets a launch in the second half of 2027. This analysis will be updated with the real prices and sizes.
