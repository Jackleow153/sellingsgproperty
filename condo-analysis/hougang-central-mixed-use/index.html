Hougang Central (mixed-use): analysis and verdict

Hougang Central / Hougang Avenue 10 · checked 25 Nov 2026 · confidence Low

Verdict: Walk away above $2,440 psf. The expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf is 10% above what the closest competitors support (about $2,320 psf for a new launch here). Buy only at or below about $2,320 psf. Skip the 4-bed: its resale price would outrun local buyers.

  • The closest competitors (The Florence Residences, Parc Vera, Riverfront Residences) work out to about $2,320 psf as a brand-new launch, after adding 5% where they were not built under the harmonised floor-area rules, a 15% new-launch premium and 10% for this project being integrated.
  • Bought at the expected $2,550 psf, the base case makes about +23% after stamp duty and selling costs by 2032 (4.1% a year); bought at $2,320 psf it makes about +35%.
  • At the base-case resale price, a 3-bed of about 1,000 sq ft costs about $3.32M; a buyer then needs about $21,600 a month and $970k upfront, against $469k to $688k that typical Hougang and Serangoon upgraders bring.

Buy at or below: $2,320 psf. Walk away above: $2,440 psf. Potential gain: +23% (Base case after costs by 2032, bought at $2,550 psf (4.1% a year). Bear +3%, bull +28%).

Verdict by unit type

  • 2-bed (about 700 sq ft): Walk away above $2,440 psf. Buy at or below $2,320 psf ($1.62M); walk away above $2,440 psf ($1.71M). At $2,550 psf: about +23% by 2032. Resale about $2.33M needs about $15,100 a month.
  • 3-bed (about 1,000 sq ft): Walk away above $2,440 psf. Buy at or below $2,320 psf ($2.32M); walk away above $2,440 psf ($2.44M). At $2,550 psf: about +23% by 2032. Resale about $3.32M needs about $21,600 a month.
  • 4-bed (about 1,345 sq ft): Walk away. Buy at or below $2,320 psf ($3.12M); walk away above $2,440 psf ($3.28M). At $2,550 psf: about +22% by 2032. Resale about $4.47M needs about $29,100 a month.

Closest competitors

3 of the 3 competitors are within 1 km. Chosen closest first, with the same tenure and a similar number of homes, completed 2000 or later where possible; executive condos are left out. Resale prices are URA medians for the past 12 months. Condos launched before June 2023 measured their areas under the old rules (including air-con ledges), so their psf is raised 5% to compare like for like.

  • The Florence Residences (780 m away, 1410 homes, Resale): $1,904 psf, about $2,529 psf as a new launch here; 2.0% a year over 4 years.
  • Parc Vera (810 m away, 452 homes, Resale): $1,587 psf, about $2,108 psf as a new launch here; 8.3% a year over 4 years.
  • Riverfront Residences (920 m away, 1472 homes, Resale): $1,747 psf, about $2,320 psf as a new launch here; 3.6% a year over 4 years.

Buy at or below = the average of the competitors' new-launch equivalents = about $2,320 psf. Walk away above = 5% more = about $2,440 psf. The developer's floor is about $2,130 to $2,250 psf.

Potential gain

Potential gain is after Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates, about $6,000 legal fees and a 2% agent fee plus 9% GST when you sell. Loan interest, property tax, maintenance and rent are not included.

  • Bear (3.7% a year): Half the base case: competing supply at the exit, or a softer market.
  • Base (7.4% a year): The average yearly 3-bed price change over the past 4 years (URA caveats) of the competitors with a suitable record (at least 10 resales in the past year), widened to the nearest suitable condos: Kovan Regency (1.1 km): Parc Vera 8.3%, Kovan Regency 6.6%. That period included the 2022–2023 rise.
  • Bull (8.4% a year): Base plus 1 point for the Cross Island Line interchange at Hougang, Confirmed for 2030.

District

This district is maturing: prices have risen steadily and there is little competing supply, but the expected launch price is well above what the nearest condos support.

Future buyers

The 3-bed and 4-bed resale prices outrun most local upgraders: exit risk.

Master Plan

Only the Cross Island Line interchange and the integrated hub itself are Confirmed. The CRL adds 1 point a year to the bull case only.

Scorecard

  • Location: 8/10. Hougang town centre on an MRT interchange (North East Line now, Cross Island Line 2030, Confirmed) with the town's largest mall on site; deep upgrader pool (591 four-room resales a year in Hougang).
  • Entry price: 4/10. Expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf against about $2,320 psf from the closest competitors (+8% to +12%).
  • Exit plan: 5/10. The 3-bed, 4-bed exit prices outrun most local upgraders; little competing private supply nearby.
  • Nearby food: 8/10. A mall of about 300,000 sq ft on site (Confirmed) and Hougang Mall about 200 m away (OneMap). Hawker centres within a 5-minute walk: Not checked yet.
  • MRT proximity: 10/10. Integrated with Hougang MRT (NE14), Confirmed by HDB's tender and the developers.

Overall 7.0 out of 10. The buy-at and walk-away prices decide the verdict; the scorecard shows how good the place is to live in.

Questions

Is Hougang Central a good investment?

At the expected $2,500 to $2,600 psf, the base case makes about +23% by 2032 after stamp duty and selling costs (4.1% a year). Bought at about $2,320 psf, in line with its closest competitors, it makes about +35%.

When will the price list be out?

Not announced. UOL targets a launch in the second half of 2027. This analysis will be updated with the real prices and sizes.

What price should I pay for a 3-bed?

At or below about $2,320 psf, about $2.32M for 1,000 sq ft, in line with its closest competitors. Walk away above about $2,440 psf ($2.44M). These are estimates until the developer releases prices.
