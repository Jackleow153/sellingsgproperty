Floor plans

  • 2-bedroom, Type BP2 (743 sq ft): layout score 6/10. Pros: Both bedrooms fit a queen bed with a wardrobe; The kitchen sits by the door, away from the living room; RC ledges are outside the strata area, so you do not pay for them. Cons: Bedrooms side by side rather than a dumbbell; No kitchen window; Long, narrow living and dining strip.
  • 3-bedroom, Type C1 (958 sq ft): layout score 3/10. Pros: Compact 3-bedroom at 958 sq ft, so a lower total price; All bedrooms are clustered together, which suits a young family. Cons: A long, angled entrance passage uses up floor area; A large AC ledge, about 6% of the area you pay for (estimate from the plan); Open kitchen without a window.
  • 4-bedroom, Type D1 (1,292 sq ft): layout score 7/10. Pros: Private lift that opens into the unit; Closed kitchen with yard and WC, plus a household shelter for storage; Four proper bedrooms in 1,292 sq ft. Cons: Long corridor past three bedrooms; Two large AC ledges, a sizeable share of the area you pay for.
  • 5-bedroom, Type E1 (1,475 sq ft): layout score 8/10. Pros: Five bedrooms in 1,475 sq ft, rare for a new condo; Dry and wet kitchens, a household shelter and a private lift; Junior master bedroom with its own bathroom. Cons: Bedrooms are small for the count; bedrooms 3 to 5 fit a single or small double bed; Long corridor along all the bedrooms.

Overall score 8.1 out of 10. The newest condo in the group, priced at a premium. AMO Residence is newly completed and resells at about 20% above its neighbours per sq ft. Mayflower MRT is about 440 m away and three primary schools are within 1 km.
