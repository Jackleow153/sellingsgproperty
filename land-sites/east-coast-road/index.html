Frequently asked questions

What is the East Coast Road land site?

When will the East Coast Road site be sold?

Tender closes 3 Dec 2026. The site is awarded after URA evaluate the bids, usually within a few weeks of the tender closing.

How many homes can the East Coast Road site have?

About 85 homes, based on the official estimate; the developer decides the final number.

When could homes at East Coast Road be launched for sale?

Developers usually launch 12 to 18 months after winning a site, so a sale launch is unlikely before late 2027 or 2028. No date can be confirmed until the site is sold.
