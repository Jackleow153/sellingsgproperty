Frequently asked questions

What is the Serangoon North View land site?

Serangoon North View is a 0.8 ha government land site at Serangoon North View in District 19 (OCR), on the 2H2026 Reserve List. URA estimates about 235 homes at a plot ratio of 2.5. Status: Open for application since 29 Sep 2026.

When will the Serangoon North View site be sold?

It is on the Reserve List, so it is only put up for tender if a developer offers a minimum price the Government accepts. Status: Open for application since 29 Sep 2026.

How many homes can the Serangoon North View site have?

About 235 homes, based on the official estimate; the developer decides the final number.

When could homes at Serangoon North View be launched for sale?

Developers usually launch 12 to 18 months after winning a site, so a sale launch is unlikely before late 2027 or 2028. No date can be confirmed until the site is sold.
