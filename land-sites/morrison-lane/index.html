Frequently asked questions

What is the Morrison Lane land site?

Morrison Lane is a 0.66 ha government land site at Morrison Lane (off Mohamed Sultan Road) in District 09 (CCR), on the Reserve List (1H2026, carried into 2H2026). URA estimates about 205 homes at a plot ratio of 2.8. Status: Open for application since 25 Feb 2026.

When will the Morrison Lane site be sold?

It is on the Reserve List, so it is only put up for tender if a developer offers a minimum price the Government accepts. Status: Open for application since 25 Feb 2026.

How many homes can the Morrison Lane site have?

About 205 homes, based on the official estimate; the developer decides the final number.

When could homes at Morrison Lane be launched for sale?

Developers usually launch 12 to 18 months after winning a site, so a sale launch is unlikely before late 2027 or 2028. No date can be confirmed until the site is sold.
