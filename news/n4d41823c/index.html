What this means这对你意味着什么

Mingtiandi reported that Bukit Sembawang plans a condo in central Singapore after buying a site for $153 million. That figure is what was paid for the land. It is not the price of a finished unit, and the homes are not for sale yet.Mingtiandi报道，武吉三巴旺（Bukit Sembawang）在以1.53亿美元买下新加坡市中心一块地之后，计划建一栋私人公寓。这个数字是地价，不是完工单位的价钱，房子现在也还不能买。

A land bid is a cost for the developer. The launch price later has to cover construction, interest and a margin on top of that land. Buyers cannot book a unit from this headline, and sellers nearby should not mark their resale up by $153 million.土地出价是发展商的成本。日后的开盘价还要盖上建筑、利息和利润。买家不能凭这则标题预订单位。附近的卖家也不该把转售价加上1.53亿美元。

The headline says central Singapore and does not name the street or a price per square foot. If you own or want a home in that part of town, wait for the project name, the unit mix and the actual launch price before you decide this site has changed your number.标题只说市中心，没有路名，也没有每平方英尺的价钱。如果你在那一带有房或想买房，等项目名称、户型和真正的开盘价出来，再判断这块地对你的数字有没有影响。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Is $153 million the price of a condo unit?1.53亿美元是一套公寓的价钱吗？ Read $153 million as the land cost reported for the site. Divide nothing in your head and call it a unit price.把1.53亿美元读成报道里的地价。不要心算之后把它当成单位售价。
  • Can buyers apply for this project now?买家现在可以申请这个项目吗？ There is no showflat in this headline. Keep looking at homes you can actually buy this month.标题里没有示范单位。这个月仍然去看你真正买得到的房子。
  • Should resale sellers near the site raise prices?地段附近的转售卖家该不该加价？ Nearby sellers should wait for a launch price and a project name. A land purchase alone does not tell a buyer what your flat is worth.附近的卖家应等项目名称和开盘价。只买下一块地，回答不了买家你的房子值多少。
