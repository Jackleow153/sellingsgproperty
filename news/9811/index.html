Luxury homes seized in $3b money laundering case prove a hard sell涉30亿元洗钱案被扣押的豪宅难以出售

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • A freehold unit at New Futura on Leonie Hill Road, owned by the wife of Wang Bingang, a suspect in the $3 billion money-laundering case, sold at auction on 28 October for $8.5 million.里恩尼山路New Futura的一个永久地契单位，业主是30亿元洗钱案嫌疑人王炳刚的妻子。它在10月28日的拍卖中以850万新元成交。
  • The price was 13% under the $9.8 million guide for the 2,691-square-foot apartment, and $500,000 above an $8 million sale of a similar-sized unit in the project in July 2025.这个2691平方英尺的单位，成交价比980万新元的指导价低13%，也比2025年7月同项目一套相近面积、以800万新元成交的单位高出50万新元。
  • It drew a single bid. It is the fifth property to sell out of 47 put on auction since 17 September.只有一个出价。自9月17日以来送拍的47个物业里，这是第五个卖出的。
  • Four other apartments, at Wallich Residence and Martin Modern, sold on 23 September for $16.28 million in total.另外四套公寓在Wallich Residence和Martin Modern，于9月23日成交，合计1628万新元。
  • More than half of the 80-plus properties seized by the Commercial Affairs Department have been put up for auction.商业事务局查封的80多个物业里，超过一半已经送去拍卖。
  • Deloitte is liquidating the assets. Only two of the three appointed brokerages have recorded sales.德勤负责清理这些资产。受委的三家经纪行里，只有两家有成交。
  • SRI sold five of its 16 properties. Edmund Tie sold an 8,880-square-foot factory in Kaki Bukit for above $3.2 million after private talks. That factory was the only one of nine it offered on 23 September to draw a bid.SRI卖出16个物业中的5个。Edmund Tie在私下洽谈后，以超过320万新元卖出加基武吉一座8880平方英尺的工厂。9月23日它推出的9个物业里，只有这座工厂有人出价。
  • High guide prices, the 60% additional buyer’s stamp duty on foreign buyers, and cheaper new launches in prime districts are weighing on demand.指导价高、外国买家60%的额外买方印花税，以及黄金地段更便宜的新盘，都在压制需求。
  • The report says Sentosa Cove homes may be harder to sell, because buyers can choose freehold city units that are easier to resell.报道说圣淘沙湾的房子可能更难卖，因为买家可以选择市中心更容易转手的永久地契单位。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a prime buyer wait for more seized homes to clear?黄金地段的买家该不该等更多被扣押的房子出清？ Waiting can bring more of this stock, but it has been slow. Since 17 September, 47 properties were auctioned and five sold. The New Futura unit went for $8.5 million on a single bid, 13% under a $9.8 million guide, and $500,000 above a similar-sized unit in the project that sold for $8 million in July 2025. A buyer who wants a forced-sale discount is looking at a thin, slow clearance. A buyer who needs a home on a normal timetable should not plan around the next CAD auction.再等，可能会有更多这类货，但出清很慢。9月17日以来拍卖了47个物业，卖掉五个。New Futura那套以850万新元成交，只有一个出价，比980万的指导价低13%，又比2025年7月同项目一套面积相近、以800万成交的单位高50万。想要法拍折扣的人，面对的是一批少而慢的货。按正常时间要房子的人，不该把计划押在下一场商业事务局的拍卖上。
  • What should an ordinary seller do with these auction prices?普通卖家该怎么看待这些拍卖价？ These prices belong to seized homes, liquidated by Deloitte, with 60% additional buyer’s stamp duty still on foreign buyers and cheaper new prime launches as the alternative. SRI sold five of 16. Edmund Tie’s only bid on 23 September was an 8,880-square-foot Kaki Bukit factory, above $3.2 million, after private talks. A willing seller of a freehold city apartment is not that seller. Matching a liquidation price gives away the difference between a forced sale and a sale the owner can refuse.这些价钱属于被扣押、由德勤清算的房子。外国买家仍要付60%的额外买方印花税，也可以改买更便宜的黄金地段新盘。SRI卖掉16个里的五个。戴德梁行9月23日九个出价里唯一成交的，是经私下洽谈、以超过320万新元卖掉的加基武吉8880平方英尺厂房。自愿出售市中心永久地契公寓的人，不是这种卖家。把开价降到清算价，让掉的是被迫出售和卖家可以拒绝之间的差价。
  • Which way is this slice of the luxury market pointing?豪宅这一角正指向哪一边？ The direction is heavy at the seized end of the prime market. More than half of the 80-plus seized properties have already been offered. The report expects Sentosa Cove to be harder still, because buyers can choose freehold city units that are easier to resell. Suburban homes and HDB flats are outside this trade. Waiting does not pull their prices down with the auctions.方向是被扣押豪宅这一端偏重。80多个被扣押物业里，超过一半已经拿出来拍卖。报道认为圣淘沙湾会更难卖，因为买家可以改选更好转手的市中心永久地契。郊区私宅和组屋不在这笔交易里。再等，也不会把它们的价钱一起拉低。
