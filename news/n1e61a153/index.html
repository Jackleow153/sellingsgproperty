HDB publishes Q3 resale flat prices by town, listing the cheapest & most expensive areas建屋局公布第三季各组屋市镇转售价格

2026/10/312026年10月31日Source: Vulcan Post来源：Vulcan Post

Key facts重点

  • HDB published Q3 2026 resale prices broken down by town, Vulcan Post reportedVulcan Post报道，建屋局公布了2026年第三季按市镇划分的转售价
  • The release covers cheaper and more expensive areas这份资料包括较便宜和较贵的地区
  • The headline does not name those towns标题没有点名这些市镇
  • Town and flat type matter more than one national average市镇和房型，比一个全岛平均数更重要

What this means这对你意味着什么

Vulcan Post reported that HDB has published third-quarter resale flat prices by town, including which areas were the cheapest and which were the most expensive. The headline does not name the towns. The table does.Vulcan Post报道，建屋发展局公布了2026年第三季按市镇划分的转售组屋价格，包括较便宜和较贵的地区。标题没有写出是哪些市镇。表格里才有。

A national movement, such as a small quarterly dip, can hide a town that is still firm and another town that has already softened. Buyers who shop across Singapore should read the row for the town they would actually live in, and for the flat type they want.全岛一个很小的季度变动，可能盖住一个仍然坚挺的市镇，和另一个已经转软的市镇。在全岛找房的买家，应该看自己真正会住的市镇，以及自己要的房型那一行。

Sellers should do the same before they copy a price from a different estate. A Queenstown price is not a Woodlands price. If your town is missing from a news summary, open HDB’s own table rather than guessing from the headline.卖家在抄另一个邻里的价钱之前，也应该先看自己的市镇。女皇镇的价钱不是兀兰的价钱。如果新闻摘要里没有你的市镇，就打开建屋局自己的表，不要凭标题猜。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Where do I see the cheap and expensive towns?较便宜和较贵的市镇要去哪里看？ Open HDB’s town table and find your flat type. A news headline that does not name the town cannot answer that for you.打开建屋局的市镇表，找到你的房型。没有写出市镇名称的标题，回答不了这个问题。
  • Can I use the national quarter to price my flat?可以用全岛的季度数字来定我这套房子的价吗？ Do not price a flat from another town’s number. Buyers will compare you with sales on your estate.不要用另一个市镇的数字来定你的房子。买家会拿你和本邻里的成交比较。
  • What should a buyer compare inside the town table?买家在市镇表里该比较什么？ If you are choosing between two towns, compare the same flat type and the same quarter. A cheaper town is only cheaper if the commute and the lease still work for you.如果在两个市镇之间选择，比较同一房型、同一季度。较便宜的市镇，只有在通勤和屋契也合适时才算便宜。
