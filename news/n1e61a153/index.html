HDB publishes Q3 resale flat prices by town, listing the cheapest & most expensive areas建屋局公布第三季各组屋市镇转售价格

2026/10/312026年10月31日

Source: Vulcan Post来源：Vulcan Post

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • HDB published Q3 2026 resale prices broken down by town, Vulcan Post reportedVulcan Post报道，建屋局公布了2026年第三季按市镇划分的转售价
  • The release covers cheaper and more expensive areas这份资料包括较便宜和较贵的地区
  • Town and flat type matter more than one national average市镇和房型，比一个全岛平均数更重要

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a buyer wait for one national price, or use the town table?买家该等一个全岛价钱，还是用按市镇的表？ Waiting for a single national average to name the right flat will not. HDB’s third-quarter 2026 release, as reported, breaks resale prices out by town and covers both the cheaper and the more expensive areas. The flat type inside the town moves the budget as much as the town does. A buyer who pauses until the average “settles” is pausing past the only useful cut of the data.等一个全岛平均价来指出该买哪一套，等不到。建屋局2026年第三季的公布，按报道是把转售价拆到市镇，较便宜和较贵的地区都有。市镇里的房型，对预算的影响不小于市镇本身。买家停下来等平均数“稳定”，错过的是这组数据里唯一有用的切法。
  • How should a seller price against cheaper and dearer towns?卖家该怎么对照较便宜和较贵的市镇来定价？ A seller in a dearer town does not inherit a cheaper town’s number, and a seller in a cheaper town does not get the dearer one. The buyer they meet is comparing towns on this release. Pricing off the island average is how both sides talk past the flat.较贵市镇的卖家继承不到较便宜市镇的数字，较便宜市镇的卖家也拿不到较贵的那个。他们遇到的买家，是拿这份公布在比较市镇。按全岛平均来开价，两边都会说偏眼前的房子。
  • What direction does a town-by-town release actually show?按市镇公布的数字，实际显示什么方向？ The direction is a split market, not one HDB price. The next quarter’s table is the next check. It is not a strategy, and this page does not itself list which town was cheapest or dearest.方向是一个分裂的市场，不是一个组屋价钱。下一季的表是下一次核对。它不是策略。这一页本身也没有列出哪个市镇最便宜或最贵。
