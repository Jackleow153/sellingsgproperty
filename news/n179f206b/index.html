November BTO exercise launches nearly 8,000 flats, including Toa Payoh and Bayshore unitsNovember 预购组屋 exercise launches nearly 8,000组屋, including 大巴窑 Bayshore 个单位

2026/11/042026年11月4日

Source: EdgeProp.sg来源：EdgeProp.sg

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • HDB’s November 2026 Build-To-Order exercise launched 7,964 flats, close to the “nearly 8,000” in the headline.建屋发展局2026年11月的预购组屋推出了7964个单位，和标题里的“近8000个”相符。
  • The flats are in seven projects across Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun.单位分布在勿洛、芽笼、三巴旺、登加、大巴窑和义顺的七个项目。
  • Toa Payoh Hillside, next to Caldecott MRT, is the only Prime project, with 1,432 flats and an 11% subsidy recovery.加利谷地铁站旁的大巴窑山坡（Toa Payoh Hillside）是唯一的优质项目，有1432个单位，补贴回收率11%。
  • Bedok has two Plus projects by the Bayshore Thomson-East Coast Line station: Bayshore Sails with 1,637 flats and Bayshore Oasis with 855. Both have a 9% subsidy recovery.勿洛有两个优选项目，靠近白沙汤申-东海岸线站：Bayshore Sails有1637个单位，Bayshore Oasis有855个。两者的补贴回收率都是9%。
  • The other Plus project is Merpati Meadow in Geylang, with 438 flats and an 8% subsidy recovery.另一个优选项目是芽笼的Merpati Meadow，438个单位，补贴回收率8%。
  • The Standard projects are Sembawang Anchor (1,310 flats), Plantation Dew in Tengah (710) and Chencharu Acres in Yishun (1,582).标准项目是三巴旺的Sembawang Anchor（1310个）、登加的Plantation Dew（710个）和义顺的Chencharu Acres（1582个）。
  • Sembawang Anchor has a wait of about two years and 10 months. Most of the other projects are under four years. Toa Payoh Hillside is about three years and nine months.Sembawang Anchor的等候时间大约两年10个月。其余多数项目不到四年。大巴窑山坡大约三年九个月。
  • This is the first sales exercise since the income ceiling rose to $16,000 a month for families and $8,000 for singles.这是家庭收入顶限提高到每月1万6000新元、单身人士提高到8000新元之后的第一轮销售。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a buyer ballot this November exercise or wait for a later one?买家该参加11月这一轮，还是等下一轮？ This exercise is the first under the higher income ceiling, so the crowd is wider than the last one. Waiting for a later sale means giving up 7,964 flats across seven projects, including the shorter wait at Sembawang Anchor, about two years and 10 months, against under four years for most of the rest and about three years and nine months at Toa Payoh Hillside. A buyer who needs keys sooner than those waits is not served by balloting. A buyer who can live with the wait, and with the Plus or Prime rules, is looking at the largest single choice of the season.这是收入顶限提高后的第一轮，人会比上一轮多。再等，就是放弃七个项目、7964个单位，包括三巴旺锚地大约两年10个月的较短等候。其余多数在四年以内，大巴窑山坡大约三年九个月。比这些等候更早需要钥匙的人，抽签帮不了。等得起、也接受优选或优质规则的人，面对的是这一季最大的一次选择。
  • What does the new supply do to sellers near these towns?新供应对这几个市镇附近的卖家有什么影响？ Sellers near Bayshore compete with two Plus projects, Bayshore Sails and Bayshore Oasis, both with a 9% subsidy recovery. Toa Payoh sellers near Caldecott compete with the only Prime project, 1,432 flats and an 11% recovery. Geylang sellers have Merpati Meadow, 438 flats and 8%. The resale seller’s advantage is a flat the buyer can move into without a multi-year wait and without the clawback. The future BTO buyer accepts both.白沙附近的卖家，对上两个优选项目：Bayshore Sails和Bayshore Oasis，补贴回收都是9%。加利谷附近的大巴窑卖家，对上唯一的优质项目，1432个单位，回收11%。芽笼的卖家对上Merpati Meadow，438个单位，回收8%。转售卖家的优势是买家不用等好几年，也没有补贴回扣。预购组屋的买家接受这两项。
  • Which way does this point the resale market?这把转售市场指向哪一边？ Almost 8,000 new flats in one exercise is more public choice, not a forecast that resale prices must fall. Standard projects in Sembawang, Tengah and Yishun pull a different buyer from the Plus and Prime stock. The impact of waiting is missing this mix. The next exercise is not promised to be cheaper or less crowded.一轮将近8000个新单位，是更多公共住房的选择，不是转售价必须下跌的预测。三巴旺、登加和义顺的标准项目，拉的是和优选、优质不同的买家。等待的代价是错过这一轮组合。下一轮没有保证更便宜或人更少。
