Hong Leong, TID place top bid of S$181.86m or S$1,755 psf ppr for Orchard plot丰隆与TID以1亿8186万新元标得乌节地段，折合每平方英尺楼面地价1755新元

2026/10/302026年10月30日Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Bidders: Hong Leong and TID, the top bid投标方：丰隆（Hong Leong）与TID，最高标价
  • Land price: $181.86 million地价：1亿8186万新元
  • Land rate: about $1,755 per square foot of allowed floor area地价率：约每平方英尺1755新元的可建楼面面积（psf ppr）
  • Location: an Orchard plot地点：乌节一块地段
  • Not for sale yet: this is a land bid, not a condo price list尚未发售：这是土地标价，不是公寓价目表

What this means这对你意味着什么

Hong Leong and TID placed the top bid for an Orchard plot: $181.86 million. Spread over the floor area the site is allowed to build, that is about $1,755 per square foot. That second figure is a land price. It is not the price of a finished condo.丰隆（Hong Leong）与TID以最高标价投得一块乌节地段：1亿8186万新元。把这个价钱分摊到地段允许建造的楼面面积，大约是每平方英尺1755新元。第二个数字是地价（psf ppr），不是一套建成公寓的价格。

No units from this site are for sale yet. By the time a project is launched, the price of an actual apartment also has to cover construction, interest and the developer’s margin. Launch prices per square foot of a unit are usually much higher than the land bid.这块地目前没有单位在卖。等到项目开盘，一套公寓（condo）的价格还要加上建筑成本、利息和发展商的利润。单位的开盘尺价，通常会比土地标价高得多。

If you own or are selling nearby, buyers may mention $1,755 psf. It is context for a future new launch. It is not a valuation of your home today.如果你在附近拥有或出售房子，买家可能会提到每平方英尺1755新元。那是未来某个新盘的背景，不是你的房子今天的估价。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Does this Orchard land bid tell you what a condo will cost?这块乌节地的标价，能告诉你公寓要卖多少吗？ A high land bid shows developers wanted that Orchard site. It does not tell you what a finished condo will cost, or what your own home is worth today.高地价说明发展商想要这块乌节地。它没有告诉你建成后的公寓要卖多少，也没有告诉你自己的房子今天值多少。
  • Should buyers pay more now because of this site?买家要不要因为这块地，现在就付更高的价钱？ If you are buying nearby now, price the home in front of you. Do not pay extra today for a project that has not been launched.如果你现在就在附近买房，给眼前这套房子定价。不要为了一个还没开盘的项目，今天就多付钱。
  • How should nearby sellers use the $1,755 psf figure?附近的卖家该如何使用每平方英尺1755新元这个数字？ Resale sellers should lean on recent sales of similar homes. A land bid is background, not your asking price.转售卖家应该依靠类似房子最近的成交。土地标价是背景，不是你的要价。
