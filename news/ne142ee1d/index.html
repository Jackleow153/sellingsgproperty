Get Your S*** Together: When Is The Right Time To Apply For A BTO With My Partner?Get S*** Together: When Right Time Apply 预购组屋 My Partner?

2026/10/302026年10月30日

Source: FZINE Singapore来源：FZINE Singapore

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • The article asks when a couple should apply together for a Build-To-Order flat. It is about timing, not a new launch.这篇文章问的是，一对伴侣什么时候应该一起申请预购组屋（BTO）。谈的是时机，不是一次新的开卖。
  • Clara says there is no fixed number of years. She and Tim applied after four years together.Clara说没有固定的年数。她和Tim在一起四年后才申请。
  • Timothy’s view is that a couple should have been together for at least about two years.Timothy的看法是，一对伴侣至少在一起大约两年。
  • Theresa says applying early can be practical because the wait for a flat is long, and only when the couple already shares a commitment.Theresa说，因为等组屋的时间很长，早点申请可以比较实际，但只有在两个人已经有共同承诺的时候。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • When does applying early help a couple, and when does waiting help?一对伴侣早点申请有什么好处，等待又有什么好处？ The article’s own range is about two years together, from Timothy, to four years in Clara and Tim’s case, and Clara’s point is that there is no fixed number. Applying early, as Theresa puts it, is practical only because the wait for a flat is long, and only if the couple already shares a commitment. Waiting for that commitment reduces the chance of a breakup during a multi-year queue. Applying before it exists risks a joint flat that is hard to unwind. The impact of waiting is a later ballot, against whatever supply is on sale then. It does not make the construction wait shorter.文章自己的范围，是Timothy说的至少大约两年，到Clara和Tim的四年。Clara的重点是没有固定年数。像Theresa说的，早点申请之所以实际，是因为等组屋的时间很长，而且只有在两人已经有共同承诺时才成立。等到那个承诺，可以降低在多年轮候里分手的可能。承诺还没有就申请，风险是一套很难拆开的联名组屋。等待的影响是更晚才抽签，面对的是当时在卖的供应。它不会把建造的等候缩短。
  • Who is the future buyer if a couple decides not to wait?如果他们决定不等，未来的买家是谁？ This is not a seller’s pricing story. Couples who decide they will not wait become resale buyers. Couples who ballot are out of the resale market for the length of the queue and then the occupation period. A seller of a resale flat meets the first group, not the second.这不是卖家的定价故事。决定不等的伴侣，会变成转售买家。去抽签的伴侣，在轮候和之后的居住年限里离开转售市场。转售组屋的卖家遇到的是前一种人，不是后一种。
  • Is there a price direction in a relationship timetable?一段感情的时间表里有没有价格方向？ There is no price call. The timing that matters is the couple’s, set against a long public queue. A later application does not buy a cheaper market. It buys a different exercise.这里没有价格判断。要紧的时间是这对伴侣的，对照的是公共组屋很长的轮候。晚一点申请，买不到一个更便宜的市场。买到的是另一轮销售。
