Key points要点

  • Home & Decor's review of Merpati Meadow places the project at the junction of Jalan Anggerek and Merpati Road, in the MacPherson and Geylang area. It has 438 units: 167 two-room Flexi and 271 four-room. The waiting time in the review is 39 months.Home & Decor 对 Merpati Meadow 的评论把项目放在 Jalan Anggerek 和 Merpati Road 的路口，麦波申和芽笼一带。共438个单位：167套二房灵活户型、271套四房。评论里的等候时间是39个月。
  • The specification list in that review states Plus, a 10-year minimum occupation period, and an 8% subsidy clawback. The prose says the project likely falls under Plus. These points follow the specification list.该评论的规格表写明 Plus、10年最低居住年限，以及8%补贴回收。正文说项目很可能属于 Plus。这里的要点依照规格表。
  • The nearest station named is Mattar MRT on the Downtown Line.点名的最近车站是滨海市区线的 Mattar 地铁站。
  • Prices in the review run from $253,000 to $300,000 for two-room Flexi type 1, from $306,000 to $378,000 for type 2, and from $545,000 to $726,000 for a four-room flat.评论中的价格：二房灵活户型第一类25万3000新元至30万新元，第二类30万6000新元至37万8000新元，四房54万5000新元至72万6000新元。
  • Facilities named are playgrounds, fitness stations, a precinct pavilion, and a preschool on the ground floor of the multi-storey car park.点名的设施有游乐场、健身站、邻里亭，以及多层停车场地面的学前中心。
  • Another Home & Decor page on this launch lists the same 438 units, the same 167 and 271 split, the same $253,000 to $726,000 price span, the same two roads, and Mattar MRT.Home & Decor 关于这一轮的另一页列出同样的438个单位、同样的167和271划分、同样的25万3000新元至72万6000新元价格范围、同样的两条路，以及 Mattar 地铁站。

What this means if you're upgrading如果你打算升级换房

Plus flats come with a longer occupation period and a subsidy clawback. If you are an upgrader comparing a resale flat, a Plus BTO and a condo, count those rules as a real cost, not just the price.Plus组屋有较长的最低居住年限，并需要回缴部分津贴。如果你在转售组屋、Plus预购组屋和公寓之间比较，要把这些规定当作真实成本，而不只是看价格。
