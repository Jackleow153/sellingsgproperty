Build-to-rent homes could appeal to young S’poreans, but can rent be kept under $2,000?建造供出租的住房或能吸引年轻新加坡人，但租金能压在2000新元以下吗？

2026/11/022026年11月2日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • Idea being explored: build-to-rent homes, kept as rentals rather than sold at launch正在探讨的想法：建造供出租的住房，长期出租，而不是开盘出售
  • Rent level under discussion: about $2,000 a month讨论中的租金：每月约2000新元
  • Who it is aimed at: younger Singaporeans who want a place of their own before they buy对象：想在买房前先有自己住所的年轻新加坡人
  • Not for sale yet: no project, price list or application date has been set尚未发售：还没有项目、价目表或申请日期

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Can a renter still save to buy if the rent is about $2,000?租金大约2000新元，租户还能存钱买房吗？ If you are renting while you save for an HDB flat or a condo, write the rent next to what you can still save each month. A figure near $2,000 only helps if your own budget still leaves a down payment.如果你一边租房、一边为HDB组屋或公寓存首期，把租金写在你每月还能存下的金额旁边。接近2000新元的租金，只有在你自己的预算仍留得出首期时才有帮助。
  • What should a landlord do if more rental homes are built?如果日后建造更多租赁住房，房东该怎么做？ Owners who let out a condo or an HDB flat should compare their asking rent with what tenants can actually pay. A new rental product later on will not fill every older unit at last year’s rent.出租公寓或组屋的屋主，应该把自己的要价和租户实际付得起的金额比较。日后即使出现新的租赁住房，也不会让每一套较旧的单位都继续租到去年的价钱。
  • Does this rental idea replace buying an HDB flat or a condo?这个租赁构想能否取代购买HDB组屋或公寓？ Do not treat a rental idea as a reason to buy now. The loan, cash down payment and monthly repayment of a real purchase still have to fit your income.不要把一个租赁构想当成现在就买房的理由。真正买房时，贷款、首期现金和每月供款仍然必须配得上你的收入。
