Three new MRT stations in the east to open on Dec 26Three new MRT stations in the east to open on Dec 26

2026/11/052026年11月5日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • Three stations open on 26 December: Bedok South and Sungei Bedok on the Thomson-East Coast Line, and Xilin on the Downtown Line.12月26日有三个地铁站启用：汤申-东海岸线的勿洛南和双溪勿洛，以及滨海市区线的锡林。
  • Sungei Bedok is an interchange between the two lines, the second after Stevens.双溪勿洛是两条线的转换站，是史蒂文森之后的第二个转换站。
  • Bedok South and Sungei Bedok complete Stage 5 of the Thomson-East Coast Line. Xilin is part of the Downtown Line 3 extension.勿洛南和双溪勿洛完成汤申-东海岸线第五阶段。锡林属于滨海市区线第三阶段的延长线。
  • Transport Minister Jeffrey Siow announced the date at Singapore Urban Mobility Week.交通部长徐芳达在新加坡城市出行周宣布了这个日期。
  • The Land Transport Authority said the stations improve access to eastern job centres, including Changi Business Park and Tampines Regional Centre.陆路交通管理局说，这些车站方便人到东部的就业中心，包括樟宜商业园和淡滨尼区域中心。
  • Stage 5 puts more than 240,000 households within a 10-minute walk of a Thomson-East Coast Line station. The Downtown Line extension does the same for more than 220,000 households.第五阶段启用后，超过24万个家庭住在汤申-东海岸线车站的10分钟步行范围内。滨海市区线延长段则为超过22万个家庭做到这一点。
  • The new stretches add about 4.3 kilometres of rail.新增路段大约4.3公里。
  • Some journeys from the east are expected to be 10 to 25 minutes shorter. A trip from Havelock to Changi Business Park now takes about 70 minutes and includes a bus.东部部分路程预计可缩短10到25分钟。现在从合乐到樟宜商业园大约要70分钟，中间还要转巴士。
  • Bedok South, along Upper East Coast Road, serves nearby homes and schools, including the Bayshore area.勿洛南站沿东海岸路上段，服务附近的住家和学校，包括白沙一带。
  • From Woodlands North, commuters will later be able to ride towards Bukit Chagar in Johor when the Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link opens in February 2027.新柔地铁（RTS）预计2027年2月通车后，乘客可以从兀兰北站一路前往柔佛的武吉查卡。
