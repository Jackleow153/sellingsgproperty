HDB resale transactions jump 19.5% q-o-q in 3Q2026 amid policy changes第三季组屋转售成交按季增加19.5%

2026/10/222026年10月22日Source: EdgeProp Singapore来源：EdgeProp

Real Estate SG

Key facts重点

  • EdgeProp: HDB resale transactions rose 19.5% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026EdgeProp：2026年第三季组屋转售成交按季增加19.5%
  • The headline links the jump to policy changes but does not name the rule标题把升幅和政策变化连在一起，但没有写出是哪一条规则
  • 19.5% is a change in the number of deals, not in the price index19.5%是成交宗数的变化，不是价格指数
  • More sales can happen while prices are flat or slightly down成交可以增加，价格同时持平或微跌

What this means这对你意味着什么

EdgeProp reported that HDB resale transactions jumped 19.5% quarter on quarter in the third quarter of 2026, and linked the rise to policy changes. The headline does not say which rule changed, and it does not say prices rose by 19.5%. Transactions and prices are different.EdgeProp报道，2026年第三季HDB转售成交按季增加19.5%，并把升幅和政策变化连在一起。标题没有说改的是哪一条规则，也没有说价格涨了19.5%。成交量和价格是两件事。

More deals can mean buyers who had waited are now willing to sign. It can also mean sellers finally accepted offers. A busier market is not the same as a more expensive market. The separate Q3 price line was a 0.1% dip, which shows volume and prices can move in different directions.成交变多，可能是等过的买家现在愿意签约，也可能是卖家终于接受了出价。市场比较忙，不等于市场比较贵。同一季另有价格微跌0.1%的说法，说明成交量和价格可以朝不同方向走。

Buyers should ask which policy the people they are competing with are reacting to, instead of assuming every rule was loosened. Sellers may see more viewings. They should still price from recent sales, because more buyers do not accept every asking price.买家应该问，和你抢房子的人到底在回应哪一条政策，不要假设每条规则都放宽了。卖家可能会看到更多看房。仍然要用近期成交定价，因为买家变多，不代表每一个开价都会被接受。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Did HDB prices jump 19.5%?组屋价格是不是涨了19.5%？ Read 19.5% as more resale deals, not as a 19.5% rise in your flat’s value.把19.5%读成转售成交变多，不是你的房子价值涨了19.5%。
  • Which policy should buyers ask about?买家该问的是哪一条政策？ Ask which rule actually changed before you assume demand is back for every town. A policy headline without the rule is incomplete.先问真正改了哪一条规则，再假设每个市镇的需求都回来了。只有政策两个字、没有规则内容的标题，是不完整的。
  • Do more transactions mean a seller can raise the price?成交变多，卖家就可以加价吗？ Sellers can expect more interest. Price from the last few sales in the block, and be ready for offers that do not match the first asking price.卖家可以预期更多兴趣。用这座楼最近几宗成交定价，并准备好出价达不到第一次开价。
