Home buyers using more cash and CPF savings to fund down payments买家用更多现金和公积金支付首期

2026/10/192026年10月19日

Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Headline: buyers are using more cash and CPF savings for down payments标题：买家用更多现金和公积金付首期
  • No percentage split is given没有给出百分比
  • Cash, CPF and the bank loan fund a purchase in different ways现金、公积金和银行贷款以不同方式支付房价
  • A larger down payment cuts the loan and also cuts what is left for the next step首期变大，贷款变小，下一步能用的钱也变少

What this means这对你意味着什么

The headline says home buyers are using more cash and CPF savings for down payments. It does not say how the split has changed, or whether prices rose. Cash, CPF and the bank loan are three different pots. Using more of the first two usually means the loan is a smaller share of the price.标题说，买房的人正在用更多现金和公积金（CPF）储蓄来付首期。它没有说比例改了多少，也没有说房价是否上涨。现金、公积金和银行贷款是三笔不同的钱。前两笔用得更多，通常代表贷款占房价的比例更小。

That can be a choice, or a requirement. A bigger cash and CPF down payment lowers the monthly repayment. It also leaves less CPF for the next home, and less cash for renovation, stamp duty and the months after you move.这可以是选择，也可以是要求。首期里现金和公积金越多，每月供款越低。但下一套房子能用的公积金就更少，装修、印花税和搬进去之后的几个月，现金也更少。

Sellers sometimes hear that buyers are “stretching”. This headline says the opposite pattern: more money up front. Buyers should still write down what is left after the down payment. A purchase that empties both cash and CPF is fragile if income drops.卖家有时听到买家在“硬撑”。这则标题说的是另一种情况：前面先拿出更多钱。买家仍然应该写下首期之后还剩什么。如果现金和公积金一起被掏空，收入一降，这笔购买就很脆。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Does a bigger CPF down payment make a home more affordable?用更多公积金付首期，房子就更负担得起吗？ List cash, CPF and the loan on one page. A larger CPF sum lowers the monthly repayment and also reduces CPF you can use later.把现金、公积金和贷款写在同一页。公积金用得更多，每月供款会下降，以后还能用的公积金也下降。
  • What should be left after the down payment?付完首期还应该剩下什么？ Keep a buffer for stamp duty, legal fees and renovation. A down payment that uses every dollar is not a comfortable purchase.留一笔给印花税、律师费和装修。把每一块钱都付进首期，不是一笔从容的购买。
  • What should sellers not assume about buyers’ cash?卖家不该对买家的现金作什么假设？ Sellers should not assume every buyer is stretched, or that every buyer can raise the offer. Ask the structure of the offer, not only the headline price.卖家不要假设每个买家都在硬撑，也不要假设每个买家都能加价。问出价的结构，不要只看标题上的总价。
