3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should anyone buy, wait, or reprice because of this pledge?有没有人该因为这项承诺而买、等，或改价？ No. The page establishes a pledge of continued HDB cooperation to weed out errant heartland massage parlours. It does not name a town, a block, a fine, or a price. Waiting for that work to finish does not change a flat’s lease or a mortgage. Using the pledge as a reason to buy cheaper, or to hold for a premium, treats a shop-use story as a valuation.不该。这一页确定的是一项承诺：建屋局会继续配合，清理组屋区里违规的按摩院。它没有点名市镇、座号、罚款或价钱。等这件事做完，不会改变一套组屋的屋契或月供。把承诺当成买得更便宜的理由，或当成等一个溢价，是把店屋用途的新闻当成估价。
  • Who is actually affected, and who is not a buyer of a flat?真正受影响的是谁，谁并不是组屋的买家？ The people this pledge is about are the operators of those parlours and the shop tenants around them. Buyers and sellers of flats are not given a new comparable. A future buyer of a heartland flat is still buying the flat. They are not buying the pledge.这项承诺针对的是这些按摩院的经营者，以及周围的店屋租户。组屋的买家和卖家没有得到新的可比成交。组屋区未来的买家，买的仍是房子。他们不是在买这项承诺。
  • Does this move the housing market?这会带动住房市场吗？ There is no property-market direction. Heartland prices are not pointed up or down by a pledge to keep cooperating on errant parlours.没有房地产市场的方向。继续配合清理违规按摩院，没有把组屋区的价钱指向更高或更低。
