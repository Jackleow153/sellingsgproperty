Home sales set to recover in Q4 with prices seen rising further on new launch momentum第四季房屋成交料回升，新盘势头或再推高价格

2026/10/242026年10月24日

Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Outlook, not a result: fourth-quarter home sales seen recovering这是展望，不是结果：第四季房屋成交料回升
  • Prices are described as rising on new-launch momentum价格被描述为受新盘势头推动而上涨
  • No project name or percentage is in the headline标题里没有项目名称或百分比
  • A forecast can be wrong, and it does not price one unit预测可能不准，也不能给一套具体的房子定价

What this means这对你意味着什么

A PropNex headline says home sales are set to recover in the fourth quarter, with prices seen rising further on new-launch momentum. That is an outlook. It is not a count of homes already sold in the quarter, and it does not name a project.博纳的标题说，房屋成交料在第四季回升，价格也可能因为新盘的势头再往上。这是展望，不是这个季度已经卖出多少套的统计，标题里也没有项目名称。

New launches can pull buyers who want a fresh lease and a showflat. They can also make resale sellers wait longer if buyers compare the two. Momentum is not a promise that every project, or every resale flat, will cost more by December.新盘会吸引想要全新屋契和示范单位的买家。买家如果两边都在比，转售卖家也可能要等更久。势头不是承诺：不是每个项目、每套转售，到了12月都会更贵。

Buyers should not rush a booking because a headline expects prices to rise. Sellers should not lift an asking price on a forecast. Use the homes on the market now, and treat the fourth-quarter line as something to check when the quarter’s numbers are actually out.买家不该因为标题预期价格上涨就赶着下订。卖家也不该因为一份预测就提高开价。看现在市场上的房子。第四季那句话，等这个季度的数字真正出来再核对。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should you buy now because prices are expected to rise?因为预期价格会涨，现在就该买吗？ Buy the home whose monthly cost you can live with. An expected rise is not a reason to skip the loan sums.买每月开销你住得下去的房子。预期上涨，不是跳过贷款计算的理由。
  • What does new-launch momentum mean for resale sellers?新盘势头对转售卖家意味着什么？ Resale sellers should ask if their buyers are also touring showflats. If they are, be ready to explain lease, space and what is already built.转售卖家要问，买家是不是也在看示范单位。如果是，就要准备解释屋契、空间，以及房子已经建好这件事。
  • When will you know if the fourth quarter really recovered?要到什么时候才知道第四季是不是真的回升了？ Wait for the quarter’s actual sales before you treat this sentence as the market. Forecasts are revised.等这个季度的真实成交出来，再把这句话当成市场。预测是会改的。
