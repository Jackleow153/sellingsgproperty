2026/10/262026年10月26日

Key points要点

  • Real Estate Asia: 597 million-dollar HDB resale transactions in Q3 2026, a record countReal Estate Asia：2026年第三季百万组屋转售597宗，次数创纪录
  • The figure counts flats sold at S$1 million or more这个数字计算的是售价达到或超过100万新元的组屋
  • It is not the median price of all resale flats它不是全部转售组屋的中位数价钱
  • A busy top end can happen in the same quarter as a small move in the broad index顶端活跃，可以和整体指数的小幅变动出现在同一季

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a normal buyer wait because 597 million-dollar sales set a record?因为597宗百万成交创了纪录，普通买家就该等吗？ The 597 sales are flats that went for S$1 million or more in the third quarter of 2026, a record count. They are not the median price of all resale flats. A buyer of an ordinary flat who waits for this number to fall is watching the top of the market. Their budget can be untouched in a quarter when the broad index barely moves. Waiting on the count does not buy a cheaper standard flat.这597宗是2026年第三季售价达到或超过100万新元的组屋，宗数创纪录。它不是全部转售组屋的中位数。买普通单位的人等这个数字下降，看的是市场顶端。在整体指数几乎不动的一季里，他们的预算可以完全没被碰到。盯着宗数等，等不来一套更便宜的标准单位。
  • Which sellers actually gained a deeper buyer pool?哪些卖家真的有了更深的买家池？ Sellers of flats that can clear $1 million had a deeper pool of completed sales to point to. Sellers below that line should not borrow the record as a valuation. The future buyer of a million-dollar flat is a specific household. The future buyer of a $500,000 flat is someone else, and this count does not describe them.卖得出100万或以上的卖家，有了更深的一批已完成成交可以引用。这条线下面的卖家，不该把纪录借来当估价。百万组屋的未来买家是特定的家庭。50万组屋的未来买家是另一个人，这个宗数没有描述他们。
  • Can a record count and a quiet index happen together?纪录宗数和安静的指数可以同时出现吗？ A busy top can sit in the same quarter as a small move in the broad index. The direction is a split market: the top end was busy, the middle is a different series. Price the flat, not the 597.顶端忙，可以和整体指数的小幅变动出现在同一季。方向是一个分裂的市场：顶端忙，中间是另一组数字。该给房子定价，不是给597定价。
