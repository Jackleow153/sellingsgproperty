Million-dollar HDB resales hit third straight monthly record in October 2026Million-dollar 组屋 resales hit third straight monthly 纪录 October 2026

2026/11/042026年11月4日

Source: 99.co来源：99.co

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • Unofficial figures from 99.co and SRX counted 238 HDB resale flats sold for at least $1 million in October 2026.99.co和SRX的非官方数据显示，2026年10月有238个转售组屋以至少100万新元成交。
  • That was the third month in a row with a new monthly high.这是连续第三个月创下单月新高。
  • October had 29 more of these sales than September’s 209, and they were 10.9% of that month’s HDB resales.10月比9月的209宗多了29宗，占当月组屋转售的10.9%。
  • The highest sale was a five-room flat at SkyParc @ Dawson in Queenstown, at $1.818 million.成交价最高的是女皇镇SkyParc @ Dawson的一个五房式单位，181万8000新元。
  • Toa Payoh had 39 million-dollar resales, Queenstown 31, and Bedok 26.大巴窑有39宗百万转售，女皇镇31宗，勿洛26宗。
  • Among non-mature towns, the highest was a five-room flat in Punggol at $1.32 million.非成熟市镇里，最高的是榜鹅一个五房式单位，132万新元。
