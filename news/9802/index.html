Thomson Reserve sells 90% of units at average of S$2,900 psf over launch weekendThomson Reserve开盘周末售出九成单位，平均每平方英尺2900新元（psf）

2026/11/022026年11月2日

Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Project: Thomson Reserve, Bright Hill Drive, Upper Thomson项目：Thomson Reserve，光明山通道，汤申路上段
  • Developers: UOL and CapitaLand Development发展商：华业集团（UOL）与凯德发展（CapitaLand Development）
  • Sold: 1,143 of 1,268 units, about 90%, on 30–31 October 2026售出：1268个单位中的1143个，约九成，2026年10月30日至31日
  • Average price: about $2,900 per square foot平均价格：约每平方英尺2900新元（psf）
  • What sold out: 3-bedroom and 4-bedroom types; most 5-bedroom homes售罄：三房和四房；五房也大部分售出

What this means这对你意味着什么

Thomson Reserve, the new condo by UOL and CapitaLand Development at Bright Hill Drive in Upper Thomson, sold 1,143 of its 1,268 units over the launch on 30 and 31 October 2026. That is about 90%. The average price of the units sold was about $2,900 per square foot. Most buyers were Singaporeans, buying both to live in and as an investment.华业集团（UOL）与凯德发展（CapitaLand Development）在汤申路上段光明山通道的新私宅（condo）Thomson Reserve，于2026年10月30日和31日售出1268个单位中的1143个，约九成。售出单位的平均价格约每平方英尺2900新元（psf）。大部分买家是新加坡人，既有自住，也有投资。

The preview drew about 32,000 visitors over two weekends. Three-bedroom and four-bedroom types sold out. Of the 125 homes left, 123 were smaller two-bedroom units and two were five-bedroom suites. Reported starting prices were about $1.5 million for a two-bedroom and about $2.5 million for a three-bedroom. PropNex chief executive Kelvin Fong said it was the highest launch-weekend sale of 2026 by number of units, ahead of the 853 units sold at Tengah Garden Residences in April.两个周末的预览吸引了约3万2000人次。三房和四房单位售罄。剩下的125个单位里，123个是较小的两房单位，两个是五房套间。报道中的起价，两房约150万新元，三房约250万新元。博纳（PropNex）首席执行官Kelvin Fong说，以单位数量计算，这是2026年开盘周末售出最多的项目，超过今年4月登加Tengah Garden Residences售出的853个单位。

A fast weekend tells you there was demand at that price, on those two days. It does not mean every remaining stack is worth the average. If you are still looking, the useful comparison is what is left. If you are selling a resale condo nearby, buyers may use $2,900 psf as a reference, so be ready to explain how your home is different.一个周末卖得快，只说明那两天、在那个价格上有需求。这不代表剩下的每一叠单位都值那个平均价。如果你还在看，真正该比较的是还剩下什么。如果你在转售附近的私宅，买家可能会拿每平方英尺2900新元当参照，所以要准备好说明你的房子有什么不同。
