Thomson Reserve sells 90% of units at average of S$2,900 psf over launch weekend

2026/11/02

Key facts

  • Project: Thomson Reserve, Bright Hill Drive, Upper Thomson
  • Developers: UOL and CapitaLand Development
  • Sold: 1,143 of 1,268 units, about 90%, on 30–31 October 2026
  • Average price: about $2,900 per square foot
  • What sold out: 3-bedroom and 4-bedroom types; most 5-bedroom homes
  • What is left: 125 units, of which 123 were smaller 2-bedroom homes and 2 were 5-bedroom suites
  • Reported starting prices: about $1.5 million for a 2-bedroom and about $2.5 million for a 3-bedroom

What this means

Thomson Reserve, the new condo by UOL and CapitaLand Development at Bright Hill Drive in Upper Thomson, sold 1,143 of its 1,268 units over the launch on 30 and 31 October 2026. That is about 90%. The average price of the units sold was about $2,900 per square foot. Most buyers were Singaporeans, buying both to live in and as an investment.

The preview drew about 32,000 visitors over two weekends. Three-bedroom and four-bedroom types sold out. Of the 125 homes left, 123 were smaller two-bedroom units and two were five-bedroom suites. Reported starting prices were about $1.5 million for a two-bedroom and about $2.5 million for a three-bedroom. PropNex chief executive Kelvin Fong said it was the highest launch-weekend sale of 2026 by number of units, ahead of the 853 units sold at Tengah Garden Residences in April.

A fast weekend tells you there was demand at that price, on those two days. It does not mean every remaining stack is worth the average. If you are selling a resale condo nearby, buyers may use $2,900 psf as a reference, so be ready to explain how your home is different.

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers

  • Does Thomson Reserve’s launch weekend mean you should buy? A 90% launch is a sign of interest, not proof you should buy. Work out the full price, the stamp duty and the monthly loan before you treat the weekend result as a reason to proceed.
  • Which Thomson Reserve units are actually left? If you are still choosing a unit, judge what is left. The $2,900 psf average hides both the dearer homes and the cheaper ones. Of the 125 unsold homes, 123 were two-bedroom and two were five-bedroom suites.
  • How should resale sellers near Upper Thomson price their home? Resale sellers near Upper Thomson should price against recent sales of similar homes, not only against this new-launch average. Buyers will ask why your condo costs more or less than $2,900 psf.
