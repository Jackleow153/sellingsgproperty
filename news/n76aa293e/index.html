Sell HDB Before or After HIP 2026: Works Take About 18 Months, 10 Working Days in Your Flat2026年家居改进计划（HIP）前卖还是后卖：工程约18个月，住户家中约10个工作日

2026/11/022026年11月2日

Source: PropertyNet.SG来源：PropertyNet.SG

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • HDB’s Home Improvement Programme booklet says a typical project takes about 18 months after a successful poll, while the work inside one flat usually takes about 10 working days.建屋局的家居改进计划手册说，投票通过后，一个典型项目大约要18个月。个别单位里面的工程通常大约10个工作日。
  • For Singapore-citizen households, the Government pays for the essential improvements. Owners pay 5% to 12.5% of the optional improvements, depending on flat type, under HDB’s 15 May 2026 announcement.新加坡公民家庭的必要改进由政府支付。自选改进由屋主付5%至12.5%，按房型而定。这是建屋局2026年5月15日宣布的。
  • A flat can be sold while the works are going on, once the minimum occupation period has been met. For a Standard flat that period is five years.只要已满最低居住年限，工程进行期间也可以卖。标准组屋的年限是五年。
  • Who chooses the optional items depends on the timing. Before the poll, it is the owner then. During the six-week selection window, seller and buyer should agree in writing and confirm with HDB who submits the choices.谁来选自选项目，要看卖出的时间。投票前，由当时的屋主选。在六个星期的选择期内，买卖双方应书面说好，并向建屋局确认由谁提交选择。
  • Waiting for the whole project to finish can mean holding the flat for about 18 months, for roughly 10 working days of work inside the unit.若要等整个项目完工才卖，可能要多持有大约18个月，换来的只是单位内大约10个工作日的工程。
  • For many flats old enough for the programme, the remaining lease often matters more to buyers than the upgrade, because the full loan and the full CPF housing grant depend on the lease lasting the youngest buyer to age 95.对许多已经符合计划屋龄的组屋，买家往往更在意剩余屋契，而不是翻新本身。因为全额贷款和全额公积金购屋津贴，取决于屋契能否让最年轻的买家住到95岁。
