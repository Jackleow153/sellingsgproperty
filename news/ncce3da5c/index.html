HDB to offer 4,300 BTO flats in February组屋 offer 4,300预购组屋组屋 February

2026/11/052026年11月5日

Source: Singapore Business Review来源：新加坡商业评论

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • HDB will launch about 4,300 Build-To-Order flats in February 2027, across five projects in Toa Payoh, Bishan, Tampines and Tengah.建屋发展局将在2027年2月推出约4300个预购组屋（BTO），分布在大巴窑、碧山、淡滨尼和登加的五个项目。
  • There is one project each in Toa Payoh, Bishan and Tengah, and two in Tampines.大巴窑、碧山和登加各有一个项目，淡滨尼有两个。
  • It is the smallest BTO release since February 2022, when a little over 4,100 flats were offered.这是2022年2月以来规模最小的一次BTO。当时推出的单位略多于4100个。
  • About 5,000 Sale of Balance Flats will go on sale at the same time.同一轮还会推出约5000个剩余组屋（Sale of Balance Flats）。
  • HDB estimates about 11,000 to 14,000 applications for the February BTO exercise.建屋局估计，2月这次BTO大约会收到1万1000至1万4000份申请。
  • The Toa Payoh project is the second in the Mount Pleasant estate, with about 1,140 flats beside the Thomson-East Coast Line station. The mix is about 450 two-room Flexi flats, 90 three-room flats and 600 four-room flats, plus an eating house, a pre-school, an active ageing centre and a residents’ network centre.大巴窑的项目是快乐山新镇的第二个BTO，约1140个单位，就在汤申-东海岸线地铁站旁边。户型大约是450个两房灵活单位、90个三房和600个四房，并设有食阁、幼儿园、乐龄活动中心和居民网络中心。
  • Analysts expect that Mount Pleasant project to be classed as Prime. More than half of the February flats, about 2,210, are in the Bishan and Toa Payoh projects.分析师预计快乐山这个项目会列为优质（Prime）组屋。2月推出的单位里，超过一半、约2210个，在碧山和大巴窑。
  • The Bishan project is about 1,070 flats, in two-room Flexi, three-room and four-room types. Analysts expect strong demand because schools such as Catholic High and Ai Tong are nearby.碧山项目约1070个单位，有两房灵活、三房和四房。分析师认为需求会强，因为附近有公教中学和爱同学校等学校。
  • Huttons sees the Bishan and Toa Payoh projects as possible Prime flats. Realion has described the Bishan project as a possible Plus flat.合登（Huttons）把碧山和大巴窑项目看成可能的优质组屋。瑞联（Realion）则把碧山项目看成可能的优选（Plus）组屋。
  • Huttons estimated that four-room flats in the Bishan project could start from about $530,000, with a subsidy recovery of about 10% if it is priced like earlier Prime launches there.合登估计，若比照当地较早的优质组屋，碧山项目的四房价格可能从约53万新元起，补贴回收率大约10%。
