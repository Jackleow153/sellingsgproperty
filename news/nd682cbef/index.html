Tampines executive flat sells for record S$1.275M, becoming the town’s second priciest HDB resale淡滨尼 executive 组屋 sells 纪录 S$1.275M, becoming town’s second priciest 组屋转售

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: 99.co来源：99.co

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • An executive flat at Block 938 Tampines Avenue 5, in Tampines Palmspring, sold for $1.275 million in November 2026.淡滨尼Palmspring、淡滨尼5道第938座的一个行政公寓（executive flat），在2026年11月以127万5000新元成交。
  • That is a new record for an executive flat in Tampines, and the town’s second-highest HDB resale of any type.这是淡滨尼行政公寓的新纪录，也是全镇各类组屋转售里的第二高价。
  • The home is 1,528 square feet, with three bedrooms, on the 7th to 9th storeys. The price is about $834 per square foot.单位1528平方英尺，三间卧室，位于7楼至9楼。价格大约每平方英尺834新元。
  • The lease began in 1988, so about 61 years remained.屋契从1988年开始，大约还剩61年。
  • The previous executive record was a 1,604-square-foot flat at Block 856F Tampines Street 82, sold for $1.24 million, or $773 per square foot, in June 2026. About 68 years were left on that lease.之前的行政公寓纪录是淡滨尼82街第856F座一个1604平方英尺的单位，2026年6月以124万新元成交，约每平方英尺773新元。当时屋契大约还剩68年。
  • The new record is 76 square feet smaller, about seven years shorter on the lease, and $35,000 higher, about 2.8%. Both flats are on the 7th to 9th storeys.新纪录的面积少了76平方英尺，屋契大约短七年，价格却高出3万5000新元，约2.8%。两套都在7楼至9楼。
  • Of the recent executive sales at or above $1.2 million in Tampines, four were between $1.2 million and $1.21 million. This sale and the June sale are the ones above that band.淡滨尼最近几宗至少120万新元的行政公寓里，有四宗在120万至121万新元之间。高出这个区间的，是这一宗和6月那一宗。
  • At 1,528 square feet it was the smallest of the seven high sales being compared, and the second-highest price.以1528平方英尺计算，它是被比较的七宗高价交易里面积最小的，价格却是第二高。
