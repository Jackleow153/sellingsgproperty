Key points要点

  • BTO demand is usually uneven across locations预购组屋的需求通常因地点而差别很大
  • A BTO launch does not by itself set nearby resale prices一次预购组屋开卖，本身不会定下附近的转售价
  • The piece asks which locations will draw demand and does not name the towns or the projects.文章问哪些地点会吸引需求，但没有点名市镇或项目。
  • A short wait and a simple daily commute are the kind of location it says will draw more applications.等候较短、每天通勤简单的地点，是它说会吸引更多申请的那一类。
