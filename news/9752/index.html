HDB resale prices have fallen for three straight quarters. So should you make your move now, or wait?组屋转售价已连跌三个季度，现在买还是再等？

2026/10/142026年10月14日Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Headline claim: HDB resale prices fell for three straight quarters标题的说法：组屋转售价连跌三个季度
  • The headline does not give the size of each quarterly move标题没有给出每一季的跌幅
  • It poses a choice: buy now or wait它提出一个选择：现在买，还是再等
  • The index is not the price of one flat指数不是一套房子的价钱

What this means这对你意味着什么

The headline says HDB resale prices have fallen for three straight quarters, and asks whether you should buy or wait. Three quarters of declines is the claim. The headline does not say how much each quarter fell. A long slide of tiny moves is not the same as a sharp drop.标题说，HDB转售价已经连跌三个季度，并问你该现在买还是再等。连跌三个季度是这则标题的说法。它没有写出每一季跌了多少。一连串很小的下跌，和一次明显的下跌，不是同一件事。

Waiting has a cost. Rent, a wedding, a child or a lease running down does not pause because the index might fall a bit more. Buying also has a cost: the flat you pick, at today’s price, with today’s loan. The right question is which cost you can carry, not which quarter looks clever.等待是有成本的。租金、结婚、孩子，或屋契一天天变短，不会因为指数也许还会再跌一点就暂停。买也有成本：你选的那套房子、今天的价钱、今天的贷款。该问的是你承担得起哪一种成本，不是哪一个季度看起来更聪明。

Sellers who hold on for a rebound should know the index has already had three softer quarters. That does not mean the fourth must fall, and it does not mean your block has fallen with the index. Buyers who wait should name a price and a date. “Wait” without either is just delay.想撑到反弹的卖家，要知道指数已经软了三个季度。这不代表第四季一定再跌，也不代表你的座楼跟着指数跌了。想等的买家应该写出一个价钱和一日期。“再等等”如果两样都没有，就只是拖延。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should HDB buyers wait for a fourth down quarter?组屋买家该不该再等第四个下跌的季度？ Do not wait for a bottom you cannot date. If the flat and the loan work now, three small quarterly moves may not be worth another year of rent.不要等一个你写不出日期的底部。如果房子和贷款现在就成立，三个很小的季度变动，未必值得再付一年租金。
  • Have all flats fallen because the index has?指数跌了，是不是所有房子都跌了？ Check your town, not only the national line. A flat can hold its price while the index inches down.看你的市镇，不要只看全岛那一条线。指数微跌时，一套房子仍可能守住价钱。
  • What should a seller do after three softer quarters?连软三个季度之后，卖家该怎么做？ Sellers should look at offers in hand. Holding out is reasonable only if you can name what you are waiting for, and you can afford the wait.卖家应该看手上的出价。继续等，只有在你说得出在等什么，而且等得起的时候，才算合理。
