Two Tiong Bahru HDB blocks to get HIP upgrade after re-poll中峇鲁两个组屋座重投后将进行家居改进计划（HIP）

2026/10/292026年10月29日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • Where: two HDB blocks in Tiong Bahru地点：中峇鲁两个组屋座
  • What was decided: the Home Improvement Programme will go ahead after a fresh poll决定：重新投票后，家居改进计划（HIP）将进行
  • What HIP usually covers: block repairs such as spalling concrete, plus some optional works inside the flat家居改进计划通常包括：组屋维修，例如混凝土剥落，以及屋内一些可选择的工程
  • Who pays: standard works are supported; optional items are paid by the owner谁付钱：标准工程有支持；可选择的项目由业主付费
  • The fresh poll is what put the works back on for these two blocks. The works do not add years to the lease.重新投票之后，这两个组屋的工程才继续。工程不会把屋契的年数加回去。
