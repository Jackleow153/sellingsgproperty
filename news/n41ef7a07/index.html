We Review The February 2027 BTO Launch Sites (Bishan, Tampines, Toa Payoh & Tengah)2027年2月预购组屋约4300套，分布在碧山、大巴窑、淡滨尼和登加

2026/11/122026年11月12日

Source: Stacked Homes来源：Stacked Homes

Key points要点

  • Stacked Homes' review of the February 2027 BTO exercise, dated 12 November, says it is the first of three 2027 launches, with about 4,300 flats in five projects in Bishan, Toa Payoh, Tampines and Tengah. The November exercise had 7,964 flats across seven projects.Stacked Homes 在11月12日评论2027年2月预购组屋，称这是2027年三次推出里的第一次，约4300套，五个项目，分布在碧山、大巴窑、淡滨尼和登加。11月那一轮是七个项目、7964套。
  • The same review puts the February 2026 launch at 4,692 flats and the June 2026 launch at 6,952. February 2027 also offers about 5,000 Sale of Balance Flats. November had none.同一篇评论把2026年2月写成4692套，2026年6月写成6952套。2027年2月另外约有5000套剩余组屋。11月没有剩余组屋。
  • About half of the February flats are expected to be Plus or Prime, in Bishan and Toa Payoh. The rest are expected to be Standard, in two Tampines projects and one in Tengah. Christine Sun, chief researcher and strategist at Realion (OrangeTee & ETC), said most of the sites look attractive, and that five-room flats are scarce in mature Tampines.2月约一半单位预计是 Plus 或 Prime，位于碧山和大巴窑。其余预计是 Standard，为淡滨尼两个项目和登加一个项目。Realion（OrangeTee & ETC）首席研究员兼策略师 Christine Sun 说，多数地段有吸引力，成熟市镇淡滨尼的五房较少。
  • The Sin Ming site in Bishan is about 1,070 flats: 470 four-room, 80 three-room and 520 two-room Flexi. Lee Sze Teck, senior director of data and analytics at Huttons Asia, said completion is within the next three years. The site is not beside an MRT. The nearest stations named are Upper Thomson on the Thomson-East Coast Line and Marymount on the Circle Line. Amenities named in the review include Catholic High, Ai Tong, Junction 8, Bishan Park and Thomson Plaza.碧山 Sin Ming 地段约1070套：四房470套、三房80套、二房灵活户型520套。博纳亚洲数据与分析高级总监 Lee Sze Teck 说，未来三年内完工。地段不在地铁站旁。点名的最近车站是汤申-东海岸线的 Upper Thomson，以及环线的 Marymount。评论点名的设施包括公教中学、爱同学校、Junction 8、碧山公园和 Thomson Plaza。
  • The review cited earlier Bishan exercises. Sin Ming Residences, from the December 2023 BTO, had a four-room first-timer rate above 3.0. Bishan Terraces in October 2025 and Lakeview Cascadia in June 2026 were Prime, and Lee said Bishan application rates have been above 3.评论引用了较早的碧山项目。2023年12月预购组屋的 Sin Ming Residences，四房首次申请人比例高于3.0。2025年10月的 Bishan Terraces 和2026年6月的 Lakeview Cascadia 是 Prime。Lee 说碧山的申请率一直高于3。
  • Huttons put usual Sin Ming four-room resale prices from $530,000. Christine Sun said a Plus classification is possible, so any subsidy recovery is not yet known. She also noted industrial buildings, heavy-vehicle parking and a busy Marymount Road nearby.博纳把新民一带四房转售的常见价格写成53万新元起。Christine Sun 说有可能列为 Plus，因此补贴回收尚未知道。她也提到附近有工业建筑、重型车辆停车场，以及繁忙的 Marymount Road。
  • The Tampines site along Simei and Upper Changi Road is 540 units, from two-room Flexi to five-room, including about 120 five-room flats. Both analysts expected Standard, with a five-year occupation period and no subsidy recovery, as with Simei Symphony in July 2025, whose four-room rate was 8.9. Stacked placed the site at Upper Changi MRT on the Downtown Line, about 500 m from Expo.淡滨尼沿 Simei 和 Upper Changi Road 的地段是540个单位，从二房灵活户型到五房，其中约120套五房。两位分析师都预计是 Standard，最低居住年限5年，没有补贴回收，和2025年7月的 Simei Symphony 一样，该项目四房申请率是8.9。Stacked 把地段放在滨海市区线 Upper Changi 地铁站，距离 Expo 约500米。
  • Price talk in the review starts four-room flats at that Simei site from perhaps $550,000. Lee said unmet demand could put the four-room rate near 5 times, and that this is the only mature-town five-room supply in the round. Sun said Tampines has had three other BTO launches since July 2025, so overall demand may be softer. Those prices and rates are the analysts' guesses, not an HDB price list.评论里的价格讨论把该 Simei 地段的四房写成也许55万新元起。Lee 说未满足的需求可能让四房申请率接近5倍，而且这是本轮唯一的成熟市镇五房供应。Sun 说淡滨尼自2025年7月以来已有另外三次预购组屋，整体需求可能较温和。这些价格和申请率是分析师的估计，不是建屋局价目。
  • Tampines Street 11 is described as 240 Community Care Apartments plus 90 four-room flats, expected to be Standard, between Tampines MRT and Simei MRT. The four-room talk starts around $520,000, and a Community Care Apartment price was not out. Stacked called it the first such project in Tampines. It said the previous batch, this month's Toa Payoh Hillside, was on a 15-year lease from $88,000. A 99.co note on the November launch describes that same lease as 40 years, so the term is not agreed.淡滨尼11街被写成240套乐龄安居花苑，另加90套四房，预计是 Standard，位于 Tampines 地铁站和 Simei 地铁站之间。四房的讨论价约52万新元起，乐龄安居花苑的价格尚未公布。Stacked 称这是淡滨尼第一个这类项目。它说上一批是本月的 Toa Payoh Hillside，15年地契，8万8000新元起。99.co 关于11月推出的说明把同一批地契写成40年，所以年期并未一致。
  • Mount Pleasant in Toa Payoh sits beside the upcoming Mount Pleasant MRT on the Thomson-East Coast Line. Stacked cited July 2025 Toa Payoh Ascent, with a four-room rate of 8.5, and February 2026 Kim Keat Crest, with 787 four-room flats and 1,065 applications, a rate of 1.4. This month's Toa Payoh Hillside, near Caldecott, had a four-room rate of almost 6 times and is Prime with 11% subsidy recovery. Sun said Mount Pleasant is further west of Toa Payoh Central and beside an expressway.大巴窑 Mount Pleasant 位于汤申-东海岸线即将开通的 Mount Pleasant 地铁站旁。Stacked 引用2025年7月的 Toa Payoh Ascent，四房申请率8.5，以及2026年2月的 Kim Keat Crest，787套四房、1065份申请，申请率1.4。本月靠近 Caldecott 的 Toa Payoh Hillside，四房申请率将近6倍，是 Prime，补贴回收11%。Sun 说 Mount Pleasant 在大巴窑中心更西，而且靠着高速公路。
  • Brickland in Tengah is this round's largest site, about 30% of the supply, near Tengah Boulevard and Brickland Road. Stacked cited November's Plantation Dew, with a four-room first-timer rate over 5 and a five-room first-timer rate of 3.8. It put Brickland about 1 km from the ACS campus after the school moves, next to the upcoming Brickland MRT. The four-room talk starts from perhaps $410,000, and the application rate was put at maybe 1 to 2 times. Those are estimates in the review, not HDB prices.登加 Brickland 是本轮最大的地段，约占供应量的30%，靠近 Tengah Boulevard 和 Brickland Road。Stacked 引用11月的 Plantation Dew，四房首次申请人比例超过5，五房首次申请人比例3.8。它把 Brickland 写成距离搬迁后的英华校园约1公里，旁边是即将开通的 Brickland 地铁站。四房的讨论价也许41万新元起，申请率也许1到2倍。这些是评论里的估计，不是建屋局价格。

What this means if you're upgrading如果你打算升级换房

A smaller BTO supply in February 2027 can keep demand for resale flats firm. If you plan to sell your flat to upgrade, that supports your selling price, but price it off recent transactions, not off the headline.2027年2月的预购组屋供应较少，可能让转售组屋的需求保持稳定。如果你打算卖掉组屋升级，这对你的售价有利，但仍要以最近的成交价来定价，而不是看标题。
