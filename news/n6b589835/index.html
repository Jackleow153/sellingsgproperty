KSH’s 1H2027 profit rises 23% on gains from Singapore condo JVsKSH 2027财年上半年净利增23.3%，新加坡公寓合资项目转盈

2026/11/152026年11月15日

Source: EdgeProp Singapore来源：EdgeProp

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • KSH Holdings reported earnings of $6.5 million for the six months to 30 September 2026. The company called that 23.3% higher than a year earlier, and the results announcement rounded the year-earlier profit to $5.3 million. EdgeProp's headline rounded the rise to 23%.KSH Holdings 报告截至2026年9月30日的六个月盈利为650万新元。公司称这比一年前高23.3%，业绩公告把一年前的盈利四舍五入为530万新元。EdgeProp 的标题把涨幅写成23%。
  • Group revenue was $76.8 million, 21.7% higher than the $63.1 million of a year earlier. Project revenue was $75.0 million, up 22.8% from $61.1 million. Rental income was $1.8 million, down from $2.1 million. Singapore Business Review reports the same revenue, project-revenue and rental figures.集团收入7680万新元，比一年前的6310万新元高21.7%。项目收入7500万新元，比6110万新元高22.8%。租金收入180万新元，低于一年前的210万新元。《新加坡商业评论》报道了同一组收入、项目收入和租金数字。
  • EdgeProp said the cost of construction rose by $15.5 million to $63.2 million, while construction earnings fell to $4.9 million from $7.2 million. The company release confirms the $15.5 million cost increase, from $47.7 million to $63.2 million.EdgeProp 称建筑成本增加1550万新元，至6320万新元，建筑业务盈利则从720万新元降到490万新元。公司文告确认建筑成本增加1550万新元，从4770万新元增至6320万新元。
  • The share of associates and joint ventures turned to a $3.7 million profit, from a $1.8 million loss a year earlier. EdgeProp said the property-development part of that swing was a $3.2 million profit, against a $3.7 million loss.联营和合资公司的应占业绩转为盈利370万新元，一年前是亏损180万新元。EdgeProp 称其中房地产开发部分是盈利320万新元，一年前则亏损370万新元。
  • EdgeProp read URA Realis caveats as at 15 November for four Singapore stakes that are launched and under construction. The Arcady at Boon Keng is freehold, 172 units, and KSH's 49% stake. It was 69% sold at an average $2,607 psf.EdgeProp 引用截至11月15日的 URA Realis 成交，列出四个已开盘、仍在建造的新加坡权益。The Arcady at Boon Keng 是永久地契，172个单位，KSH 占49%。已售69%，平均每平方英尺2607新元。
  • Sora, on Yuan Ching Road, is a 440-unit 99-year project. KSH's stake is 20%. EdgeProp put it at 55% sold and an average $2,243 psf.位于 Yuan Ching Road 的 Sora 是440个单位的99年地契项目。KSH 占20%。EdgeProp 称已售55%，平均每平方英尺2243新元。
  • Bagnall Haus is freehold, with 113 units, and KSH's stake is 12%. EdgeProp put it at 97% sold and an average $2,504 psf.Bagnall Haus 是永久地契，113个单位，KSH 占12%。EdgeProp 称已售97%，平均每平方英尺2504新元。
  • The Collective at One Sophia has a 367-unit residential component on a 99-year lease. KSH's stake is 10%. EdgeProp put that component at 26% sold and an average $2,767 psf.The Collective at One Sophia 的住宅部分有367个单位，99年地契。KSH 占10%。EdgeProp 称这部分已售26%，平均每平方英尺2767新元。
  • Sold units in those joint ventures still had more than $148 million of unrecognised revenue as at 30 September. The construction order book was about $887 million, with work running through FY2030. The company release states the same order book.这些合资项目已售单位在9月30日仍有超过1亿4800万新元未确认收入。建筑订单约8亿8700万新元，工程排到2030财年。公司文告写了同一订单规模。
  • The results put cash and fixed deposits at $142.5 million and net asset value at 50.89 cents a share. Choo Chee Onn is executive chairman and managing director.业绩显示现金和定期存款1亿4250万新元，每股资产净值50.89分。Choo Chee Onn 是执行主席兼董事总经理。
  • EdgeProp also noted Gate+ at Tukang Innovation Drive, a 10% stake launched in May. It said 46% of the 265 factory units were sold at an average $605 psf, on a 33-year lease from August 2025. Tukang Plot B, at 30%, and Thomson Gem at Upper Thomson, at 25%, had not launched.EdgeProp 还提到 Tukang Innovation Drive 的 Gate+，权益10%，5月开盘。265个厂房单位已售46%，平均每平方英尺605新元，地契从2025年8月起为33年。权益30%的 Tukang Plot B，以及 Upper Thomson、权益25%的 Thomson Gem，尚未开盘。
