New mall, parks and plans for a school under Toa Payoh West masterplan

2026/11/01

Key facts

  • Plan: more than 6,000 homes in Toa Payoh West over about 10 years, mostly HDB
  • First BTO: Toa Payoh Hillside, about 1,600 flats, next to Caldecott MRT, November 2026
  • Mix: 590 two-room Flexi, 580 four-room, 260 Community Care Apartments, about 230 rental flats
  • Blocks: five, from 31 to 43 storeys
  • Also planned: Toa Payoh’s first mall and a 1.2-hectare wellness park. A school is being studied, not confirmed
  • Community Care Apartments: first in Toa Payoh; application age falls from 65 to 55 from this exercise

What this means

HDB’s plan for Toa Payoh West adds more than 6,000 homes over about ten years, mostly public housing, with some private homes. The idea is a second centre for the town, around Caldecott MRT, alongside Toa Payoh Central. The plans include Toa Payoh’s first mall and a 1.2-hectare wellness park. A school is being studied. It has not been confirmed.

The first project you can actually apply for is Toa Payoh Hillside, in the November 2026 BTO exercise. It sits next to Caldecott MRT and has about 1,600 flats in five blocks of 31 to 43 storeys. The reported mix is 590 two-room Flexi flats, 580 four-room flats, 260 Community Care Apartments and about 230 public rental flats. These are the first Community Care Apartments in Toa Payoh. From this exercise, the age to apply for them drops from 65 to 55.

Shops, the park and a possible school can make the area easier to live in. They also take years. If you are buying nearby now, separate what is already built from what is still a plan. If you are selling, you can mention the masterplan. Buyers will still discount anything that is not open.

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers

  • Does being next to Caldecott MRT make a Toa Payoh home worth more today? Look at the walk. “Near Caldecott” only helps an HDB buyer if the station and the future mall are a trip you would make on a normal day.
  • What can you actually apply for in November, and what is still a plan? If you need a school or the mall soon, ask when they are due. The November flats are real. The school is still a study, and the mall is not open.
  • How should sellers talk about the Toa Payoh West masterplan? Resale sellers in Toa Payoh should describe the masterplan plainly. Overstating what is already built is an easy way to lose a buyer at the viewing.
