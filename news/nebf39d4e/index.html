First BTO exercise under raised income ceiling set to draw highest number of applications in two years收入顶限提高后的首轮预购组屋已有35603人申请

2026/11/112026年11月11日

Source: CNA来源：CNA

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • By 5pm on Wednesday, 35,603 people had applied for 7,964 flats in the November exercise, nearly 4.5 applicants per flat. Chee Hong Tat said this is the first exercise since the income ceiling rose, and the busiest since October 2024.截至周三下午5时，十一月这一轮有35603人申请7964个预购组屋单位，大约每单位4.5人。徐芳达说，这是收入顶限提高后的第一次发售，也是自2024年十月以来申请人数最多的一次。
  • October 2024 had 35,678 applicants for 8,573 flats. The median rate for first-timer families taking a three-room or larger flat is 2.6, against 1.4 in June and 0.9 in February. First-timer singles applying for 2-room Flexi flats have a median rate of 8.2, against 7.8 in June and 7.3 in February.2024年十月有35678人申请8573个单位。首次购房家庭申请三房及以上的中位申请率是2.6，六月为1.4，二月为0.9。首次购房单身申请2房 Flexi 的中位申请率是8.2，六月为7.8，二月为7.3。
  • In August the family income ceiling moved from S$14,000 to S$16,000, and the singles ceiling from S$7,000 to S$8,000. Chee Hong Tat called the stronger interest expected, with demand differing by project.八月起，家庭月入顶限从1万4000新元提高到1万6000新元，单身顶限从7000新元提高到8000新元。徐芳达说申请增加并不意外，各项目的需求并不相同。
  • Applications opened on Nov 4 and close at 11.59pm on Nov 11. HDB is due to publish the final count at 2pm on Thursday.申请从11月4日开始，到11月11日11.59pm截止。建屋发展局将在周四下午2时公布最终人数。
  • Toa Payoh Hillside, the only Prime project, drew 5,980 applicants for 584 4-room flats. The subsidy clawback is 11%. Lee Sze Teck notes it sits next to Caldecott MRT, with the Toa Payoh Integrated Development, including sports facilities, a polyclinic, a library and a town park, due in 2030. Some 4-room flats have a balcony, and higher floors look toward MacRitchie Reservoir.Toa Payoh Hillside 是本轮唯一的 Prime 项目，有5980人申请584个4房单位。补贴回扣率是11%。Lee Sze Teck 指出项目毗邻 Caldecott 地铁站，Toa Payoh Integrated Development 计划在2030年开放，里面有体育设施、诊疗所、图书馆和市镇公园。部分4房有阳台，较高楼层可望向 MacRitchie Reservoir。
  • A 4-room flat of 90 to 93 sq m is indicated at S$558,000 to S$832,000 before grants. Eugene Lim says nearby resale flats sold for S$820,000 to S$1.2 million.90至93平方米的4房，未计津贴的指示价是55万8000新元至83万2000新元。Eugene Lim 说附近转售价在82万新元至120万新元。
  • Bayshore Sails and Bayshore Oasis, both Plus, drew 5,213 applications for 1,165 4-room flats together, a rate of about 4.5. Christine Sun ties that to the MRT, East Coast Park, and sea views from some units.Plus 项目 Bayshore Sails 和 Bayshore Oasis 的1165个4房一共收到5213份申请，申请率约4.5。Christine Sun 把原因放在靠近地铁、East Coast Park，以及部分单位能看到海。
  • Merpati Meadow, another Plus project, has a 4-room first-timer family rate of 2.3 and a 2-room Flexi first-timer singles rate of 8.4. Plantation Dew, a Standard project in Tengah, has a first-timer singles rate of 18.2.另一个 Plus 项目 Merpati Meadow，4房首次购房家庭的申请率是2.3，2房 Flexi 首次购房单身的申请率是8.4。Tengah 的 Standard 项目 Plantation Dew，首次购房单身的申请率是18.2。
  • A Type 1 2-room Flexi flat at Plantation Dew is indicated at S$44,000 to S$166,000, depending on the lease. Sembawang Anchor is the only shorter-wait project, under three years, after almost 4,000 flats were launched there in February and June combined, and it is about a 10-minute bus ride from Sembawang MRT.Plantation Dew 的 Type 1 2房 Flexi，按地契年期，指示价为4万4000新元至16万6000新元。Sembawang Anchor 是本轮唯一等待时间较短的项目，短于三年，而该区在二月和六月已合计推出近4000个单位，到 Sembawang 地铁站大约要坐10分钟巴士。
  • Chencharu Acres has a first-timer singles rate of 8.9 for 2-room Flexi flats, and rates of 2.3, 2.6 and 2.7 for three-room, 4-room and 5-room flats.Chencharu Acres 的2房 Flexi，首次购房单身申请率是8.9；三房、4房和5房的申请率分别是2.3、2.6和2.7。
  • The Community Care Apartments at Toa Payoh Hillside drew 509 applicants for 256 flats, a rate of 2. In July the minimum age was lowered from 65 to 55, and the basic service package fee was also lowered.Toa Payoh Hillside 的社区护理公寓有509人申请256个单位，申请率为2。七月起，最低年龄从65岁降到55岁，基本服务配套的费用也降低了。
