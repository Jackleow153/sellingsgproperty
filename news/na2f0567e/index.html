IKEA Singapore and City Energy Partner on Smarter Home Design for BTO and HDB OwnersIKEA 新加坡 City Energy Partner Smarter 住宅 Design 预购组屋组屋 Owners

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: SavePoint Gaming来源：SavePoint Gaming

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • IKEA Singapore has added Life by City Energy, the home-solutions arm of the town-gas provider, to its Home Design Service.新加坡宜家把城市能源旗下的家居方案品牌Life by City Energy，加进了它的家居设计服务。
  • The partnership was announced on 3 November 2026 and is available at IKEA Alexandra and IKEA Tampines.合作在2026年11月3日宣布，可在宜家亚历山大和宜家淡滨尼使用。
  • It is the first partner added to the end-to-end service, which has helped 300 homeowners since a soft launch in 2025.这是这项一站式服务加入的第一个伙伴。服务从2025年试运行以来，已经协助300个家庭。
  • Someone furnishing a new BTO flat, renovating, or redoing a kitchen can plan gas hobs, cooker hoods, gas water heaters and gas dryers together with IKEA’s layouts, cabinets, storage and lighting.装修新的预购组屋、翻新房子或重做厨房的人，可以把煤气炉、抽油烟机、煤气热水器和煤气干衣机，和宜家的布局、橱柜、收纳和灯具一起规划。
  • The IKEA specialist stays the single contact. If gas appliances are chosen, City Energy is brought in for appliance size, placement and gas piping before work starts, then for delivery, cabinets, gas works and activation.宜家的设计专员仍然是唯一联系人。如果选了煤气电器，会在动工前请城市能源处理电器尺寸、摆放和煤气管道，之后再协调送货、橱柜、煤气工程和开通。
  • City Energy says town gas is available in all new HDB flats. Its 2026 data says about half of HDB homes that use town gas also use a gas water heater.城市能源说，所有新组屋都有城市煤气。它2026年的数据显示，使用城市煤气的组屋家庭里，大约一半也使用煤气热水器。
  • The range includes internet-connected gas appliances, aimed at using less energy and giving the household more control.产品包括可联网的煤气电器，目的是更省电、也让住户更方便控制。
