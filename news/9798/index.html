Key facts重点

  • The practical issue: people who run a condo should say when they have a personal or business link to a decision实际问题：管理公寓的人，若与某项决定有私人或商业关系，应该说明
  • Why a buyer cares: fees, upcoming works and disputes can change the real cost of a condo买家为什么在意：月费、即将进行的工程和纠纷，会改变公寓的真实成本

What this means这对你意味着什么

A former condominium chairman has lost a defamation case connected to links that were not disclosed. The court details belong to that case. The plain lesson for anyone buying or selling a condo in Singapore is about openness.一名前共管公寓主席，在一宗与未披露利益关系有关的诽谤案中败诉。案件细节属于那宗诉讼。对在新加坡买卖公寓（condo）的人来说，白话的教训是：事情要说在明处。

People who help run a condo are expected to say so when they have a personal or business link that could affect a decision, such as who the estate hires. Hiding that link can become a legal fight, not just an argument in the chat group.参与管理公寓的人，如果有可能影响决定的私人或商业关系，例如由谁承包，就应该说出来。把关系藏起来，可能变成法律纠纷，而不只是聊天群里的争执。

For a buyer, how the estate is run can matter as much as the renovation inside the unit. Disputes show up later as monthly fees, delayed repairs, or a tense sale. For a seller, those are the questions a careful buyer will ask.对买家来说，公寓怎么管理，可能和单位里的装修一样重要。纠纷日后会变成月费、拖延的维修，或一宗紧张的交易。对卖家来说，这些正是谨慎的买家会问的问题。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • What should you ask before buying a condo?买公寓之前该问什么？ Before you buy a condo, ask about monthly fees, upcoming works and any dispute you should know about. A nice unit in a poorly run estate can become expensive.买公寓之前，问清月费、即将进行的工程，以及任何你应该知道的纠纷。单位好看，但管理差的公寓，日后可能变得昂贵。
  • What must a condo council member disclose?公寓理事会成员必须披露什么？ If you sit on your estate’s council, say so when you have a link to a contractor or a decision. Keeping it quiet can become a legal problem.如果你担任管理理事会成员，和承包商或某项决定有关系时要说出来。瞒着不说，可能变成法律问题。
  • What should sellers be ready to explain?卖家该准备解释什么？ Sellers should be ready for questions about unpaid fees and big repair bills. A clear answer is better than a surprise after the option is signed.卖家应该准备好回答关于未付费用和大笔维修账单的问题。清楚的回答，好过购屋权发出后才出现意外。
