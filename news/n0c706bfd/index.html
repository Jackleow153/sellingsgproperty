BTO applications exceed 35,000, set to hit two-year high本轮预购组屋截至下午5时收到35603份申请

2026/11/112026年11月11日

Source: 联合早报来源：联合早报

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • Lianhe Zaobao reported 35,603 applications by 5pm on Wednesday 11 November for 7,964 flats in seven projects. Applications closed at 11.59pm. The paper expected the highest total since October 2024.《联合早报》报道，11月11日星期三下午5时，七个项目、7964个单位收到35603份申请。申请在晚上11时59分截止。早报预计这将是2024年10月以来最高的总数。
  • Chee Hong Tat said on Facebook that the median first-timer family rate for a three-room or larger flat was 2.6, against 1.4 in June. The income ceiling rose in August from $14,000 to $16,000 for families, and from $7,000 to $8,000 for singles. The new ceiling applies to this exercise.徐芳达在 Facebook 上说，三房或更大单位的首次申请家庭中位数是2.6，6月是1.4。收入顶限在8月提高，家庭从1万4000新元提到1万6000新元，单身从7000新元提到8000新元。新顶限适用于这一轮。
  • At Toa Payoh Hillside, a Prime project, the four-room first-timer rate was 6.7. Plantation Dew, a Standard project, was 5.3 for four-room flats. At Sembawang Anchor, three-room to five-room rates were all at or below 1.0.Prime 项目 Toa Payoh Hillside 的四房首次申请人比例是6.7。Standard 项目 Plantation Dew 的四房是5.3。Sembawang Anchor 从三房到五房的比例都在1.0或以下。
  • Two-room Flexi flats numbered 2,899, more than one third of the launch. The median rate for first-timer singles was 8.2, against 7.8 in June. At Plantation Dew the two-room Flexi rate for singles was 18.2.二房灵活户型有2899套，超过这一轮的三分之一。首次申请单身人士的中位数是8.2，6月是7.8。Plantation Dew 的二房灵活户型，单身人士比例是18.2。
  • Toa Payoh Hillside also has 256 Community Care Apartments, the first in Toa Payoh, with an application rate of 2.0. Earlier batches in Punggol and Geylang were 0.7 and 0.9. A photo caption in the report said Toa Payoh Hillside has 1,432 units.Toa Payoh Hillside 还有256套乐龄安居花苑，是大巴窑的第一批，申请率2.0。较早的榜鹅和芽笼分别是0.7和0.9。报道的图片说明写 Toa Payoh Hillside 共1432个单位。
  • Eugene Lim, key executive officer at ERA, said four-room flats there are near CHIJ Primary (Toa Payoh) and Marymount Convent Girls' School, a walk from Caldecott, and priced below nearby resale flats.ERA 关键执行官 Eugene Lim 说，那里的四房靠近 CHIJ Primary (Toa Payoh) 和 Marymount Convent Girls' School，步行可达 Caldecott，价格低于附近转售组屋。
  • Christine Sun of Realion said a low rate in Sembawang was expected, because February and June had already launched nearly 4,000 flats there.Realion 的 Christine Sun 说，三巴旺申请率低是预期之中，因为2月和6月已经在那里推出近4000个单位。
  • The report also named Merpati Meadow, Bayshore Sails, Bayshore Oasis and Chencharu Acres as projects where both the singles' two-room rate and the families' three-room-or-larger rate were above 2.0.报道还点名 Merpati Meadow、Bayshore Sails、Bayshore Oasis 和 Chencharu Acres。这些项目的单身二房比例，以及家庭三房或更大单位的比例，都高于2.0。
  • Lim said there had been no new Tengah BTO since Plantation Edge I and II in October 2023, and that the wait at Plantation Dew is about 3 years and 2 months.Lim 说，自2023年10月的 Plantation Edge I 和 II 之后，登加没有新的预购组屋。Plantation Dew 的等候时间约3年2个月。
  • The report said HDB has launched 23,928 new flats this year. Next February it expects about 4,300 flats in Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh, plus about 5,000 balance flats.报道称建屋局今年已推出23928个新单位。明年2月预计在碧山、淡滨尼、登加和大巴窑推出约4300套，另加约5000套剩余单位。

What this means if you're upgrading如果你打算升级换房

BTO competition does not affect what you can borrow for a condo. If you are a second-timer weighing a new flat against a condo, note that application rates differ a lot by project, so look at the project, not the overall figure.预购组屋的竞争不影响你买公寓能借多少。如果你是第二次购屋者，在新组屋和公寓之间考虑，要注意各项目的申请率差别很大，所以看具体项目，而不是整体数字。
