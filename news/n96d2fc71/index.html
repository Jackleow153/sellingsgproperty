Nov 2026 BTO Launched, Locations, Projects, Prices, Classifications & More2026年11月预购组屋推出7964套，七个项目等候时间都短于四年

2026/11/042026年11月4日

Source: 99.co来源：99.co

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • 99.co's November 2026 BTO note, last updated on 4 November, says HDB launched 7,964 flats in seven projects across Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun. Three are Standard, three are Plus, and one is Prime.99.co 关于2026年11月预购组屋的说明，最后更新于11月4日，称建屋局推出7964套，七个项目，分布在勿洛、芽笼、三巴旺、登加、大巴窑和义顺。三个是 Standard，三个是 Plus，一个是 Prime。
  • Every waiting time in that note is under four years. Sembawang Anchor is the shortest, at 2 years and 10 months. Bayshore Oasis and Plantation Dew are about 3 years and 2 months.说明里的等候时间都短于四年。Sembawang Anchor 最短，2年10个月。Bayshore Oasis 和 Plantation Dew 约3年2个月。
  • Shorter-waiting-time flats number 1,310, or 16.4% of the launch, and all of them are at Sembawang Anchor. Two-room Flexi flats are more than one third of the launch and appear in all seven projects.较短等候时间的单位有1310套，占这一轮的16.4%，全部在 Sembawang Anchor。二房灵活户型超过三分之一，七个项目都有。
  • This is the first exercise under the higher income ceilings of $16,000 for families and $8,000 for singles.这是收入顶限提高后的第一次推出：家庭1万6000新元，单身8000新元。
  • Before grants, the note starts five-room prices at $479,000 in Sembawang, $484,000 in Yishun and $494,000 in Tengah. Plantation Dew's 3Gen flats start at $551,000 before grants. White flats are offered there for three-room, four-room and five-room, and the Tengah section says this is the first time HDB has offered White for a five-room flat.补助前，说明里的五房起价是三巴旺47万9000新元、义顺48万4000新元、登加49万4000新元。Plantation Dew 的三代同堂组屋补助前从55万1000新元起。那里的三房、四房和五房提供 White 单位。登加这一节称，这是建屋局第一次为五房提供 White。
  • The project counts in the note add up to 7,964: Plantation Dew 710, Chencharu Acres 1,582, Bayshore Oasis 855, Merpati Meadow 438, Bayshore Sails 1,637, Sembawang Anchor 1,310 and Toa Payoh Hillside 1,432.说明里的项目数量加起来是7964：Plantation Dew 710、Chencharu Acres 1582、Bayshore Oasis 855、Merpati Meadow 438、Bayshore Sails 1637、Sembawang Anchor 1310、Toa Payoh Hillside 1432。
  • Subsidy recovery on the Plus projects is 9% at both Bayshore projects and 8% at Merpati Meadow. Toa Payoh Hillside is the only Prime project, at 11%. It has 256 Community Care Apartments. 99.co describes that lease as 40 years. Stacked Homes' February preview called the same batch a 15-year lease from $88,000, so the term is not agreed. Of the 1,432 flats, 1,176 are two-room Flexi and four-room.Plus 项目的补贴回收，两个 Bayshore 项目是9%，Merpati Meadow 是8%。Toa Payoh Hillside 是唯一的 Prime，11%。它有256套乐龄安居花苑。99.co 把地契写成40年。Stacked Homes 的2月预告把同一批写成15年地契、8万8000新元起，所以年期并未一致。1432个单位里，1176个是二房灵活户型和四房。
  • The note says the Community Care Apartment price includes senior fittings and excludes the Basic Service Package. Figures shown after the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant are illustrations, not the selling price. Applications ran from 4 to 11 November 2026. The HDB loan limit in the note is 75% of value.说明称乐龄安居花苑的价格包含乐龄配件，不含基本服务配套。显示在额外公积金住房补助之后的数字是示例，不是售价。申请期是2026年11月4日至11日。说明里的建屋局贷款上限是房价的75%。
  • Waiting times in the note are 3 years and 8 months at Bayshore Sails, 3 years and 2 months at Bayshore Oasis, 3 years and 3 months at Merpati Meadow, 2 years and 10 months at Sembawang Anchor, 3 years and 2 months at Plantation Dew, 3 years and 9 months at Toa Payoh Hillside, and from 3 years and 3 months to 3 years and 5 months at Chencharu Acres.说明里的等候时间：Bayshore Sails 3年8个月，Bayshore Oasis 3年2个月，Merpati Meadow 3年3个月，Sembawang Anchor 2年10个月，Plantation Dew 3年2个月，Toa Payoh Hillside 3年9个月，Chencharu Acres 从3年3个月到3年5个月。
  • The note says HDB has launched 23,928 new flats this year, made up of 19,608 BTO flats and 4,320 Sale of Balance Flats from February.说明称建屋局今年已推出23928个新单位，其中19608套预购组屋，以及2月起的4320套剩余组屋。
