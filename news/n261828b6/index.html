At What Point Does Lease Decay Start Affecting The Value Of Your HDB Flat?What Point Does Lease Decay Start Affecting Value 组屋组屋?

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: DollarsAndSense.sg来源：DollarsAndSense.sg

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • The article explains lease decay: an HDB flat is on a 99-year lease, and its value falls as that lease runs down. As of 2025, 77.2% of resident households live in HDB flats.文章解释的是屋契减值：组屋是99年地契，剩余年限越短，价值越低。截至2025年，77.2%的居民家庭住在组屋。
  • In 2017, when he was National Development Minister, Lawrence Wong said prices come down as leases run down, especially near the end. He was worried about older flats being sold at high prices in the hope of SERS.2017年黄循财还是国家发展部长时说，屋契尤其到了尾段，房价会跟着下降。他当时担心有人把旧组屋卖出高价，指望选择性整体重建计划（SERS）。
  • The Lease Buyback Scheme, started in 2009, lets an owner sell part of the remaining lease to HDB, keep living in the flat, and top up a CPF Retirement Account. HDB may add a bonus of up to $30,000.2009年推出的屋契回购计划，让屋主把一部分剩余屋契卖给建屋局，继续住在屋里，并把所得补进公积金退休户头。建屋局可能再给最多3万新元的红利。
  • About 16,000 households have used the scheme since 2009.2009年以来，大约1万6000个家庭用过这个计划。
  • From 2019 to 2023, the average proceeds plus bonus were about $261,000 for a 5-room or larger flat, $212,000 for a 4-room, $168,000 for a 3-room, $153,000 for a 2-room and $98,000 for a 1-room.2019年到2023年，平均所得加红利大约是：五房或更大261000新元，四房212000新元，三房168000新元，两房153000新元，一房98000新元。
  • HDB’s worked example is a fully paid 4-room flat valued at $450,000 with 65 years left. Owners aged 65 keep at least 30 years and can sell the remaining 35 years. That tail is priced at $190,000 in the example, less than the 30 years they keep, even though the tail is longer.建屋局的例子是一套已还清、估值45万新元、还剩65年屋契的四房。65岁的屋主至少保留30年，可以把尾段的35年卖出。例子里这35年值19万新元，比他们留下的30年更不值钱，尽管尾段更长。
  • The Singapore Land Authority adopted the Leasehold Table, also called Bala’s Table, on 31 July 2000. It prices a leasehold as a percentage of freehold value. The curve falls slowly for about the first 60 years, then more steeply in the last 30.新加坡土地局在2000年7月31日采用租契表，也叫Bala’s Table。它把有年限的地契定成永久地契价值的一个百分比。曲线在大约前60年下降较慢，最后30年下降更快。
  • A 2025 Singapore Management University paper said there was compelling evidence that HDB used Bala’s Table when pricing 2-room Flexi flats and when valuing leases for the Lease Buyback Scheme.新加坡管理大学2025年的一篇论文说，有充分证据显示，建屋局在为两房灵活单位定价，以及为屋契回购计划估价时，用了这张表。
