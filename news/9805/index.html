Why Singapore is exploring build-to-rent新加坡为何在研究建造供出租住房

2026/11/022026年11月2日Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Build-to-rent: homes built to be rented, not sold off at launch建造供出租：住房建来出租，而不是在开盘时卖出
  • Why it is being discussed: demand for a private place before a mortgage讨论原因：人们希望在承担房贷前先有私人住所
  • Who is driving interest: younger Singaporeans who want to live on their own推动兴趣的人：想独立居住的年轻新加坡人
  • Status: being explored, not a project you can book现状：仍在探讨，不是可以预订的项目

What this means这对你意味着什么

Build-to-rent means a block is built and then rented out over many years, instead of being carved up and sold to individual buyers at a new launch. Singapore is looking at it because more people want a private home before they take on a mortgage.建造供出租（build-to-rent）是指一整栋房子建好后，多年来用来出租，而不是在新盘时分割卖给个别买家。新加坡在研究这个做法，是因为更多人希望在承担房贷之前，先有一个属于自己的住处。

The demand described in the reports is practical: younger Singaporeans want their own front door, not a room in someone else’s flat. Privacy and a stable place to live come first. Buying an HDB flat or a condo is a much larger step, and many are not there yet.报道里的需求很实际：年轻新加坡人想要自己的家门，而不是住在别人家里的一个房间。隐私和稳定的住处排在前面。购买HDB组屋或公寓是大得多的一步，很多人还没到那一步。

This is still an idea. It is not a showflat, and it does not change today’s resale prices. The only useful test is whether the rent would leave enough money to keep saving.这仍是一个构想。它不是示范单位，也不会改变今天的转售价格。唯一有用的检验是：这样的租金是否仍让你存得下钱。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • What is build-to-rent in Singapore?新加坡的建造供出租是什么？ Renting can be a sensible bridge if the monthly cost still lets you save for an HDB or condo down payment. Add the rent, your other bills, and what is left.如果每月租金仍让你存得下HDB组屋或公寓的首期，租房可以是合理的过渡。把租金、其他账单，以及剩下的钱加总看看。
  • Should you rush a purchase because of this housing idea?要不要因为这个住房构想而急着买房？ A headline about a new way to rent is not a reason to rush a new launch or a resale purchase. Buy when the monthly loan is comfortable.一则关于新租赁方式的标题，不是急着买新盘或转售单位的理由。每月贷款还得轻松时，再买。
  • What should owners of investment units watch?投资单位的业主该留意什么？ If you are selling or renting out an investment unit, watch whether tenants later prefer purpose-built rental homes. An older condo may need a sharper rent or better condition to compete.如果你在出售或出租投资单位，要留意租户日后会不会更想住专门建造的租赁住房。较旧的公寓可能要用更合适的租金，或更好的屋况，才有竞争力。
