Tampines is S’pore’s most populous planning area, but not its fastest-growing淡滨尼是新加坡人口最多的规划区，但不是增长最快的

2026/10/292026年10月29日

Key facts重点

  • Tampines: the planning area with the most people in Singapore淡滨尼：新加坡人口最多的规划区
  • Not the fastest growing: Punggol and Sengkang added people faster over the past decade不是增长最快：过去十年，榜鹅和盛港的人口增加得更快
  • What a mature town usually offers: shops, schools and transport that are already there成熟市镇通常有什么：已经在的商店、学校和交通
  • What a fast-growing town usually offers: newer homes, and amenities that are still catching up增长快的市镇通常有什么：较新的住家，以及还在赶上的设施

What this means这对你意味着什么

Tampines has more people than any other planning area in Singapore. It is not the area that has been growing the fastest. Over the past decade, Punggol and Sengkang added people at a quicker pace.淡滨尼的人口比新加坡任何一个规划区都多。它不是增长最快的地区。过去十年，榜鹅和盛港的人口增加得更快。

A large, established population usually means the shops, schools and transport are already there. A fast-growing town usually means newer HDB flats, more construction, and amenities that are still catching up.人口多、又已经成熟的市镇，通常商店、学校和交通都已经在。增长快的市镇，通常有较新的HDB组屋、更多工地，以及还在赶上的设施。

Buyers often mix the two up. Bigger is not the same as newer, and faster growth is not the same as more convenient today. The right choice is the one that fits the home in front of you, not the size of the town on a map.买家常常把这两件事混在一起。最大不等于最新，增长快也不等于今天就更方便。合适的选择，是眼前那套房子，而不是地图上市镇的大小。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should you pick Tampines because it has the most people?要不要因为淡滨尼人口最多就选它？ Decide what you want before you compare prices: a town that is already built up, such as Tampines, or a town that is still adding homes, such as Punggol or Sengkang.比较价格之前，先决定你要什么：像淡滨尼这样已经建好的市镇，还是像榜鹅或盛港这样仍在增加住家的市镇。
  • How is a mature town different from a fast-growing one?成熟市镇和增长快的市镇有什么不同？ Do not choose Tampines only because it is the biggest. Check the specific street, the walk to the MRT, and the HDB flat or condo in front of you.不要只因为淡滨尼最大就选它。看具体的街道、走到地铁站的距离，以及眼前的组屋或公寓（condo）。
  • How should sellers describe their town?卖家该如何描述自己的市镇？ Sellers in a mature town can point to what is already there. Sellers in a fast-growing town should be honest about what is finished and what is still a construction site.成熟市镇的卖家可以指出已经在的东西。增长快的市镇的卖家，应该老实说哪些已经完工，哪些仍是工地。
