[2026 Edition] How Much Salary You Need To Earn (After Government Subsidies) To Afford The Different BTO Flat Sizes In Singapore2026年预购组屋：津贴之后不同房型要多少收入

2026/11/112026年11月11日

Source: DollarsAndSense.sg来源：DollarsAndSense.sg

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • The article works out the household income a couple would need for different November 2026 BTO flat types after subsidies, and says the sums are only a guide.文章估算，一对夫妇在政府津贴之后，需要多少家庭月入，才负担得起2026年十一月预购组屋的不同房型，并说明这些数字只供参考。
  • It puts the median monthly household income for a 3- to 5-room resale HDB at $5,872 to $9,712, and says BTO prices sit below resale because of the subsidies.文章把购买3房到5房转售组屋所需的家庭月入中位数写在5872新元到9712新元，并说预购组屋因为补贴而低于转售价。
  • From 2024, BTO launches are classed as Standard, Plus, or Prime by location, connectivity, and amenities, with larger subsidies where the location is more attractive.2024年起，预购组屋按地点、交通和周边设施分为 Standard、Plus 或 Prime。地点越吸引人，补贴越高。
  • The salary rows use the highest starting price, before grants, for each flat type and class in the November 2026 exercise. For 2-room flats the figure is the highest 99-year starting price, and a chosen unit can cost more.薪金例子采用2026年十一月这一轮里，每种房型和分类在津贴前的最高起价。2房用的是99年地契的最高起价，个别单位可能更贵。
  • From 20 August 2024, a first-timer family may get an Enhanced CPF Housing Grant of up to $120,000. A first-timer and second-timer couple may get an EHG (Singles) of up to $60,000.自2024年八月20日起，首次购房家庭的额外公积金房屋津贴最高可达12万新元。首次与第二次购房者组成的夫妇，EHG (Singles) 最高6万新元。
  • The grant can reduce the price or the loan. It cannot pay the minimum cash downpayment or the monthly instalments.这笔津贴可以用来抵房价或减轻房贷，但不能用来付最低现金首期，也不能用来付每月供款。
  • The illustrations take the grant off the price, then assume a loan of 75% of what is left and 25% in cash or CPF. The income shown is the lowest gross monthly household income that keeps repayments within 30% at 4%, using the grant tier for that income.文中的例子先从房价减去津贴，再假设借入余额的75%，其余25%用现金或公积金支付。列出的收入，是在4%利率下把供款控制在30%以内、并对应同一收入津贴档的最低家庭月入。
  • The piece says the grant changes the picture most for smaller flats, and that some households may look at a 5-room flat or a dearer location because the grant is there.文章说，这笔津贴对较小单位的价格影响更大，也可能让一些家庭因为有津贴而考虑5房，或考虑更贵的地点。
