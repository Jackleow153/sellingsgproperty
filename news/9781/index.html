2026/10/242026年10月24日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • Headline figure: HDB resale prices dipped 0.1% in Q3 2026标题数字：2026年第三季组屋转售价下跌0.1%
  • More flats were sold in the same quarter同一季卖出的组屋变多
  • 0.1% is the broad index, not one block’s price0.1%是整体指数，不是某一座楼的价钱
  • Higher volume and a slight price dip can happen together成交增加和价格微跌可以同时发生
  • More flats changed hands in the same quarter, so buyers and sellers were still completing sales while the index dipped 0.1%.同一个季度有更多组屋转手，所以指数下跌0.1%的时候，买家和卖家仍在完成交易。
