HDB Q3 resale prices dip 0.1% even as more flats sold第三季组屋转售价微跌0.1%，成交套数反而增加

2026/10/242026年10月24日

Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Headline figure: HDB resale prices dipped 0.1% in Q3 2026标题数字：2026年第三季组屋转售价下跌0.1%
  • More flats were sold in the same quarter同一季卖出的组屋变多
  • 0.1% is the broad index, not one block’s price0.1%是整体指数，不是某一座楼的价钱
  • Higher volume and a slight price dip can happen together成交增加和价格微跌可以同时发生

What this means这对你意味着什么

The PropNex headline says HDB resale prices dipped 0.1% in the third quarter of 2026, even as more flats were sold. A tenth of one percent is a small move. More sales means buyers and sellers were still meeting. The market did not stop.博纳的标题说，2026年第三季HDB转售价下跌0.1%，但卖出的组屋变多了。0.1%是很小的变动。成交变多，代表买家和卖家仍在成交。市场没有停。

A 0.1% change on the index is not a 0.1% change on your flat. Floor, lease, renovation and town can matter more than the quarter. Buyers who were waiting for a fall should ask whether 0.1% changes the monthly loan. On most flats, it does not.指数上的0.1%，不是你这套房子的0.1%。楼层、屋契、装修和市镇，往往比这个季度更要紧。一直在等下跌的买家，应该问这0.1%会不会改变每月供款。对大多数房子，不会。

Sellers who fear a slide should look at how many buyers are actually viewing. More transactions can mean a sale takes less time, even when the index is slightly down. Price the flat from nearby sales, then decide if you can wait.担心价格滑落的卖家，应该看真正来看房的人有多少。指数略跌时，成交变多仍可能代表房子卖得更快。用附近的成交定价，再决定你等不等得起。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Is a 0.1% dip a reason to delay an HDB purchase?0.1%的下跌，够不够成为推迟买组屋的理由？ Run the loan on the flat you want. A 0.1% index move rarely changes whether that monthly payment fits.用你要买的那套房子算贷款。指数动0.1%，很少会改变每月供款你负担不负担得起。
  • Does more flats sold help sellers?成交变多对卖家有帮助吗？ Sellers should use recent sales in the same block or nearby blocks. The index will not negotiate with the buyer in your flat.卖家应该用同一座或附近座楼的近期成交。指数不会和你家客厅里的买家讨价还价。
  • How should you price one flat against the index?该怎么用指数来看一套具体的房子？ More deals can mean buyers are active. It does not mean every asking price will be met.成交变多，可以代表买家还在看房。这不代表每一个开价都会被接受。
