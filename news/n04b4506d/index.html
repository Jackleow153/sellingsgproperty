Good Class Bungalow in Ridley Park put on market with undisclosed guide price里德利园优质洋房以意向求购方式推出，未公布指导价

2026/11/122026年11月12日

Source: EdgeProp.sg来源：EdgeProp.sg

Key points要点

  • Huttons Asia put a two-storey bungalow in the Ridley Park Good Class Bungalow area up for sale by expression of interest on 12 November. The release did not state a guide price.11月12日，博纳把里德利园优质洋房区一栋两层洋房以意向求购方式推出。通稿没有写指导价。
  • The site is 27,443 sq ft and largely square. The built-up area is 11,000 sq ft, with a garden, a pool, five bedrooms each with its own bathroom, a helper's room, and parking.地块27443平方英尺，大体呈方形。建筑面积11000平方英尺，有花园、泳池、五间带独立浴室的卧室、一间佣人房，以及停车位。
  • Huttons said the house could be altered or rebuilt, subject to approval. Private viewings are by appointment. The agents named are Mey Lim and Lawrence See.博纳说，房子可以改建或重建，但须先获批准。参观要预约。点名的经纪是 Mey Lim 和 Lawrence See。
  • The report says few landed caveats have been lodged nearby. One, drawn from URA data, was a bungalow on 12,299 sq ft of freehold land on Ridley Park, resold for $26.6 million in March 2021, or $2,163 psf.报道说，附近有地住宅的成交备案不多。其中一宗来自市区重建局数据：里德利园一块12299平方英尺的永久地契洋房，2021年三月以2660万新元转售，即每平方英尺2163新元。
  • A different house sold in February 2026: a freehold two-storey bungalow on 23,979 sq ft at Tanglin Hill, also inside the Ridley Park Good Class Bungalow area, for $76 million, or $3,169 psf. The report says The Business Times carried that sale in May, and names the seller as Chrissy Luo.另一栋不同的房子在2026年二月成交：Tanglin Hill 一栋永久地契两层洋房，同样在里德利园优质洋房区内，地块23979平方英尺，售价7600万新元，即每平方英尺3169新元。报道说《商业时报》在五月写过这宗成交，卖家是 Chrissy Luo。

What this means if you're upgrading如果你打算升级换房

A Good Class Bungalow sale has no bearing on an HDB-to-condo upgrade. It does not change what your flat is worth or what a condo costs.优质洋房的出售与从组屋升级到公寓无关。它不会改变你的组屋价值，也不会改变公寓的价格。
