Bishan flats with shorter waiting times among BTO launches in February碧山组屋 shorter waiting times among 预购组屋 launches February

2026/11/062026年11月6日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • HDB will launch about 4,300 BTO flats in February 2027, in five projects across four towns: one each in Toa Payoh, Bishan and Tengah, and two in Tampines.建屋发展局将在2027年2月推出约4300个预购组屋，分布在四个市镇的五个项目：大巴窑、碧山和登加各一个，淡滨尼两个。
  • HDB published the locations and the planned flat mix on 4 November. At the same time it will sell about 5,000 balance flats that are completed or still being built.建屋局在11月4日公布了地点和计划中的房型组合。同一轮还会出售约5000个剩余组屋，这些单位已经建成，或仍在建造。
  • Analysts expect the applications to be competitive because relatively few BTO flats are on sale. They pointed to Bishan, Toa Payoh, and a 460-unit project in Tampines as the likely most popular.分析师预计申请会很激烈，因为这一轮开卖的预购组屋相对少。他们点名碧山、大巴窑，以及淡滨尼一个460个单位的项目，最可能最受欢迎。
  • The Toa Payoh project is the second BTO in the Mount Pleasant estate: 1,140 flats beside the future Mount Pleasant MRT station on the Thomson-East Coast Line. Analysts expect it to be classed Prime.大巴窑的项目是快乐山新镇的第二个预购组屋：1140个单位，就在汤申-东海岸线未来的快乐山地铁站旁边。分析师预计它会被列为优质组屋。
  • The Mount Pleasant mix is 450 two-room Flexi flats, 90 three-room flats and 600 four-room flats, with an eating house, a preschool, an active ageing centre and a residents’ network centre.快乐山的户型是450个两房灵活、90个三房和600个四房，并设有食阁、幼儿园、乐龄活动中心和居民网络中心。
  • Mount Pleasant Crest, the estate’s first BTO, launched in October 2025 and was classed Prime. PropNex’s Wong Siew Ying said the new project sits at the doorstep of the future station, linking toward Orchard and the CBD, and is close to CHIJ Primary (Toa Payoh), St Joseph’s Institution and Singapore Chinese Girls’ School.快乐山的第一个预购组屋Mount Pleasant Crest在2025年10月开卖，当时列为优质组屋。博纳（PropNex）的Wong Siew Ying说，新项目就在未来地铁站门口，可通往乌节和市中心商业区，附近还有CHIJ Primary (Toa Payoh)、St Joseph’s Institution和Singapore Chinese Girls’ School。
  • Realion’s Christine Sun said the Mount Pleasant site is close to the Pan-Island Expressway, so noise and dust are a drawback, and it is relatively far from amenities in central Toa Payoh.瑞联（Realion）的Christine Sun说，快乐山地盘靠近泛岛高速公路，噪音和灰尘是缺点，而且离大巴窑市中心的设施比较远。
  • In Sin Ming, Bishan, about 1,070 flats will have a shorter wait of under three years: 520 two-room Flexi, 80 three-room and 470 four-room, plus a supermarket and a preschool. Analysts expect a Plus label because the site is in a central town but not next to a station.碧山新民约有1070个单位，等候时间较短，不到三年：520个两房灵活、80个三房和470个四房，并设有超市和幼儿园。分析师预计它会被列为优选组屋，因为地点在中部市镇，但并不紧挨地铁站。
  • The Bishan site is about 900 metres from Marymount station and about 800 metres from Upper Thomson station. Mogul.sg’s Nicholas Mak cited the limited land left in Bishan. Wong expects three to four applicants per flat for the Toa Payoh and Bishan projects.碧山这个地盘距离玛丽蒙站约900米，距离汤申路上段站约800米。Mogul.sg的Nicholas Mak说，碧山剩下可建新组屋的地不多。Wong Siew Ying预计，大巴窑和碧山这两个项目每个单位会有三到四个申请人。
  • Analysts said the 460-unit Upper Changi project in Tampines should draw more interest than the town’s other project, especially its five-room flats. The mix is 100 three-room flats and 120 each of two-room Flexi, four-room and five-room, with an eating house, a minimart and four shops, near Upper Changi and Expo stations.分析师说，淡滨尼上樟宜那个460个单位的项目，会比同市镇的另一个项目更受关注，尤其是它的五房。户型是100个三房，两房灵活、四房和五房各120个，并设有食阁、小型超市和四间店铺，靠近上樟宜站和博览站。
  • ERA’s Eugene Lim said the last five-room launch in Tampines was Simei Symphony in July 2025. PropNex’s Wong said those 100 five-room flats drew 12.8 applicants per flat. Sun expects the Upper Changi project to be Standard, like Simei Symphony. The Tampines Street 11 project has 240 community care apartments, the first in Tampines, and 90 four-room flats, for older residents who want to stay in the town. Wong said the two Tampines projects together could see an application rate of five times or more, after rates of 4.2 to 24.1 across the town’s past five launches.ERA的Eugene Lim说，淡滨尼上一轮五房是2025年7月的Simei Symphony。博纳（PropNex）的Wong Siew Ying说，那100个五房每个单位有12.8个申请人。Christine Sun预计上樟宜这个项目会像Simei Symphony一样列为标准组屋。淡滨尼11街的项目有240个社区关怀公寓，是淡滨尼的第一批，另有90个四房，面向想留在这个市镇养老的年长居民。Wong Siew Ying说，淡滨尼这两个项目合起来，申请率可能达到五倍或更高。过去五轮淡滨尼预购组屋的申请率在4.2到24.1之间。
  • Tengah’s Brickland project has 1,280 flats: 410 two-room Flexi, 80 three-room, 350 four-room and 440 five-room. Analysts expect it to be Standard, near the future Brickland station on the North-South Line. Including this exercise, at least 15,600 BTO flats are due in 2027, after 19,723 launched in 2025 and 19,608 in 2026. Chee Hong Tat said in May 2026 that HDB is likely to exceed 55,000 new homes offered from 2025 to 2027.登加Brickland项目有1280个单位：410个两房灵活、80个三房、350个四房和440个五房。分析师预计它会列为标准组屋，靠近南北线未来的Brickland站。算上这一轮，2027年至少会推出15600个预购组屋。2025年推出了19723个，2026年推出了19608个。国家发展部长徐芳达在2026年5月说，建屋局很可能超过2025年到2027年推出55000个新组屋的承诺。
