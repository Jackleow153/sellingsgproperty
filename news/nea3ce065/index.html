Even The Cheapest 4-Room HDB Flats Aren't Cheap Anymore — One Statistic Reveals Just How Rare $500K Flats Are最便宜的四房组屋也不便宜了，50万新元的单位变少

Key facts重点

  • Stacked Homes: S$500,000 4-room HDB flats are described as rareStacked Homes：50万新元的四房组屋被形容为少见
  • The headline does not publish the statistic it mentions标题没有公布它提到的那个统计数字
  • S$500,000 is not a national price for every 4-room50万新元不是全岛每一套四房的价钱
  • Town and remaining lease change what that budget can buy市镇和剩余屋契会改变这个预算买得到什么

What this means这对你意味着什么

Stacked Homes says even the cheapest 4-room HDB flats are not cheap any more, and that flats at S$500,000 have become rare. The headline teases “one statistic” and does not give it. Do not fill that gap with a guess.Stacked Homes说，就算是最便宜的四房组屋也不便宜了，50万新元的单位已经变得少见。标题提起“一个统计”，但没有把数字写出来。不要自己补一个猜测。

S$500,000 can still be a real asking price in some towns and a fantasy in others. Lease, floor and the years left matter as much as the round number. A buyer hunting only for S$500,000 may be looking in the wrong town, or at flats with a short lease.50万新元在一些市镇仍可能是真实开价，在另一些市镇则不现实。屋契、楼层和剩余年数，和这个整数一样重要。只找50万新元的买家，可能找错了市镇，或找到的是屋契很短的房子。

Sellers in towns where S$500,000 is now low should not assume every 4-room has cleared that bar. Buyers who can pay more should still ask what the extra money buys: a longer lease, a better floor, or simply a hotter estate.在50万新元已经偏低的市镇，卖家也不该假设每一套四房都越过了这条线。付得起更多的买家，仍要问多出来的钱买到了什么：更长的屋契、更好的楼层，还是只是一个更热的邻里。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Can you still buy a 4-room HDB flat for S$500,000?现在还能用50万新元买到四房组屋吗？ Search current 4-room listings in the town you would live in. Rare in one headline is not the same as unavailable in every town.在你愿意住的市镇里搜现在的四房挂牌。标题里的“少见”，不等于每个市镇都没有。
  • What is the statistic the headline does not show?标题没写出的统计到底是什么？ If a S$500,000 flat appears, read the lease and the floor before you treat it as a bargain. A low price often has a reason.如果出现一套50万新元的房子，先看屋契和楼层，再把它当成便宜。价钱低，通常有原因。
  • How should a seller use a S$500,000 headline?卖家该怎么用一个50万新元的标题？ Sellers should price from their town’s recent 4-room sales. A line about rare S$500,000 flats is not permission to add a premium with no comparable sale.卖家应该用自己市镇近期的四房成交来定价。一句“50万的四房变少了”，不是没有可比成交也可以加价的许可。
