What this means这对你意味着什么

VnExpress reported that a freehold bungalow tied to a late Singapore property tycoon has been put up for sale at nearly $65 million. “Put on sale” means an asking price. It is not a completed deal. The headline does not name the road.VnExpress报道，一栋和已故新加坡地产富商有关的永久地契洋房已经挂牌，叫价近6500万美元。“挂牌出售”是开价，不是已经完成的成交。标题没有写出路名。

Nearly $65 million is one house, on freehold land, with a well-known former owner. It does not move the price of a condo or an HDB flat. Landed buyers who are not shopping at that level can ignore the number. Sellers of ordinary landed homes should not add a zero because a trophy house is listed.近6500万美元是一栋房子，永久地契，还有一位知名的前业主。它不会带动一套公寓或一套组屋的价钱。买不起这个水平的有地住宅买家，可以忽略这个数字。普通有地住宅的卖家，也不该因为一栋顶级房子在挂牌，就给自己的价钱加一个零。

Until there is a buyer and a price that was actually paid, this is a listing. Trophy listings can sit. They can also sell quickly if the price meets a buyer who wants that exact house. Neither outcome is a signal for the rest of the market.在出现买家和真正付过的价钱之前，这只是一个挂牌。这类房子可以挂很久，也可以在价钱碰到那位非要这栋房子的买家时很快卖掉。两种结果都不是市场其他部分的信号。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Has the bungalow sold for nearly $65 million?这栋洋房是不是已经以近6500万美元卖掉了？ Treat nearly $65 million as an ask for one freehold bungalow. Wait for a transacted price before you call it the market.把近6500万美元看成一栋永久地契洋房的开价。等有了成交价，再把它称为市场。
  • Should other landed sellers follow that asking price?其他有地住宅的卖家该跟着这个开价吗？ Ordinary landed sellers should use sales on their own road. A trophy ask is not a comparable.普通有地住宅的卖家应该用自己那条路的成交。一栋顶级房子的开价不是可比对象。
  • Does this listing matter to HDB or condo owners?这个挂牌和组屋或公寓的屋主有关吗？ HDB and condo decisions do not change because a single bungalow is listed. Keep your comparison inside your own housing type.组屋和公寓的决定，不会因为一栋洋房挂牌而改变。比较要留在你自己的房屋类型里。
