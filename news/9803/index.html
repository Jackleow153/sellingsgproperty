1,310 flats in Sembawang with under 3 years’ wait to go on sale in Nov三巴旺1310个组屋单位将于11月发售，等候时间不到3年

2026/11/022026年11月2日Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Project: Sembawang Anchor, in Sembawang North项目：Sembawang Anchor，位于三巴旺北
  • Supply: about 1,310 flats in the November 2026 BTO exercise供应：2026年11月预购组屋（BTO）销售活动约1310个单位
  • Reported wait: about 2 years and 10 months报道中的等候时间：约两年10个月
  • Flat types: 2-room Flexi, 3-room, 4-room and 5-room房型：二房式灵活单位、三房、四房和五房式
  • Also in the same exercise: Bayshore Oasis in Bedok and Plantation Dew in Tengah, with waits of about 3 years and 2 months同一次销售活动：勿洛的Bayshore Oasis和登加的Plantation Dew，等候时间约三年两个月

What this means这对你意味着什么

The November 2026 Build-to-Order exercise includes about 1,310 flats at Sembawang Anchor, in the new Sembawang North estate. Reports put the wait at about 2 years and 10 months, which is under the three years in the headline. The mix runs from 2-room Flexi flats to 3-room, 4-room and 5-room flats.2026年11月的预购组屋（BTO）销售活动，包括位于新三巴旺北住宅区的Sembawang Anchor，约1310个单位。报道中的等候时间约两年10个月，短于标题里的三年。房型从二房式灵活单位（2-room Flexi），到三房、四房和五房式。

Two other projects in the same exercise are also relatively short waits. Bayshore Oasis in Bedok and Plantation Dew in Tengah are reported at about 3 years and 2 months. A shorter wait helps if you are timing a wedding, a move out of your parents’ home, or the sale of the flat you live in now.同一次销售活动里，还有两个等候时间也比较短的项目。勿洛的Bayshore Oasis和登加的Plantation Dew，报道中的等候时间约三年两个月。如果你在安排婚礼、搬离父母家，或出售现在住的组屋，较短的等候时间会有帮助。

It does not mean you will get a queue number, and it does not mean the flat will be cheap. Sembawang still has to fit your work and your daily travel. A shorter wait in the wrong town is not a win.这不代表你一定抽得到号码，也不代表单位会便宜。三巴旺仍必须配合你的工作和每天的通勤。等候时间短，但市镇不合适，并不划算。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Does a shorter HDB wait mean you should apply for Sembawang Anchor?组屋等候时间较短，就该申请Sembawang Anchor吗？ If you need keys sooner, put Sembawang Anchor on your November list and check the flat types before you apply. The shorter wait only helps if you would live there.如果你需要更早拿到钥匙，把Sembawang Anchor放进11月的名单，申请前先看房型。较短的等候时间，只有在你愿意住在那里时才有帮助。
  • How should couples time a resale and a new BTO flat?夫妻该如何安排转售和新的预购组屋？ Couples who are also selling a home should map the gap between collecting the sale money and collecting the keys. A wait under three years shrinks that gap. It does not remove the need for somewhere to stay in between.同时也在卖房的夫妻，应该算清收到卖房钱和拿到钥匙之间的空档。不到三年的等候会缩短这个空档，但中间仍需要地方住。
  • Should the short wait change your budget?较短的等候时间该不该改变你的预算？ Do not stretch your budget because the wait looks attractive. Compare Sembawang with Bayshore and Tengah on the same exercise, then check that the monthly mortgage still feels comfortable.不要因为等候时间好看就拉高预算。在同一次销售活动里比较三巴旺、碧湾和登加，再确认每月房贷仍然负担得来。
