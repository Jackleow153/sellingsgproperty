1,310 flats in Sembawang with under 3 years’ wait to go on sale in Nov三巴旺1310个组屋单位将于11月发售，等候时间不到3年

2026/11/022026年11月2日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • Project: Sembawang Anchor, in Sembawang North项目：Sembawang Anchor，位于三巴旺北
  • Supply: about 1,310 flats in the November 2026 BTO exercise供应：2026年11月预购组屋（BTO）销售活动约1310个单位
  • Reported wait: about 2 years and 10 months报道中的等候时间：约两年10个月
  • Flat types: 2-room Flexi, 3-room, 4-room and 5-room房型：二房式灵活单位、三房、四房和五房式
  • Also in the same exercise: Bayshore Oasis in Bedok and Plantation Dew in Tengah, with waits of about 3 years and 2 months同一次销售活动：勿洛的Bayshore Oasis和登加的Plantation Dew，等候时间约三年两个月

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Is the shorter wait at Sembawang Anchor a reason to ballot now?三巴旺锚地等候较短，是现在就抽签的理由吗？ For a buyer who can accept Sembawang North, about 1,310 flats with a wait of about two years and 10 months is the case for joining this November sale rather than a later one. Bayshore Oasis and Plantation Dew, in the same exercise, are about three years and two months. Waiting gives up that gap. The flat types run from 2-room Flexi through 5-room, so the buyer can be a single, a small household or a family. A buyer who will not live in Sembawang is not helped by the shorter queue.能接受三巴旺北的买家，约1310个单位、等候大约两年10个月，是参加11月这一轮而不是再等的理由。同一轮的Bayshore Oasis和登加Plantation Dew大约三年两个月。再等，就让掉这个差距。房型从二房灵活到五房，买家可以是单身、小家庭或大家庭。不肯住三巴旺的人，较短的队伍帮不了。
  • How should sellers in Sembawang and Bayshore read this?三巴旺和白沙的卖家该怎么读这则消息？ Sellers in Sembawang North will meet people who can also ballot a large new project and move in inside three years. Their resale has to win by being available now. Bayshore sellers face a buyer who may accept a longer wait for that location. This report does not price either project, so a seller should not invent a discount against an unpriced BTO.三巴旺北的卖家，会遇到也能抽一个大型新项目、三年内入住的人。转售要赢，靠的是现在就能住。白沙的卖家面对的，是可能愿意为那个地点多等的买家。这篇报道没有给两个项目定价，卖家不该对着一个还没标价的预购组屋自己编一个折扣。
  • What does a sub-three-year wait do to resale demand?不到三年的等候，对转售需求有什么影响？ A large Standard project with a wait under three years pulls buyers who have been sensitive to how long BTO takes. That can soften the hurry in nearby resale for households who can wait. It does not reset Sembawang prices, and it does not touch towns that are not in the exercise.一个等候不到三年的大型标准项目，会拉走对等候时间敏感的买家。附近等得起的家庭，转售就不那么急。它不会重定三巴旺的价钱，也碰不到不在这一轮里的市镇。
