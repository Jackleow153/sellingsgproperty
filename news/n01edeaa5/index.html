What this means这对你意味着什么

Stomp reported that a renovation creator called a 4-room Tengah BTO kitchen one of the most limited layouts he had seen, and that HDB responded. The headline does not quote HDB, and it does not give the kitchen’s measurements. This is a layout argument, not a price.Stomp报道，一名装修内容创作者把登加（Tengah）一套四房预购组屋的厨房，说成他见过最局促的户型之一，建屋发展局作出了回应。标题没有引用建屋局的原话，也没有厨房的尺寸。这是在讨论户型，不是价钱。

Tengah buyers choose a flat from a plan, often before they can stand in the finished kitchen. A tight kitchen is a daily problem if you cook. It is also easy to miss when the showflat styling uses smaller furniture. Measure the plan. Ask what is structural and what a contractor can open up.登加的买家常常在厨房还没建好时，就凭图纸选房。如果你常做饭，局促的厨房是每天的问题。示范单位如果用了较小的家具，这一点也很容易被放过。量图纸。问哪些墙是结构墙，承包商能动哪些。

Sellers of completed Tengah flats will meet buyers who have seen this kind of clip. Be ready to show the real kitchen, not a styled photo. Buyers should not reject an entire town because one stack was criticised, and they should not ignore the plan because the facade looked fine.已经建好的登加组屋，卖家会遇到看过这类短片的买家。准备让人看真正的厨房，而不是一张布置过的照片。买家不该因为一个座楼被批评就否定整个市镇，也不该因为外立面好看就忽略图纸。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should Tengah buyers worry about every 4-room kitchen?登加的买家是不是该担心每一套四房的厨房？ Read the kitchen’s width and length on the plan for the stack you want. One creator’s verdict is not a measurement.看你要的那个座楼，厨房在图纸上的宽和长。一位创作者的评价不是尺寸。
  • What can you check on a BTO floor plan?在预购组屋平面图上可以核对什么？ Ask which walls can move and which cannot. A renovation can improve finishes. It cannot always add floor area.问哪些墙可以动，哪些不能。装修可以改饰面，不一定能增加面积。
  • How should a seller answer a buyer who saw the clip?卖家该怎么回答看过短片的买家？ If you are selling a completed flat, let buyers stand in the kitchen. A clip about another stack does not describe yours, and pretending it does not exist will not help.如果你在卖已经建好的房子，让买家站进厨房。关于另一个座楼的短片描述的不是你的家。假装没有这回事，也没有帮助。
