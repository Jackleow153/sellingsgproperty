About 11,000 homes planned on Maju Forest, Gillman Barracks sites with more greenery to be retainedMaju森林与吉门军营地段计划约1万1000个住家，并保留更多绿化

2026/10/292026年10月29日Source: PropNex Property News来源：博纳房产新闻

Key facts重点

  • Sites: Maju Forest and Gillman Barracks地段：Maju森林与吉门军营（Gillman Barracks）
  • Homes planned: about 11,000计划中的住家：约1万1000个
  • Split: about 9,500 public homes and about 1,500 private units分配：约9500个公共住房，约1500个私人单位
  • Design change: more greenery kept, with some taller blocks设计改动：保留更多绿化，部分楼宇建得更高
  • Timing: a long-term plan, not a project on sale now时间：长期计划，不是现在发售的项目

What this means这对你意味着什么

Revised plans for the Maju Forest and Gillman Barracks sites set aside about 11,000 homes. About 9,500 would be public housing and about 1,500 private. The revision also keeps more greenery, with some blocks built taller so more land can stay open.Maju森林与吉门军营（Gillman Barracks）地段的修订计划，预留约1万1000个住家。其中约9500个是公共住房，约1500个是私人住宅。修订也保留更多绿化，部分组屋建得更高，好让更多土地保持开阔。

These homes are not for sale this month. A plan on this scale is carried out over many years. The first question for anyone nearby is what has actually been confirmed, and how close future blocks would sit to the home they care about.这些住家不是这个月发售。这个规模的计划要很多年才落实。附近的人首先该问的是：哪些已经确定，以及未来的楼宇会离自己在意的房子有多近。

More homes nearby can mean more people to support shops and transport. It can also mean more future competition, and a change to the view or the quiet edge that made the area attractive.附近多了住家，可以有更多人支撑商店和交通。它也可能带来更多未来的竞争，并改变当初让这个地区吸引人的景观或安静边缘。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Can you buy one of these Maju Forest or Gillman Barracks homes now?现在能买到Maju森林或吉门军营的这些住家吗？ Treat this as a long-term plan, not a BTO or a new launch you can book now. Ask what has been approved, and whether any date has been given for the first homes.把它当成长期计划，而不是你现在可以预订的预购组屋（BTO）或新盘。问清楚哪些已经批准，以及第一批住家有没有日期。
  • How could taller blocks and greenery change a nearby home?更高的楼和绿化，会如何改变附近的房子？ If trees, a view or a quiet edge are why you like a home nearby, look at the revised layout. Taller blocks and retained greenery change a view in different ways.如果树木、景观或安静的边缘是你喜欢附近一套房子的原因，去看修订后的布局。更高的楼和保留下来的绿化，会以不同的方式改变景观。
  • Should sellers add a premium because 11,000 new homes are planned?因为计划了1万1000个新住家，卖家就该加价吗？ Sellers should not add a premium just because 11,000 homes are planned. Buyers pay for what they can use today, and they discount what is still uncertain.卖家不要只因为计划了1万1000个住家就加价。买家为今天用得上的东西付钱，对还不确定的事情会打折。
