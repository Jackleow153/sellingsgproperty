How To Tell If A Singapore Condo Is Really A Good Rental Investment怎样判断一套新加坡公寓是不是真正适合收租

2026/11/082026年11月8日

Source: Stacked Homes来源：Stacked Homes

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • Stacked Homes treats gross rental yield as the year's rent divided by the price. That ratio only shows how the two numbers sit together.Stacked Homes 把毛租金回报率看成一年的租金除以房价。这个比率只说明两个数字怎么放在一起。
  • The article says the yield does not show whether the rent itself is high, whether any money is left after costs, or whether the price is justified.文章说，回报率看不出租金本身高不高，扣掉开支后还有没有剩余，也看不出这个价钱是否站得住。
  • For two-bedroom units at The Esta, it gives a median rent of $5,300 a month and a median resale price of $2.78 million, a gross yield of 2.29%, among the lower two-bedroom yields in that area. The writer places the project in the East Coast and Amber Road area.The Esta 的两房，文中给出的中位租金是每月5300新元，转售中位价是278万新元，毛回报率2.29%，在该区两房里属于较低的一档。作者把这个项目放在东海岸和 Amber Road 一带。
  • #1 Suites, in the same comparison, has a median rent of $3,500 a month and a median price of $730,000, a yield of 5.75%. The writer ties the lower price to Geylang's red-light area, so the higher yield is not, by itself, the better rental.同一组比较里，#1 Suites 的中位租金是每月3500新元，中位价格是73万新元，回报率5.75%。作者把较低的价钱和芽笼的红灯区联系在一起，所以更高的回报率本身并不代表更好的出租房。
  • One-bedroom units at The Hillford show an average yield of 5.7% on a median price of about $589,000. The article says the project is a 60-year lease from 2013, with about 47 years left, and that it was planned as a retirement resort.The Hillford 的一房平均回报率是5.7%，中位价格约58万9000新元。文章说这是2013年起的60年地契，大约还剩47年，而且项目当初是按退休度假村来规划的。
  • The writer says The Hillford can suit an owner who means to hold it and collect rent until the lease ends, and that cheaper choices near Beauty World are few. The same flat is a weaker fit for a resale exit, for living in, or for passing on.作者说，如果打算一直持有、收到地契结束，The Hillford 可以适合，而且美世界附近更便宜的选择很少。同一套房子，若还想转手、自住或留给下一代，就没那么合适。
  • Eon Shenton's average yield is 4.57%, while its price fell 2.29% a year over five years. The article's point is that a yield can rise because the price has weakened.Eon Shenton 的平均回报率是4.57%，而五年里价格按年下跌2.29%。文章的意思是，回报率上升，可以是因为房价变弱。
  • Some buyers, often near retirement, want a set monthly rent such as $3,000 or $4,000, and treat the yield, the exit, the age and the facing as secondary.有些买家，往往接近退休，要的是一个固定月租，例如3000或4000新元。回报率、退场、楼龄和朝向，对他们是次要的。
  • The article also describes owners who pass over older projects because the upkeep is heavier, and one Marine Parade owner who has left a condo empty for seven months rather than accept a lower rent or sell.文章还写到，有些屋主因为维修更重而放过较旧的项目。也有一位马林百列的屋主，一套公寓空了七个月，也不肯收较低的租金，或者卖掉。
  • It separates owners who want the rent to cover the mortgage, the maintenance and the property tax now from the more common owner who uses the rent only to reduce the cost of holding. The question it leaves is what that owner needs the condo to do.文章把屋主分成两类：一类要租金现在就盖过房贷、管理费和房产税；更常见的一类只用租金去减轻持有成本。它留下的问题是，这个屋主需要这套公寓做什么。
