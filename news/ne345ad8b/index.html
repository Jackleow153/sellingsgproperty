Yishun records its first S$1.3M HDB resale as executive flat sells for S$1,328,888义顺公寓式组屋以132万8888新元成交

2026/11/112026年11月11日

Source: 99.co来源：99.co

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • An executive flat at Block 652 Yishun Avenue 4 sold for S$1,328,888 in November 2026. The article calls it Yishun's dearest executive flat, the town's dearest HDB resale of any type, and the first HDB resale there above S$1.3 million.Yishun Avenue 4 的 Block 652 一套公寓式组屋，在2026年十一月以132万8888新元成交。文章称这是义顺最贵的公寓式组屋，也是该镇各类组屋转售的最高价，并且是镇内首宗超过130万新元的组屋转售。
  • The 3-bedroom unit sits on storeys 7 to 9 at Nee Soon Central View. It is 164 sqm, about 1,765 sqft, or about S$753 psf. The 99-year lease started in 1992, with 64 years and 8 months left.这套3房位于 Nee Soon Central View 的7楼至9楼之间，面积164平方米，约1765平方英尺，约每平方英尺753新元。99年地契自1992年起，剩余64年8个月。
  • The town's four dearest executive resales are now in Blocks 652, 654 and 661 on Yishun Avenue 4. Before this sale, the top three had sold between S$1.28 million and S$1.289 million.义顺最贵的四宗公寓式组屋转售，现在都在 Yishun Avenue 4 的 Block 652、654 和 661。此前提价最高的三宗，成交价在128万新元到128万9000新元之间。
  • At 1,765 sqft the new record is 183 sqft smaller than the earlier 1,948 sqft flats, and S$40,000 above the July 2025 Block 661 price of S$1,288,888. That earlier record had lasted about 16 months.新纪录单位1765平方英尺，比早前1948平方英尺的单位少183平方英尺，却比2025年七月 Block 661 的128万8888新元纪录高出4万新元。前一个纪录大约维持了16个月。
  • In November 2025 a larger flat in the same storey band at Block 652 sold for S$1.288 million, so this buyer paid S$40,888 more for about 183 sqft less.2025年十一月，同一楼层段的 Block 652 有一套更大的公寓式组屋以128万8000新元成交。这次买家多付了4万0888新元，面积却少了约183平方英尺。
  • From May to 10 November 2026, Yishun recorded 30 executive resales. Eight were above S$1 million. Five of those were between S$1.01 million and S$1.075 million, and only this Block 652 sale cleared S$1.2 million. The median of the 30 was about S$925,000.从五月到2026年十一月10日，义顺录得30宗公寓式组屋转售，八宗超过100万新元。其中五宗在101万新元到107万5000新元之间，只有 Block 652 这一宗超过120万新元。这30宗的中位价约为92万5000新元。
  • Three other 164 sqm flats with a 1992 lease start sold for S$1.038 million to S$1.185 million. The closest, at Block 641 in September 2026, was on storeys 10 to 12 and sold for S$143,888 less.另有三套同样164平方米、1992年起租的公寓式组屋，成交价在103万8000新元到118万5000新元。最接近的是2026年九月的 Block 641，楼层在10到12，成交价少了14万3888新元。
  • Three condos of 1,732 to 1,798 sqft in Yishun sold for S$1.83 million to S$1.978 million in 2025 and 2026, about S$501,000 to S$649,000 more than this executive flat.义顺三套面积在1732到1798平方英尺的私宅，在2025年和2026年以183万新元至197万8000新元成交，比这套公寓式组屋大约贵50万1000新元到64万9000新元。
  • The article also notes that HDB homes of 1,300 sqft and above in Yishun are listed on 99.co from S$540,000, so a large resale there is not only this record.文章也写到，义顺1300平方英尺及以上的组屋，在99.co的挂牌价从54万新元起，所以大面积转售并不只有这一宗纪录。
