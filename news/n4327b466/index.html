Singapore condo unit owner loses bid to remove vending machines outside shopsRobinson Suites 店主未能要求移走店外自动售货机

2026/11/112026年11月11日

Source: VnExpress International来源：VnExpress International

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • Boon Trading Pte Ltd, owner of three ground-floor units at Robinson Suites, said the vending machines blocked the shop windows and hurt the tenants' trade.Boon Trading Pte Ltd 拥有 Robinson Suites 的三间地面单位。公司说自动售货机挡住了店面窗户，也影响了租户的生意。
  • The project at 50 Robinson Road has 170 units: 167 homes and three commercial units, used as a stationery shop, a stockbroking firm, and a clinic.这座项目位于 50 Robinson Road，共有170个单位，其中167个是住宅，另外三间商业单位分别是文具店、证券行和诊所。
  • The management corporation put eight vending machines and trash bins on the covered common corridor outside the shops between May 2025 and May 2026.管理法人在2025年五月至2026年五月之间，把八台自动售货机和垃圾桶放在店外有盖的共有走廊上。
  • An April 2023 annual general meeting had approved leasing all or part of the common property for one to three years.2023年四月的年度大会已批准把共有产业的全部或一部分出租，租期为一到三年。
  • Director Koe Kyin Hoon said the machines blocked views and daylight, and that one tenant asked for a lower rent. In April 2026 the company asked the Strata Titles Boards to set aside the 2023 resolution because it did not name which common property could be leased.董事 Koe Kyin Hoon 说，机器挡住视线和自然光，有一名租户因此要求减租。2026年四月，公司向分层地契局申请推翻2023年的决议，理由是决议没有写明可以出租哪一部分共有产业。
  • The company also asked for the machines and bins to be removed, and for a bar on later objects that would block the shopfronts. The Boards dismissed all three applications.公司还要求移走机器和垃圾桶，并禁止日后在店面前再放会挡住窗户的东西。分层地契局驳回了全部三项申请。
  • The Boards found the management corporation could lease common property to raise income, and ordered Boon Trading to pay SGD8,000 in legal costs and SGD2,000 in disbursements.分层地契局认为管理法人可以出租部分共有产业来取得收入，并下令 Boon Trading 支付8000新元律师费和2000新元杂费。
  • The 2023 resolution was held to cover all or part of the common property. Management had told Koe of the plan beforehand and had offered the shop tenants the chance to rent that space.局方认定2023年的决议已经清楚授权出租全部或部分共有产业。管理方事先告知了 Koe，也让店内租户有机会自己租下那段位置。
  • On 11 Sept 2026, two machines were removed and the rest were moved, leaving 80.5% of the glass frontage clear at each of two shops.在2026年九月11日，管理法人移走两台机器并调整其余机器的位置，两间店各自不被挡住的玻璃店面增至80.5%。
