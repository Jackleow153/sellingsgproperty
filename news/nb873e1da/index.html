HDB launches 7,964 BTO flats across 6 towns in first exercise under higher income ceilings组屋 launches 7,964预购组屋组屋 across 6 towns first exercise under higher income ceilings

2026/11/042026年11月4日

Source: AsiaOne来源：亚洲新闻网

Key points要点

  • HDB launched 7,964 Build-To-Order flats on 4 November 2026, the last sales exercise of the year.建屋发展局在2026年11月4日推出7964个预购组屋，这是该年最后一轮销售。
  • Seven projects are on sale in Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun.七个项目位于勿洛、芽笼、三巴旺、登加、大巴窑和义顺。
  • It is the first exercise since the monthly income ceiling rose to $16,000 for families and $8,000 for singles. The new ceilings apply to HFE applications from 24 August 2026.这是家庭月入顶限提高到1万6000新元、单身人士提高到8000新元之后的第一轮。新顶限适用于2026年8月24日起提交的购屋资格信（HFE）申请。
  • Three projects are Standard, three are Plus and one is Prime.三个是标准项目，三个是优选项目，一个是优质项目。
  • Sembawang Anchor has 1,310 flats and a wait of about two years and 10 months. Plantation Dew in Tengah has 710 flats, and Chencharu Acres in Yishun has 1,582.Sembawang Anchor有1310个单位，等候约两年10个月。登加Plantation Dew有710个，义顺Chencharu Acres有1582个。
  • Bayshore Sails (1,637 flats) and Bayshore Oasis (855) in Bedok are Plus projects with a 9% subsidy recovery. Merpati Meadow in Geylang has 438 flats and an 8% recovery.勿洛的Bayshore Sails（1637个）和Bayshore Oasis（855个）是优选项目，补贴回收率9%。芽笼Merpati Meadow有438个单位，回收率8%。
  • Toa Payoh Hillside, next to Caldecott MRT, is the Prime project, with 1,432 flats and an 11% subsidy recovery. It includes Community Care Apartments, the first offered since the eligibility age fell from 65 to 55.加利谷地铁站旁的大巴窑山坡是优质项目，1432个单位，补贴回收率11%。里面有乐龄关护住宅，这是申请年龄从65岁降到55岁之后第一次推出。
  • The 2026 total reaches 23,928 flats: 19,608 BTO flats and 4,320 Sale of Balance Flats.2026年的总供应量达到23928个：19608个预购组屋和4320个剩余组屋。
  • Applications close on 11 November 2026. Ballot results are due in January 2027, and bookings start in March 2027.申请在2026年11月11日截止。抽签结果预计2027年1月公布，选购从2027年3月开始。
  • Plus and Prime flats come with extra subsidies, a 10-year minimum occupation period, and a clawback when the flat is sold.优选和优质组屋有额外补贴、10年最低居住年限，转售时要缴回一部分补贴。
  • Before grants, four-room flats were reported from about $312,000 at Sembawang Anchor and about $545,000 at Merpati Meadow. At Toa Payoh Hillside they start at about $558,000 without a balcony and $619,000 with one.未计津贴时，报道中的四房价格，Sembawang Anchor大约从31万2000新元起，Merpati Meadow大约从54万5000新元起。大巴窑山坡的四房，没有阳台的大约从55万8000新元起，有阳台的从61万9000新元起。
