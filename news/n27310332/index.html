Tengah Plantation Dew BTO Review: Rare 5-room and 3Gen units (November 2026)登加 Plantation Dew 2026年11月预购组屋：少见的五房和三代同堂组屋

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: Home & Decor Singapore来源：Home & Decor Singapore

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • Home & Decor reviews Plantation Dew as a November 2026 BTO plot on Tengah Garden Avenue, next to the Pan Island Expressway, in Tengah’s Garden District.《家饰》评述的 Plantation Dew，是2026年11月预购组屋地段，位于登加花园大道，毗邻泛岛高速公路，属登加花园区。
  • The review counts 710 sale flats, and separately 102 rental flats. The sale types are 2-room Flexi, 3-room, 4-room, 5-room and 3Gen.评述计算销售单位710个，另有出租单位102个。销售房型是二房灵活、三房、四房、五房和三代同堂组屋。
  • It calls the project Standard, with a 5-year minimum occupation period, and states a waiting time of 3 years and 2 months.评述把它列为 Standard 项目，最短居住年限5年，等候时间3年2个月。
  • The review treats the 5-room and 3Gen flats as the less common larger layouts in this project.评述把五房和三代同堂组屋，当成这个项目里较少见的较大户型。
  • Its price table, on a 99-year lease, lists 2-room Flexi flats at $131,000 to $166,000 and $158,000 to $207,000.价目表按99年屋契列出二房灵活单位：13万1000至16万6000新元，以及15万8000至20万7000新元。
  • The same table lists 3-room flats at $266,000 to $317,000 and 4-room flats at $339,000 to $445,000.同一张表列出三房26万6000至31万7000新元，四房33万9000至44万5000新元。
  • It lists 5-room flats at $494,000 to $626,000 and 3Gen flats at $551,000 to $637,000.五房是49万4000至62万6000新元，三代同堂组屋是55万1000至63万7000新元。
  • The review places the site within walking distance of the Jurong Region Line stations Tengah Park and Hong Kah, and says Jurong East is two or three stops away.评述说项目步行可到裕廊区域线的 Tengah Park 站和 Hong Kah 站，裕廊东站大约两到三站。
  • Facilities named for the project are shops, an eating house and a minimart on the ground floor, a two-storey preschool, and a kidney dialysis centre on the second storey of the multi-storey car park.文中点名的设施有地面的商店、食阁和杂货店，一所两层楼的幼儿园，以及多层停车场二楼的洗肾中心。
  • The southern edge faces the expressway. The review says outward-facing and lower-floor units on that side are the ones exposed to road noise and dust, and that Tengah is still a town under construction.南面临高速公路。评述说朝外和低层、靠近路缘的单位更容易有车声和灰尘，而且登加仍是建设中的市镇。
  • The review says Tengah has no resale transactions of its own. It uses nearby Bukit Batok and Jurong West resales as the comparison: 4-room flats about five to seven years old at $620,000 to $730,000, and 5-room flats from $780,000 to $900,000 and above.评述说登加本身还没有转售成交。它用附近武吉巴督和裕廊西的转售做比较：约五到七年的四房为62万至73万新元，五房从78万新元到90万新元以上。
