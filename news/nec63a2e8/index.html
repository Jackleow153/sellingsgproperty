Freehold condo Mirage Tower's $2,673 psf transaction sets new price benchmark永久地契公寓 Mirage Tower 以每平方英尺2673新元创下项目新高

2026/11/122026年11月12日

Source: EdgeProp.sg来源：EdgeProp.sg

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • Between 20 October and 3 November, Mirage Tower led a list of private non-landed projects that set a new price per square foot. The resale was on 26 October: a 1,227 sq ft three-bedroom for $3.28 million, or $2,673 psf.10月20日至11月3日，Mirage Tower 在一批创下每平方英尺新高的私宅项目中排在前面。成交在10月26日：一套1227平方英尺的三房，328万新元，即每平方英尺2673新元。
  • That is 7.5% above the project's former peak of $2,486 psf. The earlier sale, in July 2025, was another three-bedroom of the same size, at $3.05 million. A separate daily brief and a project directory list the same 26 October sale.这比项目先前的高位每平方英尺2486新元高出7.5%。上一宗是2025年七月另一套同样面积的三房，成交价305万新元。另一则每日简报和一个项目资料页都列出了同一宗10月26日的成交。
  • The report describes Mirage Tower as a freehold project on Kim Seng Road in District 9, with 33 storeys and 248 homes. Sizes run from studios to four-bedroom units of 570 to 1,744 sq ft, plus penthouses of 1,948 to 2,605 sq ft. It faces the Singapore River, near Robertson Quay and Great World.报道把 Mirage Tower 写成第9邮区 Kim Seng Road 的永久地契项目，33层，248个单位。面积从工作室到四房的570至1744平方英尺，另有1948至2605平方英尺的复式。它面向新加坡河，靠近 Robertson Quay 和 Great World。
  • Caveats in the same report show three resales in 2023 at an average of $2,189 psf. Six units sold this year averaged $2,469 psf, nearly 13% higher.同一篇报道里的成交记录显示，2023年有三宗转售，平均每平方英尺2189新元。今年已有六个单位成交，平均每平方英尺2469新元，高出将近13%。
  • In September a 1,744 sq ft three-bedroom sold for $4.2 million, a new high for the dollar amount of one Mirage Tower unit. At that size the price is about $2,409 psf. A project directory lists the same September sale.九月有一套1744平方英尺的三房以420万新元成交，是 Mirage Tower 单套总价的新高。按这个面积，大约每平方英尺2409新元。一个项目资料页列出了同一宗九月成交。
  • The Tessarina, a freehold condo on Wilby Road in Bukit Timah, was next. On 27 October a 1,313 sq ft three-bedroom sold for $3.4 million, or $2,589 psf, just above a 29 April sale of a 1,615 sq ft four-bedroom at $4.12 million, or $2,550 psf. The report describes Tessarina as 443 units in five blocks of six to 10 storeys, built in 2003.排在后面的是武吉知马 Wilby Road 的永久地契公寓 The Tessarina。10月27日，一套1313平方英尺的三房以340万新元成交，即每平方英尺2589新元，略高于4月29日一套1615平方英尺四房的412万新元，即每平方英尺2550新元。报道说 The Tessarina 有443个单位，五座6至10层的楼，2003年建成。
  • The same report's lowest new psf price was at Marina Collection in Sentosa Cove. On 28 October a 2,347 sq ft four-bedroom sold for $3.15 million, or $1,342 psf, under a January 2020 sale of a 2,185 sq ft four-bedroom at $3 million, or $1,373 psf. The report says the project is 99-year, completed in 2011, with 124 units.同一篇报道里，新的每平方英尺低位出现在圣淘沙湾的 Marina Collection。10月28日，一套2347平方英尺的四房以315万新元成交，即每平方英尺1342新元，低于2020年一月一套2185平方英尺四房的300万新元，即每平方英尺1373新元。报道说这个项目是99年地契，2011年建成，共124个单位。
  • That report also said UOB was selling 38 Marina Collection apartments after a loan dispute: 17 three-bedroom and 21 four-bedroom units, preview from 6 to 13 November, bookings on 14 November, on an as-is basis, from $1,269 psf and about $1,340 psf on average, roughly 20% under this year's Sentosa Cove resale average.报道还说，大华银行因一宗贷款纠纷，正在出售38套 Marina Collection：17套三房、21套四房，11月6日至13日预览，11月14日预订，按现状出售，从每平方英尺1269新元起，平均约1340新元，大约比今年圣淘沙湾转售均价低20%。

What this means if you're upgrading如果你打算升级换房

One record resale in a freehold city-fringe project tells you little about suburban condos. Compare like with like: the same region, age and unit size as the condo you are looking at.一个永久地契市区边缘项目的单笔转售纪录，对郊区公寓说明不了太多。要比较同类：同一区域、相近楼龄和面积的公寓。
