Small fraction of residential rent disputes involve corporate tenants; current laws protect landlords: MuraliSmall fraction residential 租金 disputes involve corporate tenants; current laws protect landlords: Murali

2026/11/052026年11月5日

Source: Business Times来源：商业时报

Key points要点

  • Senior Minister of State for Law Murali Pillai told Parliament that only a small share of residential rent disputes in the Small Claims Tribunal involve a company or other entity.律政部高级政务部长穆仁理在国会说，小额索赔法庭里，住宅租金纠纷只有一小部分涉及公司或其他机构。
  • From 2021 to 2025, the tribunal received about 1,100 to 1,840 claims a year related to residential leases.2021年到2025年，小额索赔法庭每年大约收到1100至1840宗与住宅租约有关的索赔。
  • Of those, about 14 to 40 claims a year, roughly 1% to 2%, named rental arrears and a company or other entity as the respondent.其中每年大约14至40宗，约1%至2%，索赔人写明是欠租，而对方是公司或其他机构。
  • He was answering questions about landlord protections during an investigation into alleged cheating, after a court found a man had used shell companies to live in homes without paying rent.他是在回答如何保障房东的提问。当时有调查正在查一起涉嫌行骗的案子。法院已认定一名男子用空壳公司住进住宅却不付租金。
  • The courts do not track this at a finer level. Murali said the small-claims numbers are only a rough guide, because they rely on what claimants write and the system does not record whether the other party is a landlord or a tenant.法院没有更细的分类。穆仁理说，小额索赔的数字只是大致参考，因为数字来自索赔人自己的描述，系统也不会记下对方是不是房东或租户。
  • Larger claims go to the civil courts, and that filing system also does not record whether a claim is for unpaid rent.较大的索赔在民事法庭提出，那里的系统同样不会记下索赔是不是欠租。
  • For residential leases of up to two years, the Small Claims Tribunal can already order payment of arrears and return of the property, if the claim is within its limit.两年或更短的住宅租约，如果金额在限额内，小额索赔法庭已经可以下令付清欠租并交还房子。
  • A landlord with a clear case can also get an order for vacant possession without a full trial. Under the Distress Act, a landlord can apply for a writ to recover arrears without first notifying the tenant.案情清楚的房东也可以在不经完整审讯的情况下，取得收回房屋的命令。根据Distress Act，房东可以申请令状追讨欠租，不必先通知租户。
  • If a company signs the lease, the company is liable, not its directors or shareholders. The court can still set the company form aside where it is used to cheat, and hold the people behind it responsible.租约若由公司签署，负责的是公司，不是董事或股东。如果公司被用来行骗，法院仍可以揭开公司的外壳，追究背后的人。
  • Murali said forcing every landlord to take a personal guarantee would remove flexibility and still would not stop someone determined to cheat. The Council for Estate Agencies publishes checklists for landlords and tenants.穆仁理说，若强制每位房东都要个人担保，会少了弹性，也挡不住存心行骗的人。房地产代理理事会有给房东和租户的核对清单。
  • Where a company tenant delays the return of a flat, a landlord can seek double rent for the period until handover, under the Civil Law Act.公司租户拖延交还房子时，房东可以根据Civil Law Act，就拖到实际交房的那段时间索取双倍租金。
  • He said policy should not leave either landlords or tenants carrying an unfair burden.他说，政策不该让房东或租户任何一方承担不公平的负担。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a landlord wait for a new law before renting to a company?房东该不该等新的法律，再租给公司？ Waiting for a tougher rule is the weaker side of this report. From 2021 to 2025, company or other-entity arrears cases in the Small Claims Tribunal were about 14 to 40 a year, roughly 1% to 2% of residential-lease claims. The courts do not track the problem more finely than that. The tools for a lease of up to two years are already there: arrears, return of the flat, a vacant-possession order without a full trial, and a distress writ. A landlord who pauses lettings until Parliament invents a new protection is pausing income against a small slice of cases.等一条更严的法律，是这份报告里较弱的一边。2021年到2025年，小额索赔法庭里公司或其他机构欠租的案子每年大约14到40宗，约占住宅租约索赔的1%至2%。法院没有更细的统计。两年以内的租约，现有工具已经包括追欠租、交还房子、不经完整审讯收回房屋，以及扣押令状。房东如果要等国会另立一条保护，停租损失的是租金，对上的却是很小的一部分案子。
  • What is in this for landlords and for tenants?这对房东和租户分别意味着什么？ The landlord’s protection is against the party that signed. A company on the lease is liable as a company, not automatically through its directors, though a court can look through the company where it is used to cheat. A personal guarantee remains a choice. Murali said making it mandatory would remove flexibility and still would not stop someone determined to cheat. A tenant who is a real operating company is not the shell-company case this debate is about. The future renter is unchanged. What changes is how carefully an owner checks who is signing.房东能追究的是签署租约的那一方。公司署名，负责的是公司，不会自动追到董事。公司若被用来行骗，法院仍可以揭开外壳。个人担保仍是选择。穆仁理说，强制每个人都做个人担保会少了弹性，也挡不住存心行骗的人。正常经营的公司租户，不是这场所辩论的空壳公司。未来的租户没有变，变的是业主核对接约人有多仔细。
  • Does this change the direction of rents?这会改变租金的方向吗？ This is not a rent forecast. It does not say rents are rising or falling. Where a company tenant holds over, the Civil Law Act route to double rent until handover is a cost of delay for that tenant, not a new market rent. Owners who rent should use the existing checklists now. Waiting does not make the court statistics any sharper.这不是租金预测，没有说租金会升或会跌。公司租户拖延交房时，按Civil Law Act追讨交房前的双倍租金，是那个租户拖延的代价，不是新的市场租金。出租的业主现在就该用现有的核对清单。再等，法院的统计也不会更细。
