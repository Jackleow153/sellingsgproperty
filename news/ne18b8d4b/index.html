Key points要点

  • A 61-year-old woman was found dead at the foot of Block 286A Toh Guan Road in Jurong East.一名61岁女子被发现倒在裕廊东杜亚干路第286A座组屋楼下，已经去世。
  • This was at about 11.20am on Friday, 23 October 2026.时间是2026年10月23日星期五，大约上午11时20分。
  • Police said she had fallen from a height.警方说她是从高处坠下。
  • She was found motionless, and an SCDF paramedic pronounced her dead at the scene.她被发现时已经没有动静，民防部队救护人员在现场宣布死亡。
  • Preliminary investigations found no foul play. Police investigations are still ongoing.初步调查没有发现刑事成分。警方调查仍在进行。
