Key points要点

  • EdgeProp, citing HDB data: a Dawson five-room resale at S$1.818 millionEdgeProp引用建屋局数据：道森五房转售181.8万新元
  • Described as an all-time high for an HDB flat被形容为组屋成交的历史高价
  • It is one transaction, not a town-wide price这是一宗交易，不是整个市镇的价钱

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Can a buyer or a seller act on $1.818 million by itself?买家或卖家能不能只凭181万8000新元就行动？ Not beyond the sentence that is actually known. EdgeProp, citing HDB data, reported a Dawson five-room resale at S$1.818 million and described it as an all-time high. This page does not give the floor or the remaining lease. A buyer who rushes, or who waits for the rest of the market to follow, is treating one transaction as a town price. The impact of waiting for more detail is better. The impact of waiting for every flat to reprice is not supported.不能超出已经知道的那一句。EdgeProp引用建屋局数据，报道道森一套五房转售181万8000新元，并写成历史高价。这一页没有楼层，也没有剩余屋契。买家如果抢着买，或等整个市场跟上来，就是把一宗交易当成市镇价钱。等更多细节，是有用的等。等每一套房子重新定价，则没有依据。
  • Who is allowed to use this as a comparable?谁才可以把这笔当成可比成交？ The seller who can cite it owns a Dawson five-room of a similar kind. Everyone else is borrowing a number without the flat that produced it. The buyer of that one home is not the buyer of the block next door. Future buyers of central five-room flats will ask for floor and lease before they accept the high as relevant.可以引用它的卖家，拥有的是同类的道森五房。其他人是在借一个数字，却没有产生这个数字的那套房子。买下那一套的人，不是隔壁座的买家。中部五房的未来买家，会先问楼层和屋契，才肯把这个高价当成相关。
  • What direction is safe to read from one all-time high?从一宗历史高价，可以稳妥地读出什么方向？ A record can sit on top of an otherwise steady market. The safe direction is that the top of Dawson five-room resale has a new high print. It is not a direction for the HDB index, and it is not a valuation of a flat this report does not describe.一宗纪录可以放在一个大体平稳的市场上面。稳妥的方向是：道森五房转售的顶端有一个新的高价。它不是组屋指数的方向，也不是这篇报道没有描述的那套房子的估价。
