1 in 5 prime-region private home resale deals made at a loss in Q3第三季核心中央区近两成私宅转售亏钱

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: Business Times来源：商业时报

Key points要点

  • Business Times, citing Cushman & Wakefield, said nearly one in five prime-location private-home resales lost money in the third quarter of 2026. The count covers caveats with a prior purchase inside the preceding 10 years.《商业时报》引用 Cushman & Wakefield，称2026年第三季近五分之一的黄金地段私宅转售亏钱。统计涵盖此前10年内有买入记录的成交。
  • Islandwide, 4.6% of the matched deals were at a loss, up from 3.7% the quarter before and 3.3% a year earlier. The paper called that the highest share since the second quarter of 2022, when it was 6.9%.全岛配对成交中，4.6%亏钱，高于前一季的3.7%和一年前的3.3%。报道称这是2022年第二季以来最高，当时是6.9%。
  • The study matched 2,021 transactions in the third quarter of 2026, and 93 of them were at a loss, which the paper called about one in 22.研究配对了2026年第三季的2021宗成交，其中93宗亏钱。报道称之为大约每22宗有一宗。
  • The Core Central Region accounted for 49% of the loss-making deals, and 19.5% of Core Central Region deals in the count were in the red, up from 17.7% the quarter before.核心中央区占亏钱成交的49%。该区成交里有19.5%亏钱，高于前一季的17.7%。
  • The loss share was 4.5% in the Rest of Central Region, up from 2.7%, and 1.6% in the Outside Central Region, up from 1.3%. Cushman put the Rest of Central Region at 30% of the loss-making deals and the Outside Central Region at 20%.中央区其余部分的亏损比例是4.5%，高于前一季的2.7%。中央区以外是1.6%，高于1.3%。Cushman 把中央区其余部分写成占亏钱成交的30%，中央区以外占20%。
  • The five largest percentage losses and the five largest dollar losses among non-landed homes were all 99-year leases, and all in the Core Central Region.非有地住宅里，百分比亏损最大的五宗和金额亏损最大的五宗，都是99年地契，而且都在核心中央区。
  • The steepest percentage loss was a 710 sq ft flat at Marina One Residences, sold in August for $1.25 million, about 32% below the $1.83 million paid in July 2018. The holding period was 8.1 years, and the annualised loss was 4.6%.百分比亏损最大的是 Marina One Residences 一套710平方英尺单位，8月以125万新元售出，比2018年7月买入的183万新元低约32%。持有8.1年，年化亏损4.6%。
  • The largest dollar loss was a 1,604 sq ft unit at OUE Twin Peaks, sold in August for $3.88 million after a March 2017 purchase at $4.9 million. The paper put the loss at about $1 million, or 20%, an annualised 2.4% over about 9.4 years.金额亏损最大的是 OUE Twin Peaks 一套1604平方英尺单位，8月以388万新元售出，2017年3月的买入价是490万新元。报道把亏损写成约100万新元，或20%，约9.4年的年化亏损是2.4%。
  • The median gain on profitable resale and sub-sale deals was 24.8%, down from a peak of 30.4% in the second quarter of 2025. The largest dollar gain was a 4,704 sq ft unit at Leedon Residence, sold in July for $14.3 million. The profit was $3.75 million on a 2017 price of $10.55 million, a gain of 35.5%, or 3.3% annualised over 9.5 years. All five of the biggest profit-making deals by dollar amount were freehold, and three of those five were in the Core Central Region.赚钱的转售和预售成交，收益中位数是24.8%，低于2025年第二季30.4%的高点。金额收益最大的是 Leedon Residence 一套4704平方英尺单位，7月以1430万新元售出。利润375万新元，2017年买入价1055万新元，收益35.5%，9.5年的年化收益是3.3%。按金额计，赚钱最多的五宗都是永久地契，其中三宗在核心中央区。
  • Among executive condominiums, the five largest percentage gains were all at Hundred Palms Residences, from 138.9% to 163.9% over about nine years. The largest was a 1,055 sq ft unit sold in September for $2.26 million, bought in July 2017 for $860,000. The paper states a gain of 163.9% and about $1.4 million, or 11.2% annualised over 9.2 years.执行共管公寓里，百分比收益最大的五宗都在 Hundred Palms Residences，约九年里从138.9%到163.9%。最大的一宗是1055平方英尺，9月以226万新元售出，2017年7月以86万新元买入。报道写明收益163.9%、约140万新元，9.2年的年化收益是11.2%。
  • Excluding executive condominiums, the five biggest percentage gains were 83.9% to 88.1%, and four of the five were freehold. The top was a 1,410 sq ft unit at The Sea View, sold in September for $4.1 million after an October 2016 purchase at $2.18 million, a gain of 88.1%, or 6.5% annualised. The analysis excludes transaction costs and stamp duties.剔除执行共管公寓后，百分比收益最大的五宗是83.9%到88.1%，其中四宗是永久地契。最高的是 The Sea View 一套1410平方英尺单位，9月以410万新元售出，2016年10月的买入价是218万新元，收益88.1%，年化6.5%。这项分析不含交易成本和印花税。
