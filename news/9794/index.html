Private home launch pipeline set to climb to 14-year high in 20272027年私人住宅推盘量料创14年新高

2026/10/312026年10月31日

Source: The Straits Times来源：海峡时报

Key points要点

  • Source of the forecast: Realion (OrangeTee) 2027 market outlook预测来源：Realion（OrangeTee）2027年市场展望
  • About 16,800 new homes in more than 30 projects, including five ECs超过30个项目、约1万6800个新住家，包括五个执行共管公寓（EC）
  • About 14,800 private units excluding ECs, the most since 15,885 units in 2013剔除执行共管公寓后约1万4800个私人单位，是2013年1万5885个单位以来最多
  • Where they sit: about 8,424 in the suburbs, 3,434 on the city fringe, 2,943 in the core central region分布：郊区约8424个，城市边缘约3434个，核心中央区约2943个
  • Projects named: Bayshore Drive (about 1,280 units), Lentor Signature, Hougang Central Residences被点名的项目：碧湾通道（约1280个单位）、Lentor Signature、Hougang Central Residences

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Is 2027’s launch pipeline a reason for a private buyer to wait?2027年的新盘管道，是私宅买家等待的理由吗？ A buyer who can wait until 2027 is looking at about 16,800 new homes in more than 30 projects, including five executive condos, and about 14,800 private units excluding those ECs, the most since 15,885 in 2013. About 8,424 of the private units are in the suburbs, 3,434 on the city fringe and 2,943 in the core. Waiting is a supply argument. It is not a promise that 2027 prices will be lower. A buyer who needs a home in 2026 is buying before that slate exists.能等到2027年的买家，面对的是超过30个项目、约1万6800个新住家，包括五个执行共管公寓。剔除EC后约1万4800个私人单位，是2013年15885个以来最多。私人单位大约8424个在郊区、3434个在城市边缘、2943个在核心区。等待是一个供应论点。它不是2027年价钱会更低的保证。2026年就要房子的人，是在这批供应出现之前买。
  • Which sellers meet that future buyer first?哪些卖家会先遇到那个未来的买家？ Resale sellers in the suburbs, and near named projects such as Bayshore Drive with about 1,280 units, Lentor Signature and Hougang Central Residences, are the ones a 2027 buyer can compare with a new launch. A seller who must move this year is selling to someone who cannot see that choice yet. Their buyer is today’s occupier or investor, not next year’s launch crowd.郊区的转售卖家，以及被点名项目附近的卖家，例如约1280个单位的碧湾通道、Lentor Signature和Hougang Central Residences，才会被2027年的买家拿来和新盘比。今年必须卖的人，卖给的是还看不到那个选择的人。他们的买家是今天的自住者或投资者，不是明年的开盘人群。
  • What direction does the largest slate in years set?多年来最大的一批供应，设定了什么方向？ The direction of private new supply is up into 2027, the heaviest in more than a decade once ECs are set aside. More choice usually limits how far resale sellers can stretch. It does not, by itself, mean prices fall, and it does not describe the HDB resale index.私人新供应的方向是到2027年增加。剔除EC之后，这是十多年来最重的一批。选择多了，通常会限制转售卖家能开多高。它本身不代表价钱下跌，也没有描述组屋转售指数。
