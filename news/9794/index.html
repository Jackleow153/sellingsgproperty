Private home launch pipeline set to climb to 14-year high in 2027

2026/10/31

Key facts

  • Source of the forecast: Realion (OrangeTee) 2027 market outlook
  • About 16,800 new homes in more than 30 projects, including five ECs
  • About 14,800 private units excluding ECs, the most since 15,885 units in 2013
  • Where they sit: about 8,424 in the suburbs, 3,434 on the city fringe, 2,943 in the core central region
  • Projects named: Bayshore Drive (about 1,280 units), Lentor Signature, Hougang Central Residences

What this means

Home buyers are likely to have more new private projects to compare in 2027. Realion, the research arm of OrangeTee, expects more than 30 projects to be ready for launch, including five executive condominiums, yielding about 16,800 homes. Leave out the ECs and the estimate is about 14,800 private units, the most since 2013, when 15,885 units were launched.

More than half of those private units, about 8,424, are expected in the suburbs, outside the central region. About 3,434 are expected on the city fringe and about 2,943 in the core central region. Projects named as ones to watch include a 1,280-unit site at Bayshore Drive, Lentor Signature in Lentor Central, and Hougang Central Residences. Suburban prices are usually the ones HDB upgraders can reach.

More choice is not the same thing as lower prices. Land and construction still have to be paid for. If you need a home before 2027, waiting is a guess. If you are selling a resale flat or condo in the next year or two, some buyers may look at a new launch instead.

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers

  • Will more new launches in 2027 make Singapore homes cheaper? More launches in 2027 do not guarantee cheaper homes. Compare a home you can buy now with what it costs you to keep waiting.
  • What does the 2027 supply mean for an HDB upgrader? HDB upgraders who can wait should use the extra suburban supply to be picky about the MRT walk, the layout and the total price, not only a brochure discount.
  • What does a bigger launch pipeline mean for resale sellers? Resale sellers should not assume a busy new launch nearby will lift their price. Some buyers will simply pick a 2027 project instead.
