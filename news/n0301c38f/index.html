November BTO Draws Over 35,600 Applications — With Two-Room Flats Seeing Record Number of Applicants2026年十一月预购组屋申请超过35600份

2026/11/112026年11月11日

Source: Stacked Homes来源：Stacked Homes

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • At 5pm on 11 November, the November 2026 exercise had more than 35,600 applicants for 7,964 flats, an overall rate of 4.5. That is the highest rate since August 2022 and the most applications since October 2024.11月11日下午5时，2026年十一月这一轮有超过35600人申请7964个单位，整体申请率4.5。这是自2022年八月以来最高的申请率，也是2024年十月以来申请人数最多的一次。
  • Applications were 46.4% higher than the 23,894 filed in the June 2026 exercise. The round has seven projects in Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun, and applications were still open until 11:59pm that day.申请人数比2026年六月的23894份高出46.4%。这一轮有七个项目，分布在 Bedok、Geylang、Sembawang、Tengah、Toa Payoh 和 Yishun，当天申请开放至11:59pm。
  • Lee Sze Teck of Huttons Asia links the turnout to an income ceiling of $16,000 a month for families and $8,000 for singles, and to the Bayshore and Toa Payoh sites.Huttons Asia 的 Lee Sze Teck 把人潮联系到家庭月入顶限每月1万6000新元、单身8000新元，以及 Bayshore 和 Toa Payoh 的地点。
  • Toa Payoh Hillside has the dearest four-room flat in the exercise at $832,000, and still drew almost 6,000 applicants. It is the Prime project. Lee points to sports facilities, a polyclinic, a library and a new town park, the Toa Payoh Integrated Development opening in 2030, some balconies, and MacRitchie views from higher floors.Toa Payoh Hillside 的四房是本轮最贵的，价格83万2000新元，仍有将近6000人申请。它是 Prime 项目。Lee 提到体育设施、诊疗所、图书馆和新市镇公园，Toa Payoh Integrated Development 将于2030年开放，部分单位有阳台，较高楼层可望向 MacRitchie Reservoir。
  • Christine Sun says five-room flats in Tengah drew first-timer families at 3.8 and second-timer families at 21.3, against 0.3 and 2.5 for the same flat type in Sembawang. She also links extra interest in the Tengah project to white flats, which leave the layout more open.Christine Sun 说，Tengah 五房的首次购房家庭申请率是3.8，第二次是21.3；Sembawang 同房型是0.3和2.5。她也把 Tengah 项目多出来的兴趣，联系到白胚单位，因为户型可以自己安排。
  • Toa Payoh Hillside is the only project with Community Care Apartments. They drew 494 applicants for 296 flats, a rate of 2.0. The minimum age fell from 65 to 55, and the monthly fee was cut by 18%.Toa Payoh Hillside 是唯一设有社区护理公寓的项目，494人申请296个单位，申请率2.0。最低年龄从65岁降到55岁，每月费用降低18%。
  • The senior rate for a two-room Flexi flat at Toa Payoh Hillside was 6.1, above the rate at Toa Payoh Ascent in July 2025. The article says 43,610 residents aged 55 and above live in HDB flats in Toa Payoh, nearly 36% of the town's HDB residents.Toa Payoh Hillside 的两房 Flexi，年长者申请率是6.1，高于2025年七月的 Toa Payoh Ascent。文章说 Toa Payoh 的组屋里有43610名55岁及以上居民，约占该镇组屋居民的36%。
  • Two-room Flexi flats drew 12,670 applications, the most for that flat type in three years. The previous high was 11,188 in October 2025. Among first-timer singles, Plantation Dew in Tengah reached 17.9.两房 Flexi 共收到12670份申请，是三年来这个房型最多的一次。上一次高位是2025年十月的11188份。首次购房单身之中，Tengah 的 Plantation Dew 达到17.9。
  • Eugene Lim says Tengah had no BTO launch after Plantation Edge I and II in October 2023. Plantation Dew's wait is three years and two months. Two-room Flexi prices there start at $11,000 after grants, against $34,000 at Plantation Edge, with nearby resale prices of $322,500 to $388,000.Eugene Lim 说，Tengah 自2023年十月的 Plantation Edge I 和 II 之后就没有再推出预购组屋。Plantation Dew 的等待时间是三年两个月。那里的两房 Flexi 在计入津贴后从1万1000新元起，Plantation Edge 当时是3万4000新元，附近转售价是32万2500新元到38万8000新元。
  • Mohan Sandrasegeran points to Plantation Plaza, Parc Point, the Jurong Region Line, and ACS (Primary) moving to Tengah in 2030. At Bayshore Sails, a Plus project, the three-room first-timer rate was 2.8 and the second-timer rate was 54.9, with 718 applications for 90 flats.Mohan Sandrasegeran 提到 Plantation Plaza、Parc Point、Jurong Region Line，以及 ACS (Primary) 在2030年迁到 Tengah。Plus 项目 Bayshore Sails 的三房，首次购房家庭申请率2.8，第二次54.9，90个单位有718份申请。
  • Chencharu Acres had 560 applications for 84 three-room flats, a first-timer rate of 2.3 and a second-timer rate of 25.3, and it is one bus stop from Khatib MRT. Among four-room flats, Toa Payoh Hillside's second-timer rate was 42.7 and the first-timer rate was 6.7, with 5,980 applications recorded.Chencharu Acres 的84个三房有560份申请，首次购房家庭申请率2.3，第二次25.3，距离 Khatib 地铁站一个巴士站。四房之中，Toa Payoh Hillside 的第二次购房家庭申请率是42.7，首次是6.7，文章记录到5980份申请。
