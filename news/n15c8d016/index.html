Bayshore Sails BTO 2026: What Bedok HDB Owners Selling Now Face, 4-Room Median $600,000Bayshore Sails 2026年预购组屋：勿洛四房转售中位数为60万新元

2026/11/052026年11月5日

Source: PropertyNet.SG来源：PropertyNet.SG

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • PropertyNet.SG writes about HDB’s launch on 4 November 2026 of 7,964 flats across seven projects, including the Plus project Bayshore Sails in Bedok.PropertyNet.SG 写的是建屋发展局2026年11月4日的推介：七个项目共7964个单位，其中包括勿洛的 Plus 项目 Bayshore Sails。
  • The article says this is the first BTO exercise since the family income ceiling rose to $16,000 and the singles ceiling to $8,000.文章说，这是家庭入息顶限调高到1万6000新元、单身顶限调高到8000新元之后的第一轮预购组屋。
  • In the HDB table it reprints, Bayshore Sails 4-room flats start from $559,000 before grants and from $504,000 including grants. The nearby resale range in that table is $905,000 to $985,000.文中引用的建屋发展局表格显示，Bayshore Sails 四房未计津贴由55万9000新元起，计入津贴后由50万4000新元起。同一表格里的附近转售是90万5000至98万5000新元。
  • The article says that nearby range is for flats close to the site, and is higher than Bedok as a whole.文章说，这个附近区间指的是靠近地段的单位，高于整个勿洛的水平。
  • The same table puts 2-room Flexi flats from $208,000 before grants and from $88,000 with grants, and 3-room flats from $409,000 before grants and from $319,000 with grants, against nearby resale deals of $662,000 to $705,000.同一张表里，二房灵活单位未计津贴由20万8000新元起，计入津贴后由8万8000新元起。三房未计津贴由40万9000新元起，计入津贴后由31万9000新元起。附近转售是66万2000至70万5000新元。
  • Bayshore Sails is Plus, with a 9% subsidy recovery and a 10-year minimum occupation period. The article says booking is tentatively from March 2027, so the flats cannot be resold before 2037, and likely later, because the 10 years start at key collection.Bayshore Sails 属 Plus，津贴收回9%，最短居住年限10年。文章说订购暂定2027年3月开始，因此2037年之前不能转售，而且很可能更晚，因为这10年从领取钥匙起算。
  • Applications in the article run from 4 to 11 November 2026, with queue positions in January 2027.文中的申请期是2026年11月4日至11日，排队序号在2027年1月公布。
  • From November 2025 to October 2026, the article’s count of HDB resale registrations puts the Bedok 4-room median at $600,000, up 2.6% on the previous 12 months, from 539 deals. The Singapore 4-room median in the same table is $630,000.2025年11月至2026年10月，文章统计的组屋转售登记显示，勿洛四房中位数为60万新元，比前12个月高2.6%，共539宗。同一张表里，全岛四房中位数是63万新元。
  • In that same 12-month count, the Bedok 3-room median is $415,000 from 503 deals, down 3.1%. The 5-room median is $804,000 from 230 deals, up 4.4%. The executive median is $1,020,000 from 73 deals, up 2.3%.同一段12个月，勿洛三房中位数是41万5000新元，503宗，下跌3.1%。五房中位数是80万4000新元，230宗，上升4.4%。公寓式组屋中位数是102万新元，73宗，上升2.3%。
  • Across all flat types, the article counts 1,363 Bedok deals at a median of $556,888, up 1.6%.所有房型合计，文章计算勿洛有1363宗成交，中位数55万6888新元，上升1.6%。
  • It also gives a Bedok 4-room rent median of $3,380 a month for an owner who has already met the minimum occupation period: 5 years for Standard flats, and 10 years for Plus and Prime.文章还给出勿洛四房的租金中位数，每月3380新元，条件是屋主已经满最短居住年限：Standard 为5年，Plus 和 Prime 为10年。
  • The article reports an LTA announcement on 4 November 2026 that Thomson-East Coast Line Stage 5 and the Downtown Line 3 extension open on 26 December 2026, adding Bedok South, the Sungei Bedok interchange and Xilin.文章引述陆路交通管理局2026年11月4日的公布：汤申–东海岸线第五阶段和滨海市区线第三阶段延长线于2026年12月26日开通，新增 Bedok South、Sungei Bedok 转车站和 Xilin。
