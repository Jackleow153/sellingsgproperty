Rare, roomy and pricey? Inside Singapore's niche jumbo HDB flat market宽敞也昂贵？新加坡巨型组屋这个小众市场

2026/11/012026年11月1日

Source: CNA来源：CNA

Key points要点

  • CNA, 1 November 2026: a feature on the niche market for jumbo HDB flatsCNA，2026年11月1日：关于巨型组屋小众市场的报道
  • Jumbo flats are usually two HDB flats combined, so stock is small巨型组屋通常是两套HDB组屋打通，数量少
  • Eligibility and the remaining lease still decide whether you can buy one能不能买，仍要看购买资格和剩余屋契

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a buyer wait for jumbo flats to become easier to find?买家该不该等巨型组屋变得比较好找？ Waiting will not make jumbo flats ordinary. They are usually two HDB flats combined, so the stock stays small. A buyer who needs a normal four- or five-room flat should shop that market. A buyer who specifically wants the space has a thin list and should not expect a later launch to create more of them.再等，巨型组屋也不会变成普通货。它们通常是两套组屋打通，数量一直少。需要普通四房或五房的人，该去那个市场找。专门要这个空间的人，清单很薄，不该指望以后的开卖会多造出几套。
  • Who can actually buy one, and what does that mean for the seller?谁真正买得了，对卖家意味着什么？ Eligibility and the remaining lease still decide whether a person can buy one. That is a smaller future buyer than the crowd at a normal resale. A seller of a jumbo has few comparables and few qualified buyers. Pricing it off a standard five-room sale misses both the scarcity and the people who are allowed to bid. The impact of a long wait is a shorter lease, which can knock eligible buyers out.购买资格和剩余屋契仍然决定一个人买不买得了。未来的买家比普通转售的人少。巨型组屋的卖家，可比成交少，合格的买家也少。拿标准五房的成交来定价，既错过稀缺，也错过有资格出价的人。等得太久的影响是屋契更短，可能把本来合格的买家排除掉。
  • Does this niche move the wider HDB market?这个小众会带动整体组屋市场吗？ This is a niche, not the direction of HDB prices. Scarcity can hold the few jumbos up. It does not lift the town index, and a jumbo sale is not a comparable for the flat next door that was never combined.这是小众，不是组屋价钱的方向。稀缺可以托住少数巨型组屋。它不会抬起市镇指数，一宗巨型组屋的成交也不是隔壁那套从未打通的单位的可比价。
