Rare, roomy and pricey? Inside Singapore's niche jumbo HDB flat market宽敞也昂贵？新加坡巨型组屋这个小众市场

2026/11/012026年11月1日Source: CNA来源：CNA

Key facts重点

  • CNA, 1 November 2026: a feature on the niche market for jumbo HDB flatsCNA，2026年11月1日：关于巨型组屋小众市场的报道
  • Jumbo flats are usually two HDB flats combined, so stock is small巨型组屋通常是两套HDB组屋打通，数量少
  • The headline does not state a price, a floor area or a town标题没有给出价钱、面积或市镇
  • Eligibility and the remaining lease still decide whether you can buy one能不能买，仍要看购买资格和剩余屋契

What this means这对你意味着什么

CNA looked at Singapore’s jumbo HDB flats and described the market as rare, roomy and pricey. A jumbo flat is usually two HDB flats combined. There are not many of them, so one listing can look louder than the rest of the resale market.CNA看了新加坡的巨型组屋（jumbo flat），用罕见、宽敞、昂贵来形容这个市场。巨型组屋通常是两套HDB组屋打通。数量不多，所以一套挂牌会比普通转售市场更显眼。

The headline does not give a new price, a size, or a town. That is the useful warning. A buyer who wants the extra space still has to check whether they are allowed to buy that flat, how many years are left on the lease, and what a normal 5-room in the same town costs.标题里没有新的价钱、面积或市镇。这本身就是提醒。想要更大空间的买家，仍然要核对自己能不能买、屋契还剩多少年，以及同一个市镇一套普通五房组屋要多少钱。

Sellers of jumbo flats should not treat “pricey” as a number they can write on the listing. Buyers comparing a jumbo with a private condo should put the monthly loan, the conservancy or maintenance, and the lease next to each other before they decide the jumbo is the bigger home.卖巨型组屋的人，不能把“昂贵”当成可以写上挂牌的数字。如果买家同时在看私人公寓，应该把每月供款、管理费和屋契放在一起，再决定巨型组屋是不是更大的家。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Is a jumbo HDB flat just a large resale flat?巨型组屋是不是就是一套比较大的转售组屋？ Ask HDB whether your household can buy that jumbo flat, and read the remaining lease. Extra rooms do not remove those rules.先问建屋发展局，你的家庭能不能买这套巨型组屋，并看清剩余屋契。房间多，不会取消这些规则。
  • What should a buyer compare before paying a jumbo premium?买家在支付巨型组屋溢价之前该比较什么？ Put the jumbo’s price next to a 5-room in the same town and, if you are also looking at condos, next to the condo’s monthly cost. Space is only a bargain if the loan still fits.把巨型组屋的价钱和同市镇的五房组屋放在一起。如果也在看公寓，就把每月开销放在旁边。贷款仍负担得起，空间才算划算。
  • Can a seller use the word pricey as the asking price?卖家可以把“昂贵”直接当成开价吗？ Price the flat from recent sales of a similar size and lease. A feature calling the market pricey is not a valuation.用相近面积和屋契的近期成交来定价。一篇说市场昂贵的报道，不是估价。
