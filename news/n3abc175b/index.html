Singapore explores build-to-rent model as demand for independent living grows新加坡 explores build-to-租金 model demand independent living grows

2026/11/032026年11月3日

Source: Borneo Bulletin来源：Borneo Bulletin

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • The Ministry of National Development said home ownership remains central, and there is also demand for longer-stay rental housing from younger Singaporeans who want to live on their own or rent before they buy, and from people who come to work or study.国家发展部说，拥屋仍然是住房政策的核心。同时，想先独立生活或先租后买的年轻新加坡人，以及来工作或求学的人，也需要可以住得较久的租赁住房。
  • National Development Minister Chee Hong Tat said in August that the authorities are looking at build-to-rent, co-living and long-stay serviced apartments for that demand.国家发展部长徐芳达在8月说，当局正在研究建造供出租（build-to-rent）、共享居住和长住型服务式公寓，来回应这种需求。
  • Build-to-rent means a block is built to be rented for years, rather than sold off at a launch.建造供出租是指整栋房子建来出租多年，而不是在开盘时卖掉。
  • Sociologist Tan Ern Ser said most Singaporeans still prefer to own, and a build-to-rent option could be a stopgap for people who want their own space.社会学者陈恩赐说，多数新加坡人还是更想买房。建造供出租可以给想有自己空间的人一个过渡。
  • A co-living pilot under the SG Youth Plan drew interest from more than 600 Singaporeans aged 21 to 35 for just over 100 units, priced about 30% below market.新加坡青年计划下的一个共享居住试点，100多个单位吸引了超过600名21岁至35岁的新加坡人。租金大约比市价低30%。
  • Private long-stay serviced apartments, introduced in 2023, are one form of build-to-rent. Since December 2023, two government land-sale sites for them have been awarded, and 21 applications on private land have been approved.2023年推出的私人长住型服务式公寓，是建造供出租的一种。2023年12月以来，两幅允许这类项目的政府售地已批出，私人土地上有21份申请获批。
  • OCBC’s Chez Anbu put a sustainable rent for young people who still plan to buy later at about $1,200 to $1,800 a month.华侨银行的Chez Anbu估计，还打算以后买房的年轻人，负担得起的月租大约是1200至1800新元。
  • One researcher pictured mostly studios and one-bedroom units of about 350 to 550 square feet, plus some two-bedroom units.一名研究者设想，这类房子大多是约350至550平方英尺的工作室和一房单位，再加上一些两房单位。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should a young buyer wait for build-to-rent instead of buying?年轻买家该不该等建造供出租，而不去买？ Waiting for a build-to-rent flat to replace a purchase is waiting without a date. The Ministry of National Development still puts home ownership at the centre. Build-to-rent, co-living and long-stay serviced apartments are being looked at for people who want their own place before they buy, or who are here to work or study. Nothing in this report is a project a buyer can book. A buyer who can take an HDB flat or a condo now is not helped by pausing for a model that is still a study.等一套建造供出租的房子来代替购买，是在等一个没有日期的东西。国家发展部仍把拥屋放在中心。建造供出租、共居和长住服务式公寓，是在研究给那些想先有自己的地方、或来工作读书的人。这份报道里没有一个可以预订的项目。现在买得起组屋或公寓的人，停下来等一个仍在研究的模式，得不到帮助。
  • Who is this housing for, and who is it not for?这是给谁住的，又不是给谁的？ The demand signal is rental, not a fall in sale prices. A co-living pilot drew more than 600 Singaporeans aged 21 to 35 for just over 100 units at about 30% below market. OCBC’s Chez Anbu put a sustainable rent for young people who still plan to buy at about $1,200 to $1,800 a month. That is a different conversation from an about-$2,000 build-to-rent idea discussed elsewhere. Landlords of today’s flats are not yet competing with a new purpose-built block. The likely tenant, if it is built, is a younger person in a studio or one-bedroom of about 350 to 550 square feet.需求信号在租金，不在售价下跌。一项共居试验有超过600名21至35岁的新加坡人，争100多个单位，价钱大约比市价低30%。华侨银行旗下Chez Anbu认为，仍打算以后买房的年轻人，负担得起的租金大约每月1200至1800新元。这和别处讨论的大约2000新元建造供出租，不是同一件事。今天的房东还没有对上新建的专用出租楼。如果建成，租户多半是住约350至550平方英尺单房或一房的年轻人。
  • What does the idea do to prices and to rents?这个想法对房价和租金有什么影响？ Two government land-sale sites for long-stay serviced apartments have been awarded since December 2023, and 21 private-land applications have been approved. That is rental supply at the margin. It does not point HDB or condo prices down. The direction of the ownership market is unchanged: most Singaporeans still prefer to own, and this is a stopgap beside that.2023年12月以来，两幅长住服务式公寓的政府售地已经批出，私人土地上也批准了21份申请。这是边缘上的出租供应。它没有把组屋或公寓售价往下指。拥屋市场的方向没变：多数新加坡人仍想拥有，这只是旁边的一个过渡。
