One in five prime-region private home resale deals sold at a loss in Q3第三季黄金地段私人住宅转售，每五宗有一宗亏本

2026/11/012026年11月1日

Source: Business Times来源：商业时报

Key facts重点

  • Business Times, third quarter of 2026: one in five prime-region private resale deals sold at a loss《商业时报》，2026年第三季：黄金地段私人转售每五宗有一宗亏本
  • Share of loss-making deals reported at 4.6%, the highest in four years亏本交易比例报道为4.6%，是四年来最高
  • Executive condos were reported as the segment with the biggest percentage gains执行共管公寓（EC）被报道为涨幅百分比最大的板块
  • A loss is measured against what that owner paid, not against the market index亏本是对照那位屋主的买入价，不是对照市场指数

What this means这对你意味着什么

Business Times reported that one in five private-home resale deals in the prime region sold at a loss in the third quarter of 2026. The same report put the share of loss-making deals at 4.6%, the highest in four years, and said executive condos posted the biggest percentage gains.《商业时报》报道，2026年第三季，黄金地段的私人住宅转售里，每五宗就有一宗是亏本卖出。同一则报道把亏本交易的比例写在4.6%，是四年来最高，并指执行共管公寓（EC）的涨幅百分比最大。

A loss here means that owner sold for less than they paid. It is not the same thing as the official price index falling. One central condo can lose money while a suburban executive condo still shows a gain. Prime region usually means the central districts buyers treat as the top of the private market.这里的亏本，是指屋主卖出价低于当初买入价。这和官方房价指数下跌不是同一件事。一套市中心公寓可以亏本，郊区的执行共管公寓仍可能上涨。黄金地段通常指买家心目中私人市场最高的那一片中央区。

If you are buying in those districts, do not read “one in five” as a discount on the unit you like. Ask what that owner paid, and when. If you are selling at a paper loss, the headline is not a reason to cut the price before you know what similar homes on your street actually achieved.如果你在这些区买房，不要把“五宗里有一宗”当成你看中的那套已经打折。先问清那位屋主当年用什么价钱、在哪一年买入。如果你账面是亏的，也不要因为这则标题就先降价，先看你那条街上类似房子的真实成交。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Does one loss-making resale mean central condos are cheap now?一宗亏本转售，是不是代表市中心公寓现在变便宜了？ Check the owner’s purchase price and the year they bought. A loss on one stack does not reset the asking price of the flat next door.核对那位屋主的买入价和买入年份。一套单位亏本，不会自动改写隔壁单位的开价。
  • What should a seller sitting on a paper loss do with this headline?账面亏损的卖家该怎么看这则标题？ Get two or three recent sales of a similar size before you drop your price. A headline about other people’s losses is not a valuation of your home.先拿两三宗面积相近的近期成交，再决定要不要降价。别人亏本的标题，不是你这套房子的估价。
  • Why can executive condos rise while some prime-region homes lose money?为什么执行共管公寓可以涨，黄金地段的一些房子却亏本？ Executive condos and prime-region condos are different products, with different buyers and different rules. Do not use one segment’s gain to price the other.执行共管公寓和黄金地段私人公寓是不同产品，买家和规则都不一样。不要用一个板块的涨幅去定另一个板块的价钱。
