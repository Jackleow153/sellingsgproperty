Real estate房地产

Location, growth prospects drive strongest BTO demand since 2022地段和增长前景带动2022年以来最强的预购组屋申请

2026/11/132026年11月13日

Source: Singapore Business Review来源：新加坡商业评论

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • At 5pm on 11 November, Huttons counted 35,603 applicants for 7,964 flats in the November exercise, about 4.5 people per flat. The Straits Times and EdgeProp reported the same 5pm count.11月11日下午5时，Huttons 统计十一月这一轮有35603人申请7964个单位，大约每单位4.5人。《海峡时报》和 EdgeProp 也报道了同一个下午5时的数字。
  • Huttons called that the highest application rate since August 2022, and the largest number of applications since October 2024. The Straits Times reports both comparisons as well.Huttons 称这是2022年八月以来最高的申请率，也是2024年十月以来申请人数最多的一次。《海峡时报》同样写出了这两项比较。
  • The busiest project in the report was Toa Payoh Hillside, with almost 6,000 applicants. EdgeProp reported the same turnout. Its four-room flats were the dearest in the exercise, at $832,000.报道里申请最集中的是 Toa Payoh Hillside，将近6000人。EdgeProp 也报道了这个人数。那里的四房是本轮最贵的，价格83万2000新元。
  • Kelvin Fong, chief executive of PropNex, said the project held 18% of the flats on offer and about 28% of the applicants. He said applicants were accepting a 10-year minimum occupation period, tighter resale and rental rules, and a subsidy recovery for a central location.博纳首席执行官 Kelvin Fong 说，这个项目占推出单位的18%，却约占申请人的28%。他说申请人接受10年最低居住年限、更紧的转售和出租规则，以及补贴回收，以换一个中心地段。
  • Huttons pointed to the site beside Caldecott MRT, planned commercial and community amenities, and unobstructed views of MacRitchie Reservoir from some units.Huttons 提到项目就在 Caldecott 地铁站旁，日后有商业和社区设施，部分单位可无遮挡望向 MacRitchie Reservoir。
  • The 35,603 figure is the 5pm reading on 11 November. The Straits Times said applications stayed open until 11.59pm that night, so this is not a final close count.35603这个数字是11月11日下午5时的读数。《海峡时报》说申请当天开放到晚上11时59分，所以这不是截止后的最终人数。
