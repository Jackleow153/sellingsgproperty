Key points要点

  • The article’s own figure is S$1.818 million, not S$1.8 billion. That is the price of a five-room HDB resale in Queenstown, described as the highest on record.文章里的成交价是181万8000新元，不是18亿元。这是女皇镇一个五房式转售组屋，被写成有记录以来的最高价。
  • The 30 October transaction was for a unit at Block 96 Dawson Road, in SkyParc @ Dawson.10月30日的交易来自道森路第96座，属于SkyParc @ Dawson。
  • The flat is about 111 square metres, or 1,195 square feet, on the 40th to 42nd storeys, at about $1,521 per square foot.单位大约111平方米，即1195平方英尺，位于40楼至42楼，约每平方英尺1521新元。
  • About 94 years remain on the lease. SkyParc @ Dawson was completed in 2020.屋契大约还剩94年。SkyParc @ Dawson在2020年建成。
  • Another unit in the project sold for $1.7 million in February.这个项目另有一个单位在2月以170万新元成交。
  • The seller’s agent, Ted Wong of ERA, said the flat drew more than eight offers, and the top three were above $1.8 million.卖方经纪、ERA的Ted Wong说，这个单位收到超过八个出价，前三个都超过180万新元。
  • He pointed to the size, the central location, the amenities, a view of greenery, and a small supply of five-room flats. Only 74 of the 284 units in the block are five-room.他说原因包括面积、市中心地段、配套、绿化景观，以及五房式供应少。这座楼284个单位里，只有74个是五房式。
  • From June 2025 to September 2026, one five-room flat on Holland Drive and four on Dawson Road sold between $1.58 million and $1.7 million.2025年6月到2026年9月，荷兰通道有一个五房式、道森路有四个五房式，成交价在158万至170万新元之间。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • Should anyone treat this as a new price for five-room flats?这该被当成五房式的新价钱吗？ The figure to use is S$1.818 million, not S$1.8 billion. Acting on the billion number would be acting on a mistake. Even the million-dollar print is one five-room flat at Block 96 Dawson Road, SkyParc @ Dawson, about 1,195 square feet on storeys 40 to 42, with about 94 years left. Waiting for this sale to pull ordinary five-room prices up or down is waiting on the wrong flat. From June 2025 to September 2026, one five-room on Holland Drive and four on Dawson Road sold between $1.58 million and $1.7 million.该用的数字是181万8000新元，不是18亿元。按十亿去行动，是按一个错误行动。就算是百万这一笔，也只是道森路第96座、SkyParc @ Dawson的一套五房式，约1195平方英尺，40至42楼，屋契大约还剩94年。等这一笔把普通五房的价钱拉高或拉低，等的是另一套房子。2025年6月到2026年9月，荷兰通道一套五房、道森路四套五房，成交在158万至170万新元。
  • Which seller has a real comparable, and who is the buyer?哪个卖家才有真正的可比成交，买家是谁？ The seller who can use the print owns one of the 74 five-room flats in that 284-unit block. The buyer who paid it beat more than eight offers, and the top three were above $1.8 million. That buyer wanted size, a central address, amenities and a view, in a project completed in 2020. A seller of a smaller Queenstown flat, or of a five-room somewhere else, does not have this buyer.用得上这笔成交的卖家，拥有的是这座284个单位里74套五房式之一。付这个价的买家，压过了超过八个出价，前三个都超过180万。他要的是面积、市中心、配套和景观，项目在2020年建成。女皇镇更小的单位，或其他地方的五房，没有这个买家。
  • What is the direction, once the billion figure is set aside?把十亿那个数字放下之后，方向是什么？ The top of central HDB resale is still being bid, including a $1.7 million sale in the same project in February. That is the direction of a scarce product: new, large, high floor, long lease, Queenstown. It is not the direction of the HDB index, and it is not a billion-dollar market.中部组屋转售的顶端仍有人出价，包括这个项目2月的一笔170万。方向属于稀缺的产品：新、大、高楼、长屋契、女皇镇。它不是组屋指数的方向，也不是一个十亿元的市场。
