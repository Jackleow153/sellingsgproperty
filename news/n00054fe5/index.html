Cat Licensing in Singapore: What HDB Residents Need to KnowCat Licensing 新加坡: What 组屋 Residents Need Know

2026/10/202026年10月20日

Source: PropertyGuru来源：PropertyGuru

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • The transition for pet-cat licensing ran from 1 September 2024 to 31 August 2026. From 1 September 2026, keeping a pet cat without a licence is an offence.宠物猫牌照的过渡期是2024年9月1日到2026年8月31日。从2026年9月1日起，饲养没有牌照的宠物猫即属违法。
  • An HDB household may keep up to two cats, plus one approved small-breed dog.一组屋家庭最多可以养两只猫，外加一只获准的小型犬。
  • A cat owned before 1 September 2024 can stay even if that puts the household over the cap, as long as it was licensed during the transition.2024年9月1日之前已经饲养的猫，只要在过渡期内办了牌照，即使超过上限也可以继续养。
  • A household that missed the transition may license and keep only up to two cats in an HDB flat. In a private home the limit is up to three cats.错过过渡期的家庭，在组屋里最多只能办牌照并饲养两只猫。私人住宅的上限是三只猫。
  • The fine is up to $4,000.罚款最高4000新元。
  • From 1 September 2026, a one-year licence costs S$15 for a sterilised cat and S$90 for a cat that is not sterilised.从2026年9月1日起，已绝育的猫一年牌照15新元，未绝育的猫一年牌照90新元。
  • The cat has to be microchipped by a vet. A first-time applicant also takes a free AVS course of about 20 to 30 minutes.猫必须由兽医植入微芯片。第一次申请的人还要上大约20到30分钟的AVS免费课程。
  • Owners are told to check windows and doors. A tenant should agree the cat with the flat owner before signing the lease.文章也提醒主人检查窗户和门。租户应在签约前先和屋主说好养猫。

3 Takeaways for Buyers & Sellers给买家与卖家的3个要点

  • What should a buyer check before acting on this headline?买家在根据这则标题行动之前该核对什么？ HDB buyers should compare the headline with the actual flat, the remaining lease, the town and the monthly mortgage. Walk away from any purchase that only makes sense because of a headline.买家要核对房子、地点和每月能负担的开销。只因为一条标题而划算的交易，就不该成交。
  • What should a seller avoid assuming?卖家不该擅自假设什么？ HDB sellers should not copy one headline price onto their own block. Buyers will ask about floor, lease and condition. Show the lease, the size and recent nearby sales rather than a screenshot of the news.卖家要用屋契、面积和附近的成交来回答问题，而不是只拿出新闻截图。
  • Which figure, if any, is safe to repeat?哪些数字可以复述？ Only repeat figures that are in the headline. Do not fill gaps with a guess about grants, timing or a project that was not named.只复述标题里出现的数字。标题没有写的补助、时间表或项目名称，不要自己补。
