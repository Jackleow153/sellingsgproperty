5 Of Singapore’s Most Expensive HDB Flats For Sale五套在售组屋叫价156万至190万元

2026/11/132026年11月13日

Source: Stacked Homes来源：Stacked Homes

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • A Stacked Homes review on 13 November listed five HDB flats for sale, all on 99-year leases, with asking prices from $1,560,000 to $1,899,999. The floor areas run from about 1,130 sq ft to just over 2,000 sq ft. These are asking prices in that list, not completed resale prices.11月13日的 Stacked Homes 评论列出五套在售组屋，都是99年地契，叫价从156万新元到189万9999新元。面积大约从1130平方英尺到刚过2000平方英尺。这些是该名单里的叫价，不是已成交的转售价。
  • The highest ask was $1,899,999 for a flat at 147 Mei Ling Street, with TOP in 1993. Stacked put the size at 1,651 sq ft, or $1,150.82 psf, with 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, a walk from Queenstown MRT.最高叫价是美龄街147号的189万9999新元，入伙年份1993年。Stacked 写面积1651平方英尺，合每平方英尺1150.82新元，四房三卫，步行可达 Queenstown 地铁站。
  • Pinnacle @ Duxton, at 1 Cantonment Road, with TOP in 2009, was listed at $1,738,888 for 1,130 sq ft, or $1,538.84 psf, above the 30th storey. The review's prose calls it a five-room corner unit, while the listing line says 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms.Pinnacle @ Duxton 位于广东民路1号，2009年入伙，叫价173万8888新元，面积1130平方英尺，合每平方英尺1538.84新元，在30楼以上。正文称它是五房转角单位，列表则写三房两卫。
  • A flat at 107A Bidadari Park Drive, with TOP in 2021, was listed at $1,560,000 for 1,292 sq ft, or $1,207.43 psf, with 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. Stacked described it as a multi-generation flat whose minimum occupation period was reached this year, within a walk of Woodleigh and Potong Pasir.比达达利园道107A，2021年入伙，叫价156万新元，面积1292平方英尺，合每平方英尺1207.43新元，四房三卫。Stacked 称这是三代同堂组屋，最低居住年限于今年届满，步行可达 Woodleigh 和 Potong Pasir。
  • An executive maisonette at 454 Sin Ming Avenue, with TOP in 1989, was listed at $1,600,000 for 2,013 sq ft, or $794.83 psf, with 5 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, between Bright Hill and Upper Thomson.Sin Ming Avenue 454号的公寓式组屋，1989年入伙，叫价160万新元，面积2013平方英尺，合每平方英尺794.83新元，五房两卫，位于 Bright Hill 和 Upper Thomson 之间。
  • A flat at 464A Clementi Avenue 1, with TOP in 2022, was listed at $1,588,888 for 1,216 sq ft, or $1,306.65 psf, above the 25th storey, a walk from Clementi MRT. The prose calls it a five-room flat, while the listing line says 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms.金文泰1道464A，2022年入伙，叫价158万8888新元，面积1216平方英尺，合每平方英尺1306.65新元，在25楼以上，步行可达 Clementi 地铁站。正文称五房，列表写三房两卫。

What this means if you're upgrading如果你打算升级换房

These are asking prices, not sales. If you own a big, well-located flat, price it off recent transacted prices in your block, not off the highest listings, before you size your condo budget.这些是叫价，不是成交价。如果你拥有地点好、面积大的组屋，先用同一座组屋最近的成交价来估价，而不是看最高的挂牌价，再定公寓预算。
