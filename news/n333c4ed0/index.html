UOB prices 38 Marina Collection units from $1,269 psf, below recent Sentosa Cove resalesUOB 价格38 Marina Collection 个单位 $1,269每平方英尺, below recent 圣淘沙 Cove resales

2026/10/292026年10月29日

Source: EdgeProp.sg来源：EdgeProp.sg

Real Estate SG

Key points要点

  • UOB is selling 38 units at Marina Collection in Sentosa Cove, with asking prices from $1,269 per square foot.大华银行正在出售圣淘沙湾Marina Collection的38个单位，叫价从每平方英尺1269新元起。
  • The batch is 17 three-bedroom units and 21 four-bedroom units.这批房子有17个三房式和21个四房式。
  • The average asking price is about $1,340 per square foot, about 20% below this year’s average for Sentosa Cove resales.平均叫价大约每平方英尺1340新元，比今年圣淘沙湾转售的平均价低大约20%。
  • Marina Collection has 124 units. It was launched in late 2007 and completed in 2011.Marina Collection共有124个单位。2007年底开盘，2011年建成。
  • The sale is linked to an old case of loans taken at inflated prices. PropNex is the exclusive agent.这次出售和一宗较早的、以偏高价格贷款的旧案有关。博纳（PropNex）是独家代理。
  • Three-bedroom units run from about 1,873 square feet without a private enclosed space to 2,099 square feet with one. Asking prices are about $2.588 million ($1,381 per square foot) to $2.786 million ($1,327 per square foot).三房式大约从1873平方英尺（没有私人围蔽空间）到2099平方英尺（有私人围蔽空间）。叫价大约258万8000新元（每平方英尺1381新元）到278万6000新元（每平方英尺1327新元）。
  • Four-bedroom units run from about 2,185 to 2,390 square feet. Asking prices are about $2.948 million ($1,349 per square foot) to $3.11 million ($1,301 per square foot).四房式大约2185至2390平方英尺。叫价大约294万8000新元（每平方英尺1349新元）到311万新元（每平方英尺1301新元）。
  • Zaobao reported that the lowest price per square foot is a ground-floor four-bedroom with a private enclosed space: 2,390 square feet, asking about $3.03 million.《联合早报》报道，每平方英尺最低的是底层带私人围蔽空间的四房式，2390平方英尺，叫价大约303万新元。
  • Twelve of the units are vacant and 26 are tenanted. The leases run from the fourth quarter of 2026 to the fourth quarter of 2028.其中12个单位空置，26个有租户。租约从2026年第四季到2028年第四季。
  • Viewings were due to start on the Friday after the report, 6 November.看房定于报道后的那个星期五、11月6日开始。
