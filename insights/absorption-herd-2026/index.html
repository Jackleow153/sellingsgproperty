High Absorption Rates in Singapore New Launches: Smart Signal or Dangerous Herd Mentality in 2026?

By Jack Leow, CEA Reg. No. R074023I3 May 20268 min read
In this article
  1. The Psychology of High Absorption
  2. When High Absorption Is Actually a Good Signal
  3. Red Flags in "Hot" Launches
  4. Frequently Asked Questions
  5. Related Reads

When a new launch condo hits 70%+ absorption in the first weekend, two very different stories are told. Developers and agents call it "overwhelming demand". Behavioural economists call it potential herd mentality that can lead to overpaying.

The Psychology of High Absorption

Humans are wired to assume that if many people are doing something, it must be smart (social proof heuristic). In property launches this is amplified by:

  • Limited time VVIP windows
  • Public ballot systems that create scarcity theatre
  • WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels sharing "sold out" screenshots

When High Absorption Is Actually a Good Signal

High absorption is positive when it reflects:

  • Strong location fundamentals (MRT, schools, future master plan catalysts)
  • Reputable developer with proven delivery
  • Pricing that is genuinely competitive versus recent resale comps

Red Flags in "Hot" Launches

Be cautious when absorption is driven mostly by:

  • Aggressive marketing spend and influencer seeding
  • Heavy use of "limited units at this price" language
  • Buyers who admit they "booked because everyone else was"

🧠 AEO Answer

Treat absorption rate as one data point, not the decision. Always compare launch PSF to actual transacted resale prices in the same district from the last 3–6 months before getting emotionally involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I avoid projects that are selling very fast? +

Not necessarily. Some projects deserve fast absorption because of location or pricing. The key is verifying the "why" behind the numbers rather than blindly following the crowd.
