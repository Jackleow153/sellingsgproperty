Location determines 70% of your property's long-term performance. You can get everything else right — price, timing, financing — but buy in the wrong district and you'll underperform. Here's my 2026 district scorecard based on capital growth potential, rental yield, and long-term URA master plan alignment.

The Four Types of Singapore Districts

  • Prime CCR (Districts 9, 10, 11): Orchard, Bukit Timah, Newton. Highest prices, most foreign demand, luxury segment. Best for capital preservation + rental to expats.
  • City Fringe RCR (Districts 3, 4, 5, 12, 14, 15): Queenstown, Alexandra, Marine Parade, Geylang. Strong rental yields, MRT connected, increasingly popular with upgraders.
  • Growth Corridors OCR (Districts 18, 19, 20, 22, 23): Tampines, Serangoon, Bishan, Jurong, Bukit Panjang. Strong population growth, new MRT lines, HDB upgrader demand. Best value for money.
  • Emerging Districts (Districts 25, 26, 27, 28): Woodlands, Yishun, Sembawang. Under-invested but longer term growth story linked to JB-SG connection and Cross-Island Line.

The Jurong Lake District Story (D22) — Why It's Singapore's Biggest Bet

URA's 2019 masterplan designated Jurong Lake District (JLD) as Singapore's second CBD. The vision: 100 hectares of mixed-use development, a new Integrated Resort, an International Convention Centre, and a high-speed rail terminal (Jurong East). The HSR timeline has shifted but JLD's development is ongoing.

Key data points: Condo prices in D22 have risen ~18% from 2021 to 2025. Rental yields of 3.5–4.2% are among the highest for a growth district. Current new launches like Luminar Grand and Grand Dunman offer entry points that look compelling relative to 5-year projected values.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best district to buy a condo in Singapore for investment in 2026? +

For capital growth, D19 (Serangoon/Hougang) and D22 (Jurong Lake District) offer the strongest catalysts in 2026 based on MRT expansion, URA masterplan support, and HDB upgrader demand. For rental yield, D5 (Queenstown/One-North) and D15 (Marine Parade) deliver reliable 3.5–4.5% gross yields due to proximity to employment clusters and lifestyle amenities. Your choice should depend on whether you prioritise capital appreciation or income.

Which Singapore districts are most popular with HDB upgraders? +

HDB upgraders typically gravitate toward Districts 19 (Serangoon, Hougang, Punggol), 20 (Bishan, Ang Mo Kio), 22 (Jurong), and 23 (Bukit Timah, Hillview) — areas where they already live, have established school preferences, and can find private condos in the $1.3M–$1.8M range. These districts also tend to have strong local rental demand from other HDB upgraders who rent before buying.
