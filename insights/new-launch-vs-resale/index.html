New Launch vs Resale Condo in 2026: Which Should You Buy?

New Launch Condos vs Resale Condos in Singapore 2026: which should you buy? Short answer — new launch condos win for most HDB upgraders with a 5+ year horizon thanks to progressive payments (lower upfront cash), brand new facilities and first-mover pricing in growth areas. Resale condos win if you need to move in now or want immediate rental yield. There is no universal winner — only the right choice for your timeline, ABSD position and cash flow. Full data-driven breakdown below.

When New Launch Makes More Sense

  • You're an upgrader with HDB proceeds. The progressive payment means your cash and CPF from HDB sale can work harder during the construction period.
  • You plan to hold for 5+ years. New launches typically see the strongest appreciation from launch price to TOP and from TOP to resale market.
  • You want the latest amenities. New launches have pools, gyms, co-working spaces, and smart home features that resale condos from 10 years ago don't.
  • You're buying in a new growth corridor (e.g., Jurong Lake District, Tengah, Woodlands North MRT). Resale supply may be limited; new launches capture emerging demand early.

When Resale Makes More Sense

  • You need to move in immediately. If you've already sold your HDB or have a firm relocation date, resale gives you certainty of occupation.
  • You're an investor who needs rental yield now. With SORA-linked mortgages, rental income helps offset your mortgage cost. New launches can't generate rental income for years.
  • Established locations you prefer. Some of Singapore's most sought-after addresses (Orchard, East Coast, Buona Vista) have strong resale supply with proven rental demand.
  • You have strong negotiation skills. In a softer resale market, motivated sellers can be negotiated significantly below asking price.

🏆 The Bottom Line

For long-term wealth building (5–10 year horizon), new launches in growth districts tend to outperform. For investors needing immediate yield or buyers who need immediate occupation, resale often wins. When in doubt, model both scenarios with a mortgage broker and compare the 5-year projected returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are new launch condos more expensive than resale in Singapore? +

Generally yes — new launches command a premium PSF (price per square foot) over comparable resale condos in the same district. This is partly because they offer fresh lease, new facilities, and developer incentives. However, new launch PSF has historically increased from launch to TOP by 5–15%, and many buyers who entered at launch price have sold at significant gains by the time the development received TOP.

How does the progressive payment scheme work for new launch condos? +

Under Singapore's Normal Progressive Payment (NPP) scheme, you pay in stages tied to construction milestones: typically 20% on booking (Option to Purchase), then 5–10% tranches as each stage is completed (foundation, structural work, etc.), with the balance 25% on TOP. This spreads your cash outflow over 3–5 years and means you're not fully committed to the full mortgage amount until the unit is ready to occupy.
