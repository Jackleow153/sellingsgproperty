Dunearn House Eligibility: Singaporeans, PRs, Foreigners & ABSD

This is where many buyers get tripped up. Eligibility itself is straightforward, but the stamp duty impact varies dramatically depending on who you are.

Who Can Buy Dunearn House?

Dunearn House is a private condominium (not an Executive Condominium), so:

  • Singapore Citizens (SC): Eligible. No restrictions.
  • Singapore PRs: Eligible. No restrictions.
  • Foreigners: Eligible to buy condos in Singapore. No SLA approval needed (unlike landed property).
  • Companies/Trusts: Eligible, but face higher ABSD.

There's no Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) like HDB or EC. You can rent out, sell, or use it however you want (subject to SSD timing — see below).

The Practical Takeaway

  • First-time SC buyers get the best deal — minimal stamp duty load.
  • PR first-time buyers face a meaningful 5% ABSD bump.
  • Second property buyers (SC) need to add $560k+ in ABSD — this changes the whole investment math.
  • Foreigners essentially pay 60% extra in ABSD — most foreign buyers structure purchases very carefully or wait for local PR status.

If you're a married couple where one spouse is foreign, there are legitimate structuring options (e.g., buying in the Singaporean spouse's name only) that can save substantial ABSD. Speak to a property lawyer before committing.

Dunearn House Progressive Payment Scheme Example

One of the biggest cashflow advantages of buying a new launch is the Progressive Payment Scheme (PPS). You don't pay for the full unit upfront — you pay in stages as construction progresses.

Typical PPS Schedule (Illustrative)

For a $2.8M unit (3-bedroom example), here's roughly how the payments would flow:

TOP (Temporary Occupation Permit)25%$700,000$2,484,600

Why PPS Matters

  • You only finance progressively — your bank loan disburses in stages too, meaning lower monthly interest in the early years.
  • You have time to plan cashflow — typically 4–5 years between OTP and TOP.
  • You can sell before TOP (subsale) if you need to, though SSD applies if within 3 years.

⚠️ Watch-out

The 25% TOP payment is the biggest single lump sum. Make sure your bank loan and CPF are confirmed well before that milestone — many buyers forget to plan for this.

CPF, LTV & Mortgage: How Much Can You Actually Afford?

Let's get practical. Here's a rough cashflow framework for a $2.8M Dunearn House unit.

Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits

Assuming this is your first private property and you have no existing housing loans:

  • Maximum LTV: 75% of purchase price (i.e., $2.1M)
  • Minimum down payment: 25% ($700,000) — of which 5% must be cash and 20% can be cash or CPF

If you already have an outstanding housing loan, LTV drops to 45% on this purchase. Two outstanding loans = 35% LTV. This is why upgraders need to plan their HDB/condo timing carefully.

Approximate Monthly Mortgage

Assuming:

  • Loan amount: $2.1M
  • Tenure: 30 years
  • Indicative interest rate: 3.5% (2026 market range)

Estimated monthly instalment: ~$9,430

Note: During PPS, your monthly payment will be lower (since only part of the loan is disbursed), then ramp up as more stages release.

TDSR Reality Check

The Total Debt Servicing Ratio (TDSR) caps your monthly debt obligations at 55% of gross monthly income. For a $2.1M loan:

  • You need roughly $17,000+ gross monthly household income to qualify comfortably
  • This typically translates to ~$220,000+ annual household income

If you're nowhere near this, the 2BR units (~$1.9M) require closer to $13,000–$14,000 monthly income — more accessible for younger upgrader couples.

CPF Usage

You can use CPF Ordinary Account (OA) for:

  • Down payment (after the initial 5% cash)
  • Monthly mortgage repayments
  • Stamp duties (reimbursable from CPF after payment)

There's a CPF Withdrawal Limit of 120% of the property's Valuation Limit, but for a new launch where you're buying at market price, this is rarely a binding constraint until much later.

How to Register for Dunearn House Showflat & VVIP Preview (July 2026)

The VVIP preview is where serious buyers get the best stack selection and early-bird discounts (typically 3–8% off public launch pricing).

Steps to Register

  1. Register your interest via official developer channels or an accredited agent (no commission to you as buyer in Singapore — agent fees are paid by the developer).
  2. Get on the VVIP list — this gives you priority access before public launch.
  3. Attend the VVIP preview at the showflat (location confirmed closer to date).
  4. Make a Cheque Booking if you decide to proceed — this reserves your unit for a short window before OTP.
  5. OTP issuance — once you commit, you sign the Option to Purchase and pay the 5% booking fee.
  6. Sign S&P within 8 weeks and pay BSD + ABSD.

Tips for Booking Day

  • Decide your stack preferences in advance — orientation, floor level, view.
  • Have your funds ready — booking cheque needs to clear quickly.
  • Confirm bank loan In-Principle Approval (IPA) before booking — don't assume.
  • Don't get rushed — if your top 3 stacks are taken, it's okay to walk away.

Common Buyer Mistakes to Avoid

After helping many buyers through new launch decisions, here are the most common slip-ups:

  1. Skipping IPA before booking day — you'll be locked into an OTP without confirmed financing.
  2. Forgetting ABSD remission rules — married couples buying jointly may qualify for ABSD remission on the second property if the first is sold within 6 months.
  3. Underestimating stamp duty — BSD alone on $2.8M is ~$104,600. Plan cash for this.
  4. Choosing stack by price alone — orientation and floor matter for liveability and resale.
  5. Ignoring the SSD timing — Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell within 3 years.
  6. Stretching TDSR to the max — leave buffer for interest rate increases. Stress-test at 4%+.
  7. Confusing TOP with legal completion — you move in at TOP, but legal completion (CSC) is 12 months later.
  8. Not coordinating HDB sale timing — if you're an upgrader, mistiming the sale of your HDB can trigger ABSD you weren't expecting.
