How Much CPF Can You Use for a Private Property in Singapore? (2026 Guide)

CPF is most Singaporeans' largest financial asset — and most people dramatically underestimate how powerful it is for property financing. When used correctly, your CPF Ordinary Account (OA) can fund your downpayment, legal fees, and monthly mortgage instalments — potentially eliminating any out-of-pocket cash requirement for years.

CPF OA vs CPF SA: What Can Be Used?

Only your CPF Ordinary Account (OA) can be used for private property purchase. Your Special Account (SA) and Medisave Account (MA) cannot be used for property.

What Can CPF OA Be Used For?

  • Downpayment (up to the Withdrawal Limit)
  • Monthly mortgage instalments
  • Legal and stamp duty fees
  • Renovation loan repayment (under certain conditions)
  • Fire insurance premiums

The CPF Withdrawal Limit Explained

Here's where most people get confused. CPF has two key limits for private property:

LimitWhat It Means
Withdrawal Limit (WL)Maximum CPF OA you can use = 120% of Valuation Limit (VL)
Valuation Limit (VL)Lower of purchase price or market valuation
Basic Retirement Sum (BRS)After age 55, you must retain BRS in CPF before withdrawing more

Example: You buy a $1,500,000 condo (valued at $1,500,000). The Valuation Limit = $1,500,000. The Withdrawal Limit = 120% × $1,500,000 = $1,800,000. You can use up to $1,800,000 of CPF OA across the full property lifecycle (downpayment + monthly instalments), as long as you haven't hit that cap.

How Much CPF Does a Typical Upgrader Have?

For a Singaporean couple aged 35–40, each with ~15 years of work history:

AgeTypical CPF OA Balance
30 years old~$60,000–$80,000
35 years old~$100,000–$140,000
40 years old~$150,000–$200,000
45 years old~$200,000–$280,000

Note: Balances above are rough estimates before any CPF housing withdrawal for existing HDB loans.

💡 Practical Implication

After selling your HDB and refunding CPF (with accrued interest), your CPF OA is typically replenished — and can immediately be re-deployed for your private property downpayment. A couple aged 38 with clean CPF records could easily have $250,000–$350,000 combined in CPF OA to deploy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use CPF to pay for a new launch condo before it is completed? +

Yes. Under the progressive payment scheme, CPF can be used to fund each payment tranche as construction milestones are hit. You can also use CPF OA for the initial 20% downpayment (5% cash + up to 15% from CPF OA, depending on LTV). Monthly mortgage instalments can be paid from CPF OA once the loan starts disbursing.

What happens to CPF when I sell my private property? +

When you sell, you must refund all CPF OA funds used for the property back into your CPF OA account, plus 2.5% per annum accrued interest. This refund is made from the sale proceeds before you receive any cash. The refunded amount returns to your CPF OA and can be re-used for future property purchases.
