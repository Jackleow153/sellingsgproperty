Dunearn House is the first private residential launch in the transformative Bukit Timah Turf City masterplan — and at a land cost that positions it as one of the most compelling CCR value plays of 2026. Developed by Frasers Property, Sekisui House and CSC Land Group, this 380-unit 99-year leasehold condo on Dunearn Road offers families and upgraders a rare chance to buy into Singapore's next major growth precinct at new-launch pricing.

This guide covers everything you need to know: the project details, the value case, the risks, and what serious buyers should do next.

What Is Dunearn House?

Dunearn House is a 99-year leasehold condominium along Dunearn Road in District 11 (Core Central Region). Developed by a joint venture of Frasers Property, Sekisui House, and CSC Land Group, it sits on a 145,226 sq ft site and comprises 380 units across five blocks of 10 to 19 storeys, designed by award-winning local architecture firm Ong & Ong.

5 Reasons Dunearn House Is a Compelling Buy

1. You Are Buying Into Singapore's Next Major Growth Precinct — First

This is the single most important reason serious buyers are paying attention. Dunearn House is the first and only private residential launch within the Bukit Timah Turf City masterplan precinct to date.

Under the URA Draft Master Plan 2025, the former Bukit Timah Turf City site — a vast 176-hectare former racecourse — is being transformed into a brand-new residential estate planned to deliver between 15,000 and 20,000 new public and private homes. Notably, this will include the first HDB flats to be built in Bukit Timah in nearly 40 years.

This is the same first-mover playbook we have seen work well at Bayshore (two Thomson-East Coast Line stations, new waterfront township), and before that at Lentor (where early-bird buyers saw healthy capital appreciation as each subsequent launch refined and reinforced the precinct's identity). In Turf City's case, the scale and prestige of the location means the upside story is arguably even more powerful.

The masterplan vision is ambitious: green and nature-integrated living, 27 conserved heritage buildings (including the iconic Grandstand), a future Turf City MRT station on the Cross Island Line (CRL) expected around 2032, and ecological corridors linking residents to Bukit Timah Nature Reserve. By buying Dunearn House now, you are entering at the start of that multi-decade value-creation journey, not midway through.

2. The Developer Consortium Is World-Class

Developer quality matters enormously in a new launch — it affects build quality, design execution, and your property's long-term reputation in the resale market.

The Dunearn House consortium brings together three credible names:

  • Frasers Property — A Singapore-headquartered, SGX-listed developer with a global portfolio of residential, commercial, industrial, and logistics assets. Their Singapore residential track record includes Rivière, North Park Residences, and One Holland Village Residences.
  • Sekisui House — Japan's largest homebuilder by revenue, known for precision construction, sustainable design, and consistently high product quality.
  • CSC Land Group — An established Singapore developer known for projects like The Lilium and The Gazania.

The fact that nine developer groups submitted bids at the GLS tender — the strongest CCR tender participation since May 2018 — tells you that institutional confidence in this location is very high.

3. The Location Ticks Every Box for Families and Investors

Dunearn Road has long been one of Singapore's most coveted residential addresses. It offers a rare combination of prestige, connectivity, and everyday practicality.

Transport Connectivity

  • Sixth Avenue MRT (DT7) is within a 5–10 minute walk. The Downtown Line provides seamless access to the CBD (City Hall in under 20 minutes), Orchard Road, and Buona Vista.
  • By car, Dunearn Road connects quickly to both the CTE and PIE.
  • Future Cross Island Line (CRL) Turf City station (targeting ~2032) will add a second MRT line to the precinct.

The Education Belt — Singapore's Most Coveted School Cluster

For families, this is the single most powerful lifestyle draw. The schools within close proximity read like a who's who of Singapore's most sought-after institutions:

Primary: Methodist Girls' School (Primary), Nanyang Primary School, Pei Hwa Presbyterian Primary School, Henry Park Primary School, Raffles Girls' Primary School.

Secondary & JC: Nanyang Girls' High School, Hwa Chong Institution, Methodist Girls' School (Secondary), Singapore Chinese Girls' School, National Junior College, Hwa Chong Junior College.

Being within 1–2 km of these schools translates directly into Phase 2C and Phase 2B priority admission advantages, one of the most powerful practical benefits of living in this part of Singapore.

4. The Land Price Is Competitive Relative to CCR Benchmarks

The GLS tender winning bid was S$1,410 psf ppr. To put that in context:

  • The second-highest bidder (CDL) came in at S$1,360 psf ppr — a gap of only 3.7%.
  • In February 2024, UOL Group and Singapore Land Group paid S$1,617 psf ppr for an Orchard Boulevard GLS plot in District 9.
  • Other 2025–2026 CCR GLS sites have seen land costs well above S$1,400–S$1,600 psf ppr.

Working from a land breakeven of approximately S$2,558 psf, analysts have estimated the launch price range at around S$2,700–S$3,100 psf, with CBRE projecting S$2,900–S$3,000 psf. More conservative independent estimates suggest a base case around low-S$3,000 psf.

It is also worth noting that a second adjacent Dunearn Road GLS parcel was awarded on 4 May 2026 at S$1,625 psf ppr — a significant step-up from the S$1,410 psf ppr paid for the Dunearn House site. This directly validates that the first-mover price advantage for Dunearn House buyers is real.

5. The Void in the CCR Leasehold Market Is Real

There is a genuine shortage of mid-sized 99-year leasehold condominiums in premium CCR sub-markets like Bukit Timah, Holland Village, and River Valley. These areas have historically been dominated by freehold projects.

Dunearn House, as a 380-unit mid-sized 99-year leasehold condo, fills this gap and speaks directly to a new generation of buyers who value modern design, full facilities, fresh lease tenure, and transparent new-launch pricing over freehold bragging rights.

Who Is Dunearn House Best Suited For?

Own-Stay Families

The combination of the education belt, green living, future MRT access, and quality developer execution makes Dunearn House an exceptionally strong long-term family home. If you are planning for primary school registration within the next 3–5 years, this is one of the most strategically well-located new launches you will find.

Upgraders from HDB

If you are upgrading from an HDB flat — particularly in mature estates like Bishan, Ang Mo Kio, Toa Payoh, or even Queenstown — Dunearn House represents a chance to enter a prestigious CCR address at new-launch pricing. As an upgrader buying a second residential property, you will be subject to ABSD of 20% unless you sell your HDB first. Timing your sale carefully (including the 15-month timeline) is critical.

Investors

For investors, the long-term capital appreciation thesis is anchored by the Turf City masterplan, future CRL connectivity, and scarce CCR supply. Rental demand from expat families drawn by the nearby international schools is robust.

Risks and Considerations to Keep in Mind

  • 99-year leasehold: Like all GLS condos, Dunearn House is leasehold. For a 30-year investment horizon, a fresh 99-year lease starting 2025 should not be a material concern.
  • Construction and TOP timeline: Expected TOP is 2029–2030. If you need a home urgently, this may not work for your timeline.
  • Land cost is not cheap: At S$1,410 psf ppr, launch pricing will sit comfortably in the S$3,000 psf range.
  • Precinct is still developing: The schools, MRT, green corridors, and masterplan amenities will take years to fully materialise.
  • ABSD for additional buyers: If this is not your first property, run the full financial model including ABSD, SSD lock-in, and TDSR constraints before committing.

Understanding the Payment Scheme

As a new launch GLS project, Dunearn House will use the Normal Progressive Payment Scheme (NPS):

  1. Option to Purchase (OTP): 5% booking fee payable in cash
  2. Exercise of OTP (within 3 weeks): 15% (cash + CPF), plus Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD)
  3. Foundation completion: 10%
  4. Structural framework: 10%
  5. Partition walls: 5%
  6. Ceiling/roofing: 5%
  7. Doors, windows, plumbing: 5%
  8. Car park/roads/drains: 5%
  9. Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP): 25%
  10. Final completion (CSC): 15%

This means your payments are spread across the construction timeline (2026–2030), easing the cashflow burden.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the expected launch price for Dunearn House? +

Based on post-tender analyst estimates from CBRE and SRI, the expected launch price is around S$2,900–S$3,100 psf, with a base-case consensus settling around low-S$3,000 psf. Official prices will only be released by the developer at the showflat preview, expected in July 2026.

Where exactly is Dunearn House located? +

Dunearn House is on Dunearn Road in District 11, within the Core Central Region (CCR). It is within walking distance of Sixth Avenue MRT Station (Downtown Line, DT7) and lies along the planned Bukit Timah–Rochor Green Corridor.

Is Dunearn House freehold or leasehold? +

It is 99-year leasehold, awarded from the 2025 Government Land Sales programme.

How many units does Dunearn House have? +

There are approximately 380 units across five blocks of 10 to 19 storeys, with a mix of 2-bedroom, 3-bedroom, 4-bedroom, and 5-bedroom layouts. A 1-bedroom + study configuration may also be available.

What schools are near Dunearn House? +

The development sits in the Bukit Timah education belt, within close proximity to Methodist Girls' School (Primary and Secondary), Nanyang Girls' High School, Nanyang Primary School, Hwa Chong Institution, National Junior College, and Henry Park Primary School, among others. This is one of Singapore's most coveted school clusters for primary school balloting purposes.

What is the ABSD I need to pay for Dunearn House? +

ABSD depends on your citizenship and how many properties you own. As of 2026: Singapore Citizens buying their first property pay 0% ABSD; their second property, 20%. Permanent Residents buying their first property pay 5%; second, 30%. Foreigners pay 60% on any residential purchase.

When is the expected TOP for Dunearn House? +

The expected Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) is projected to be between 2029 and 2030, subject to construction progress and regulatory approvals.
