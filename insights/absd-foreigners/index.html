60% ABSD for Foreigners: Is Singapore Property Still Investable in 2026?

For most non-FTA foreigners, buying residential new launch condos in Singapore in 2026 is extremely challenging due to the 60% ABSD. However, the market remains investable through commercial properties (0% ABSD), shophouses, and strategic pathways like PR or corporate structures for the right high-net-worth buyer.

In April 2023 Singapore raised ABSD for foreigners to 60%. Two years later, headlines still say the door is closed — but smart capital is finding creative entry points, especially via new launch commercial assets and established developer projects.

ABSD Rates for Foreign Buyers in 2026 (Quick Reference)

Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) stacks on top of standard BSD. Here are the current effective rates for residential property:

Buyer Profile1st Property2nd Property3rd+
Singapore Citizen0%20%30%
Singapore PR (1st property)5%30%35%
Foreigner (most)60%60%60%
US / Swiss / Icelandic / Norwegian (FTA)0%20%30%

On a $2M new launch condo, a typical foreigner pays $1.2M ABSD — pushing the all-in cost to $3.2M. This is why residential new launches are largely the domain of locals and PRs in 2026.

Who Still Gets 0% or Low ABSD on Residential?

Only nationals of FTA countries (US, Switzerland, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway) enjoy Singapore Citizen treatment on their first residential purchase. Everyone else from China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, UK, Australia, Hong Kong etc. pays the full 60%.

The Best Workaround: Commercial Property & Shophouses (0% ABSD)

The smartest foreign capital in 2026 is moving into commercial assets, which carry 0% ABSD for all nationalities. Popular options include:

  • Conservation shophouses in Tanjong Pagar, Chinatown or Joo Chiat — strong 3–4.5% gross yields + capital growth. See examples from developers active in mixed-use new launches.
  • Strata offices in the CBD — 3.5–4% yields, liquid, low management.
  • Industrial / logistics units — higher 5–6% yields for those comfortable with the asset class.

🏢 2026 Foreign Buyer Playbook

If you are non-FTA, start with commercial or shophouse opportunities. Residential new launch condos only make sense if you plan to become a PR or citizen, or are buying ultra-luxury for lifestyle (where price elasticity is higher). Projects like Aurea show the premium residential segment still attracts serious capital despite ABSD.

Psychology of the 60% ABSD Buyer

Loss aversion is powerful. The 60% "tax" feels like an immediate 60% loss, even though it is a one-time cost on an appreciating asset in a safe jurisdiction. UHNW buyers reframe it as the price of entry into one of Asia's most stable wealth stores.

Actionable Strategies for Foreign Buyers 2026

  1. FTA nationals: Move quickly on D9/D10 or D19 new launch condos for 2–3% yields + capital upside.
  2. Non-FTA: Prioritise shophouses and commercial strata. Speak to developers with integrated commercial components in their new launches.
  3. PR pathway: Time your property purchase with your PR application — PRs pay only 5% ABSD on the first residential property.
  4. Long-term hold: The ABSD percentage of total value drops every year the asset appreciates. 10+ year horizons make the hit much smaller in relative terms.

Frequently Asked Questions — ABSD & Foreign Buyers 2026

Is there any legal way for a foreigner to avoid 60% ABSD on a new launch condo? +

No reliable loopholes. IRAS actively targets avoidance schemes. The legitimate routes are FTA nationality, becoming a Singapore PR/citizen, or buying commercial property instead. Always verify with a qualified solicitor before any structure.

Can foreigners buy shophouses in new launch mixed-use developments? +

Yes. Foreigners can freely purchase commercial shophouses and strata-titled commercial units with 0% ABSD. In mixed-use new launches, the commercial component is usually clearly separated and ABSD-free.

How much ABSD do Singapore PRs pay on their first condo? +

Only 5% ABSD on the first residential property as a PR. On a $1.5M new launch condo that is a $825,000 saving versus a foreigner. This is why many foreign investors pursue PR status and property strategy in parallel.
