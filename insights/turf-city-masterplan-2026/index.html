The Bukit Timah Turf City masterplan is the defining long-term catalyst for Dunearn House and other new launches in the area through 2030 and beyond. Knowing the exact timeline for the Cross Island Line MRT, conserved heritage buildings, new parks, and community facilities will help you evaluate if the project's 99-year leasehold and ~$2,800–$3,100 psf pricing is a smart long-horizon bet.

Dunearn House is the first private residential launch inside this 176-hectare former racecourse site. The URA's vision will transform it into a new residential precinct with 15,000–20,000 homes (public and private), conserved heritage assets, green corridors, and major transport upgrades. This is not a small precinct upgrade — it is one of the largest masterplans in central Singapore in decades.

What is the Turf City Masterplan?

The former Singapore Turf Club site (closed in 2024) is being redeveloped under the URA Master Plan. Key elements include:

  • Conservation of the iconic Grandstand and other heritage structures for lifestyle, F&B and community uses.
  • A new Turf City MRT station on the Cross Island Line (CRL) Phase 2, targeted for 2032.
  • Extensive parks and the Bukit Timah–Rochor Green Corridor linking to the Nature Reserve.
  • Mix of HDB and private housing, with first HDBs in Bukit Timah in nearly 40 years.
  • Improved connectivity via existing Sixth Avenue MRT (Downtown Line) and future CRL.

💡 2026 Key Takeaway for Buyers

Buying Dunearn House now means you are betting on the masterplan delivering over 10–15+ years. Early buyers capture the largest uplift when infrastructure (especially the CRL station) materialises, but must tolerate 5–8 years of ongoing construction.

How the Masterplan Directly Benefits Dunearn House

  1. Transport premium: Walking distance to Sixth Avenue MRT today + future Turf City CRL station in 2032 creates a dual-MRT location rare in CCR.
  2. Heritage + lifestyle: Conserved Grandstand buildings will become F&B, retail and community hubs within 5–10 min walk.
  3. Green connectivity: Direct links to Bukit Timah Nature Reserve and the new green corridor improve liveability and long-term desirability.
  4. Supply scarcity: Limited new private launches in this pocket for years to come supports pricing power.
  5. School + family demand: The overall precinct will reinforce Bukit Timah's status as a top family address.

Risks and Things to Watch

  • Construction disruption will be significant for the first 5–7 years after TOP.
  • The pace of heritage cluster and park delivery depends on government timelines — delays are possible.
  • Future HDB launches in the precinct could increase overall supply and affect resale dynamics.
  • 99-year leasehold in a traditionally freehold area means long-term value depends heavily on the masterplan succeeding.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When will the Cross Island Line Turf City station open? +

Targeted for 2032 as part of CRL Phase 2. This will be a major catalyst for Dunearn House and the broader precinct.

What heritage buildings will be conserved? +

The iconic Grandstand and several other historic structures from the old Turf Club are planned for conservation and adaptive reuse as lifestyle and community spaces.

Will there be new parks and green spaces? +

Yes. A major component is the Bukit Timah–Rochor Green Corridor and multiple new parks that will link residents directly to the Nature Reserve.

How will this affect Dunearn House resale value? +

Historically, first-mover projects in large masterplans see the strongest capital appreciation once infrastructure is delivered. However, buyers must be prepared for 5–10 years of precinct maturation and construction activity.

Is the masterplan guaranteed to be delivered on time? +

Government masterplans are generally reliable but subject to adjustments. The CRL station date (2032) is the most important single milestone for value. Monitor URA updates closer to 2030.
