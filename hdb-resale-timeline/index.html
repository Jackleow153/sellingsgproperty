Assumptions used

  • Request for Value: only if the buyer needs HDB's valuation; due by the next working day after the option date. HDB says the value is typically available within 10 working days. HDB, OTP important notes
  • OTP exercise: defaults to day 21, the last day (by 4pm). Adjustable to model an earlier exercise.
  • Resale application: defaults to 7 days after exercise, when both portions are in. Either party may submit first; the other must submit within 7 calendar days.
  • HDB acceptance (projected window): HDB accepts within 28 working days of both portions being submitted, if everything is in order; that is the late end. Applications in order are often accepted sooner, so the early end assumes 20 working days (about 4 weeks): our estimate, not an HDB figure. Working days skip weekends and Singapore public holidays (MOM, 2025 to 2027).
  • Endorsement: HDB prepares the resale documents about 21 days after acceptance; both parties endorse them and pay the fees online. HDB, acceptance and approval
  • Approval: HDB grants approval once all documents are endorsed. HDB gives no fixed date, so it is shown as a window before completion.
  • Completion and handover: within 8 weeks (56 days) of acceptance, on the date stated in HDB's acceptance letter, which HDB will not bring forward.
  • Extension of stay: optional; the date shown is the latest allowed, 3 months after completion. HDB, temporary extension of stay
  • Real case: both portions submitted on 20 Oct 2026, completed on 18 Jan 2027 (90 days). The calculator's window for that submission date is 13 to 25 Jan 2027.
  • All dates after submission are projections. HDB's acceptance letter gives your actual completion date.

Questions sellers and buyers ask

How long does an HDB resale take from OTP to keys?

About 100 to 120 days. The buyer has 21 days to exercise the OTP, both parties then submit the resale application, HDB accepts it within up to 28 working days, and completion follows within 8 weeks (56 days) of acceptance. In a real case, both portions were submitted on 20 Oct 2026 and the resale completed on 18 Jan 2027, 90 days later.

How long is the OTP option period for an HDB resale flat?

21 calendar days. The buyer must exercise the Option to Purchase by 4pm on the 21st calendar day after it is granted. The option fee is $1 to $1,000, and the option and exercise fees together cannot exceed $5,000.

When must the buyer submit the Request for Value?

By the next working day after the option date, if the buyer needs HDB's valuation, for example for a housing loan or CPF use. HDB says the value is typically available on the HDB Flat Portal within 10 working days.

How long does HDB take to accept a resale application?

Up to 28 working days after both parties have submitted their portions, if everything is in order. Either party may submit first; the other must submit within 7 calendar days. HDB tells both parties by SMS and email when it accepts.

When do the buyer and seller endorse the resale documents?

HDB prepares the documents about 3 weeks after it accepts the application. Both parties endorse them on the HDB Flat Portal and pay the fees online within the time HDB gives, or the application may be cancelled. HDB grants approval once all documents are endorsed.

When is the HDB resale completion date?

Within 8 weeks (56 days) of HDB's acceptance of the resale application, on the date stated in HDB's acceptance letter. HDB treats that date as the earliest possible completion and will not bring it forward.

Can the seller stay in the flat after completion?

Yes, if the buyer agrees. HDB allows a temporary extension of stay of up to 3 months from the resale completion date, and no further extension beyond that.
