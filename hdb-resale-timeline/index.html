Assumptions used

  • OTP exercise: defaults to Day 21 (the maximum window under HDB rules). Adjustable to model earlier exercise.
  • Portal submission: defaults to 7 days after OTP exercise. Adjustable — HDB allows submission up to 60 days after exercise.
  • HDB acceptance: 5 weeks after portal submission (35 days) — upper bound of the typical 4–5 week HDB range, used to give a conservative estimate.
  • Approval letter: about 2 weeks after HDB acceptance (14 days used).
  • Completion & handover: about 4 weeks after approval letter (28 days used).
  • Extension of stay: optional — the date shown is the latest allowable (up to 3 months post-completion).
  • The timeline uses conservative (later) estimates where a range exists, so actual completion may arrive sooner. Weekends and public holidays are not adjusted for.
