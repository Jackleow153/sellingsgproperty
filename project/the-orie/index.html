The Orie

Lorong 1 Toa Payoh · District 12 (RCR)

Launched · Jan 202599 Years Leasehold777 units

The Orie is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Lorong 1 Toa Payoh in District 12 (RCR), developed by City Developments Ltd, Frasers Property & Sekisui House Pte Ltd), with 777 homes. It launched on Jan 2025. Expected completion (TOP): 2028/2029 (Q2 2028 per developer site; corrected from EdgeProp anomaly). Nearest MRT: ~5 min to Braddell MRT (also near Caldecott MRT for showflat).
