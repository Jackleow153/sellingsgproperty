Berlayar Drive (GLS)

Berlayar Drive (former Keppel Club, Greater Southern Waterfront) · District 04 (RCR)

Upcoming · Not announced99 LH415 units

Berlayar Drive (GLS) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Berlayar Drive (former Keppel Club, Greater Southern Waterfront) in District 04 (RCR), developed by Intrepid Investments & GuocoLand (Singapore) (Hong Leong Group), with 415 homes. Land cost: $576.8M (about $1,515 psf ppr). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Second private housing site on the former Keppel Club land in the Greater Southern Waterfront. URA awarded it on 7 August 2026 to Intrepid Investments and GuocoLand for $576.8M (about $1,515 psf ppr). URA estimates about 415 homes. The developers have not announced a launch date, a project name or prices.
