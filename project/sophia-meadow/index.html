Sophia Meadow

Launched · 1 Aug 2026103-year leasehold41 units

Sophia Meadow is a 103-year leasehold private condominium at 132 Sophia Road (Mount Sophia) in District 09 (CCR), developed by Sin Thai Hin Development (Pte) Ltd, with 41 homes. It launched on 1 Aug 2026 at an average of about $2,650 psf. Expected completion (TOP): VP 30 Nov 2029 (expected). Nearest MRT: ~9 min walk to Dhoby Ghaut MRT.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Boutique 41-unit, five-storey development at 132 Sophia Road, Mount Sophia (D9 CCR). 103-year leasehold. Preview from 25 July 2026, booking from 1 August 2026. Indicative pricing about $2,500 to $2,800 psf, with one-bedders from about $1.27M. Launch-weekend take-up was not publicly reported.
