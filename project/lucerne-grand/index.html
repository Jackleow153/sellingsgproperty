Lucerne Grand

Lakeside Drive (Jurong Lake District) · District 22 (OCR)

Launched · 3 Oct 202699 LH570 units

Lucerne Grand is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Lakeside Drive (Jurong Lake District) in District 22 (OCR), developed by City Developments Limited, with 570 homes. It launched on 3 Oct 2026 at an average of about $2,480 psf. Expected completion (TOP): Est. 2030. Nearest MRT: Directly connected to Lakeside (EW26) MRT.

Data as of 11 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

CDL's 570-unit mixed-use project with the Lucerne Galleria retail podium (including a supermarket), directly linked to Lakeside MRT. First new private launch in the Lakeside precinct since Lake Grande in 2016. Launched 3 October 2026: 350 units (61.4%) sold by 6pm on 4 October at an average of $2,480 psf. About 93% of buyers were Singapore citizens. Land bought at $1,132 psf ppr in June 2025.
