Hudson Place Residences

Media Circle (one-north / Queenstown planning area) · District 05 (RCR)

Launched · May 202699 LH327 units

Hudson Place Residences is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Media Circle (one-north / Queenstown planning area) in District 05 (RCR), developed by Qingjian Realty, Forsea Holdings, CYZ Land & Jianan Capital, with 327 homes. It launched on May 2026. Expected completion (TOP): ~2029 (VP 30 Sep 2029). Nearest MRT: ~10 min walk to one-north (CC23) / Buona Vista (CC22/EW21); developer offers 1-year shuttle.

Data as of 11 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
