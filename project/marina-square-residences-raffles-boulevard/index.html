Marina Square Residences (Raffles Boulevard)

6 Raffles Boulevard (Marina Square) · District 01 (CCR)

Upcoming · Not announcedTenure TBC204 units

Marina Square Residences (Raffles Boulevard) is a mixed-use condominium at 6 Raffles Boulevard (Marina Square) in District 01 (CCR), developed by Singapore Land Group (UOL Group), with 204 homes. The developer has not announced a launch date or prices. Expected completion (TOP): 2031 (whole redevelopment, developer target).

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

A new 49-storey tower of 204 large homes (three to five bedrooms and penthouses) within Singapore Land Group's partial redevelopment of the Marina Square complex, announced on 1 September 2026 with completion targeted for 2031. The mall is set to close on 31 March 2027 for works. Launch timing, tenure and prices have not been announced.
