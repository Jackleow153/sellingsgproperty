Coastal Cabana (EC)

Jalan Loyang Besar · District 17 (OCR)

Launched · Jan 202699 LH748 units

Coastal Cabana is a 99-year leasehold executive condominium (EC) at Jalan Loyang Besar in District 17 (OCR), developed by Qingjian Realty, Forsea Holdings, ZACD Group & Jianan Capital, with 748 homes. It launched on Jan 2026. Expected completion (TOP): Q1 2029 (VP 31 Mar 2029). Nearest MRT: ~650m to Pasir Ris MRT.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
