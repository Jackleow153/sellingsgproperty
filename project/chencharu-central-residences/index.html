Chencharu Central Residences

Chencharu Close (Khatib, Yishun) · District 27 (OCR)

Upcoming · Jan 2027 (preview, est.)99 LH875 units

Chencharu Central Residences is a 99-year leasehold mixed-use condominium at Chencharu Close (Khatib, Yishun) in District 27 (OCR), developed by Evia Real Estate, Gamuda Land & Ho Lee Group, H108), with 875 homes. It is expected to launch in Jan 2027 (preview, est.); prices have not been released. Nearest MRT: ~8–10 min walk to Khatib (NS14) MRT.
