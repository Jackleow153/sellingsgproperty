Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS)

Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) · District 16 (OCR)

Upcoming · Not announced99 LH1,280 units

Bayshore Drive (mixed-use GLS) is a 99-year leasehold mixed-use condominium at Bayshore Drive (Bedok South) in District 16 (OCR), developed by Frasers Property-led consortium with Sunway MCL, Sekisui House & Lum Chang, with 1,280 homes. Land cost: $2.128B (about $1,323 psf ppr). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices. Nearest MRT: Bedok South (TE30), under construction.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

The largest site in the 1H2026 GLS programme: a 5.74 ha commercial-and-residential plot at the future Bedok South MRT station. URA awarded it on 20 July 2026 to the Frasers Property-led Gemini consortium for $2.128B (about $1,323 psf ppr). URA estimates about 1,280 homes plus retail space. No launch date, project name or prices have been announced.
