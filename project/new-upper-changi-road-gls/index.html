New Upper Changi Road (GLS)

New Upper Changi Road (Bedok) · District 16 (OCR)

Upcoming · Not announced99 LH1,010 units

New Upper Changi Road (GLS) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at New Upper Changi Road (Bedok) in District 16 (OCR), developed by UOL Group, Singapore Land Group & CapitaLand Development, CL Sapphire), with 1,010 homes. Land cost: $1.425B (about $1,537 psf ppr). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices. Nearest MRT: Bedok (EW5), walking distance (reported).

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Large residential site in mature Bedok, near the Bedok integrated transport hub. URA awarded it on 4 September 2026 to a UOL, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand joint venture for $1.425B (about $1,537 psf ppr). URA estimates about 1,010 homes. No launch date, project name or prices have been announced.
