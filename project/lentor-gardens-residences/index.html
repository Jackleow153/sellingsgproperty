The Thesis

The one number that changes everything: $920 psf ppr

Most reviews skim over land cost. Sit with this number for a moment, because it is the entire case for this project.

When Kingsford Group won the tender in April 2025, they paid $429.23 million — about $920 psf ppr. The second-place bidder, a serious consortium of Hong Leong Holdings, Mitsui Fudosan and China Construction, came in just 1.7% lower at $905 psf ppr.

Land cost is the foundation under every new-launch price. Developers stack construction, financing, marketing and margin on top, then arrive at the selling price. A lower base gives Kingsford a choice: price competitively and sell fast, or hold pricing and keep a fatter margin. Either way, buyers are looking at a project with built-in cushion that no other Lentor launch has.

The catch: a land-cost advantage does not guarantee a cheap selling price. It only creates the option. Whether Kingsford passes that saving to you is the real question — and we will know the answer on 4 July 2026.

Unit Mix & Floor Plans

Built for families, not just investors

Unusually for a Lentor launch, there are no 1-bedroom units. Roughly half the project is 2-bedders for couples and investors; nearly half is genuine family-sized 3BR and 4BR. Most layouts use a dumbbell configuration that puts the master and common bedrooms on opposite ends, cutting wasted hallway.

The strata terrace: a scarce product

Three units. That is it. A strata terrace is a landed-style home — your own front door, your own private garden — held under strata title with shared condominium facilities and security. You get the feel of landed living without the maintenance burden or the eye-watering quantum of a freehold landed home. There is no equivalent in Lentor Modern, Lentor Hills Residences, Lentor Mansion or any other neighbouring project. Expect these three to be priced at a clear premium to the project average.

Location & Lifestyle

Nature on one side, the MRT on the other

The site sits directly beside the upcoming Lentor Hillock Park, with trails connecting toward Thomson Nature Park and the Central Catchment Nature Reserve. The developer reports that every unit faces either park or pool — no carpark or expressway facings.

— MRT

Lentor MRT (TE5), 5–7 min walk

Thomson–East Coast Line direct to Orchard (~15 min), Marina Bay and the CBD with no transfer. A covered linkway is planned. The future Bright Hill interchange (TEL + Cross Island Line) is two stops away.

— Schools

Established education belt

Anderson Primary, CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls', Mayflower Primary, Presbyterian High and Nanyang Polytechnic are all within reach. Verify the 1km MOE zone for your specific stack before relying on it.

— Daily life

Mall, childcare and shops at the door

Lentor Modern's integrated mall sits across the road — supermarket, pharmacy, gym and 50+ shops. The development itself adds three ground-floor retail units and a childcare centre.

— Drivers

Expressway access

Quick links to the CTE and SLE, with the upcoming North–South Corridor set to cut city-bound travel times further.

Buyer Fit

Is it right for you?

Strong fit if you are…

  • An HDB upgrader with sale proceeds, wanting a fresh 99-year lease with schools, MRT and nature in one package.
  • A young family prioritising green space, walkability and a community-feel development.
  • A buyer who wants a strata terrace without paying full landed-property quantum.
  • A long-term own-stay holder comfortable with a ~2029 completion.
  • A value-conscious investor comparing entry PSF within the OCR.

Probably not if you…

  • Are a short-term flipper — SSD and the new-launch holding period make quick exits costly.
  • Are chasing dramatic capital growth — Lentor's appreciation has been steady, not explosive.
  • Need immediate occupation — completion is more than three years away.
  • Want an integrated mall within the same development — that's Lentor Modern, not this.
Risks to Weigh

What could go wrong

  1. Pricing discipline is unprovenThe land-cost advantage only helps buyers if Kingsford passes it on. Price to Lentor comps and the value case weakens.
  2. Late-cycle supplyBy completion, five earlier Lentor projects will be resale-ready, competing for the same exit pool.
  3. A longer waitA ~2029 TOP means three-plus years before keys — longer than near-complete or earlier launches.
  4. Steady, not spectacular, growthThe precinct has appreciated at low single-digit rates. Treat this as a fundamentals play, not a momentum trade.
