Marina Gardens Lane (2026 GLS site)

Marina Gardens Lane (Marina South) · District 01 (RCR)

Upcoming · Not announced99 LH390 units

Marina Gardens Lane (2026 GLS site) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Marina Gardens Lane (Marina South) in District 01 (RCR), developed by EL Development Pte Ltd, with 390 homes. Land cost: $450.1M (about $1,243 psf ppr). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

The third GLS housing site in Marina South, opposite One Marina Gardens. EL Development was the only bidder at $450.1M (about $1,243 psf ppr), and URA awarded it the site on 26 October 2026. URA estimates about 390 homes. No launch date, project name or prices have been announced.
