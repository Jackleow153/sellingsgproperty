Yishun 10 Redevelopment

51 Yishun Central 1 (Yishun 10) · District 27 (OCR)

Upcoming · Mid-2027 (developer target)Tenure TBC110 units

Yishun 10 Redevelopment is a mixed-use condominium at 51 Yishun Central 1 (Yishun 10) in District 27 (OCR), developed by Frasers Property, with 110 homes. It is expected to launch in Mid-2027; prices have not been released. Expected completion (TOP): 2031 (developer target). Nearest MRT: Yishun (NS13), next to the site.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Frasers Property plans to replace the Yishun 10 cinema mall with about 110 homes above a street-level retail podium and a landscaped facilities deck. Frasers targets a sales launch in mid-2027 and completion in 2031; Yishun 10 is expected to close on 2 March 2027. No project name or prices have been announced.
