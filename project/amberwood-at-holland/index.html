Amberwood at Holland

30–52 Holland Link (Holland Plain) · District 10 (CCR)

Launched · 26 Sep 202699 LH212 units

Amberwood at Holland is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at 30–52 Holland Link (Holland Plain) in District 10 (CCR), developed by Sim Lian Group Pte Ltd), with 212 homes. It launched on 26 Sep 2026 at an average of about $3,019 psf. Expected completion (TOP): Est. 2030. Nearest MRT: ~15 min walk to King Albert Park (DT6) MRT.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

First condo in the new low-rise Holland Plain precinct: 212 three- to five-bedroom homes in 11 blocks of four to six storeys (plot ratio 1.4). Land bought at $1,432 psf ppr in July 2025. Launched 25–26 September 2026: 23 of 212 units (11%) sold, out of 70 units released in phase one, at an average of about $3,019 psf. Sim Lian also holds the adjacent Holland Plain plot (see 2027 tab).
