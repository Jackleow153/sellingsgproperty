Upper Thomson Road (Parcel A)

Upper Thomson Road (Springleaf) · District 26 (OCR)

Upcoming · Late 2026–2027 (est.)99 LH595 units

Upper Thomson Road (Parcel A) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Upper Thomson Road (Springleaf) in District 26 (OCR), developed by Wee Hur Property & GSC Holdings, with 595 homes. Land cost: $613.9M (about $1,062 psf ppr). It is expected to launch in Late 2026–2027; prices have not been released. Nearest MRT: Springleaf (TE4), next to the site (reported).

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Residential site with ground-floor commercial space beside Springleaf Residence and Springleaf MRT. URA awarded it to Wee Hur Property and GSC Holdings for $613.9M (about $1,062 psf ppr) after a five-bid tender that closed in October 2025. About 595 homes are planned. Market reports point to a preview between late 2026 and 2027; the developer has not announced a date, a project name or prices.
