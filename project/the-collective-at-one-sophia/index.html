The Collective at One Sophia

Sophia Road · District 09 (CCR)

Launched · Nov 202499 Years Leasehold367 units

The Collective at One Sophia is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Sophia Road in District 09 (CCR), developed by Sophia Residential Pte Ltd / Sophia Commercial Pte Ltd (JV: CEL Development, SingHaiyi Crystal & Ultra Infinity), with 367 homes. It launched on Nov 2024. Expected completion (TOP): Dec 2029 (expected). Nearest MRT: ~3 min to Bencoolen MRT.
