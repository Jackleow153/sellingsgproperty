Tengah Garden Residences

Tengah Garden Walk / Avenue (Tengah) · District 24 (OCR)

Launched · Apr 202699 LH863 units

Tengah Garden Residences is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Tengah Garden Walk / Avenue (Tengah) in District 24 (OCR), developed by Hong Leong Holdings, GuocoLand & CSC Land Group, with 863 homes. It launched on Apr 2026. Expected completion (TOP): ~2030 (Q3 2029 / Dec 2030 est.). Nearest MRT: ~1 min covered walk to upcoming Hong Kah MRT (Jurong Region Line).

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
