Chuan Park

Lorong Chuan · District 19 (OCR)

Launched · Nov 202499 LH916 units

Chuan Park is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Lorong Chuan in District 19 (OCR), developed by Kingsford Development & MCC Land, with 916 homes. It launched on Nov 2024. Expected completion (TOP): Q4 2027 (est.; vacant possession by 30 Sep 2028). Nearest MRT: next to Lorong Chuan MRT.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
