Duet@Emily

Mount Emily Road (Nos. 2, 4 & 6) · District 09 (CCR)

Launched · 2026Freehold20 units

Duet@Emily is a freehold private condominium at Mount Emily Road (Nos. 2, 4 & 6) in District 09 (CCR), developed by ZACD Property Pte Ltd (ZACD Group), with 20 homes. It launched on 2026 at an average of about $2,500 psf. Nearest MRT: ~5 min walk to Rochor or Little India MRT; Dhoby Ghaut interchange nearby.

Data as of 11 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Boutique 20-unit freehold project pairing a restored heritage building with a new residential annex at Mount Emily Road (D9 CCR). One- and two-bedroom units plus loft units. Huttons listed it as a May 2026 launch; URA caveats show sales from July 2026 at about $2,430 to $2,690 psf. Units sold are not published here because the developer has not released a total.
