Canberra Drive EC

Canberra Drive (Sembawang) · District 27 (OCR)

Upcoming · Not announced99 LH185 units

Canberra Drive EC is a 99-year leasehold executive condominium (EC) at Canberra Drive (Sembawang) in District 27 (OCR), developed by Santarli, Heeton, Kay Lim & Sunray consortium, with 185 homes. Land cost: $163.9M (about $825 psf ppr). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices. Nearest MRT: Canberra (NS12), nearby.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Small EC site near Canberra MRT and the first EC tender under the May 2026 rules (10-year minimum occupation period; $18,000 income ceiling). The tender closed on 1 October 2026 with 13 bids, and the top bid of $163.9M (about $825 psf ppr) set a record EC land rate. HDB awarded the site in October 2026 (reported). About 185 homes. No launch date or prices have been announced.
