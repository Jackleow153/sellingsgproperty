Pinery Residences

Tampines Street 94 (Tampines West) · District 18 (OCR)

Launched · Mar 202699 LH588 units

Pinery Residences is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Tampines Street 94 (Tampines West) in District 18 (OCR), developed by Hoi Hup Realty & Sunway MCL, with 588 homes. It launched on Mar 2026. Expected completion (TOP): Q1 2030 (VP 9 Oct 2031). Nearest MRT: direct underground link (24-hour) to Tampines West (DT31) MRT via Pinery Mall.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
