Parktown Residence

Tampines Street 62 · District 18 (OCR)

Launched · Feb 202599 Years Leasehold1,193 units

Parktown Residence is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Tampines Street 62 in District 18 (OCR), developed by UOL, CapitaLand Development & Singapore Land, with 1,193 homes. It launched on Feb 2025. Expected completion (TOP): 30 Jun 2030 (VP 30 Jun 2030). Nearest MRT: ~2 min (453m) to Tampines North MRT.
