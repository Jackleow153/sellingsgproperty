Loyang Valley Redevelopment

Loyang Valley (Loyang) · District 17 (OCR)

Upcoming · Not announcedTenure TBCabout 1,249 units

Loyang Valley Redevelopment is a private condominium at Loyang Valley (Loyang) in District 17 (OCR), developed by SingHaiyi Group and partners (consortium), with about 1,249 homes. Land cost: $880M (collective sale). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices. Nearest MRT: Future Loyang station (Cross Island Line).

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Loyang Valley, a 362-unit condo on an 840,648 sq ft site next to the future Loyang station on the Cross Island Line, was sold en bloc to a SingHaiyi-led consortium for $880M in April 2026. EdgeProp estimates the site could hold about 1,249 new homes; the developer has not announced plans, a launch date or prices.
