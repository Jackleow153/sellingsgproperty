Upcoming · Feb 2027 (reported)99-year leasehold1,056 units

One Chuan Grove is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan in District 19 (OCR), developed by Chuan Grove Pte Ltd (Sing Holdings Residential 65%, Sunway Developments 35%), with 1,056 homes. It is expected to launch in Feb 2027; prices have not been released. Expected completion (TOP): 16 Jul 2030. Nearest MRT: About 400 m to Lorong Chuan MRT (CC14).

Data as of 11 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

One Chuan Grove is a 99-year leasehold condominium of 1,056 homes at Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan, District 19. Developer: Chuan Grove Pte Ltd (Sing Holdings Residential 65%, Sunway Developments 35%). It combines both 2025 Chuan Grove land parcels. The third Chuan Grove Reserve List site is separate and unsold. Nearest MRT is Lorong Chuan (CC14), about 400 m. Launch is expected in February 2027. Preview is reported for 13 February 2027 and booking for 27 February 2027. No developer price list had been released as of 11 November 2026. Analyst estimates put the average around $2,600 to $2,850 psf. Completion is reported as 16 July 2030.
