Springleaf Residence

Upper Thomson Road · District 26 (OCR)

Launched · Aug 202599 LH941 units

Springleaf Residence is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Upper Thomson Road in District 26 (OCR), developed by GuocoLand & Hong Leong Holdings, with 941 homes. It launched on Aug 2025. Expected completion (TOP): Q4 2031 (EdgeProp). Nearest MRT: ~260m to Springleaf MRT.
