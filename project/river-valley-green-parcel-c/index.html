River Valley Green (Parcel C)

River Valley Green (Great World) · District 09 (CCR)

Upcoming · 2027 (est.)99 LH470 units

River Valley Green (Parcel C) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at River Valley Green (Great World) in District 09 (CCR), developed by Sunway MCL & CSC Land Group), with 470 homes. Land cost: $750.6M (about $1,730 psf ppr). It is expected to launch in 2027; prices have not been released. Nearest MRT: Great World (TE15), nearby.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

The fifth and final GLS site in the River Valley Green precinct near Great World. URA awarded it on 23 June 2026 to Sunway MCL and CSC Land Group for $750.6M (about $1,730 psf ppr) after four bids. URA estimates about 470 homes. Market reports expect a 2027 launch; no name or prices have been announced.
