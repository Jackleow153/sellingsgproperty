8 Thomson Lane

8 Thomson Lane (off Thomson Road, Novena) · District 11 (CCR)

Upcoming · Not announced105-year leasehold776 units

8 Thomson Lane is a 105-year leasehold private condominium at 8 Thomson Lane (off Thomson Road, Novena) in District 11 (CCR), developed by Sustained Land and partners (consortium), with 776 homes. Land cost: $578M (plus land betterment charge, est. $436M). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

A Sustained Land-led consortium agreed to buy a 105-year leasehold interest in this 203,073 sq ft hilltop site for $578M (reported in August 2026), with plans for about 776 homes in towers of more than 36 storeys. EdgeProp estimates a further land betterment charge of about $436M. No launch date, project name or prices have been announced.
