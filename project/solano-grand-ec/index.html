Solano Grand (EC)

Senja Close (Bukit Panjang) · District 23 (OCR)

Upcoming · Q1 2027 (est.)99 LH302 units

Solano Grand is a 99-year leasehold executive condominium (EC) at Senja Close (Bukit Panjang) in District 23 (OCR), developed by City Developments Limited, with 302 homes. It is expected to launch in Q1 2027; prices have not been released. Nearest MRT: ~6 min walk to Jelapang LRT; 2 stops to Bukit Panjang (DT1) MRT.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
