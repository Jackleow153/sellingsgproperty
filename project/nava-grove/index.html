Nava Grove

38–42 Pine Grove · District 21 (CCR)

Launched · Nov 202499 LH552 units

Nava Grove is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at 38–42 Pine Grove in District 21 (CCR), developed by MCL Land & Sinarmas Land, with 552 homes. It launched on Nov 2024. Expected completion (TOP): 2028 (VP 14 Nov 2028). Nearest MRT: ~1km / bus to Dover MRT (no easy walking MRT).

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).
