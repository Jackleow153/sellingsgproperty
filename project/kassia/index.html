Kassia

Flora Drive · District 17 (OCR)

Launched · Jul 2024Freehold276 units

Kassia is a freehold private condominium at Flora Drive in District 17 (OCR), developed by Tripartite Developers (Hong Leong Holdings, CDL & TID) (Tripartite Developers Pte Ltd), with 276 homes. It launched on Jul 2024. Expected completion (TOP): Q4 2029 (VP no later than ~Nov 2029). Nearest MRT: bus to Tampines East MRT (~15 min walk).
