Wynwood Grand (EC)

Woodlands Drive 17 (Woodlands South) · District 25 (OCR)

Upcoming · Jan 2027 (booking, est.)99 LH430 units

Wynwood Grand is a 99-year leasehold executive condominium (EC) at Woodlands Drive 17 (Woodlands South) in District 25 (OCR), developed by City Developments Limited, with 430 homes. It is expected to launch in Jan 2027 (booking, est.); prices have not been released. Nearest MRT: ~300m to Woodlands South (TE3) MRT.
