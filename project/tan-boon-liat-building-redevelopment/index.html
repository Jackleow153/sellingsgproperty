Tan Boon Liat Building Redevelopment

315 Outram Road (Havelock) · District 03 (RCR)

Upcoming · Not announcedFreeholdUnits TBC

Tan Boon Liat Building Redevelopment is a freehold private condominium at 315 Outram Road (Havelock) in District 03 (RCR), developed by Kingsford Group. Land cost: $950M (collective sale). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices. Nearest MRT: Havelock (TE16), next to the site.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Kingsford Group agreed in July 2026 to buy the freehold Tan Boon Liat Building for $950M, the largest collective sale of 2026 so far. URA has advised rezoning the industrial site to residential with commercial on the first storey at a plot ratio of 4.9; reports point to twin residential towers of up to about 48 storeys, subject to approval. Unit count, launch date and prices have not been announced.
