Orchard Boulevard (2026 GLS site)

Orchard Boulevard / Tomlinson Road · District 10 (CCR)

Upcoming · Not announced99 LH110 units

Orchard Boulevard (2026 GLS site) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Orchard Boulevard / Tomlinson Road in District 10 (CCR), developed by Intrepid Investments & TID Residential (Hong Leong Group), with 110 homes. Land cost: $181.86M (about $1,755 psf ppr). The developer has not announced a launch date or prices.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Small prime residential site near Orchard Road and Tomlinson Road. The tender drew five bids; URA awarded it on 4 November 2026 to Intrepid Investments and TID Residential, whose top bid was $181.86M (about $1,755 psf ppr). About 110 homes are planned. No launch date, project name or prices have been announced.
