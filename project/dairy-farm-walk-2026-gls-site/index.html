Dairy Farm Walk (2026 GLS site)

Dairy Farm Walk (Dairy Farm) · District 23 (OCR)

Upcoming · 2027 (est.)99 LH480 units

Dairy Farm Walk (2026 GLS site) is a 99-year leasehold private condominium at Dairy Farm Walk (Dairy Farm) in District 23 (OCR), developed by ABR Holdings, LWH Holdings, Macly Capital & RP Ventures (Roxy-Pacific), with 480 homes. Land cost: $427.0M (about $962 psf ppr). It is expected to launch in 2027; prices have not been released.

Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Sources: URA, developer announcements and published launch reports (EdgeProp, Stacked, 99.co, The Business Times).

Overview

Low-density residential site next to the Narra Residences plot. URA awarded it to a consortium of ABR Holdings, LWH Holdings, Macly Capital and RP Ventures (a Roxy-Pacific subsidiary) for $427.0M (about $962 psf ppr) after a five-bid tender that closed on 22 January 2026. About 480 homes are planned. Market reports expect a 2027 launch; the developers have not confirmed a name, date or prices.
